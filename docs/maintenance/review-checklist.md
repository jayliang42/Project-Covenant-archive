# Human Review Checklist | 人工审阅清单

> Status: Draft (AI draft; not human reviewed) | 状态：Draft（AI 草稿，未人工审阅）

Check the cited sources, Scripture references, quotation boundaries and clarity yourself; automated tests do not establish historical accuracy or theological agreement. / 下列15页先按新读者价值，再按历史表述误导风险排序。请亲自核对来源、经文编号、引文边界和易懂程度；自动测试不能证明史实准确或神学观点一致。
