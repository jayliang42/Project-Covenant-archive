### Personal offline-download steps | 个人离线下载步骤

1. Open the [ISV Foundation resource page](https://isvbible.org/resources/) and follow its current e-Sword route.
2. In e-Sword, use its built-in resource downloader and select **International Standard Version** from the English Bible resources.
3. If you retain a downloaded module or personal reading file alongside this checkout, put it under `Bible_Translations/Private_Downloads/`. Git ignores that directory deliberately.
4. Keep the file unmodified and private. Do not commit, mirror, email, or otherwise redistribute the full text from this repository.

The exact app availability, device support, price, and module format are controlled by the external providers and can change. This project links to their official entry points rather than hosting installers or Bible-text files.

## Why It Can Be ESL-Friendly | 为什么它对 ESL 读者相对友好

The ISV Foundation says the translation is written in modern conversational English and is readable for people whose English is a second language. Its introductory material gives a target reading level of grades 8–9 and describes its aim as clear, natural English without slang, national colloquialisms, or confusing regionalisms. On that basis, **ISV is a sensible ESL-friendly version to try**—especially for readers who find older or highly formal English difficult.

这不是说 ISV 对每位英语学习者都一定最简单，也不是对所有英文译本作出客观排名。上述“ESL 友好”是基于译者／出版方公开说明的使用定位，而不是本资料库做的可读性实验。圣经仍含有古代文化、神学词汇、诗歌与比喻；初学者可把它和中文译本并读，并记录自己真正读不懂的词句。

### Practical reading pattern | 实际阅读方式

1. **Read one paragraph in ISV first.** Mark unfamiliar words, repeated words, and the speaker or setting.
2. **Compare with a Chinese translation.** Ask whether the difference is English phrasing, an ancient cultural reference, or a genuine interpretive question.
3. **Check the surrounding paragraph before a dictionary.** Biblical words change nuance according to context.
4. **Use a second English translation when it matters.** ISV is a study aid, not a shortcut around careful reading or the original-language issues behind every translation.

Useful starting passages: Mark 1, Luke 15, John 1, John 15, Ephesians 2, and Philippians 2. They give narrative, parable, Gospel discourse, letter, and poetic material rather than making a judgment from a single verse.

## Translation Profile | 翻译定位

| Question | Responsible summary |
| --- | --- |
| What kind of translation is it? | The publisher calls it a new translation from Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek, rather than a revision or a paraphrase. |
| What is its stated method? | The publisher describes it as “literal-idiomatic”: trying to remain close to the source text while communicating in contemporary English. |
| Is it a replacement for all other translations? | No. A translation involves choices. Compare versions where a wording affects interpretation, and consult notes or scholarly resources for serious study. |
| Does “ESL-friendly” equal “the easiest Bible”? | No. It is an informed reading recommendation based on the publisher’s stated design goals, not a universal readability verdict. |
| Does the free edition contain every study feature? | No. The publisher says free editions omit footnotes and maps; paid editions include them. |
