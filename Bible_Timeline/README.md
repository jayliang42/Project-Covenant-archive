## How to Read Responsibly | 怎样负责任地使用资料

1. Read the biblical passage before the historical note.
   先读经文，再读历史材料。
2. Ask what a source directly establishes and what it only makes plausible.
   区分材料直接确认什么、只是使什么更可理解。
3. Keep event dates, book formation, surviving manuscripts, and later reception separate.
   把故事年代、成书／编集、现存抄本与后期接收分开。
4. Do not use archaeology as a shortcut around interpretation or faith.
   不把考古当作跳过释经与信仰思考的捷径。
