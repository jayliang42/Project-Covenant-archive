| 10 | 撒母耳记下 | 大卫作王 | 承接大卫受膏与扫罗王朝终结 | 建立大卫之约，同时诚实记载大卫犯罪及王室后果 | 应许中的王朝已经建立，却仍需要一位比大卫更完全的王 | **2 Samuel**<br>**Setting:** David’s reign.<br>**Earlier:** David’s anointing and Saul’s dynasty ended.<br>**Contribution:** Davidic covenant, honestly narrated sin and royal consequences.<br>**Handoff:** Dynasty established but needs a king more complete than David. |
| 11 | 列王纪上 | 所罗门至南北国分裂初期 | 承接大卫王位与建殿预备 | [圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)建成、国势达到高峰，又因偶像崇拜走向分裂 | 北国和南国都进入背约、[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)警告与衰败进程 | **1 Kings**<br>**Setting:** Solomon to early divided kingdoms.<br>**Earlier:** David’s throne and [temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple) preparations.<br>**Contribution:** Temple and national peak followed by idolatrous division.<br>**Handoff:** Both kingdoms enter covenant failure, prophetic warning, decline. |
| 12 | 列王纪下 | 分裂王国至[犹大](../Glossary/README.md#judah)[被掳](../Glossary/README.md#exile) | 承接以利亚事工和分裂王国危机 | 解释北国亡于亚述、南国亡于巴比伦是长期背约的结果 | 国、王、地、殿皆失，读者等待归回和更深更新 | **2 Kings**<br>**Setting:** Divided kingdoms to [Judah](../Glossary/README.md#judah)’s [exile](../Glossary/README.md#exile).<br>**Earlier:** Elijah’s ministry and kingdom crisis.<br>**Contribution:** Northern fall to Assyria and southern fall to Babylon explained as sustained covenant failure.<br>**Handoff:** Kingdom, king, land, temple lost; return and deeper renewal awaited. |
| 13 | 历代志上 | 从亚当家谱到大卫晚年；被掳后视角 | 在[被掳归回](../Glossary/README.md#return)后的处境中重新回望身份 | 强调大卫、利未人、敬拜与建殿预备，回答“我们是谁” | 大卫把建殿使命交给所罗门，引向历代志下 | **1 Chronicles**<br>**Setting:** Adam’s genealogies to late David; postexilic perspective.<br>**Earlier:** Returnees reconsider identity.<br>**Contribution:** David, Levites, worship, temple preparations explain who the people are.<br>**Handoff:** David hands temple task to Solomon, leading to 2 Chronicles. |
| 14 | 历代志下 | 所罗门至古列诏令 | 承接建殿预备 | 以圣殿、敬拜、改革和寻求神为中心重述犹大历史 | 以古列允许归回收尾，直接打开以斯拉记 | **2 Chronicles**<br>**Setting:** Solomon to Cyrus’ decree.<br>**Earlier:** Temple preparations.<br>**Contribution:** Retells Judah through temple, worship, reform, seeking God.<br>**Handoff:** Cyrus permits return, directly opening Ezra. |
| 15 | 以斯拉记 | 波斯时期的归回与重建 | 承接古列诏令 | 记述圣殿重建、律法教导和群体身份恢复 | 殿已重建，城与群体秩序仍待修复，引向尼希米记 | **Ezra**<br>**Setting:** Persian-period return/rebuilding.<br>**Earlier:** Cyrus’ decree.<br>**Contribution:** Temple restoration, law teaching, community identity.<br>**Handoff:** Temple rebuilt; city and community order need Nehemiah. |
| 16 | 尼希米记 | 波斯时期的耶路撒冷重建 | 承接归回群体仍然脆弱的处境 | 城墙重建、群体改革与重新立约 | 外在恢复仍不能根治人心反复，留下对更完全更新的等待 | **Nehemiah**<br>**Setting:** Persian-period Jerusalem rebuilding.<br>**Earlier:** Returnees remain vulnerable.<br>**Contribution:** Walls, reform, renewed covenant.<br>**Handoff:** External restoration cannot cure recurring hearts; fuller renewal awaited. |
| 17 | 以斯帖记 | 波斯帝国中的散居犹太人 | 补充“并非所有人都归回”的历史图景 | 神的名字虽未出现，护理与保守却贯穿民族危机 | 显明圣约群体在列国中仍蒙保守，为两约之间散居处境提供背景 | **Esther**<br>**Setting:** Jewish diaspora in Persia.<br>**Earlier:** Not everyone returned.<br>**Contribution:** Providence preserves people in crisis although God’s name is absent.<br>**Handoff:** Covenant community preserved among nations; background to intertestamental diaspora. |

<a id="canon-one-handoff-chain-through-all-66-books"></a>
## One Handoff Chain through All 66 Books | 十、把 66 卷压缩成一条接力链

**Creation and promise**<br>
**创造与应许**
Genesis raises questions of world, sin, and promise → Exodus through Deuteronomy form a redeemed covenant people.<br>
创世记提出世界、罪与应许的问题 → 出埃及记至申命记形成蒙救赎的立约子民。

**Land and kingdom**<br>
**土地与王国**
Joshua through Samuel narrate land, disorder, and kingship → Kings explains exile through covenant failure → Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, and Esther show God’s people continuing through return and diaspora.<br>
约书亚记至撒母耳记讲得地、失序与王的兴起 → 列王纪说明王国因背约走向被掳 → 历代志、以斯拉记、尼希米记、以斯帖记从归回和散居两面回答“神的百姓如何继续存在”。

**Prayer, wisdom, and prophetic hope**<br>
**祷告、智慧与先知盼望**
Poetry and wisdom teach worship and life in every age → Prophets explain judgment and focus hope on new covenant, new heart, Spirit, coming king, divine kingdom, and new creation.<br>
诗歌智慧书教人怎样在每个时代敬拜和生活 → 先知书解释审判，并把盼望集中到新约、新心、圣灵、将来的王、神国与新创造。

**Christ and church**<br>
**基督与教会**
The Gospels proclaim Jesus’ fulfillment of promise → Acts follows Spirit-enabled mission to the nations → Letters shape cross-cultural churches’ faith and life.<br>
四福音宣告耶稣成全应许 → 使徒行传记福音靠圣灵走向万国 → 书信塑造跨文化教会的信仰与生活。

**Consummation**<br>
**终局**
Revelation gathers Eden, exodus, temple, kings, prophets, cross, and resurrection into the Lamb’s victory and new heavens and earth.<br>
启示录把伊甸、出埃及、圣殿、君王、先知、十字架与复活汇入羔羊得胜和新天新地。
