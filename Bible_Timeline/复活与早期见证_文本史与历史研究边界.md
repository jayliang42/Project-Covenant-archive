Without these distinctions, readers may wrongly call any tomb, Roman decree, or later controversy decisive proof of resurrection. The opposite mistake is to suppose that because archaeology cannot test divine action, historical research can know nothing about early resurrection belief.<br>
这七层若不分开，很容易出现两种相反误读：一是把任何墓穴、罗马诏令或较晚论战称为“复活铁证”；二是因为考古不能检验神的行动，就说历史研究对早期复活信仰什么也不能知道。

<a id="resurrection-1-corinthians-15-3-8-one-of-the-earliest-datable-resurrection-proclamations"></a>
## 1 Corinthians 15:3–8: One of the Earliest Datable Resurrection Proclamations | 二、林前 15:3–8：最早可定年的复活宣告之一

<a id="resurrection-1-what-the-passage-says"></a>
### 1. What the Passage Says | 1. 经文本身说了什么

Paul explicitly speaks of receiving and passing on tradition, then lists the following. The Chinese wording below is retained; this English list describes its content rather than replacing a [Bible](../Glossary/README.md#bible) translation.<br>
保罗明确使用“我当日所领受又传给你们”的语言，随后列出：

- [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) died for sins.
  [基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)为罪而死；
- He was buried.
  被埋葬；
- He was raised on the third day.
  第三日复活；
- Appearances to Cephas, the Twelve, more than five hundred brothers, James, and all the [apostles](../Glossary/README.md#apostle).
  向矶法、十二使徒、五百多弟兄、雅各和众使徒显现；
- Paul adds his own experience of an appearance at the end of the witness chain.
  保罗再把自己所经历的显现接在见证链末端。

First Corinthians is usually placed in the mid-50s CE. Gallio’s proconsulship of Achaia anchors Paul’s Corinthian activity around 51–52 CE, with the letter generally later. Thus 1 Corinthians 15 clearly predates the four Gospels in their surviving completed forms.<br>
《哥林多前书》通常放在主后 50 年代中期；迦流任亚该亚方伯的年代锚点，使保罗在哥林多的活动能落在主后 51–52 年前后，书信则一般稍后。这样，林前 15 明显早于四部福音书现存完成形态。

<a id="resurrection-2-how-far-can-we-identify-pre-pauline-tradition"></a>
### 2. How Far Can We Identify Pre-Pauline Tradition? | 2. “前保罗传统”可以说到哪一步

The language of receiving and handing on, expressions atypical of Paul, and the arrangement lead scholars widely to recognize earlier material from at least 1 Corinthians 15:3 onward. They debate:<br>
“领受—传递”的措辞、非保罗惯用表达和排列结构，使研究者广泛承认林前 15:3 起至少包含保罗此前接到的传统材料。争论主要在：

- Whether it ends at 15:5 or extends through 15:6–7.
  传统只到 15:5，还是延伸到 15:6–7；
- When and where Paul received it.
  保罗何时、何地领受；
- Whether the original formula already contained every clause.
  原始公式是否已经包含全部短句；
- Whether burial and third-day resurrection imply an empty tomb or express continuity between death and resurrection.
  “被埋葬”和“第三日复活”是否隐含空墓，还是只表达死亡与复活的连续性。

A cautious formulation is that this tradition predates both Paul’s delivery of it to Corinth and the surviving letter. A fixed form within two or three years of the crucifixion is one possible reconstruction, not a date written on a manuscript.<br>
因此，稳妥表述是：“该传统早于保罗把它传给哥林多人，也早于现存《哥林多前书》。”常见的“十字架后两三年就固定成形”是可能重建之一，不能写成抄本上直接标明的日期。

<a id="resurrection-3-what-p46-establishes"></a>
### 3. What P46 Establishes | 3. P46 证明什么

P46, commonly dated paleographically to the late second or early third century CE, preserves First Corinthians and an early text of chapter 15. It physically attests the letter’s transmission. It is not Paul’s autograph, and its copying date cannot replace the letter’s composition date.<br>
P46（主后 2 世纪末至 3 世纪初的通行古文字学约数）保存《哥林多前书》及林前 15 的早期文本，是“这封信如何流传”的实物证据。它不是保罗原稿，其制作年代也不能代替书信写作年代。

**Evidence assessment:**<br>
**证据结论：**

- **A:** the text of 1 Corinthians 15 exists; P46 physically witnesses an early Pauline letter collection.
  **A：** 林前 15 文本存在；P46 是早期保罗书信集的实物见证。
- **A/B:** by the 50s CE Paul was transmitting a death–burial–resurrection–appearance tradition older than his letter.
  **A／B：** 保罗在主后 50 年代已经传递一个比书信更早的死亡—埋葬—复活—显现传统。
- **C:** the tradition’s exact wording, original location, and precise formation date.
  **C：** 传统的逐词范围、最初地点和精确形成年份。
- **Not established:** independent checking of the five hundred people’s names; an empty-tomb narrative supplied by Paul; or a miracle proved by the manuscript itself.
  **不能推出：** 五百多人名单已经被独立核查；保罗给出了空墓叙事；抄本本身证明神迹。

## Gospel Resurrection Narratives: Shared Core and Narrative Differences | 三、四福音的复活叙事：共同核心与叙事差异

<a id="resurrection-1-first-let-each-gospel-speak-in-its-own-order"></a>
### 1. First Let Each Gospel Speak in Its Own Order | 1. 先让每卷按自己的次序说话

| Comparison / 比较点 | Matthew 28 / 马太福音 28 | Mark 16:1–8 / 马可福音 16:1–8 | Luke 24 / 路加福音 24 | John 20–21 / 约翰福音 20–21 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First visitors | Mary Magdalene and the other Mary | Mary Magdalene, Mary mother of James, Salome | A group of women, several later named | Initially focuses on Mary Magdalene |
| 首批到墓者 | 抹大拉的马利亚和另一个马利亚 | 抹大拉的马利亚、雅各的母亲马利亚、撒罗米 | 一群妇女，后来列名数人 | 起初聚焦抹大拉的马利亚 |
| Time description | After Sabbath, approaching dawn | Very early, at sunrise | At dawn | Early, still dark |

| Geographical focus | Message and climax point to Galilee | Promised Galilee meeting, ending at women’s fear | Jerusalem, Emmaus, waiting in the city | Jerusalem in chapter 20; Sea of Tiberias in chapter 21 |
| 地理重心 | 信息和高潮都指向加利利 | 预告在加利利相见，叙事却停在妇女惧怕 | 以耶路撒冷、以马忤斯及在城中等候为重心 | 20 章在耶路撒冷，21 章转到提比哩亚海 |
| Commission | Make disciples of all nations | Unfinished task of telling disciples and Peter | Witness and wait for power from above | Sent as the Father sent Jesus; shepherd his sheep |
| 差遣重点 | 使万民作门徒 | “去告诉门徒和彼得”留下未完成使命 | 作这些事的见证，并等候从上头来的能力 | “父怎样差遣我，我也照样差遣你们”；牧养主的羊 |

Darkness and sunrise could describe different stages of a journey or simply different authorial time descriptions. One messenger and two are not necessarily logically exclusive. Yet the texts lack enough times and movements to establish a seamless minute-by-minute harmony. Recognize each book’s choices and emphasis before judging compatibility or leaving questions open; do not predetermine either complete identity or necessary contradiction.<br>
“天还黑”与“日出时”可以指一段行程的不同阶段，也可能只是不同作者的时间描写；一位使者与两位使者也未必在纯逻辑上互相排斥。但经文没有给出足够钟点和人物移动资料，让读者证明一张毫无缝隙的分钟级合参表。稳妥方法是先承认各卷的选择和重心，再判断哪些差异可相容、哪些只能保留开放，而不是先决定“必须完全一样”或“必定互相矛盾”。

<a id="resurrection-2-a-minimum-shared-narrative"></a>
### 2. A Minimum Shared Narrative | 2. 可以辨认的最低共同叙事

All four connect Mary Magdalene with the morning after burial. Their minimum common narrative framework is:<br>
四卷都以抹大拉的马利亚连接安葬后的清晨；叙事共有的最低骨架是：

1. Early on the first day of the week, women who followed Jesus visit the tomb.
   七日第一日清早，有跟随耶稣的妇女到墓地；
2. The stone has moved, and Jesus’ body is absent from its resting place.
   石头已经移开，耶稣的遗体不在安放处；
3. Each ultimately interprets the empty tomb through the proclamation of resurrection, rather than leaving characters to infer it from emptiness alone.
   各卷最终都把空墓解释为“他已经复活”，而不是单靠空墓让人物自行推论；
4. The women’s fear, confusion, joy, or report prompts other disciples’ responses.
   妇女的惧怕、困惑、欢喜或报告引出其他门徒的回应；
5. Promises or accounts of appearances, renewed discipleship, and commissioning follow.
   显现的[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)或叙事、门徒更新和差遣传统随后展开。

This is a **shared narrative core**, not an established minute-by-minute sequence. Names, messenger numbers, first reports, appearance order, and geographical climaxes differ. Naming only two people does not itself establish that only two were present.<br>
这是**共同叙事核心**，不是已经证明的逐分钟顺序。四卷没有给出完全相同的人名清单、使者数目、最初报告内容、显现次序或地理高潮；“只列两人”也不能自动证明“现场只有两人”。

<a id="resurrection-3-four-gospels-do-not-mean-four-independent-depositions"></a>
### 3. Four Gospels Do Not Mean Four Independent Depositions | 3. “四福音”不等于“四份彼此独立的口供”

Distinguish four books from four independent chains of information:<br>
研究四福音的证据关系，必须把“有四部书”与“有四条独立资料链”分开：

- Most scholars hold that Matthew and Luke used Mark substantially. Shared empty-tomb features cannot mechanically count as three independent testimonies.
  多数学者认为马太、路加在相当范围内使用了马可，因此三卷对空墓叙事的共同点不能机械计作三次独立作证；
- Matthew and Luke also reshape and expand traditions for theological, geographical, and community purposes. This is neither necessarily arbitrary invention nor a recording of every spoken word.
  马太和路加也按各自神学、地理与群体需要改写、扩充传统；这既不等于任意虚构，也不等于逐句录音；
- John’s relationship to the Synoptics remains debated. Independent tradition, shared oral tradition, knowledge of Synoptic traditions, or knowledge of written texts are competing possibilities. Do not assume the answer when counting independent witnesses.
  约翰与对观福音的关系仍有争论：可以主张它保存独立传统，也可以主张作者知道共同口传、对观传统或某些成书文本；不能先把争论答案写进“独立见证数”；
- First Corinthians 15 predates the Gospels’ surviving completed forms but does not narrate women visiting the tomb. It supplies another layer of early death–burial–resurrection–appearance proclamation, not a fifth empty-tomb report.
  林前 15 比四福音现存完成形态早，却没有叙述妇女到墓的场景。它为早期死亡—埋葬—复活—显现宣告提供另一层资料，不应被假装成第五份空墓报道。

The [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament) preserves several interrelated resurrection traditions with partly different formation histories. That description better fits the texts than either four reporters independently obtaining identical depositions or later authors merely copying predecessors and therefore having no historical value.<br>
因此，最准确的表述是：“新约保存多种彼此关联、形成史不完全相同的复活传统。”这比“四名记者分别采访后给出完全一致口供”或“后书只会抄前书，所以没有历史价值”都更符合文本实际。

<a id="resurrection-4-women-at-the-tomb-memory-burial-roles-and-narrative-function"></a>
### 4. Women at the Tomb: Memory, Burial Roles, and Narrative Function | 4. 妇女为何在墓前：历史记忆、丧葬角色与叙事功能

Mary Magdalene appears in all four and is the morning narrative’s most stable named figure. Mark does not introduce the women suddenly in chapter 16: they watch the crucifixion (15:40–41), see the burial location (15:47), and bring spices (16:1). They link death, burial, and empty tomb.<br>
抹大拉的马利亚在四卷中都出现，是清晨叙事最稳定的具名人物。尤其在马可福音，妇女并非 16 章突然加入：她们远观十字架（15:40–41）、看见安放地点（15:47）、又带香膏到墓前（16:1），形成“死亡—安葬—空墓”的人物连续线。

[Josephus, Antiquities 4.219, evidence anchor 114](./史料与考古旁证索引.md), written around 93/94 CE, requires two or three witnesses and excludes women from formal testimony. It establishes how at least one late-first-century Jewish male elite expressed courtroom rules, with three limits:<br>
[约瑟夫斯《犹太古史》4.219（锚点 114）](./史料与考古旁证索引.md)约在主后 93/94 年写成，其中要求两三名见证人，并说正式作证不应接纳妇女。这能证明至少一位第一世纪末犹太男性精英以这种方式陈述法庭规则，却必须加三层限制：

1. Deuteronomy 19:15 specifies two or three witnesses without explicitly excluding women. Josephus’ addition must not be presented as Moses’ original wording.
   申命记 19:15 只写两三名见证人，没有明文排除妇女；约瑟夫斯的附加说法不能直接冒充[摩西律法](../Glossary/README.md#law)原句；
2. Courtroom eligibility differs from family news, mourning, burial practice, religious proclamation, and everyday credibility. It cannot mean that no ancient person ever believed women.
   法庭资格不等于家庭消息、哀悼、埋葬实践、宗教宣告和日常可信度；不能推成“古代所有人从不相信女性”；
3. Women could naturally undertake mourning, watching graves, and burial care. Their visit has a plausible social setting as well as literary significance in the Gospels’ reversal of disciples’ roles.
   妇女本来就可能承担哀悼、守望墓地和安葬照料，因此她们到墓前既有社会史上的自然性，也有福音书中门徒角色倒转的文学意义。

Women’s first discovery of the empty tomb may add historical plausibility or preserve early memory. But arguing that women could not testify, authors therefore could never invent this, and the tomb is consequently proved empty turns probability into deduction. The criterion of embarrassment is one factor alongside character continuity, literary function, [Gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospels) relationships, early tradition, and audience.<br>
所以，“妇女首先发现空墓”可以增加某种历史可信度，也可能保存早期记忆；但流行的“女性不能作证 → 作者绝不会这样写 → 空墓必然已获证明”把概率性判断变成了演绎证明。所谓“尴尬准则”只能是多个判断因素之一，还须结合人物连续性、文学功能、福音书彼此关系、早期传统和作者受众共同评估。

<a id="resurrection-5-from-deuteronomy-to-the-mishnah-testimony-rules-changed-and-varied"></a>
### 5. From Deuteronomy to the Mishnah: Testimony Rules Changed and Varied | 5. 从申命记到米示拿：证词规则不是一条无变化的直线

Comparison by date and case type shows that a blanket claim about ancient Jewish rejection of women’s testimony is too broad:<br>
把几份材料按年代和案件类型并列，会看到“古代犹太人不接受女性证词”过于笼统：

| Source / 材料 | Approximate date / 大致年代 | Express rule / 明写的规则 | Cautious use / 最稳妥的用途 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deuteronomy 19:15 | Scriptural law | Two or three witnesses, not one; no explicit gender exclusion | Starting text for later interpretation |
| 申 19:15 | 经文法律层 | 单一见证不足，须两三名见证人；没有明写性别排除 | 建立后世解释所处理的经文起点 |
| Philo, Special Laws 4.53–54, anchor 115 | First half of first century CE | Rejects a sole witness because one may err; no added women/slaves exclusion | Another Jewish restatement before or broadly near Josephus; silence does not establish eligibility in all courts |
| 斐洛《特殊律法》4.53–54（锚点 115） | 第一世纪上半叶作品 | 重申不能只收一名见证人，并解释一人可能误认；没有附加妇女／奴仆排除 | 证明与约瑟夫斯大致同时代以前已有另一种犹太重述；沉默不能反推斐洛准许妇女在所有法庭作证 |
| Josephus, Antiquities 4.219, anchor 114 | 93/94 CE | Prefers three, requires at least two; excludes women and slaves | Formal restrictions expressed by a late-first-century elite author |
| 约瑟夫斯《犹太古史》4.219（锚点 114） | 主后 93/94 年 | 偏好三人、至少二人，并明确排除妇女和奴仆 | 说明第一世纪末一位犹太精英所表达的正式证言限制 |
| Mishnah Shevuot 4:1, anchor 116 | Compiled around 200 CE | Witness oath applies to men, not women, excluding relatives and others ineligible | Later rabbinic formal rule, not directly transferable to Jesus’ time |
| 《米示拿·誓言》4:1（锚点 116） | 约主后 200 年编定 | “证言的誓言”适用于男子、不适用于妇女，并排除亲属等不合资格者 | 说明较晚拉比法中的正式证言通则；不能直接倒推到耶稣时代 |
| Mishnah Yevamot 15:1, 15:4; Eduyot 6:1, anchor 116 | Same later rabbinic collection | Accepts woman’s own report and a single witness for husband’s death/remarriage, with conflict-of-interest exclusions | Exceptions depend on case, status, consequences; lack of general eligibility does not mean all speech is untrustworthy |
| 《米示拿·耶瓦莫特》15:1、15:4；《见证集》6:1（锚点 116） | 同一较晚拉比文集 | 在丈夫死亡与准许寡妇再婚的案件中，接受妇女本人陈述、单一见证人，并讨论哪些女性因利害冲突不被接受 | 证明同一法律传统会按案件、身份与后果设置例外，不能把“不具一般正式资格”译成“任何话都不可信” |

The comparison yields four methodological conclusions:<br>
这张表带来四个方法结论：

1. **Do not flatten chronology.** Philo, Josephus, and the Mishnah do not record the same court, place, or year.
   **时间不能压平。** 斐洛、约瑟夫斯和《米示拿》不是同年、同地、同一法庭留下的记录；
2. **Keep rules with their cases.** Property, crime, oaths, marriage status, a husband’s death, and ordinary reports need not use identical evidential thresholds.
   **规则不能脱离案件。** 财产、刑事、誓言、婚姻身份、丈夫死亡和日常报告不必采用完全相同的证据门槛；
3. **Norms are not enforcement statistics.** A legal author’s prescription does not establish every court’s practice.
   **规范不等于执行统计。** 法律作者说“应当怎样”不能自动证明每个法庭“实际怎样”；
4. **Gospels are not court transcripts.** The women are narrative figures, mourners, people who know the burial place, and messengers. Male inspection, group appearances, and commissioning also form each Gospel’s credibility structure.
   **福音书不是法庭笔录。** 妇女在墓前首先是叙事人物、哀悼者、安葬地点知情者和复活消息传递者；后来男性查看、群体显现和差遣叙事也构成各卷自己的可信度结构。

The cautious conclusion is neither that women’s testimony posed no difficulty nor that mentioning women guarantees truth. Women could face disadvantage in some formal public settings, while ancient sources recognize situational differences and the Gospels assign them socially natural, narratively crucial burial roles.<br>
因此，最稳妥的历史判断不是“妇女证词毫无尴尬”，也不是“只要写妇女就必然真实”，而是：妇女在某些正式公共语境中可能处于不利位置；与此同时，古代材料本身承认场景差异，福音书也给妇女安排了社会上自然、文学上关键的丧葬角色。

<a id="resurrection-6-how-each-gospel-builds-narrative-credibility"></a>
### 6. How Each Gospel Builds Narrative Credibility | 6. 四卷各自怎样建立叙事可信度

The women’s report is not the sole endpoint. Each Gospel links death, burial, empty tomb, interpretation, appearance, and commissioning through its own process of recognition:<br>
妇女的报告不是四福音论证的唯一终点。每卷都用自己的方式，把受死、安葬、空墓、解释、显现与差遣连成一条辨认过程：

| Gospel / 福音书 | Continuity of observers / 观察者连续性 | First reports and checking / 初步报告与核查 | Interpretation and recognition / 解释与辨认 | Narrative goal / 叙事终点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Matthew | Women see death, 27:55–56; two Marys face tomb, 27:61 | Angel sends report; Jesus meets women, 28:1–10; guards report to chief priests, generating rival theft account, 28:11–15 | Eleven meet Jesus in Galilee; some doubt, 28:16–17 | Risen authority and mission to all nations, 28:18–20 |
| 马太 | 妇女看见受死（27:55–56），抹大拉的马利亚和另一个马利亚又坐在坟墓对面（27:61） | 天使吩咐她们报告；途中耶稣亲自向她们显现（28:1–10）；守卫另向祭司长报告，形成“门徒盗尸”的对抗叙事（28:11–15） | 十一门徒按指示到加利利见耶稣；有人仍疑惑（28:16–17） | 复活主宣告权柄，差遣门徒使万民作门徒（28:18–20） |
| Mark | Named women see death, 15:40–41, burial place, 15:47, and bring spices, 16:1 | Young man points to empty place and directs report to disciples/Peter; 16:8 ends in flight, fear, and silence | Earliest attainable 16:1–8 lacks male inspection or narrated appearance; messenger explains and promises Galilee | Leaves reporting and following unfinished for disciples/readers; do not import 16:9–20 into what 16:1–8 narrates |
| 马可 | 具名妇女看见受死（15:40–41）、安放地点（15:47），再带香膏到墓前（16:1） | 少年人指出安放处已空，吩咐她们告诉门徒和彼得；16:8 却在她们逃离、惧怕及“没有告诉任何人”处停止 | 现存最早可达到的 16:1–8 没有男性查看或显现场景；读者只得到使者的解释与“往加利利去”的应许 | 结尾把尚未完成的报告和跟随任务交到门徒及读者面前；不可偷用 16:9–20 补成 16:1–8 本身已经叙述的内容 |
| Luke | Galilean women see death, 23:49; follow Joseph and observe tomb and body, 23:55–56 | Report dismissed by Eleven and others; Peter inspects, 24:9–12 | Emmaus recognition through Scripture and bread; Simon report; gathered group sees, touches, watches eating; opened Scripture, 24:13–49 | Witness and wait for power in city, leading into Acts |
| 路加 | 从加利利来的妇女看见受死（23:49），跟着约瑟，看见坟墓和遗体怎样安放（23:55–56） | 她们报告十一使徒和其余的人，却被当作胡言；彼得随后到墓前查看（24:9–12） | 以马忤斯路上由经文解释到擘饼认出；聚集群体又听见西门的报告，继而看见耶稣、触摸并看他吃鱼；耶稣开他们的心明白经文（24:13–49） | “你们就是这些事的见证”，并在城里等候从上头来的能力；叙事接入《使徒行传》 |
| John | Mary at cross, 19:25; not explicitly at burial, undertaken by Joseph/Nicodemus, 19:38–42 | Moved stone first suggests removal; Peter and beloved disciple inspect linen, 20:1–10 | Empty tomb remains ambiguous; seeing/believing differs from scriptural understanding; Mary recognizes Jesus when named, 20:11–18 | Group, Thomas, and Galilee encounters connect seeing, touching, Spirit, writing, and shepherding, 20:19–31; chapter 21 |
| 约翰 | 抹大拉的马利亚在十字架旁（19:25），但约翰没有明说她观看安葬；安葬由亚利马太的约瑟和尼哥底母完成（19:38–42） | 她看见石头挪开，最先推断遗体被移走；彼得和所爱的门徒查看细麻布（20:1–10） | 空墓本身仍有歧义：所爱的门徒“看见就信”，二人却尚不明白经文；马利亚在被呼名后认出耶稣，随后报告“我已经看见了主”（20:11–18） | 聚集门徒、多马及加利利海边的相遇，把看见、触摸、[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)、书写目的和牧养差遣接在一起（20:19–31；21 章） |

Five observations follow:<br>
这四条链带来五个观察：

1. **The women begin a witness chain; they are not its isolated entirety.** Matthew connects them to the Eleven; Luke adds Peter’s inspection, Scripture, and a group encounter; John follows an initial body-removal inference with tomb details, personal recognition, and Thomas. Mark’s earliest attainable text suspends the task at 16:8.
   **妇女是见证链的开端，不是孤立的全部。** 马太把她们接到十一门徒，路加加入彼得查看、经文解释和群体相遇，约翰把“遗体被移”的初步判断接到墓中细节、呼名辨认和多马场景；马可现存最早可达到的文本则把任务悬在 16:8。
2. **Each preserves disbelief, misunderstanding, and hesitation.** Characters are not perfect witnesses who immediately derive complete theology from an empty tomb.
   **各卷保留不信、误解和迟疑。** 它们没有把人物写成一见空墓便立刻得出完整神学结论的完美证人。
3. **Inspections and group appearances confirm within the narrative, but are not new independent archives.** Peter’s inspection and group encounters remain events narrated by Gospel authors. More characters do not mechanically equal more external sources.
   **查看与群体显现在叙事中承担确认作用，却不是新的独立档案。** 彼得查看、十一门徒或聚集群体相遇仍是各福音作者所叙的事件，不能只因人物增多就机械换算成同等数量的外部史料。
4. **Scriptural interpretation identifies events and their meaning without replacing bodily encounter.** Luke combines Scripture, sight, touch, and eating. John distinguishes seeing and believing from not yet understanding Scripture.
   **经文解释说明事件的身份和意义，不代替身体相遇。** 路加把经文、看见、触摸和吃食并列；约翰也把“看见就信”与“尚不明白经文”分开。
5. **Credibility structures differ.** Matthew stresses conflicting reports and universal mission; Mark fear and unfinished mission; Luke cumulative checking and opened Scripture; John mistaken recognition, personal encounter, communal witness, and written purpose. Respect those differences.
   **可信度结构并不相同。** 马太突出两种报告的冲突和普世差遣，马可让读者面对惧怕与未完成使命，路加强调累积核查与经文开启，约翰则从误认、个人辨认走向群体见证和书写目的。比较时应尊重这些差异。

<a id="resurrection-mark-s-ending-honest-manuscript-reading-and-a-firmer-basis-for-faith"></a>
## Mark’s Ending: Honest Manuscript Reading and a Firmer Basis for Faith | 四、马可福音结尾：诚实处理抄本，不让信仰依赖脚注

<a id="resurrection-1-surviving-evidence"></a>
### 1. Surviving Evidence | 1. 现存证据

- Fourth-century Codex Sinaiticus preserves Mark 16:1–8 and begins Luke on the same page.
  四世纪的西奈抄本在同一页保存可 16:1–8，随后开始路加福音；
- Fourth-century Codex Vaticanus has a closing title after Mark 16:8, leaves the next column blank, and begins Luke on the following page.
  四世纪的梵蒂冈抄本在可 16:8 后写卷尾题记，下一栏留白，路加福音从下一页开始；
- Other traditions preserve a shorter ending, the longer ending at 16:9–20, or both together.
  另一些传统保存较短结尾、可 16:9–20 的较长结尾，或两种结尾并列；
- Irenaeus quotes Mark 16:19 in the late second century. The longer ending is ancient, not simply a medieval fabrication.
  爱任纽在二世纪末已引用可 16:19，说明较长结尾很古老，不能简单称为中世纪伪造；
- The 2021 Editio Critica Maior: Mark and recent textual criticism still treat 16:8 as the earliest attainable ending. Whether Mark intended it or an earlier ending was lost remains discussable.
  2021 年出版的 *Editio Critica Maior: Mark* 及近期文本批判研究仍把 16:8 视为“最早可达到的文本”结尾；至于马可是否故意停在这里，还是更早结尾遗失，仍可讨论。

<a id="resurrection-2-implications-for-reading"></a>
### 2. Implications for Reading | 2. 这对读经意味着什么

Mark 16:1–8 already contains an empty tomb, a messenger’s announcement, and resurrection proclamation. Resurrection, appearances, commissioning, and ascension also occur in Matthew, Luke, John, Acts, and Paul’s letters. Therefore:<br>
可 16:1–8 本身已经有空墓、天使信息和“他已经复活”的宣告。基督复活、显现、差遣与升天也见于马太、路加、约翰、使徒行传和保罗书信。因此：

- Footnotes on Mark 16:9–20 express textual honesty, not concealment.
  现代译本给可 16:9–20 加脚注是文本诚实，不是掩盖；
- Do not build a complete doctrine of baptism, exorcism, tongues, handling snakes, or drinking poison on Mark 16:16–18 alone.
  不宜只靠可 16:16–18 建立洗礼、赶鬼、说方言、手拿蛇或喝毒物的完整教义；
- Disputed textual status for the longer ending does not erase New Testament resurrection testimony as a whole.
  也不应因较长结尾的文本地位有争议，就说新约复活见证整体消失。

<a id="resurrection-burial-empty-tomb-and-the-holy-sepulchre-site"></a>
## Burial, Empty Tomb, and the Holy Sepulchre Site | 五、埋葬、空墓与圣墓教堂遗址

<a id="resurrection-1-attested-background"></a>
### 1. Attested Background | 1. 可确认的背景

[Yehohanan’s remains, anchor 48](./史料与考古旁证索引.md), show that one crucified person in first-century Jerusalem later entered a Jewish burial setting. This refutes the absolute claim that crucified people could never be buried; it does not establish Jesus’ specific crucifixion or burial arrangements.<br>
[约哈难遗骸（锚点 48）](./史料与考古旁证索引.md)说明第一世纪耶路撒冷确有被钉者后来进入犹太墓葬体系的个案；它反驳“所有被钉者一律不可能埋葬”的绝对说法，却不能证明耶稣的具体钉法或埋葬过程。

Recent archaeology beneath the Holy Sepulchre identifies extended quarrying followed by agricultural and burial use before major fourth-century Constantinian construction. Roman, Byzantine, medieval, and modern alterations have transformed the traditional chamber. The visible site is not an undisturbed first-century tomb.<br>
圣墓教堂近年的考古工作确认，现建筑下方原有长期采石活动，之后有农业和丧葬用途，四世纪才出现大规模君士坦丁教堂工程。传统墓室经历罗马、拜占庭、中世纪和近现代多次改建，今日所见不是一座未经扰动的第一世纪墓。

<a id="resurrection-2-judgments-requiring-separate-assessment"></a>
### 2. Judgments Requiring Separate Assessment | 2. 不能跳过的判断

| Claim / 命题 | Current grade / 目前可给的等级 | Reason / 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarrying and burial before Christian buildings | A | Recorded strata, rock surfaces, and building phases |
| 教堂下方有基督教建筑以前的采石与丧葬活动 | A | 有地层、岩面和建筑阶段记录 |
| Possible first-century burial environment outside city | B | Compatible with topography and tomb types; wall line and local dates require combined study |
| 此地在第一世纪具备城外墓地环境的可能 | B | 与早期地形及墓葬类型相容，但城墙线路与局部年代仍需综合研究 |
| Traditional site preserves memory of Jesus’ tomb | B/C | Fourth-century tradition and compatible topography, with gaps before third century |
| 传统地点保存了耶稣墓的历史记忆 | B／C | 有四世纪传统和地形上的相容性，也存在三世纪以前证据链缺口 |
| Present chamber is Jesus’ original resting chamber | C | Heavy alteration; tomb type cannot uniquely establish identity |
| 今日墓室就是耶稣曾安放的原始墓室 | C | 遗址改造严重，身份认定不能由墓型唯一决定 |
| The tomb proves resurrection | Not established | Even established identity would not reveal divine action in the 30s CE |
| 该墓证明耶稣复活 | 不成立 | 即使身份成立，考古也不能观察主后 30 年代的神圣行动 |

Choosing between the Garden Tomb and Holy Sepulchre requires burial dates, wall topography, tradition chains, and alteration history, rather than modern aesthetic preference.<br>
“花园墓”与“圣墓教堂”之间的选择，也不能按哪个地点更符合现代审美决定；必须比较墓葬年代、城墙地形、传统链和改造史。

<a id="resurrection-3-main-empty-tomb-models"></a>
### 3. Main Empty-Tomb Models | 3. 空墓研究的主要模型

Two questions must be distinguished: **was the tomb found empty**, and **if so, why**? Ordinary historical methods can compare probabilities for the former; the latter involves competing accounts of human action, tradition formation, and theological worldview. The main models range beyond accepting everything literally or inventing everything:<br>
空墓讨论其实包含两个问题：**墓是否被发现为空**，以及**若为空，原因是什么**。前一个可用通常历史方法比较概率；后一个会进入人类行为、传统形成和神学世界观的竞争解释。常见模型并非只有“照字面全收”与“全部虚构”两端：

| Model / 模型 | Main claim / 核心主张 | Emphasis / 主要强调 | Still to explain / 尚须解释 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early historical core | Burial and women’s discovery of empty place; later Gospel emphases | Continuity of women and place; several Gospel accounts; Jerusalem setting | Differences, Paul’s silence about visit, how early tradition extends |
| 早期历史核心 | 耶稣获安葬，妇女发现安放处为空；各福音后来按自身重点叙述 | 妇女—安葬地点的连续性、空墓传统的多卷保存、耶路撒冷背景 | 福音书差异、保罗未叙墓前故事、传统可追溯到多早 |
| Historical burial with developing details | Body treated/buried, but owner, place, later movement, discovery uncertain; memory and development combined | Varied Roman execution and Jewish burial practices; Gospel editing | Specific reburial/mistaken-place proposals lack direct evidence; stable women’s names and resurrection interpretation |
| 历史安葬、细节逐步发展 | 耶稣确被处理或安葬，但墓主、地点、后来移葬或发现过程不确定；现有叙事混合记忆与发展 | 罗马处刑和犹太葬俗的多样性、福音书形成期的编辑 | 具体移葬／误认方案常缺直接证据，如何产生稳定的妇女人名与复活解释 |
| Literary/proclamatory formation | Empty-tomb story follows appearance belief to narrate bodily resurrection, fulfillment, reversal | Paul emphasizes appearances; Mark’s literary structure; later expansions | Persistence of place and women; later formation alone does not exclude historical core |
| 文学—宣告形成 | 空墓故事在复活显现信仰之后形成，用叙事方式表达身体复活、经文应验和神的逆转 | 保罗传统重显现而不讲发现空墓；马可叙事的文学结构；后卷扩写 | 为何安葬地点与妇女传统如此持久；“后来形成”本身不自动等于“没有历史核心” |
| Specific natural event | Removal, wrong tomb, theft, or other observable cause of emptiness or perceived emptiness | Empty tomb does not uniquely imply resurrection; John 20:2 and Matthew 28:11–15 preserve removal controversies | Who, when, why; appearance proclamation, changed disciples, evidential gaps |
| 具体自然事件 | 遗体被移、到错墓、有人盗取，或其他可观察原因造成墓空／墓被认为空 | 空墓本身不唯一推出复活；约 20:2、太 28:11–15 已保存移尸争论 | 每个方案都需交代谁、何时、为何，并同时解释显现宣告、门徒改变和资料缺口 |
| [Faith](../Glossary/README.md#faith) interpretation of bodily resurrection | Divine bodily resurrection jointly explains tomb, appearances, proclamation, changed group | Jewish eschatological setting; bodily continuity/transformation in 1 Corinthians 15; Gospel encounters | Divine action is not an instrument-measured variable; conclusion combines history, worldview, faith |
| 身体复活的信仰解释 | 空墓、显现、早期宣告和群体转变由神使耶稣身体复活得到整体解释 | 犹太末世复活语境、林前 15 的身体连续与更新、福音书的相遇叙事 | “神的行动”不是考古仪器可直接测量的变量；结论包含历史推论、世界观与信仰判断 |

Each model has many versions. No single object has produced scholarly unanimity. Fair comparison asks not only which details a model explains but what additional assumptions it needs for all the evidence. Paul’s silence does not establish ignorance of an empty tomb; four Gospel accounts do not establish four entirely independent traditions.<br>
这些模型内部仍有许多版本，学界没有靠一项器物达成单一结论。公平比较不能只问“我的模型能解释哪一点”，还要问“它为了解释全部资料，额外假设了什么”。同样，保罗没有叙述空墓不能直接证明他不知道；四福音都叙空墓也不能直接证明四条传统完全独立。

These sources trace several directions of reception, rather than a flat list of additional witnesses. Justin and Celsus engage tensions visible in the Gospels; Ignatius insists on bodily reality against apparent suffering and resurrection. Dura’s images bring Gospel themes into baptism but lack labels fixing their interpretation. Irenaeus and Tertullian place resurrection within the Creator’s renewal of bodies, prophetic fulfillment, judgment, and life. Apostolic Tradition, Marcellus, and Rufinus show initiation questions, continuous confession, and local variants. N of 325 and C2 first fully witnessed in 451 belong to conciliar definition and reception, distinct from the Western R/Apostles’ Creed family. They attest continued confession, argument, imagery, teaching, community identity, and conciliar norms, not an origin at the event scene for every item. Distinguish first-century events and proclamation, Gospel narrative, second-century confession and polemic, rules of faith, baptismal questions, liturgical images, local continuous creeds, conciliar definitions, and later liturgical texts.<br>
这些材料呈现的不是一条平面的“更多证人”清单，而是不同方向的接收史。游斯丁和克理索显示，后世反驳抓住了福音书自身没有掩盖的张力；伊格那丢坚持真实身体，以回应把受苦和复活化为表象的主张；杜拉壁画显示福音意象进入洗礼空间，却也提醒没有文字标签的图像不可强行锁定。爱任纽和特土良把复活放回创造主更新肉身、完成先知应许并把历史带向审判和生命的整条故事；《使徒传统》、马尔凯鲁斯和鲁非努又分别照见故事进入入门问答、连续文本和地方变体。325 年 N 与 451 年首见的 C2 则属于会议定义及其接收史，不能并入西方 R／《使徒信经》支系。它们证明复活宣告**被继续认信、争辩、视觉化、编入教导纲要，并用于群体身份与会议规范**，不是证明每项材料都源自事件现场。研究时至少要分开“第一世纪事件与最早宣告”“福音书内叙事”“第二世纪认信／争论”“信仰规则”“洗礼问答”“礼仪图像”“地方连续信条”“会议定义”和“后来通行礼仪文本”。

| 圣灵与教会使命 | 圣灵贯穿先知性的宣告；各地教会传讲同一骨架 | 升天的基督差圣灵引导信徒 | 把先知、基督与[五旬节](../Glossary/README.md#pentecost)连起来，同时不把两位作者的不同强调抹平 |
| Return and new creation | Glorious return, recapitulation, universal bodily resurrection | Glorious return, righteous/wicked raised, flesh restored | 1 Corinthians 15 and Revelation 20–22 connect Jesus’ resurrection to beginning of new creation |
| 再来与新创造 | 基督荣耀再来、使万有归一并叫全人类的肉身复起 | 基督荣耀再来；义人与恶人复活，肉身复原 | 把耶稣复活接到林前 15 与启 20–22：复活不是故事终点的附录，而是新创造的开端 |

The outlines share a broadly similar narrative order but differ in wording and emphasis; Tertullian may depend on Irenaeus. The cautious conclusion is a **stable narrative framework with variable expression** in the late second and early third centuries, not an identical modern creed everywhere. The rule of faith identifies Scripture’s beginning, turns, and goal. It is neither a second story above Scripture nor a substitute for reading each book in context.<br>
两份纲要的故事次序高度相似，措辞和重点却并不完全相同；特土良也可能受爱任纽影响。因此最稳妥的结论是：二世纪后期至三世纪初出现了**稳定的故事骨架和可变的表达**，不是已经有一份各地逐字相同的现代信经。“信仰规则”帮助群体辨认经文从哪里开始、经过哪些转折、走向什么结局；它不是凌驾于经文之外的第二部故事，也不能替代对每卷圣经自身文脉的阅读。

<a id="resurrection-from-rule-of-faith-to-baptismal-questions-and-continuous-creed-development-not-a-seamless-autograph-chain"></a>
#### From Rule of Faith to Baptismal Questions and Continuous Creed: Development, Not a Seamless Autograph Chain | 从信仰规则到洗礼问答和连续信条：可见的是演进，不是一条无缝原稿链

| Visible stage / 可见阶段 | Textual form / 文本形态 | Securely observable / 可稳妥确认 | Remaining gaps / 必须保留的空白 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Irenaeus/Tertullian, about 180–200 | Variable polemical restatements of rule of faith/truth | Relatively stable creation–prophets–Christ–Spirit–return–bodily resurrection framework | Not identical conciliar/baptismal text everywhere; possible dependence |
| 爱任纽／特土良，约主后 180–200 年 | 作者在论战中以不同措辞复述“信仰／真理规则” | 创造—先知—基督—圣灵—再来—身体复活的次序相对稳定，表达仍可伸缩 | 不是各地逐字相同的会议或洗礼文本；两位作者之间可能有依赖 |
| Surviving Apostolic Tradition 21 | Three baptismal questions/answers with three immersions | Transmitted initiation includes third-day resurrection, ascension, judgment, church, bodily resurrection | Greek original, author, location, editing, clause dates not settled by Hippolytus/215 label |
| 《使徒传统》21 的现传规程 | 三题三答的洗礼问答，并伴随三次入水 | 传本确实把第三日复活、升天、审判、教会与肉身复活用于入门身份 | 原希腊文、作者、地点、最终编辑和各句年代不能仅凭“希坡律陀／215 年”标签解决 |
| Marcellus to Julius, 340/341 | Continuous first-person Greek confession | Earliest fully visible R; important later Western ancestor | Debate over quotation, adaptation, or role in formation; earlier Roman text cannot be restored clause by clause |
| 马尔凯鲁斯致犹流书，主后 340／341 年 | 连续的第一人称希腊文认信 | 这是古罗马信条 R 最早完整可见的形式，并成为后来西方信经的重要祖本 | 马尔凯鲁斯引用、改写还是促成文本仍有争论；更早罗马前身不能逐句复原 |
| Rufinus, about 404 | Latin clause commentary on Aquileia, explicitly comparing Rome | Shared R framework with local descent and this-flesh clauses | Not original liturgical documents from both places; apostolic composition legend not composition evidence |
| 鲁非努《信经评注》，约主后 404 年 | 拉丁文逐条解释阿奎莱亚次序，并明确比较罗马形式 | R 的共同骨架在罗马／阿奎莱亚继续可见，同时已有“降在阴间”“这肉身复活”等地方差异 | 鲁非努的比较不是两地礼文原件；使徒共同写成的起源传说不是成文史证据 |
| Later common Apostles’ Creed | Continuing Western additions, adjustments, stabilization | Continuity of many R clauses can be traced | Do not back-project additions or treat twelve-apostle authorship as established composition history |
| 后来通行的《使徒信经》 | 西方地区文本继续增补、调整并趋于定型 | 可从 R 追踪许多核心条款的延续 | 不能把后加条款倒灌给马尔凯鲁斯，更不能把传统的“十二使徒逐条写成”当作成文史结论 |

Three concurrent facts matter more than assigning one year to a suddenly complete creed: **the framework predates surviving fixed texts; liturgical use encourages stability; fixed texts still vary regionally and develop.** Apostolic Tradition 21 illuminates baptismal questions but does not fill every gap between Irenaeus/Tertullian and R. Marcellus supplies a reliable latest-attestation point for a continuous confession, not grounds for moving every word two centuries earlier. Rufinus directly shows that around 404 one must still distinguish Roman and Aquileian forms.<br>
这条线最值得保留的不是“某年忽然写出整份信经”，而是三个同时存在的事实：**故事骨架早于现存固定文本；礼仪使用会促使表达趋于稳定；固定以后仍有地域变体和继续发展。** 因此，《使徒传统》21 可以帮助观察洗礼问答，却不能单独填平爱任纽／特土良与 R 之间的全部缺口；马尔凯鲁斯文本可以给连续信条设一个可靠的最迟见证点，却不能把整份文字无证据地提前两个世纪；鲁非努则直接证明到约 404 年仍须说“罗马形式”和“阿奎莱亚形式”，而非假定一份到处逐字相同的《使徒信经》。

<a id="resurrection-keep-two-creed-families-distinct"></a>
#### Keep Two Creed Families Distinct | 两条信经谱系必须分开

| Family / 谱系 | Securely visible point / 可靠可见节点 | Later stages / 后续节点 | Valid relationship / 可说的关系 | Invalid relationship / 不可说的关系 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Western baptismal/local family | R, first continuous witness in Marcellus 340/341 | Rufinus compares Rome/Aquileia around 404; later T continues developing | Trace shared framework, many clauses, recitation, additions/omissions | R, Aquileia, current Apostles’ Creed not one identical apostolic autograph |
| 西方洗礼／地方支系 | 古罗马信条 R：马尔凯鲁斯 340／341 年最早连续见证 | 鲁非努约 404 年比较罗马与阿奎莱亚；后来通行文本 T 继续形成 | 核心故事和许多条款延续，地方背诵、增补和删略可追踪 | R、阿奎莱亚形式与今天《使徒信经》不是一张逐字相同的使徒原稿 |
| Eastern conciliar/doctrinal family | Nicene N of 325 | 381 council/lost doctrinal tome; full C2 first clearly attested 451; later liturgy | C2 claims Nicene continuity while retaining, expanding, rewording N | Not simply appended sentences to N or a longer Western R/Apostles’ Creed |
| 东方会议／教义支系 | 325 年尼西亚信经 N | 381 年会议与失传教义长卷；451 年首次完整明见的 C2；后来礼仪接收 | C2 自称与尼西亚信仰一致，并保留、扩展或改写许多 N 的内容 | C2 不是“给 N 末尾直接续写几句”，也不是西方 R／《使徒信经》的长版 |

N and C2 differ in more than length. N addresses the 325 controversy through the Son’s eternal generation, uncreated status, and shared substance with the Father, with only a brief clause on the Spirit. C2 includes Mary, Pilate, burial, scriptural fulfillment, the Spirit and prophets, church, baptism, resurrection, and coming life. Its first clear full conciliar witness is in 451; whether every word was adopted in 381 is reopened by recent C1/C2 reconstructions using Nestorius’ sermons. **N is a secure 325 conciliar definition; the 381 council reaffirmed and interpreted Nicene faith; the 451 records supply C2 and its attribution to the 150 fathers. The exact textual process remains a research question.**<br>
N 与 C2 的区别不仅是长短。N 为 325 年争议集中说明圣子“受生、非受造、与父同质”，圣灵部分只有短句；C2 则补足马利亚、彼拉多、埋葬、“按圣经”、圣灵与先知、教会、洗礼、死人复活和来世生命等整条故事节点。另一方面，C2 在 451 年才首次有完整、明确的会议见证；其每一字是否由 381 年会议正式通过，近年又因聂斯托里讲道所支持的 C1／C2 新重建而重新开放。最稳妥的读法是：**325 年 N 是可确定的会议定义；381 年会议确实重申并阐释尼西亚信仰；451 年档案给 C2 全文和“150 位教父”归属；三者之间的精确文本过程属研究问题。**

<a id="resurrection-two-frequently-misused-roman-sources"></a>
## Two Frequently Misused Roman Sources | 六、两项经常被误用的罗马材料

<a id="resurrection-1-the-nazareth-inscription"></a>
### 1. The Nazareth Inscription | 1. “拿撒勒敕令”

BnF item Froehner.VIII.13 has a twenty-two-line Greek decree prohibiting interference with bodies and tomb-closing stones under threat of death. Its collector only recorded shipment from Nazareth in 1878. It lacks a modern excavation context.<br>
法国国家图书馆藏 Froehner.VIII.13 是一块二十二行希腊文墓葬敕令，禁止移动尸体、封墓石等行为，并以死刑相威胁。收藏者只记它在 1878 年“由拿撒勒寄来”，没有现代发掘语境。

A 2020 stable-isotope study identifies upper quarries on Kos as the likeliest marble source and proposes a link with the desecration of the tyrant Nikias’ tomb under Augustus. This strongly weakens a decree issued specifically in response to Jesus’ empty tomb. Marble provenance does not establish that the finished slab never moved elsewhere; the exact issuing context remains open.<br>
2020 年稳定同位素研究把石材最可能来源指向科斯岛上部采石场，并提出它与奥古斯都时期科斯僭主尼基亚斯墓被毁事件有关。这一研究强烈削弱“罗马皇帝听到耶稣空墓后专门发布敕令”的流行解释；石材来源仍不等于成品必然从未运往别处，精确颁布处境也要保持开放。

**Appropriate use:** an authentic inscription illustrating Roman protection of burial and penal language.<br>
**正确用法：** 它是罗马帝国墓葬保护与刑罚语言的真实铭文。
**Inappropriate use:** direct Roman official corroboration of resurrection, body-theft controversy, or Matthew 28.<br>
**错误用法：** 称它为罗马官方承认复活、盗尸争论或马太福音 28 章的直接证据。

<a id="resurrection-2-tacitus-annals-15-44"></a>
### 2. Tacitus, Annals 15.44 | 2. 塔西佗《编年史》15.44

Writing around 116 CE, Tacitus says Christus suffered the supreme penalty under Pontius Pilate during Tiberius’ reign. The movement spread from Judaea to Rome, where Nero later punished people called Christians.<br>
塔西佗约在主后 116 年写道，“Christus”在提庇留统治时被本丢·彼拉多处以极刑，此运动从犹太传到罗马，后来尼禄惩罚被称为基督徒的人。

This early-second-century Roman author is openly hostile to Christians. His text is useful for the following:<br>
这是一位对基督徒明显不友善的罗马作者所保存的早期二世纪文本，适合说明：

- Memory of Jesus’ execution under Pilate had entered Roman historiography.
  耶稣在彼拉多时期被处决的记忆已经进入罗马史叙述；
- An identifiable Christian community around 64 CE in Rome is central to Tacitus’ account.
  主后 64 年前后罗马已有可辨认的基督徒群体，是塔西佗叙事的核心；
- The movement survived its founder’s death.
  运动在创始者被杀后没有消失。

Tacitus mentions neither empty tomb nor appearances, ascension, or third-day resurrection. He does not identify his information as archives, interrogation, Pliny, Josephus, or common knowledge; recent scholarship still debates sources and the Neronian persecution narrative. It should not be called independent Roman archival verification of resurrection.<br>
它没有提空墓、显现、升天或“第三日复活”。塔西佗也没有交代这几句资料来自档案、审讯、普林尼、约瑟夫斯还是流行知识；近期研究仍讨论信息来源及尼禄迫害叙事。因此不宜把它称为“罗马档案独立验证复活”。

<a id="resurrection-how-historical-research-can-express-conclusions"></a>
## How Historical Research Can Express Conclusions | 七、历史研究可以怎样表达结论

| Claim / 命题 | Suggested grade / 建议等级 | Explanation / 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jesus executed under Pilate | A | Christian texts, Tacitus, Pilate-period background mutually comparable |
| 耶稣在彼拉多执政时期被处死 | A | 基督教文本、塔西佗及彼拉多时代背景可互相参照 |
| Disciples very early proclaimed resurrection and appearance tradition | A/B | Strong letter dating and received/transmitted structure in 1 Corinthians 15 |
| 门徒群体很早就宣讲耶稣已复活，并诉诸显现传统 | A／B | 林前 15 的书信年代和“领受—传递”结构很强 |
| Some followers sincerely believed they experienced appearances | B | Explains early proclamation; experience type and names cannot each be externally verified |
| 若干跟随者真诚相信自己经历了复活主显现 | B | 能解释早期宣告；经验性质和个别名单无法逐项外部核实 |
| Jesus was buried | B | Early formula and multiple narratives agree; Jewish burial context feasible; tomb, place, arrangements disputed |
| 耶稣被安葬 | B | 早期公式与多卷叙事相合，犹太墓葬背景可行；具体墓型、地点和安葬安排仍有争论 |
| Women’s empty-tomb tradition predates Gospels’ surviving final forms | B/C | Complete in Mark and retained later; pre-Markan extent/date not directly measured from manuscripts |
| 妇女发现空墓的传统早于四福音现存最终形态 | B／C | 马可已有完整叙事，后卷持续保存妇女核心；传统在马可以前的范围和年代不能由现存抄本直接测定 |
| Jesus’ resting place found empty on first day of week | B/C | Shared core and continuity support, with literary dependence, Pauline silence, burial-detail, and development debates |
| 耶稣的安放处在七日第一日被发现为空 | B／C | 有共同叙事核心及地点连续性论据，也有文学依赖、保罗沉默、埋葬细节和传统发展争论 |
| History has proved/disproved divine resurrection | Not established | Divine cause and meaning of unique event, not repeatable experiment |
| 历史方法已经证明或证伪“神使耶稣从死里复活” | 不成立 | 这是对独特事件之神圣原因与意义的判断，不是可重复实验结论 |

Methodological naturalism ordinarily compares observable human causes; it does not establish metaphysical naturalism. Believers may infer that God raised Jesus through historical reasoning and faith. Researchers may propose visions, grief experiences, cognitive change, treatment of remains, group memory, or legendary development. Every model must address death, burial, early proclamation, appearance language, empty-tomb tradition, women’s roles, textual differences, and community expansion, rather than selecting one favorable detail.<br>
方法论自然主义只说明历史研究通常先比较可观察的人类原因；它不等于已经证明形而上学自然主义。信徒可以在历史论证基础上作出“神使耶稣复活”的信仰判断，研究者也可以提出异象、哀伤经验、认知转变、遗体处置、群体记忆或传奇发展等自然解释。任何方案都应说明它如何同时处理死亡、安葬、早期宣告、显现语言、空墓传统、妇女角色、文本差异和群体扩展，而不是只挑最有利的一项。

Distinguish a genuine experience from the external event used to explain it. Researchers may judge that disciples sincerely believed they saw the risen Jesus while explaining their experiences differently. Faith further affirms that God’s raising Jesus caused those appearances. The claims are related but distinct.<br>
还要避免把“经验真实”与“所解释的外在事件真实”混为一层。研究者可以判断某些门徒真诚相信见过复活的耶稣，却对经验成因作不同解释；信仰则进一步宣告这些显现确实由神使耶稣复活而发生。两层有关联，却不是同一个命题。

<a id="resurrection-resurrection-ascension-and-the-whole-biblical-story"></a>
## Resurrection, Ascension, and the Whole Biblical Story | 八、复活、升天与整本圣经故事线

Resurrection means more than Jesus’ soul remaining alive:<br>
复活不是“耶稣的灵魂仍活着”这么单薄，而是：

1. **Creation:** bodies and a world under death in Genesis begin entering new creation.
   **创造线：** 创世记中受死亡辖制的身体与世界，开始进入新创造；
2. **[Exodus](../Glossary/README.md#exodus):** Jesus crosses death’s power and leads his people into new life.
   **[出埃及](../Glossary/README.md#exodus)线：** 耶稣越过死亡权势，带领属他的人进入新生命；
3. **Kingship:** [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) 2, 16, and 110 converge in apostolic proclamation; the suffering [Messiah](../Glossary/README.md#messiah) is exalted to rule.
   **王权线：** [诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) 2、16、110 篇在使徒宣讲中汇合，受苦[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)被高举掌权；
4. **[Temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple):** God’s presence reaches the church through risen Lord and Spirit, rather than centering solely on one building.
   **[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)线：** 神的同在不再只围绕一座建筑，而借复活主和圣灵临到教会；
5. **Mission:** the Gospel endings commission the nations; Acts begins from there.
   **使命线：** 四福音的结尾不是“故事结束”，而是万国差遣；使徒行传从这里开始；
6. **Consummation:** in 1 Corinthians 15 Christ is the firstfruits; his resurrection points toward general resurrection and death’s final abolition.
   **终局线：** 林前 15 把基督称为初熟果子；他的复活指向众人复活、死亡最终被废去。

Ascension is royal language of exaltation, the divine right hand, and sending the Spirit, rather than merely withdrawal into distant space. Acts 1’s cloud, witness, and return promise connect Daniel 7, Psalm 110, Pentecost, and Revelation’s new creation.<br>
升天也不是耶稣退到遥远空间，而是“被高举、坐在神右边、差遣圣灵”的王权语言。徒 1 的云彩、见证和再临应许，把但以理书 7 章、诗篇 110 篇、五旬节和启示录的新创造连成一条线。
