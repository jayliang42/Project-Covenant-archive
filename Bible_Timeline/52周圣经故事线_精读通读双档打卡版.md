> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> Choose focused passages or the full reading track for the same 52-week story. The full track covers the Protestant [Bible](../Glossary/README.md#bible)’s 66 books and 1,189 chapters. The linked research plan explains each week’s connections and evidence limits.<br>
> 同一条五十二周故事线提供重点精读和全本通读两档。通读档覆盖新教传统六十六卷、一千一百八十九章。配套研究计划说明每周的故事连接和旁证限制。

> Choose a track suited to you and follow one storyline through the year. **Deep reading** focuses on each week’s turning points and connections; **through-reading** covers all 66 books and 1,189 chapters of the Protestant Bible in 52 weeks. Here 52 means weeks or story units; the linked 52-book selection is a separate route.<br>
> 用法：选择适合自己的档位，用一年沿着同一条故事线读经。**核心精读档**抓住每周转折和书卷连接；**全本通读档**在 52 周内完整读完新教传统 66 卷、1,189 章。这里的“52”指 52 周／52 个故事单元；字面上的 52 卷路线另见[精选主读与 14 卷桥接版](./52卷圣经故事主线_精选与14卷桥接版.md)。

<a id="track-boundaries"></a>
### Using the Tracks Responsibly | 使用边界

- Deep reading includes some deliberate rereading, such as Daniel and Malachi in week 34. **The claim of no omitted or repeated chapters applies only to the 1,189-chapter through-reading column.**
  精读列会有少量跨周重读，例如第 34 周重读《但以理书》和《玛拉基书》来跨入两约之间；**1,189 章“无漏章、无重复”只指全本通读列**。
- Through-reading follows natural story units rather than equal weekly chapter counts. Genealogy, poetry, narrative, and prophetic oracles also differ in length, so chapter totals alone do not measure the work.
  通读量按故事自然单元安排，不追求每周章数完全相同。家谱、诗歌、叙事和[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)神谕的单章长度也不能只用数字衡量。
- [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) accompanies weeks 1–44 as a prayer thread, turning historical study toward worship, lament, confession, and hope. In week 32, use the linked 150-psalm index to trace [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament) quotation, reuse, and echoes.
  《[诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)》作为祷告支线贯穿第 1–44 周。这样历史不会只成为资料，也不断被转化为敬拜、哀哭、认罪与盼望；第 32 周可配合[150 篇—新约引用、重用与回响索引](./诗篇150篇_新约引用重用与回响索引.md)反查。
- Week 34 completes the wisdom books in through-reading. Deep reading returns to Daniel and Malachi alongside the linked intertestamental study. Whether the deuterocanonical or apocryphal books are canonical depends on [church](../Glossary/README.md#church) tradition; they are not included in this 66-book count.
  第 34 周的“全本通读”收束智慧书；“核心精读”则重读但以理与玛拉基，并配合[两约之间专题](./两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md)。次经／旁经是否作为[正典](../Glossary/README.md#canon)，应按所属[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)传统处理，不计入本表 66 卷覆盖数。

<a id="weekly-five-steps"></a>
### Five Steps Each Week | 每周五步

1. `□` Record the date and read this week’s handoff sentence first.
   `□` 写下日期，先读本周“接力句”。
2. `□` Complete your chosen track. Mark unclear passages without rushing to look up answers.
   `□` 完成所选档位，经文不明处先作记号，不急着查答案。
3. `□` Use the linked 205-anchor crosswalk to find this week’s sources. Mark the EV, BG, MS, RX, or UT type first, then read at least one statement of what the material can and cannot support.
   `□` 到[205 个旁证锚点对应表](./旁证锚点_52周与66卷对应表.md)查本周史料，先标 EV／BG／MS／RX／UT 类型，再至少读一项“能支持／不能支持”。
4. `□` Write one sentence: What did God do? How did people respond? What does the next stage still lack?
   `□` 写一句总结：神做了什么？人怎样回应？故事下一步缺少什么？
5. `□` If this week appears among the eleven-theme matrix’s key handoff points, put its code beside the date. Return to the research plan for the main-thread handoff or companion question; one week may have several codes.
   `□` 若本周列在[十一条主题线矩阵的关键接力站](./52卷十一条主题线_逐卷追踪矩阵.md)，就在日期旁写下主题代码，并回到研究版完成“主干接力”或“伴线追问”；同周可以有多个代码。
