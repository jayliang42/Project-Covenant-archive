<a id="evidence-types-distinguish-two-axes"></a>
## Distinguish Two Axes | 一、先分清两条轴

- **A–D measures strength:** how strong this particular judgment is.
  **A–D 是强度：** 这项具体判断有多强。
- **EV: event/person.** External sources touch people, governmental action, war, displacement, execution, historical coordinates.
  **EV｜事件／人物：** 外部材料触及人物、政权行动、战争、流亡、处决等历史坐标。
- **BG: institution/background.** Cities, law, labor, administration, worship, comparative literature explain context.
  **BG｜制度／环境：** 城市、法律、劳动、行政、敬拜或比较文学使处境可理解。
- **MS: manuscript transmission.** Copies, fragments, versions attest circulation at a time/place.
  **MS｜文本抄传：** 抄本、残卷或译本证明文本在某时某地流传。
- **RX: interpretation/reception.** Exegesis, festivals, controversy, liturgy, lists, creeds show use.
  **RX｜解释／接收：** 古代释经、节期实践、争论、礼仪、书目或信经说明文本怎样被使用。
- **UT: not directly testable.** Objects cannot decide miracles, revelation, atonement, resurrection, theological fulfillment. UT bounds extrapolation; it is not evidence.
  **UT｜不可直接检验：** 神迹、启示、赎罪、复活和神学实现不能由器物本身裁定。UT 是停止外推的边界，不是证据。

More types do not make events more certain; fewer do not lower a book’s value. A book may have grade-A MS without contemporary EV, or strong EV without external proof of its theological interpretation.<br>
类型多不等于事件更确定；类型少也不等于书卷较不重要。一本书可以有 A 级 MS，却没有同期 EV；也可以有很强的 EV，却仍不能由外部材料证明它对事件的神学解释。

<a id="evidence-types-3-universal-rx-entry-coverage-does-not-mean-equal-depth"></a>
### 3. Universal RX Entry Coverage Does Not Mean Equal Depth | 3. 66 卷都有 RX，不等于接收深度相同

Batch 71 used Melito/Origen named catalogues to close 22 RX gaps. Each of 66 now has ancient use, commentary, version, liturgy, or naming, not equally early or specific reception.<br>
第 71 批以梅利托、俄利根两份具名旧约书目闭合最后 22 卷的 RX 类型空白；这表示 66 卷现在都至少有一项古代使用、注释、版本、礼仪或列名入口，不表示每卷已经有同样早、同样具体的接收史。

- **CAT: catalogue.** Naming within a collection does not automatically establish commentary or local liturgical reading.
  **CAT／书目列名**只能证明某作者把一卷列入某个集合；不能自动证明他逐句注释或当地礼仪实际宣读。
- **USE: literary use.** Stronger evidence of a text entering argument than naming alone, but unnamed use still requires a dependence argument.
  **USE／文学使用**比只有列名更能说明文本进入论证，但未注明书名时仍须论证直接依赖。
- **COM: continuous commentary.** Shows interpretive method within a particular community/time.
  **COM／连续注释**可以观察具体解释方法，却只代表相关群体和时代。
- **LEC: liturgical selection.** Actual selected passages do not make unselected books noncanonical.
  **LEC／礼仪选读**证明实际选段，不等于未选书卷被判为非正典。
- **VER: version/collection.** Inclusion in language/carrier does not imply identical scope everywhere.
  **VER／译本或合订本范围**证明某种语言／载体收录，不等于所有地区范围一致。

At 204–205, Melito omits Esther and does not name Lamentations; Origen’s surviving enumeration omits the Twelve and includes Letter of Jeremiah. Their explicit evidence complements without forming one seamless ancient Protestant-39 list. See [counting and course guide](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md).<br>
尤其是锚点 204–205：梅利托没有列《以斯帖记》，也没有明写《哀歌》；俄利根现传枚举漏《十二先知》，并把《耶利米书信》纳入耶利米组合。二者可以按明文互补，却不能拼成一张无缝、等同现代新教 39 卷的古代清单。详见[22／24／39 卷与 52 卷路线桥接](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md)。

## How to Use the Table | 五、怎样使用这张表

1. Before reading a book, check type, then anchor’s A–D strength and source.
   读每卷前先看“主要类型”，再进入锚点索引阅读 A–D 强度和来源。
2. For MS without EV, say text existed by a date, not archaeology proved the event.
   若有 MS 无 EV，把结论写成“这段文本至迟在某时已流传”，不要写成“考古证明故事发生”。
3. For EV, separate people, places, governmental actions, theological interpretation into different sentences; one item cannot cross every layer.
   若有 EV，把人物、地点、政权行动和神学解释拆成不同句子；不要用同一证据跨越层次。
4. For BG/UT, explain intelligible background and limits on directly testing proclamation, without false certainty or silence-as-disproof.
   若只有 BG／UT，诚实写“背景可理解、核心宣告不可由考古直接检验”，不必伪造确定感，也不能把沉默直接当反证。
5. Changing anchors/types requires checking selected route, eleven-thread matrix, weekly crosswalk, maintenance log together.
   调整任何一卷锚点或类型时，必须同步检查 52 卷精选版、十一条主题矩阵、52 周对应表和维护日志。
