<a id="t1-23a-8"></a>
## 1. Main Conclusions | 一、先给结论

The safest reconstruction neither claims archaeology has proved Joshua city by city nor declares the whole account fiction because one site does not fit. Instead:<br>
这一阶段最稳妥的历史重建，不是“考古已经逐城证明《约书亚记》”，也不是“有一处遗址不合，所以整段都是虚构”，而是：

1. By the late thirteenth century BCE, Egyptian sources attest a group called Israel in Canaan.
   主前 13 世纪末，埃及文献已确认迦南地存在一个名为“以色列”的群体。
2. Thirteenth–twelfth-century Canaan experienced declining Egyptian power, city-state breakdown, local destruction, population movement, and economic change.
   主前 13–12 世纪的迦南经历埃及霸权衰退、城邦体系解体、局部毁坏、人口移动和经济重组。
3. Jericho, Ai, and Hazor do not share one date or destruction pattern. Hazor most clearly preserves a major fire at the end of the Late Bronze Age.
   耶利哥、艾城、夏琐并没有呈现同一年代、同一模式的毁坏层；只有夏琐较清楚地保存了晚青铜时代末的大火毁城。
4. Hundreds of small farming/herding villages appeared in the central highlands in the twelfth–eleventh centuries. Many likely belonged to communities later forming Israel, but pots and houses do not identify ethnicity like identity cards.
   主前 12–11 世纪，中央山地出现数百个小型农牧村落；其中相当一部分很可能属于后来形成以色列的群体，但陶器和房屋本身不能像身份证一样标明族群。
5. At the same time, Philistine culture with Aegean connections appeared along the southern coast; Ashkelon ancient DNA also indicates a brief influx of European-related ancestry around the Bronze–Iron transition.
   同期南部沿海出现带爱琴海联系的非利士文化；亚实基伦古 DNA 也显示青铜—铁器时代转折时有一次短暂的欧洲相关基因输入。
6. Dispersed villages, regional conflicts, and temporary leaders in Judges fit an early Iron Age setting, but archaeology cannot individually identify Deborah, Gideon, Samson, and others.
   士师时代的分散村落、区域性冲突和临时领袖，与铁器时代初期的社会图景相容；但考古不能逐一确认底波拉、基甸、参孙等个人。
7. Three Qumran Judges and four Samuel [manuscripts](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript) extend background archaeology into direct text history. 4Q49’s shorter Judges 6:7–10 and 4Q51’s longer Nahash reading are real ancient variants, not automatically original texts.
   昆兰三份《士师记》和四份《撒母耳记》残卷把本阶段从考古背景延伸到直接文本史；4Q49 的士 6:7–10 短读与 4Q51 的拿辖长读都是真实古代变体，却不能自动判为“原版”。
8. In the eleventh–tenth centuries, some villages were abandoned and some centers fortified as political integration grew: a suitable transition for Saul, David, and Solomon.
   到主前 11–10 世纪，部分村落被放弃，一些中心开始筑防，政治整合增强；这为扫罗、大卫、所罗门时代提供了合适的转型背景。
9. The ninth-century Tel Dan inscription calls [Judah](../Glossary/README.md#judah)’s dynasty House of David. This major external witness supports memory of a dynastic founder, not every Samuel biography detail.
   特尔但碑在主前 9 世纪以“大卫之家”称呼[犹大](../Glossary/README.md#judah)王朝，是这一阶段最重要的王朝外证；它支持大卫作为王朝奠基者的历史记忆，却不能证明《撒母耳记》中每一段生平。
10. Khirbet Qeiyafa shows a planned fortified center in the Judah lowlands about 1000 BCE, challenging claims of no complex tenth-century organization. Its rulers and identification with Shaaraim remain disputed.
    基伯特·凯雅法证明约主前 1000 年犹大低地已有规划严整的设防中心；它对“10 世纪犹大毫无复杂政治组织”的说法构成挑战，但遗址究竟由谁统治、是否就是沙拉音，仍有争论。
11. Solomonic gates are not a uniformly certified group. Hazor, Gezer, and Megiddo have different dating/attribution debates; some researchers now assign Megiddo’s six-chamber gate to the ninth-century Omrides.
    所谓“所罗门城门”并不是一组已经盖章确认的建筑：夏琐、基色、米吉多的年代和归属并不完全一致，尤其米吉多六室城门现多被一些研究者下调至主前 9 世纪暗利王朝。

In one sentence:<br>
一句话概括：

> Archaeology supports the broad emergence of Israel during the Late Bronze–Iron transition and movement from highland villages to kingdoms over generations; individual Joshua campaigns, David’s state size, and Solomon’s projects need separate assessment.<br>
> 考古支持“以色列在晚青铜—铁器时代转折中出现，并在数代之内由山地村落社会走向王国”的大轮廓；至于约书亚各战役、大卫王国规模和所罗门具体工程，证据强弱必须逐项判断。

## 3. First Read Joshua’s Own Narrative Carefully | 三、先读清《约书亚记》自己怎样叙事

<a id="t1-23a-48"></a>
### 3.1 Summaries of Comprehensive Victory | 3.1 全面得胜的总结

Joshua offers broad summaries:<br>
《约书亚记》有宏观总结：

- Joshua defeats the kings of the hill country, Negev, lowlands, and slopes (Joshua 10:40).
  约书亚击杀“山地、南地、高原、山坡和一切的王”（书 10:40）。
- Joshua takes the whole land (Joshua 11:16, 23).
  约书亚“夺了那全地”（书 11:16、23）。
- God delivers surrounding enemies into their hands (Joshua 21:44).
  神“将他们四围的一切仇敌都交在他们手中”（书 21:44）。

From the book’s theological perspective, these announce decisive resistance overcome and land promises beginning realization.<br>
这些句子从全书神学角度宣告：主要抵抗已被击破，分地的[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)可以开始落实。

<a id="t1-23a-58"></a>
### 3.2 Lists of Unfinished Possession | 3.2 尚未完成的清单

The same book explicitly says:<br>
同一卷书又明确说：

- Much land remains when Joshua is old (Joshua 13:1–6).
  约书亚年老时，仍有“许多未得之地”（书 13:1–6）。
- Jerusalem’s Jebusites remain (Joshua 15:63).
  耶路撒冷的耶布斯人尚未被赶出（书 15:63）。
- Gezer’s Canaanites still live among Ephraim (Joshua 16:10).
  基色的迦南人仍住在以法莲中间（书 16:10）。
- Manasseh cannot expel several cities’ inhabitants, later imposing labor (Joshua 17:12–13).
  玛拿西不能赶出若干城的居民，后来只是使其服苦役（书 17:12–13）。
- Judges 1 continues listing peoples the tribes failed to expel.
  《士师记》1 章继续列出各支派未能赶出的居民。

Thus taking the whole land need not mean every city destroyed in one campaigning season and all inhabitants immediately gone. Ancient war accounts use comprehensive victory language; the book distinguishes military breakthrough, land allotment, and long settlement.<br>
因此，“夺了全地”不必读成“每一座城在同一季军事行动中被毁、所有原居民立刻消失”。古代战争叙事常用全面性语言表达决定性胜利；本书内部则把军事突破、领土分配和长期定居放在不同层次。

<a id="t1-23a-70"></a>
### 3.3 Three Narrative Levels | 3.3 三个叙事层次

| Level / 层次 | Biblical material / 经文内容 | Archaeological comparison / 与考古对话的方式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisive campaigns | Jericho, Ai, southern/northern coalitions, Hazor | Compare each site’s dates, defenses, destruction |
| 决定性战役 | 耶利哥、艾、南方与北方联盟、夏琐 | 可逐遗址比较年代、城防和毁坏层 |
| Land allotment | Tribal inheritances in Joshua 13–21 | Territorial/covenantal claims, not actual control everywhere |
| 土地分配 | 书 13–21 章按支派划分产业 | 是领土权利与盟约秩序，不等于每处都已实际控制 |
| Long occupation | Remaining peoples, intermarriage, labor, repeated conflict | Compare coexistence, gradual expansion, mixed early Iron society |
| 长期占领 | 未赶出的居民、通婚、劳役、反复冲突 | 与铁器时代初期长期共存、渐进扩张和混合社会更容易对话 |

This distinction matters:<br>
这一区分很重要：

> Reducing Joshua to a blitz eliminating all Canaanites is difficult both archaeologically and in relation to Joshua/Judges’ own accounts.<br>
> 若把《约书亚记》压缩成“一次闪电战消灭全部迦南人口”，不仅考古资料难以支持，连《约书亚记》和《士师记》的内部叙述也会彼此冲突。

## 4. Historical Setting: Canaan Was Not a Power Vacuum | 四、历史舞台：迦南不是权力真空

<a id="t1-23a-86"></a>
### 4.1 Late Bronze Egyptian Rule | 4.1 晚青铜时代的埃及统治

Through much of the Late Bronze Age, Canaan’s city-states lay within Egyptian influence. Local rulers, palaces, and villages operated under garrison, diplomatic, and tribute pressures.<br>
在晚青铜时代大部分时期，迦南城邦处于埃及帝国势力范围。地方有各自的城主、宫殿和附属村庄，却受埃及驻军、外交和贡赋体系影响。

This means:<br>
这意味着：

- Canaan was a network of cities and countryside, not one centralized nation-state.
  “迦南”不是一个中央集权国家，而是许多城邦与乡村网络。
- Israel’s entry/formation cannot be reduced to war between two modern nation-states.
  以色列进入或形成的过程，不只是两个现代民族国家之间的战争。
- Local fighting, city revolt, pastoral settlement, escape from palace control, and external migration could coexist.
  地方冲突、城邦反叛、牧民定居、逃离宫殿控制的人口、境外迁入群体，可以同时存在。

The University of Chicago’s Institute for the Study of Ancient Cultures [Ancient Israel catalogue](https://oi.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oimp31.pdf) outlines Egyptian control, then Egyptian/Mycenaean/Syrian–Anatolian decline after about 1200 BCE and new regional communities.<br>
[芝加哥大学古代文化研究所的《Ancient Israel》展览图录](https://oi.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oimp31.pdf)概述：晚青铜时代迦南受埃及控制；约主前 1200 年后，埃及、迈锡尼及叙利亚—安纳托利亚体系衰退，新的区域群体在权力真空中形成。

<a id="t1-23a-98"></a>
### 4.2 The Bronze Age Collapse Was Not One Disaster | 4.2 “青铜时代崩溃”不是单一灾难

Palaces and trade networks across the eastern Mediterranean broke down in the late thirteenth–twelfth centuries. Possible factors include:<br>
主前 13 世纪末到 12 世纪，东地中海许多宫殿和贸易网络瓦解。可能因素包括：

- Major wars and imperial withdrawal;
  大国战争与帝国撤退；
- Regional drought and food pressure;
  区域性旱灾和粮食压力；
- Internal revolt and inequality;
  内部叛乱与社会分层危机；
- Sea/land migration;
  海上与陆上人口迁移；
- Disrupted long-distance trade;
  长距离贸易中断；
- Mutually amplifying pressures.
  各因素互相放大。

Sea Peoples alone cannot explain the whole collapse; Israel is not the sole cause of every Canaanite destruction.<br>
“海上民族来袭”不能独自解释整个崩溃，以色列也不能被视为所有迦南毁城的唯一原因。

<a id="t1-23a-111"></a>
### 4.3 Merneptah: Earliest External Israel | 4.3 梅尔内普塔碑：最早的“以色列”外证

Around 1208 BCE, Pharaoh Merneptah’s victory stele names Israel among Canaan campaigns.<br>
约主前 1208 年，埃及法老梅尔内普塔的胜利碑在迦南战绩中提到“以色列”。

Most securely, it shows:<br>
它最可靠地说明：

- A group called Israel by an outside power already in Canaan;
  当时迦南已有一个被外部政权称为“以色列”的群体；
- A determinative usually read as people rather than city-state;
  其书写限定符通常被理解为“人群”而非城邦；
- Israel’s emergence cannot be placed far beyond the twelfth century BCE.
  以色列的形成不能晚到主前 12 世纪之后很久。

It does not show:<br>
它不能说明：

- When or whence this group entered Canaan;
  这个群体何时、从哪里来到迦南；
- Whether everyone shared an Exodus experience;
  是否所有成员都有共同出埃及经历；
- The year of Joshua’s campaigns;
  约书亚的战役发生在何年；
- The organization of the biblical twelve tribes at that time.
  经文所述十二支派在当时已经形成何种组织。

**Evidence grade: A** for name, Canaan setting, late thirteenth century; not direct evidence for Exodus/conquest routes.<br>
**证据等级：A**（名称、迦南地域、13 世纪末）；对出埃及和征服路线不是直接证据。

## 5. Three Cities Need Separate Conclusions | 五、三座焦点城市：不能用一个结论包办

<a id="t1-23b-210"></a>
## 5.1 Jericho / Tell es-Sultan | 5.1 耶利哥／Tell es-Sultan

<a id="t1-23b-212"></a>
### Scripture | 经文

Joshua 2 and 6 depict a fortified entry city west of the Jordan: Israel circles it, walls fall, city burns.<br>
约书亚记 2、6 章把耶利哥写成约旦河西岸入口处的设防城市。以色列人绕城，城墙倒塌，城市被焚。

<a id="t1-23b-216"></a>
### What the Site Establishes | 遗址确定了什么

- Identification of Tell es-Sultan with ancient Jericho is secure.
  Tell es-Sultan 与古代耶利哥的认定很稳。
- A very long occupation history; [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1687/) lists about 29 major phases.
  此地有极长的居住史。[联合国教科文组织](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1687/)列举约 29 个主要居住阶段。
- Middle Bronze Jericho was a large fortified city, severely burned.
  中青铜时代耶利哥曾是大型设防城市，并遭严重火毁。
- High-precision radiocarbon places the major end-Middle-Bronze destruction in the late seventeenth/sixteenth century BCE, preliminary calibration about 1600–1525 BCE. See Bruins/van der Plicht in [Radiocarbon](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/tell-essultan-jericho-radiocarbon-results-of-shortlived-cereal-and-multiyear-charcoal-samples-from-the-end-of-the-middle-bronze-age/49BF732173E7890A2B0EC1B21CB6A817).
  高精度碳十四研究把这次中青铜时代末毁坏放在主前 17 世纪末或 16 世纪；初步校准集中到约主前 1600–1525 年。参见 Bruins 与 van der Plicht 的[《Radiocarbon》研究](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/tell-essultan-jericho-radiocarbon-results-of-shortlived-cereal-and-multiyear-charcoal-samples-from-the-end-of-the-middle-bronze-age/49BF732173E7890A2B0EC1B21CB6A817)。

<a id="t1-23b-223"></a>
### Difficulties | 难点

That dramatic destruction:<br>
这次壮观的城防毁坏：

- Predates common thirteenth-century entry models by centuries;
  比常见的晚期进入方案（约主前 13 世纪）早数百年；
- Often also appears too early for fifteenth-century models;
  对约主前 15 世纪的早期方案，也常显得偏早；
- Does not mean no Late Bronze activity, but settlement size, defenses, date remain disputed; cannot simply describe a flourishing city matching Joshua 6.
  晚青铜时代耶利哥并非完全没有活动，但其规模、城防状态和具体年代仍有争论，不能简单描述成一座与书 6 完全对应的繁荣大城。

<a id="t1-23b-231"></a>
### How to Conclude | 怎样下结论

| Claim / 说法 | Assessment / 评价 |
| --- | --- |
| Jericho had walls and fire destruction | Correct, but date the most conspicuous destruction |
| “耶利哥确实有古城墙和火毁” | 正确，但必须注明那次最明显的毁坏年代 |
| Fallen walls prove Joshua 6 | Overstated; chronology is central dispute |
| “倒塌城墙已证明约书亚记 6 章” | 过度；年代对应正是核心争议 |
| No Late Bronze inhabitants, therefore Scripture false | Also overstated; distinguish later activity/erosion/preservation |
| “晚青铜时代此地绝对无人，所以经文必假” | 也过度；后期活动、侵蚀与地层保存仍须细分 |
| One of conquest models’ hardest sites | Safest assessment |
| “耶利哥是征服模型最棘手的遗址之一” | 最稳妥 |

**Evidence grade: B** for geography/ancient fortification background; specific destruction equated with Joshua 6 is **C**.<br>
**证据等级：B**（城市地理与古代城防背景）；把特定毁坏层等同书 6 为 **C**。

<a id="t1-23b-244"></a>
## 5.2 Ai / Usually Identified with et-Tell | 5.2 艾城／通常认定为 et-Tell

<a id="t1-23b-246"></a>
### Scripture | 经文

Joshua 7–8 recount initial defeat, Achan’s offense, then ambush, capture, and burning.<br>
约书亚记 7–8 章记述第一次进攻失败，亚干事件之后，以色列用伏兵攻取并焚烧艾城。

<a id="t1-23b-250"></a>
### Usual Identification | 通行遗址认定

East of Bethel, et-Tell’s terrain and Ai/ruin naming tradition make it the most common candidate.<br>
et-Tell 位于伯特利以东，地形和“艾／废墟”的名称传统使它成为最常见的候选地。

Systematic excavations found:<br>
系统发掘发现：

- A substantial fortified Early Bronze city;
  一座规模可观、设防的早青铜时代城市；
- Destruction at the Early Bronze end;
  早青铜时代末被毁；
- A long interval without a Middle/Late Bronze city;
  此后长时间没有中、晚青铜时代城市；
- A small village only in Iron I.
  铁器时代 I 期才出现较小村落。

Pennsylvania radiocarbon samples attest [Early Bronze and Iron Age remains](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8AB039BCBE1F826FEE240542C2F562F5/S0033822200003532a.pdf/university-of-pennsylvania-radiocarbon-dates-xix.pdf); Callaway’s excavation summary is in [Biblical Archaeologist](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3209413).<br>
宾夕法尼亚大学公布的 et-Tell 碳十四样本说明该遗址有[早青铜时代与铁器时代遗存](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8AB039BCBE1F826FEE240542C2F562F5/S0033822200003532a.pdf/university-of-pennsylvania-radiocarbon-dates-xix.pdf)。Joseph Callaway 的发掘报告概要见[《Biblical Archaeologist》](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3209413)。

<a id="t1-23b-263"></a>
### Difficulties | 难点

If et-Tell is biblical Ai, neither common entry chronology has the expected contemporary Late Bronze fortified city to conquer.<br>
若 et-Tell 就是经文的艾城，那么常见的早期或晚期进入迦南年代都缺少一座当时可被攻取的晚青铜时代设防城。

Main explanations:<br>
主要解释包括：

1. **Wrong identification:** biblical Ai lies elsewhere nearby.
   **遗址认定有误**：经文的艾城应在附近别处。
2. **Narrative explaining ruins:** later inhabitants developed a conquest tradition around famous ruins.
   **叙事解释古废墟**：后来居民以一座著名废墟为背景形成征服传统。
3. **A smaller city:** a small settlement/strongpoint within the ruins was attacked.
   **经文所称“城”规模较小**：被攻击的是废墟区域中的小型聚落或据点。
4. **Revised chronology:** minority reassignment of pottery/destruction dates.
   **年代体系需要调整**：少数研究者主张重新判定陶器或毁坏层。

Khirbet el-Maqatir/Nisya have been proposed, but no broad consensus has displaced et-Tell; full, checkable formal publication remains necessary.<br>
Khirbet el-Maqatir、Khirbet Nisya 等替代地点曾被提出，但尚未形成取代 et-Tell 的广泛共识；相关发掘和释读必须等待完整、可复核的正式出版。

<a id="t1-23b-276"></a>
### How to Conclude | 怎样下结论

> Ai strongly challenges city-by-city archaeological proof of conquest and warns against deciding the answer before selecting a site.<br>
> 艾城是逐城“考古证实征服”说最明显的反例，也是提醒我们不能先决定答案、再挑选遗址的关键个案。

**Evidence grade: A** for et-Tell’s major occupation sequence; biblical identification **B/C**; alternatives **C**.<br>
**证据等级：A**（et-Tell 的主要居住序列）；“et-Tell 就是经文艾城”为 **B/C**；各替代地点为 **C**。

## 5.3 Hazor / Tel Hazor | 5.3 夏琐／Tel Hazor

<a id="t1-23b-286"></a>
### Scripture | 经文

- Joshua 11 calls Hazor chief among northern kingdoms; Joshua defeats their coalition and burns it.
  约书亚记 11 章称夏琐是北方诸国之首；约书亚击败联盟，并用火焚烧夏琐。
- Judges 4–5 again mentions Jabin king at Hazor and Sisera.
  士师记 4–5 章又出现“迦南王耶宾在夏琐作王”和将军西西拉。
- 1 Kings 9:15 includes Hazor in Solomon’s construction.
  列王纪上 9:15 把夏琐列入所罗门的建筑工程。
- 2 Kings 15:29 records Tiglath-pileser III’s capture in the area.
  列王纪下 15:29 记亚述王提革拉毗列色三世攻取夏琐一带。

These span centuries; not all Hazor material is one event/king.<br>
这些经文跨越多个世纪，不能把所有“夏琐”材料压成同一次事件或同一位王。

<a id="t1-23b-295"></a>
### What the Site Establishes | 遗址确定了什么

- A major Bronze Age southern-Levant city, about 200 acres; [Hebrew University Hazor project](https://archaeology.huji.ac.il/tel-hazor) surveys excavations/publications.
  夏琐在青铜时代是南黎凡特最大的城市中心之一，面积约 200 英亩；[希伯来大学夏琐项目](https://archaeology.huji.ac.il/tel-hazor)提供发掘与出版概览。
- Violent fire destruction at Late Bronze end.
  晚青铜时代末，城市经历剧烈火毁。
- Microarchaeology of gate deposits confirms major burning; see [Hebrew University peer-reviewed record](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/fire-at-the-gate-of-hazor-a-micro-geoarchaeological-study-of-the-).
  对城门沉积的微观考古研究确认一次重大火灾毁坏；参见[希伯来大学同行评议成果页](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/fire-at-the-gate-of-hazor-a-micro-geoarchaeological-study-of-the-)。
- Iron Age rebuilding, further northern-kingdom/Assyrian development.
  铁器时代城市后来重新建设，并在北国与亚述时期继续发展。

<a id="t1-23b-302"></a>
### What Remains Unknown | 仍不能确定什么

The destroyer’s name is not written in burned soil. Explanations include:<br>
毁城者身份没有写在烧土里。候选解释包括：

- Joshua’s Israelite force;
  约书亚领导的以色列军；
- Inhabitants revolting against the palace;
  城内人口反抗王宫；
- Sea Peoples/other migrants;
  海上民族或其他迁移群体；
- Neighboring powers;
  邻近政治势力；
- Combined conflicts during system collapse.
  晚青铜时代体系崩溃中的复合冲突。

Fire is clearly relevant to Joshua 11, but correlation is not exclusive attribution. Archaeology dates/describes burning more securely than identifies who burned it and why.<br>
夏琐的大火与约书亚记 11 章具有明显相关性，但相关性不是独一归因。考古确认“何时、怎样烧毁”比确认“谁为何焚烧”更有把握。

### How to Conclude | 怎样下结论

| Question / 问题 | Current assessment / 目前判断 |
| --- | --- |
| Important Late Bronze city? | Yes,A |
| 晚青铜时代夏琐是否是一座重要城市？ | 是，A |

| 城市是否遭受大火毁坏？ | 是，A |

| 是否与书 11 的大致时代背景相容？ | 是，B |

| 铁器时代城防是否必为所罗门所建？ | 有支持者，也有年代争议，C |

## 5.4 Why the Three Cannot Be Bundled | 5.4 为什么三城不能打包

| Site / 遗址 | Main findings / 主要考古结果 | Relation to traditional conquest dates / 与传统征服年代的关系 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jericho | Most conspicuous fortified-city fire at Middle Bronze end | Usually too early |
| 耶利哥 | 最显著的设防城火毁在中青铜时代末 | 通常偏早 |
| Ai/et-Tell | Long gap after Early Bronze city; small Iron I village | Expected Late Bronze city absent |
| 艾城／et-Tell | 早青铜时代大城后长期中断，铁器 I 才有小村 | 缺少应有的晚青铜城 |
| Hazor | Large Late Bronze city/violent end-period fire | Compatible, destroyer unknown |
| 夏琐 | 晚青铜时代末有大型城市和猛烈火毁 | 明显相容，但毁城者不明 |

One army conquering cities rapidly would normally suggest greater chronological consistency. Current evidence instead points toward:<br>
若同一支军队在很短时间内逐城攻取，通常期待更一致的年代图景。现有资料反而指向：

- Real places/regional-conflict memories preserved in tradition;
  传统保存了真实地点与区域冲突的记忆；
- Destructions by different forces in different years;
  个别城的毁坏可能属于不同年份、不同势力；
- Careful distinction between Joshua’s literary integration and historical events;
  《约书亚记》的文学整合与历史事件之间需要细致区分；
- Entry/settlement as potentially complex processes across generations.
  “进入”和“定居”可能是跨越数代的复合过程。

<a id="t1-23b-343"></a>
## 6. Four Major Formation Models | 六、四种主要形成模型

<a id="t1-23b-345"></a>
### 6.1 Unified Military Conquest | 6.1 统一军事征服模型

**Claim:** an Israelite group from Egypt under Joshua defeats major Canaanite cities in a short period, then allots land.<br>
**主张**：一支从埃及出来、由约书亚领导的以色列群体，在较短时期攻破迦南主要城邦，然后分地。

**Strengths:**<br>
**优点**：

- Reads Joshua 1–12’s military framework most directly;
  最直接顺读约书亚记 1–12 章的军事框架；
- Compatible with some destructions, including Hazor;
  夏琐等部分毁坏层可与之相容；
- Explains shared Exodus/[covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant) tradition as identity’s core.
  能解释一个共同出埃及—[立约](../Glossary/README.md#covenant)传统为何成为以色列身份核心。

**Difficulties:**<br>
**困难**：

- Jericho, Ai, other chronologies differ;
  耶利哥、艾城和其他城的年代不一致；
- Many cities lack contemporary destruction;
  许多城没有同一时期的毁坏层；
- Highland material culture shows substantial Canaanite continuity.
  高地物质文化总体呈现相当多的迦南连续性。

<a id="t1-23b-361"></a>
### 6.2 Peaceful Infiltration / Pastoral Settlement | 6.2 和平渗入／牧民定居模型

**Claim:** seminomadic groups gradually settle thinly populated highlands as farmers; conflict comes in later expansion rather than one general attack.<br>
**主张**：半游牧群体逐渐进入人口较少的山地，定居务农；冲突发生在后续扩张而非一次总攻。

**Strengths:**<br>
**优点**：

- Explains many new small highland villages;
  适合解释山地大量新小村落；
- Fits prolonged, regional, recurring Judges conflict;
  适合《士师记》长期、分区、反复冲突的图景；
- Does not require simultaneous city destruction.
  不要求所有城同时被毁。

**Difficulties:**<br>
**困难**：

- May understate warfare, migration, shared memories;
  可能低估真实战争、人口迁徙和共同记忆；
- Cannot alone explain some violent destructions;
  不能单独解释某些猛烈毁坏层；
- Peaceful does not describe every region.
  “和平”并不是所有地区的实际情况。

<a id="t1-23b-377"></a>
### 6.3 Internal Canaanite Social Transformation | 6.3 迦南内部社会转型模型

**Claim:** early Israel mainly emerges within Canaan: farmers, herders, marginal groups form highland societies/identities after city-state collapse.<br>
**主张**：早期以色列主要来自迦南内部；农民、牧民、边缘群体在城邦体系瓦解后形成新的山地社会和身份。

**Strengths:**<br>
**优点**：

- Fits pottery/language/lifestyle continuity;
  与陶器、语言和生活方式的连续性相符；
- Explains similarity followed by differentiation;
  解释为何早期以色列文化与迦南文化既相近又逐渐分化；
- Includes people escaping palace economic control.
  能容纳逃离宫殿经济控制的人群。

**Difficulties:**<br>
**困难**：

- Excluding all outside elements makes central Exodus tradition harder to explain;
  若排除一切外来成分，就很难解释出埃及传统为何如此核心；
- Material continuity does not rule out migration;
  物质连续性不等于人口从未迁入；
- Identity may incorporate many origins, not one path.
  身份形成可以吸收多种来源，未必是单一路径。

<a id="t1-23b-393"></a>
### 6.4 Composite Formation | 6.4 复合形成模型

**Claim:** later Israel combines diverse groups/processes:<br>
**主张**：后来称为“以色列”的共同体由多种群体和过程汇合：

- Local Canaanite farmers/herders;
  迦南本地农民与牧民；
- Lowland-to-highland migrants;
  从低地迁向山地的人；
- Possible small groups with Egyptian/Exodus experience;
  可能带有埃及／出埃及经验的小群体；
- Transjordanian populations;
  约旦河东人口；
- People incorporated through war, marriage, covenant, worship.
  通过战争、婚姻、盟约和敬拜被吸纳的人。

**Strengths:**<br>
**优点**：

- Accommodates regional differences;
  最能容纳遗址的地区差异；
- Takes Exodus tradition and Canaanite continuity seriously together;
  能同时认真对待出埃及传统与迦南文化连续性；
- Resonates with Rahab/Gibeonites joining in Joshua and Ruth’s foreign woman;
  与《约书亚记》中加入以色列的喇合、基遍人，以及《路得记》的外族女性相呼应；
- Explains looser Israelite groups preceding monarchy.
  能解释“以色列”先是较松散群体，后来才形成王国。

**Project’s provisional position:**<br>
**本项目暂定**：

> Use this as a working historical framework, not a final answer proving all details. It accommodates evidence rather than becoming another absolute doctrine.<br>
> 把复合模型作为历史研究的工作框架，但不把它当作已经证明全部细节的最终答案。它是一把容纳现有材料的伞，不是新的绝对教条。

A [Near Eastern Archaeology synthesis of Iron I landscapes](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3210703) likewise stresses Late Bronze continuity alongside hundreds of new villages; no single model explains all evidence.<br>
[《Near Eastern Archaeology》对铁器 I 景观的综合](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3210703)也强调：物质文化既有晚青铜时代连续性，也有数百个新村落；没有任何单一模型足以解释全部资料。

<a id="t1-23c-541"></a>
## 7. Judges: Spiritual Breakdown amid Dispersed Villages | 七、《士师记》：分散村落社会的属灵解体

<a id="t1-23c-543"></a>
## 7.1 Central Highland Settlement Growth | 7.1 中央山地的聚落激增

Many small farming/herding settlements appeared in the twelfth–eleventh centuries BCE.<br>
主前 12–11 世纪，中央山地出现大量小型农牧聚落。

Common features:<br>
常见特征包括：

- Small villages;
  规模不大的村落；
- Grain, olives, grapes, livestock together;
  谷物种植、橄榄、葡萄与畜牧结合；
- Storage pits, terraces, household production;
  储藏坑、梯田和家庭生产；
- The multiroom/four-room house tradition later widespread;
  后来广见的多空间／“四室式”房屋传统；
- Relatively few palaces or royal monuments.
  相对缺少大型宫殿和王室纪念碑。

Mario Liverani’s [Cambridge chapter](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/israels-history-and-the-history-of-israel/new-society-c-11501050/46B4CA61957F15CB994630FBBC42D13E) notes more than 250 Iron I highland sites in intensive surveys and a mixture of local tribes, farmers, herders.<br>
Mario Liverani 的[剑桥大学出版社章节](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/israels-history-and-the-history-of-israel/new-society-c-11501050/46B4CA61957F15CB994630FBBC42D13E)提到，密集调查已辨认出 250 多个铁器 I 期山地遗址，并把居民描述为本地部落、农民与牧民等因素的混合。

<a id="t1-23c-557"></a>
### Objects Do Not Directly Identify Ethnicity | 不能把器物直接等同族群

- Not eating pork does not let bones alone identify ethnicity/[faith](../Glossary/README.md#faith) with certainty.
  一个家庭不用猪肉，不等于仅凭骨头就能百分之百判断其民族或信仰。
- Four-room houses were not exclusive to one group.
  四室式房屋并非只被一个族群使用。
- Pottery crossed political/ethnic boundaries.
  陶器类型常跨越政治与族群边界。
- Israelite identity may clarify through worship, kinship memory, military alliances, boundary interaction.
  “以色列”身份可能在共同敬拜、亲族记忆、战争联盟和边界互动中逐渐变得清楚。

Scholars debate calling all highland villagers Israelite:<br>
学界对能否把所有高地村民都称为以色列仍有讨论：

- [Robert Miller](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1357794) combines settlement patterns, continuity, Merneptah in support of identification.
  [Robert Miller](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1357794)认为聚落模式、物质连续性和梅尔内普塔碑共同支持早期以色列辨识。
- [Israel Finkelstein](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3210562) emphasizes longer settlement cycles, warning against fixed ethnic labels too early.
  [Israel Finkelstein](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/3210562)更强调长期山地定居循环，并警告不要过早赋予所有村落固定民族标签。

A cautious formulation:<br>
稳妥说法是：

> Highland villages were a major social base of early Israel, without requiring every inhabitant to share one clearly bounded identity from the outset.<br>
> 山地村落是早期以色列形成的主要社会基地之一，但不必假设所有居民从第一天就拥有完全相同、边界清晰的身份。

<a id="t1-23c-573"></a>
## 7.2 Why This Fits Judges | 7.2 为什么这与《士师记》很相配

Judges lacks a stable central government:<br>
《士师记》没有一个稳定中央政府：

- Regional crises;
  危机多为地区性；
- Judges not necessarily influential across all tribes;
  士师影响范围不一定覆盖十二支派；
- Uneven tribal response;
  各支派响应不一；
- Towns, villages, clans, tribes more prominent than state institutions;
  城镇、村落、宗族和部落比国家机构更重要；
- Civil war as dangerous as outside enemies.
  内战与外敌同样危险。

Deborah’s song names participating and nonparticipating tribes, at ships/sheepfolds (Judges 5:14–18): loose coalition mobilization rather than later standing army.<br>
底波拉之歌甚至点名哪些支派参战、哪些支派留在船边或羊圈（士 5:14–18）。这不像后期王国的常备军，更像松散联盟的动员。

Archaeology cannot prove Deborah summoned a given tribe in a given year, but makes that structure historically intelligible.<br>
考古不能证明“底波拉确实在某年召集某支派”，但它使这种社会结构显得历史上可理解。

<a id="t1-23c-587"></a>
## 7.3 More Than Political Failure | 7.3 “循环”不只是政治失败

The cycle:<br>
士师记的循环是：

> Covenant breach → oppression → cry → God raises deliverer → temporary peace → renewed breach<br>
> 背约 → 受压 → 呼求 → 神兴起拯救者 → 暂时平安 → 再度背约

Archaeology studies cities, trade, weapons, diet, not directly the theological causation of oppression by idolatry.<br>
考古只能研究城市兴衰、贸易、武器和饮食，不能直接发掘“以色列因偶像崇拜而受压”这一神学因果。

The text stresses:<br>
经文的重点是：

- External enemies are not the deepest problem;
  外敌不是最深的问题；
- No king means more than no administrative center;
  没有王不只是缺乏行政中心；
- Everyone doing as they please reflects refusal of the Lord’s kingship;
  “各人任意而行”显示百姓拒绝耶和华作王；
- Later kings who break covenant do not automatically solve it.
  后来的君王若同样背约，也不会自动解决问题。

<a id="t1-23c-602"></a>
## 7.4 Three Cave-Identified Judges Manuscripts: A Local Short Reading Is Not the Original Whole Book | 7.4 三份具编号洞穴来源的《士师记》残卷：局部短读不能变成整卷“原版”

1Q6,4Q49/4QJudgᵃ,4Q50/4QJudgᵇ are square-Hebrew leather fragments dated broadly around the first century BCE:<br>
1Q6、4Q49／4QJudgᵃ、4Q50／4QJudgᵇ 是三份方体希伯来文皮卷残片，抄写年代约跨主前一世纪前后：

| Manuscript / [抄本](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript) | Surviving scope / 现存范围 | Main observation / 最重要的文本观察 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1Q6 | Judges 6:20–22,8:1 uncertain, passages of 9 | Tiny Gideon/Abimelech portions cannot classify whole book |
| 1Q6 | 士 6:20–22、8:1（存疑）、9 章多段 | 只保存基甸／亚比米勒小片段，篇幅太短，不能据此给全书归类 |
| 4Q49 | Judges 6:2–6,11–13 | 6:6 goes straight to 6:11, lacking Masoretic unnamed-prophet rebuke 6:7–10 |
| 4Q49 | 士 6:2–6、11–13 | 从 6:6 直接接 6:11，没有马所拉本 6:7–10 的无名[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)责备 |
| 4Q50 | Judges 19:5–7,21:12–25 | Generally near Masoretic tradition, with chapter 21 forms/line lengths/possible longer-shorter differences |
| 4Q50 | 士 19:5–7、21:12–25 | 整体接近马所拉传统，但第 21 章仍见词形、行长及可能长短读法差异 |

4Q49 proves ancient Gideon-opening texts were not word-for-word identical. Its missing 6:7–10 alone does not explain why:<br>
4Q49 的短读至少证明：古代《士师记》传本在基甸故事开头并非逐字一致。它却不能单独回答为什么没有 6:7–10：

- An earlier literary form before prophetic speech was added;
  可能保存了较早、尚未加入先知讲话的文学形态；
- Copying omission;
  可能发生了抄写遗漏；
- Deliberate scribal/editorial abbreviation.
  也可能是某位抄写者或编者作了缩略。

All three are argued. Robert Rezetko’s research survey includes earlier-edition and accidental/intentional omission models. Sparse fragments do not turn a local shorter reading into a recovered original short whole book.<br>
这三种解释都有人主张。Robert Rezetko 对研究史的梳理显示，学者既有把短读视为较早文学版的方案，也有把它解释为意外或有意省略的方案；残片稀少，不能把“此处短”扩大成“整卷存在一套已恢复的原始短版”。

Privately held XJudges was published among texts of unknown provenance; later work reopens provenance/authenticity. It is excluded from three cave-identified core witnesses and cannot bear conclusions without reliable context.<br>
另有私人收藏的 `XJudges` 残片，正式出版归入“来源不明文本”，近年研究又重新追问其来源与真实性。本项目不把它加入三份有编号洞穴来源的核心计数，也不让一件无可靠考古语境的材料承担文本史结论。

These are for textual criticism, separating judges-era events from copying centuries later, not contemporary Gideon/Abimelech/Benjamin-war archives.<br>
这些卷轴的正确用途是文本批判：它们把“士师时代是否发生某事”和“数百年后这卷书怎样抄传”分开。它们不是基甸、亚比米勒或便雅悯战争的同期档案。

## 8. Philistines: Background for Judges and Samuel | 八、非利士人：士师与撒母耳时代的重要旁证

<a id="t1-23c-628"></a>
## 8.1 Egyptian Images and Sea Peoples | 8.1 埃及图像与“海上民族”

Ramesses III’s Medinet Habu reliefs/inscriptions recount wars with northern/sea peoples; Chicago’s [formal publication](https://oi.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/saoc12.pdf) preserves naval images/text.<br>
拉美西斯三世的麦地那哈布神庙浮雕和铭文记述与若干“北方／海上民族”的战争。芝加哥大学古代文化研究所的[麦地那哈布铭文正式出版](https://oi.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/saoc12.pdf)保存了海战图像和文本。

**Peleset** is often connected with Philistines, but:<br>
其中的 **Peleset** 常与后来的非利士人联系，但要注意：

- Egypt’s list has several groups, not a documentary of Philistines invading alone;
  埃及名单包含多个群体，不是“非利士人单独入侵”的纪录片；
- Royal victory reliefs serve propaganda;
  王室浮雕表现法老大胜，带有宣传功能；
- Strong Peleset–Philistine connection does not identify each migrant’s homeland.
  Peleset 与 Philistine 的联系很强，却不能由图像确定每个移民的具体家乡。

<a id="t1-23c-638"></a>
## 8.2 Coastal Material Culture | 8.2 沿海物质文化

Early Iron Age Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron, Gath, Gaza show:<br>
铁器时代初期，亚实基伦、亚实突、以革伦、迦特和迦萨地区出现：

- Aegean-style pottery forms/decoration;
  带爱琴海传统的陶器形式与装饰；
- New cooking, diet, architecture;
  新的烹饪、饮食和建筑习惯；
- Continuing mixture with local Canaanite traditions;
  与本地迦南传统持续混合；
- Emerging locally distinctive Philistine culture.
  后来发展出具有地方特色的非利士文化。

Do not describe an isolated pure foreign culture unchanged for centuries; migrants quickly interacted locally.<br>
所以非利士人不应描述成保持“纯外来文化”数百年的封闭群体。他们在迁移之后很快与本地人口互动。

<a id="t1-23c-649"></a>
## 8.3 Ashkelon Ancient DNA | 8.3 亚实基伦古 DNA

A 2019 peer-reviewed study analyzed ten Bronze–Iron Age individuals:<br>
2019 年同行评议研究分析亚实基伦青铜—铁器时代十名个体：

- Early Iron samples show previously absent European-related ancestry;
  铁器时代早期样本出现此前没有的欧洲相关遗传成分；
- Supporting actual migration at the transition;
  这支持青铜—铁器转折时确有外来人口迁入；
- By later Iron Age the signal was no longer distinguishable, possibly diluted in local Levantine ancestry.
  到较晚铁器时代，这一信号已不再可辨，可能被本地黎凡特基因库稀释。

See Feldman et al., [Science Advances](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6609216/).<br>
原始研究见 Feldman 等人的[《Science Advances》论文全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6609216/)。

Supports:<br>
它能支持：

- Cultural change involved some population movement, not only goods/ideas.
  非利士文化变化并非完全只有商品或观念传播，至少有部分人口迁移。

Does not establish:<br>
它不能支持：

- All Philistines from one European region;
  所有非利士人都来自同一个欧洲地区；
- No substantial local component;
  非利士城市人口没有大量本地成分；
- Specific Samson/Goliath/battle details.
  参孙、歌利亚或某场圣经战役的具体细节。

**Evidence grade: A/B** for sampled Ashkelon population change; extension to all Philistines **C**.<br>
**证据等级：A/B**（亚实基伦样本中的人口变化）；外推到全部非利士人为 **C**。

<a id="t1-23c-671"></a>
## 8.4 Samson, Samuel, Saul Contexts | 8.4 与参孙、撒母耳、扫罗故事的关系

Judges 13–16 and 1 Samuel depict highland–coast frontier conflicts. Geographically:<br>
士师记 13–16 章与撒母耳记上多次描写山地和沿海之间的边界冲突。地理上：

- Dan/Shephelah near Philistine cities;
  但支派和示非拉靠近非利士城市；
- Samson around Timnah, Sorek, Ashkelon, Gaza;
  参孙故事在亭拿、梭烈谷、亚实基伦、迦萨一带展开；
- Ebenezer, Aphek, Michmash, Elah reflect contests over routes.
  以便以谢、亚弗、密抹、以拉谷等战场反映山地与沿海通道争夺。

External evidence places Philistine culture/power in relevant times/regions, not Samson’s pillars or Goliath’s weapons.<br>
外部材料证明非利士文化和政治势力处于正确的时代与地区，却不等于已找到参孙的柱子或歌利亚的武器。

<a id="t1-23c-681"></a>
### On a Philistine Iron Monopoly | 关于“非利士人垄断铁器”

1 Samuel 13:19–22 describes no Israelite smiths, Philistine sharpening of farm tools, and only Saul/Jonathan armed in battle.<br>
撒母耳记上 13:19–22 说以色列境内没有铁匠，百姓须下到非利士人那里磨农具；战时扫罗和约拿单才有刀枪。

Do not turn it into:<br>
不要把它改写成：

- Philistines invented iron;
  “非利士人发明了铁”；
- Israel knew nothing of iron;
  “当时以色列人完全不知道铁”；
- Archaeology proved a nationwide patent system.
  “考古已证明一项全国性的专利制度”。

The account more plausibly describes production control and unequal armaments in a particular conflict. Iron spread gradually while bronze remained in use.<br>
经文更像在描述特定冲突中的生产控制和军备不平衡。铁器技术在这一时期逐渐扩散，但青铜仍长期使用。

## 9. Assessing Two Popular Online Claims | 九、两个网络热门宣称：应怎样处理

<a id="t1-23c-697"></a>
## 9.1 Mount Ebal’s Joshua Altar | 9.1 以巴路山“约书亚祭坛”

Adam Zertal interpreted an early Iron stone structure as Joshua 8:30–35’s altar. Disputes include:<br>
Adam Zertal 在以巴路山发掘一座铁器时代早期石构建筑，并解释为约书亚记 8:30–35 的祭坛。主要争议包括：

A possible early highland ritual-site candidate, not a confirmed Joshua altar.<br>
目前可把它列为“早期山地仪式遗址的可能候选”，不能写成“约书亚祭坛已经确认”。

**Evidence grade: C.**<br>
**证据等级：C。**

<a id="t1-23c-710"></a>
## 9.2 Mount Ebal’s Curse Tablet | 9.2 以巴路山“诅咒铅片”

A 2023 publication reads X-ray scans of a folded lead piece wet-sifted from old excavation spoil as Late Bronze Hebrew curse with YHW; see original [Heritage Science claim](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-023-00920-9).<br>
2023 年发表者主张，一块从旧发掘弃土中湿筛出的折叠铅片，经 X 射线断层扫描可读出含 YHW 神名的晚青铜时代希伯来诅咒。原始主张见[《Heritage Science》论文](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-023-00920-9)。

Key problems:<br>
关键问题：

- Spoil, not a sealed in-place context;
  铅片来自弃土，不是原位封闭地层；
- Major disagreement whether marks are letters;
  扫描划痕是否构成字母存在重大分歧；
- Criticism of orientation/joins/paleographic dating;
  字母方向、拼合和古文字学年代受到批评；
- Metal source alone dates neither manufacture nor inscription.
  铅来源只能说明原料金属来源，不能单独确定制作或刻写年代。

A 2024 [formal response](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-023-01130-z) in the same journal finds images insufficient for identifiable internal/external writing, much less an early Hebrew curse.<br>
2024 年同刊的[正式反驳](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-023-01130-z)认为，现有影像不足以证明内部或外部存在可辨识文字，更不能确立所宣称的早期希伯来诅咒。

Project position:<br>
本项目处理：

> Exclude from core evidence until independent, reproducible epigraphic consensus.<br>
> 在取得独立、可复核的铭文学共识前，不把这件铅片纳入圣经时间线的核心证据。

**Evidence grade: D, currently outside core; reconsider with new data/independent verification.**<br>
**证据等级：D（目前不纳入核心）；若未来有新数据与独立验证，再重新评级。**

## 10. Ruth: A Family Thread amid Disorder | 十、《路得记》：混乱时代中的家族线

Ruth is neither palace nor battle history; it shifts from judges’ violence to:<br>
《路得记》不是王宫史，也不是战役记录。它把镜头从士师的暴力移到：

- Famine/cross-border migration;
  饥荒与跨境迁移；
- Moab–Bethlehem family ties;
  摩押与伯利恒之间的家庭关系；
- Widows’ survival;
  寡妇的生存处境；
- Gleaning, land, kin responsibility;
  拾穗、土地和亲属责任；
- A foreign woman joining Israel;
  外族女子进入以色列；
- Preparation for David’s genealogy.
  大卫家谱的预备。

<a id="t1-23c-742"></a>
### Historical Context | 历史上能说明什么

- Long contact, rivalry, migration between Judah’s hills and Moab plateau.
  犹大山地和摩押高原之间确有长期往来、竞争与迁移。
- Harvest, family land, clan protection fit Iron Age rural life.
  农业收割、家族土地和宗族保护是铁器时代乡村社会可理解的背景。
- Ninth-century Mesha attests later independent Moab/conflict, not the earlier narrative’s Ruth/Boaz.
  主前 9 世纪的米沙碑可证明后来摩押确实是独立政治实体，并与以色列冲突；但它比路得故事假定的时期晚，不能证明路得或波阿斯。

<a id="t1-23c-748"></a>
### Four Qumran Manuscripts Date Visible Text, Not Excavated Persons | 四份昆兰残卷回答的是“文本何时可见”，不是“人物是否出土”

Alongside farming/kinship context, four Hebrew material witnesses provide direct text evidence (anchor 161):<br>
《路得记》除了士师时代的农业与亲属制度背景，还有四份直接的希伯来文实物见证（锚点 161）：

| Manuscript / 抄本 | IAA period / 文物局时期分类 | Main visible scope / 主要可辨范围 | Significance / 证据意义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4Q104/4QRuthᵃ | Hasmonean | Ruth 1:1–12 | Opening: judges, Bethlehem–Moab, Naomi urges return |
| 4Q104／4QRuthᵃ | 哈斯摩尼时期 | 得 1:1–12 | 保存“士师秉政”、伯利恒—摩押迁移与拿俄米劝返的故事开场 |
| 4Q105/4QRuthᵇ | Herodian | Ruth 1:1–15 | Overlaps 4Q104 for comparison between copies |
| 4Q105／4QRuthᵇ | 希律时期 | 得 1:1–15 | 与 4Q104 重叠，可比较同一段在不同副本中的抄写 |
| 2Q16/2QRuthᵃ | Herodian | Ruth 2:13–23,3:1–8,4:3–4 | Gleaning, threshing floor, land [redemption](../Glossary/README.md#redemption) |
| 2Q16／2QRuthᵃ | 希律时期 | 得 2:13–23；3:1–8；4:3–4 | 保存拾穗、禾场和赎地三个关键生活场景 |
| 2Q17/2QRuthᵇ | Hasmonean | Ruth 3:13–18 | Boaz promises to address redeemer responsibility |
| 2Q17／2QRuthᵇ | 哈斯摩尼时期 | 得 3:13–18 | 保存波阿斯承诺处理[救赎](../Glossary/README.md#redemption)亲属责任的段落 |

These separate two questions:<br>
这四份残卷使两个问题可以分开：

1. **Text history:** several Hebrew copies by late Second Temple period, preserving four narrative stages.
   **文本史**：第二圣殿后期已有多份《路得记》希伯来文副本，现存范围跨越故事四个阶段。
2. **Event history:** none is a judges-era document or identifies Ruth/Boaz in historical archives.
   **事件史**：没有一件残卷是士师时代文件，也没有一件点名历史档案中的路得或波阿斯。

Multiple copies do not establish Qumran interpretation of David’s genealogy or later fixed Shavuot liturgy. Text copied cannot be upgraded to whole narrative archaeologically verified.<br>
多份抄本不能自动证明昆兰群体怎样解释大卫家谱，也不能证明当时已经形成后来七七节诵读的固定礼仪。残片可证“这段文字被抄写”，不能把“文本存在”升级为“全部叙事已由考古验证”。

<a id="t1-23c-766"></a>
### Theological Role | 神学作用

Ruth shifts Israel’s formation from ancestry toward trust/covenant loyalty:<br>
路得记把“以色列如何形成”的问题从血统转向信靠与圣约忠诚：

- Rahab accepted during entry;
  喇合在进入迦南时被接纳；
- Ruth accepted in judges’ era;
  路得在士师时代被接纳；
- Both foreign women enter David’s and ultimately [Messiah](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)’s genealogy (Matthew 1:5).
  两位外族女性都进入大卫、最终进入[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)家谱（太 1:5）。

> Amid everyone doing as they please, God preserves David’s family through ordinary faithfulness.<br>
> 在“各人任意而行”的时代，神借普通人的忠诚保存大卫家族线。

## 11. From Judges to Kings: Why Political Integration? | 十一、从士师到君王：政治整合为什么发生

<a id="t1-23d-918"></a>
## 11.1 Biblical Explanation | 11.1 经文的解释

Reasons for demanding a king include:<br>
以色列要求王的原因包括：

- Samuel’s age and sons’ misconduct (1 Samuel 8:1–5);
  撒母耳年老，儿子不行他的道（撒上 8:1–5）；
- Wanting to be like other nations;
  希望“像列国一样”；
- A ruler to govern and lead war;
  需要王治理和带领战争；
- Nahash’s Ammonite threat confirming kingship (1 Samuel 11;12:12);
  亚扪人拿辖的威胁推动王权确认（撒上 11；参 12:12）；
- Philistine pressure throughout Saul’s reign.
  非利士军事压力贯穿扫罗时代。

Kingship receives a two-sided assessment:<br>
经文对王权保持双重评价：

- Deuteronomy 17:14–20 permits a king under law;
  申命记 17:14–20 已容许受律法约束的王；
- 1 Samuel 8 warns of royal claims on children, fields, produce;
  撒母耳记上 8 章警告王会征用儿女、田地和出产；
- The issue is not kingship itself, but rejecting God and kings refusing divine direction.
  真正问题不是“有王”本身，而是百姓拒绝神、王又不受神话语约束。

<a id="t1-23d-934"></a>
## 11.2 Archaeological Transition | 11.2 考古的转型图景

From late Iron I to Iron IIA:<br>
从铁器 I 末到铁器 IIA：

- Abandoned/reorganized highland villages;
  许多早期山地村落被放弃或重组；
- Stronger defenses at selected centers;
  少数地点开始出现更强城防；
- Greater administration, storage, road control;
  行政、储存和区域道路控制增强；
- Family/tribal networks absorbed by kingship.
  家族与部落网络逐渐被王权吸纳。

This supplies social context, not the first king’s name Saul.<br>
这提供了王国形成的社会背景，却不能独自告诉我们第一位王名叫扫罗。

A 2025 Cambridge [Bible’s First Kings chapter](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/bibles-first-kings/abandoned-rural-villages-and-the-beginning-of-highlands-fortifications/4B0C0D39461BD67CA4507A67CA2FB4CA) studies abandonment and earliest fortifications together, showing continuing archaeological debate.<br>
2025 年剑桥大学出版社《The Bible’s First Kings》的[相关章节](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/bibles-first-kings/abandoned-rural-villages-and-the-beginning-of-highlands-fortifications/4B0C0D39461BD67CA4507A67CA2FB4CA)把村落放弃与最早山地筑防并列研究，说明这一转型仍是当前考古讨论的焦点。

## 12. Saul: Strong Setting, Weak Direct Identification | 十二、扫罗：历史背景强，直接人证弱

<a id="t1-23d-951"></a>
### Biblical Location | 经文定位

Benjaminite Saul centers on Gibeah, Michmash, Gilgal, facing:<br>
扫罗来自便雅悯，活动中心包括基比亚、密抹和吉甲；他面对：

- Philistines;
  非利士人；
- Ammonites;
  亚扪人；
- Amalekites;
  亚玛力人；
- Internal tribal integration;
  内部部落整合；
- Succession crisis with David.
  与大卫之间的王位危机。

<a id="t1-23d-961"></a>
### External Evidence Limits | 外部证据边界

No generally accepted contemporary outside inscription directly names:<br>
目前没有公认的同期外部铭文直接提到：

- Saul;
  扫罗；
- Samuel;
  撒母耳；
- Jonathan;
  约拿单；
- Jabesh-gilead’s specific 1 Samuel 11 battle.
  基列雅比与撒上 11 的具体战役。

Tell el-Ful is often Saul’s Gibeah, but layers, building dates, Saul’s palace attribution remain disputed. Safest:<br>
Tell el-Ful 常被认作扫罗的基比亚，但遗址层位、建筑年代和是否能称为“扫罗宫殿”长期有争论。稳妥写法是：

> Gibeah’s geography helps explain Benjamin’s center; a fortress cannot become Saul’s palace solely by biblical expectation.<br>
> 基比亚的地理认定可帮助理解便雅悯核心区；遗址中的堡垒不能仅凭经文预期就标为扫罗王宫。

<a id="t1-23d-974"></a>
### Four Qumran Samuel Manuscripts: Nahash’s Longer Reading Explains Narrative, Not Events | 四份昆兰《撒母耳记》残卷：拿辖长读解释叙事，不等于已经证明事件

1Q7,4Q51/4QSamᵃ,4Q52/4QSamᵇ,4Q53/4QSamᶜ are Hebrew Samuel manuscripts broadly third–first centuries BCE:<br>
昆兰留下 1Q7、4Q51／4QSamᵃ、4Q52／4QSamᵇ、4Q53／4QSamᶜ 四份希伯来文《撒母耳记》抄本。它们约跨主前三至一世纪：

| Manuscript / 抄本 | Surviving scope/type / 现存范围与性质 | Use limit / 使用边界 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1Q7 | Small 2 Samuel 18/20/21/23 fragments | Text witness, not David’s late-life archive |
| 1Q7 | 撒下 18、20、21、23 章小残片 | 直接文本见证，不是大卫晚年事件档案 |
| 4Q52 | 1 Samuel 16/19–21/23; IAA Early Hellenistic | Earlier manuscript is not prophetic autograph |
| 4Q52 | 撒上 16、19–21、23 章片段；文物局归早期希腊化时期 | 较早抄本不等于先知原稿 |
| 4Q53 | 1 Samuel 25/2 Samuel 14–15;Hasmonean | Small Nabal/Absalom portions |
| 4Q53 | 撒上 25、撒下 14–15 章片段；哈斯摩尼时期 | 只覆盖拿八事件和押沙龙叙事的小部分 |
| 4Q51 | Hundreds of fragments across 1 Samuel 1–2 Samuel 24;about 50–25 BCE | Broad scope but about 15% survives, not complete |
| 4Q51 | 数百残片横跨撒上 1 至撒下 24；约主前 50–25 年 | 覆盖广却估计只存全书约 15%，不能称完整卷 |

Between 1 Samuel 10:27/11:1,4Q51 preserves a longer narrative absent from Masoretic text: Nahash oppressed Gad/Reuben, gouging right eyes; refugees in Jabesh face renewed siege. Similar Josephus Antiquities 6.68–71 makes humiliation demand intelligible.<br>
4Q51 在撒上 10:27 与 11:1 之间保存一段马所拉文本没有的较长叙述：亚扪王拿辖先前压迫迦得、流便人并剜去右眼，逃到基列雅比的人后来又受到围攻。这段文字与约瑟夫斯《犹太古史》6.68–71 有近似内容，使拿辖为何提出“剜右眼”的屈辱条件更容易理解。

Smoother narrative is no automatic originality:<br>
但“叙事更顺”仍不是原始性的自动证明：

- Cross et al. argue original passage later lost by skipped lines;
  Cross 等人主张较长段落原属正文，后来抄本因跳行而遗失；
- Others see early editorial expansion;
  另有研究把它视为早期编辑扩充；
- Josephus/4Q51 agreement witnesses transmission, not independent contemporary Iron Age reports.
  约瑟夫斯与 4Q51 的近似说明传统流传，却不是两份彼此独立的铁器时代现场记录。

Many 4Q51 readings resemble underlying Old Greek traditions: Greek differences can reflect Hebrew exemplars, not always free translation. Conversely, not every Greek reading is necessarily earlier.<br>
4Q51 许多读法又与古希腊译本的底层传统相近。这证明希腊本差异有时来自另一种希伯来底本，不能一概归为译者自由发挥；反过来，也不能把每个希腊读法一概宣布为更原始。

<a id="t1-23d-995"></a>
### Saul’s Storyline Role | 扫罗在故事线中的作用

Saul is more than a simply bad king:<br>
扫罗不是简单的“坏王”：

- Called and moved by the Spirit;
  他确实蒙召并曾被[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)感动；
- Contributes early military integration;
  他带来早期军事整合；
- Repeatedly substitutes calculation for obedience;
  但他反复以政治计算取代顺服；
- His failure shows kings too remain under covenant.
  他的失败说明王也必须在约之下。

## 13. David: Person, Dynasty, State Size Are Separate Questions | 十三、大卫：人物、王朝与国家规模要分开

<a id="t1-23d-1008"></a>
## 13.1 Tel Dan | 13.1 特尔但碑

Aramaic victory-stele fragments were found in 1993/1994 and are usually dated to the ninth century BCE. Most epigraphers read **bytdwd** as בית דוד / House of David.<br>
1993、1994 年在特尔但发现的亚兰文胜利碑残片，通常定于主前 9 世纪。关键表达 **bytdwd** 多数铭文学者读作“בית דוד／大卫之家”。

<a id="t1-23d-1012"></a>
### Supports | 能支持

- A neighboring kingdom identifies Judah by a David-named dynasty over a century after David;
  犹大在大卫之后约一个多世纪，被邻国以一个“大卫”命名的王朝称谓识别；
- The name is not confined to much later religious literature;
  “大卫”不是只在很晚的宗教文学中才出现的名字；
- Dynastic memory fits 2 Samuel 7 and Kings’ Davidic descendants.
  王朝延续记忆与撒母耳记下 7 章、列王纪持续称犹大诸王为大卫后裔相符。

[BASOR epigraphic discussion](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1357180) and [Jewish/Israel Museum loan exhibit](https://thejewishmuseum.org/exhibitions/tel-dan-stele/) use the mainstream House of David reading.<br>
[BASOR 的铭文学讨论](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/1357180)把该词读为“House of David”；[犹太博物馆与以色列博物馆借展说明](https://thejewishmuseum.org/exhibitions/tel-dan-stele/)也采用这一主流读法。

<a id="t1-23d-1020"></a>
### Does Not Establish | 不能支持

- No biography;
  石碑没有大卫生平；
- No every 1 Samuel 17 Goliath detail;
  它不告诉我们歌利亚战役是否按撒上 17 每个细节发生；
- No territory, troop strength, wealth, administrative scale;
  它不能确定大卫的疆域、军队人数、财富或行政规模；
- Dynastic memory is not proof of every later court narrative.
  “王朝记忆存在”不等于“后来的所有宫廷叙事都已被证明”。

<a id="t1-23d-1027"></a>
### Minority Objections | 少数异议

Damaged unvocalized text allows alternatives; [Ehud Ben Zvi](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/030908929401906403) discusses them. Dynastic House of David remains mainstream.<br>
破损、无元音的古代文字允许提出其他解释。[Ehud Ben Zvi 的论文](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/030908929401906403)提醒还存在替代读法。不过“某王朝／大卫之家”仍是当前主流理解。

**Evidence grade: A−** for dynasty; particular battle reconstruction **C**.<br>
**证据等级：A−**（大卫王朝称谓）；具体战争复原为 **C**。

<a id="t1-23d-1033"></a>
## 13.2 Khirbet Qeiyafa | 13.2 基伯特·凯雅法／Khirbet Qeiyafa

Elah Valley frontier between Judah hills/Philistine plains. Excavations show:<br>
该遗址位于以拉谷，靠近犹大山地与非利士平原的边界。发掘显示：

- Fortified city;
  设防城市；
- Planned wall and adjoining houses;
  规划式城墙和邻墙房屋；
- Two gates;
  两座城门；
- Destruction after short occupation;
  短期居住后毁坏；
- Pottery, inscriptions, religious objects;
  陶器、铭文和宗教器物；
- Radiocarbon dates around the late eleventh/early tenth century BCE.
  碳十四年代约在主前 11 世纪末至 10 世纪初。

A second round of charred olive-pit dating places the city about 1000 BCE; see [Hebrew University record](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/king-davids-city-at-khirbet-qeiyafa-results-of-the-second-radioca/) and [Radiocarbon state-formation study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/state-formation-in-judah-biblical-tradition-modern-historical-theories-and-radiometric-dates-at-khirbet-qeiyafa/0F8B4D6E883C99098BF8070BE472719D).<br>
对烧焦橄榄核的第二轮研究把城市定在约主前 1000 年；参见[希伯来大学研究成果页](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/king-davids-city-at-khirbet-qeiyafa-results-of-the-second-radioca/)及[《Radiocarbon》国家形成研究](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/state-formation-in-judah-biblical-tradition-modern-historical-theories-and-radiometric-dates-at-khirbet-qeiyafa/0F8B4D6E883C99098BF8070BE472719D)。

<a id="t1-23d-1046"></a>
### What It Changes | 它改变了什么

It shows:<br>
它表明：

- A planned fortified lowland center mobilizing substantial labor about 1000 BCE;
  约主前 1000 年，犹大低地至少有一个规划良好、能够投入大量劳力的设防中心；
- Tenth-century regional organization cannot universally be only scattered illiterate villages;
  10 世纪的区域组织不能一概描述为“只有零散、不识字的小村”；
- Elah’s strategic Judah–Philistine interaction frontier.
  以拉谷确实是犹大与非利士文化互动的战略边区。

<a id="t1-23d-1054"></a>
### Remaining Disputes | 仍有何争论

- Judahite, Saulide, Canaanite, or other population;
  居民究竟属于犹大、扫罗政权、迦南或其他区域群体；
- Whether two gates identify Shaaraim, possibly related to double gates in Hebrew;
  两座城门是否足以把遗址认作“沙拉音”（希伯来文与“双门”可能相关）；
- Direct Davidic control or more complex early networks;
  该城受大卫直接控制还是属于更复杂的早期政治网络；
- Whether one frontier city represents Judah’s whole state size.
  一个边区城市能否代表整个犹大国家规模。

**Evidence grade: A** for city/date; political attribution **B/C**; named biblical identification **C**.<br>
**证据等级：A**（设防城与年代）；政治归属为 **B/C**；与具体经文地名相等同为 **C**。

<a id="t1-23d-1063"></a>
## 13.3 David’s Jerusalem | 13.3 大卫的耶路撒冷

2 Samuel 5–6 makes Jebusite capture, capital, and ark arrival major turns.<br>
经文把大卫攻取耶布斯人的城、设立首都、把约柜迎入城中作为关键转折（撒下 5–6）。

Excavation is difficult:<br>
耶路撒冷考古特别困难：

- Continuous settlement, later leveling/fill/reuse;
  古城持续居住，后代建筑反复削平、填筑和再利用；
- No routine comprehensive Temple Mount excavation;
  圣殿山无法进行常规全面发掘；
- Steep slopes, erosion/later disturbance;
  城址陡峭，土层易侵蚀或被后期工程扰动；
- Large structures built/used across periods.
  同一大型石构可能跨多期修筑和使用。

Frequently discussed remains:<br>
常被讨论的遗存有：

- Stepped Stone Structure;
  阶梯石结构；
- So-called Large Stone Structure;
  所谓大型石结构；
- Gihon defenses/water installations;
  基训泉防御与供水设施；
- Tenth-century pottery/scattered buildings.
  10 世纪陶器和零散建筑。

Main interpretations:<br>
主要解释分歧：

1. Davidic palace/fortress components;
   这些大型结构是大卫时代宫殿／堡垒的一部分；
2. Earlier core reused;
   核心结构更早，后期继续使用；
3. Upper structures mainly later Iron Age;
   上层建筑主要属于更晚铁器时代；
4. Existence of David-era Jerusalem distinct from large imperial capital.
   “大卫时代耶路撒冷存在”与“它是否是大型帝国首都”必须分别讨论。

No in-place David-reign plaque reading David’s palace has been found.<br>
目前没有发现一块来自大卫在位时期、写着“大卫王宫”的原位铭牌。

A 2024 PNAS study adds 103 radiocarbon dates from five areas, attesting twelfth–tenth-century settlement and placing marked westward expansion/large building chiefly in ninth century onward. Calibration plateaus/regional atmospheric offsets caution against identifying one king from one sample.<br>
2024 年 *PNAS* 研究为讨论加入更坚实的年代底座：团队从五个发掘区建立 103 个碳十四日期，确认耶路撒冷在主前 12–10 世纪已有聚落活动，并把明显向西扩展和大规模建筑主要放到主前 9 世纪及以后。研究还识别碳十四校准平台与区域大气偏移，说明一个样本很少能直接锁定某位君王。

This constrains two exaggerations:<br>
这组数据同时限制两种夸大：

- Tenth-century Jerusalem was not completely empty/archaeologically blank;
  不能再把主前 10 世纪耶路撒冷说成完全无人或考古空白；
- Settlement does not prove a massive David–Solomon imperial capital.
  也不能把“有聚落”直接改写成“已证明大卫—所罗门的庞大帝国首都”。

See [Regev et al.,PNAS 121](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11087761/).<br>
参考：[Regev 等，*PNAS* 121：铁器时代耶路撒冷碳十四年代](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11087761/)。

Safest conclusion:<br>
因此最稳妥的结论是：

> Tenth-century Jerusalem has archaeological evidence, but current remains cannot precisely reconstruct the full state portrayed in Samuel.<br>
> 10 世纪耶路撒冷并非考古空白，但现有遗存不足以精确重建撒母耳记所描绘王国的全部规模。

<a id="t1-23d-1103"></a>
## 13.4 Davidic Covenant’s Theological Place | 13.4 大卫之约的神学位置

2 Samuel 7 advances the Bible’s storyline:<br>
撒母耳记下 7 章把整条圣经线推进一大步：

- Abraham: descendants, land, [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing) nations;
  亚伯拉罕之约：后裔、土地、万国得福；
- Sinai: priestly kingdom;
  西奈之约：以色列成为[祭司](../Glossary/README.md#priest)国度；
- David: throne/offspring/kingdom promises focus on his house;
  大卫之约：王位、后裔和国度的应许集中到大卫家；
- [Prophets](../Glossary/README.md#prophet): righteous Davidic branch;
  先知：期待公义的大卫苗裔；
- [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament): Jesus called David’s son.
  新约：耶稣被称为大卫之子。

Archaeology can test dynastic title House of David, not measure God’s covenant faithfulness.<br>
考古可以讨论“大卫之家”是否为真实王朝称谓，却不能用石碑测量神对大卫之约的信实。

## 14. Solomon: Temple, Construction, Chronology | 十四、所罗门：圣殿、城建与年代争论

<a id="t1-23e-1242"></a>
## 14.1 Solomon’s Temple | 14.1 所罗门圣殿

1 Kings 5–8 places construction at the reign’s center:<br>
列王纪上 5–8 章把建殿写成所罗门统治核心：

- God’s name dwells in Jerusalem;
  神的名居住在耶路撒冷；
- Ark enters Holy of Holies;
  约柜进入至圣所；
- Exodus and Davidic covenant converge in dedication prayer;
  出埃及与大卫之约在献殿祷告中汇合；
- Israel’s center also opens to foreign petitioners.
  圣殿既是以色列中心，也向外邦祷告者开放。

Archaeological limits:<br>
考古边界：

- Traditional location on present Temple Mount/Haram al-Sharif cannot undergo routine excavation;
  传统圣殿位置在今日圣殿山／尊贵禁地，无法进行常规考古发掘；
- No generally accepted in-place Solomonic Temple remains for direct dating;
  没有公认的所罗门圣殿原位建筑残件可供直接测年；
- Syrian–Anatolian/Levantine long-room temples/decorative parallels explain architectural vocabulary, not provenance of alleged Temple objects.
  叙利亚—安纳托利亚和黎凡特有若干长殿式神庙及装饰平行，可说明建筑语言，却不能证明某块器物来自所罗门圣殿。

Tel Moza is nearby and chronologically closer: an Iron IIA temple begun about late tenth/early ninth century BCE, east entrance, courtyard, altar, offering table, bones, cult objects, comparable northern-Syrian long-room form. It attests sanctuary architecture in Judah, not Solomon’s Temple site/copy. See [Tel Moza project](https://www.telmoza.org/) and anchor 90.<br>
特尔摩撒提供一个更靠近耶路撒冷、年代也较接近的比较实例：这座铁器时代 IIA 神庙约始建于主前 10 世纪末至 9 世纪初，有向东入口、院落、祭坛、供桌、动物骨骼和祭祀器物，并与北叙利亚长殿式传统可比较。它证明这种圣所建筑语言在犹大真实存在，却不是所罗门圣殿的遗址或复制品。见[特尔摩撒发掘项目](https://www.telmoza.org/)及索引锚点 90。

Unprovenanced antiques allegedly from the Temple stay outside core evidence.<br>
不要把任何来源不明、据说“出自圣殿”的古董纳入核心证据。

<a id="t1-23e-1261"></a>
## 14.2 Hazor, Megiddo, Gezer: Three Gates | 14.2 夏琐、米吉多、基色“三城门”

1 Kings 9:15 names Jerusalem, Hazor, Megiddo, Gezer. Twentieth-century archaeology linked similar six-chamber gates to Solomon.<br>
列王纪上 9:15 提到所罗门建造耶路撒冷、夏琐、米吉多和基色。20 世纪考古曾把三地相似的六室城门与所罗门工程相连。

Now assess separately:<br>
现在必须分开看：

<a id="t1-23e-1267"></a>
### Hazor | 夏琐

- Iron defenses/administrative buildings;
  有铁器时代城防和行政建筑；
- Excavators still argue some defenses tenth-century;
  发掘者继续主张部分城防可归于 10 世纪；
- Phasing/Solomonic association require combined pottery, layers, radiocarbon.
  具体分期和与所罗门的关联仍需依赖陶器、地层与碳十四综合判断。

<a id="t1-23e-1273"></a>
### Gezer | 基色

- Several boundary inscriptions securely identify site;
  基色身份由多块边界铭文稳固确认；
- New radiocarbon begins Stratum 8 monumental construction in 998–957 BCE (68.3% highest posterior density;95.4%: 1023–942 BCE);
  新的碳十四序列把第 8 层纪念性建设开端置于主前 998–957 年（68.3% 最高后验密度；95.4% 为 1023–942 年）；
- Gate, casemate wall, administrative buildings are therefore tenth-century, political attribution separate.
  六室门、城廓式城墙和大型行政建筑因此确实进入主前 10 世纪，但政治归属仍需另证。

See [2023 PLOS ONE dating](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0293119) and [excavators’ gate discussion](https://jjar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jjar/files/ortiz1_and_wolff_2021_jjar_1_221-240.pdf).<br>
参见 2023 年[《PLOS ONE》基色碳十四研究](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0293119)及发掘者的[城门讨论](https://jjar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jjar/files/ortiz1_and_wolff_2021_jjar_1_221-240.pdf)。

<a id="t1-23e-1281"></a>
### Megiddo | 米吉多

- Six-chamber gate/ashlar palaces once attributed to Solomon;
  六室门、方石宫殿和大型建筑曾被归给所罗门；
- A 2025 synthesis dates VA–IVB palaces/gate to early ninth-century Omrides;
  2025 年对 VA–IVB 层的综合研究把两座方石宫殿和六室门下调到主前 9 世纪前半、暗利王朝；
- Old tourism labels cannot replace layers/absolute chronology.
  这说明旧的“所罗门宫殿／城门”旅游标签不能替代地层和绝对年代。

[Chicago exhibit](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/Museum/ISAC%20Megiddo%20Exhibit%20Brochure.pdf) cautions against only biblical labels; [2025 Near Eastern Archaeology](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/736851) gives Omride redating. Strong conclusion, not an instruction to force Gezer/Hazor into identical dates.<br>
[芝加哥大学米吉多展览资料](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/Museum/ISAC%20Megiddo%20Exhibit%20Brochure.pdf)提醒参观者，不应只透过圣经标签理解遗址；[2025 年《Near Eastern Archaeology》研究](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/736851)给出暗利王朝重分期。该结论有力，却不应倒过来强迫基色或夏琐采用完全相同年代。

<a id="t1-23e-1289"></a>
### Methodological Caution | 方法提醒

Similar gate shape does not automatically mean:<br>
“城门形状相似”不自动表示：

- One architect;
  同一建筑师；
- One year;
  同一年建造；
- One king;
  同一位王下令；
- The excavated gate is exactly 1 Kings 9:15’s.
  列王纪上 9:15 特指的就是今天挖出的那一座门。

Hebrew syntax connecting earthworks/wall and city list is also debated; see [united-monarchy methodological assessment](https://openscholar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jjar/files/keimer_2021_jjar_1_68-101.pdf).<br>
而且该节希伯来文对“修筑土垒、城墙”与随后城市清单的语法关系也有讨论。参见[对联合王国考古方法的评估](https://openscholar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jjar/files/keimer_2021_jjar_1_68-101.pdf)。

**Evidence grades:**<br>
**证据等级：**

- Cities/Iron Age construction:A;
  城市与铁器时代工程存在：A；
- Individual tenth-century buildings:B/C by site;
  个别建筑定在 10 世纪：依遗址为 B/C；
- Direct Solomonic attribution:C.
  直接署名为所罗门工程：C。

## 14.3 How Large Was the United Kingdom? | 14.3 联合王国到底有多大

Current positions form a spectrum:<br>
可把当前观点理解为一条光谱：

| Model / 模型 | Main view / 主要看法 | Evidence risk / 证据风险 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large empire | Wide territory, mature administration, monuments | Later buildings assigned too early |
| 大型帝国模型 | 大卫、所罗门统治广阔领土，有成熟行政和大型工程 | 容易把后期建筑过早归入 10 世纪 |
| Regional state | Jerusalem core, Judah/corridors, varied northern alliance/control | Better accommodates Qeiyafa/Jerusalem differences |
| 区域国家模型 | 耶路撒冷为核心，控制犹大及若干战略走廊，与北方有不同程度联盟／支配 | 较能容纳凯雅法与耶路撒冷材料的差异 |
| Small highland chiefdom | Limited tenth-century Jerusalem/Judah, later statehood | Must explain early fortifications/rapid Davidic memory |
| 小型山地酋邦模型 | 10 世纪耶路撒冷与犹大规模有限，真正国家化较晚 | 需要解释早期设防中心及“大卫之家”迅速形成的记忆 |

Evidence more readily answers:<br>
现有证据更容易回答：

- Tenth-century political complexity? **Yes.**
  10 世纪有没有政治复杂化？**有。**
- Fortified centers/organized labor? **Yes.**
  有没有设防中心和劳力组织？**有。**
- Likely historical Davidic dynasty? **Yes.**
  大卫王朝是否很可能是历史王朝？**是。**

Harder questions:<br>
较难回答：

- Borders identical to Samuel’s maximal extent?
  联合王国边界是否与撒母耳记的最大范围完全相同？
- Extent of direct Jerusalem administration in northern cities?
  北方城市在何种程度受耶路撒冷直接行政控制？
- How ancient royal literary convention shapes Kings’ wealth numbers?
  列王纪所述财富数字应按何种古代王权文体理解？

## 14.4 Timna Copper: Wealth Networks, Not Certified Solomon’s Mines | 14.4 提姆纳铜业：财富网络与“所罗门矿山”误称

Site 34’s modern nickname Slaves’ Hill is not its ancient name. Fourteen short-lived samples place peak production early tenth century BCE. Defenses, smelting, pens, food remains show organized complex society/long-distance trade.<br>
提姆纳谷 Site 34 的现代昵称是“奴隶山”。14 个短寿命样本把铜生产高峰放在主前 10 世纪前半；防御、冶炼、畜栏和食物遗存显示，经营者是组织严密、参与远距离贸易的复杂社会。

This illuminates 1 Kings 7 copper and 9–10 trade, and reminds researchers that nonsedentary groups may run specialized industries while remaining less visible to city-focused archaeology.<br>
这项发现能照明列王纪上 7 章大量铜器和 9–10 章区域贸易的物质世界，也提醒研究者：非定居群体可以拥有高度专业化的产业，却在一般城市考古中不易被看见。

Three equations fail:<br>
但三个等号都不能成立：

- Tenth century BCE ≠ Solomon’s ownership;
  主前 10 世纪 ≠ 所罗门所有；
- Modern Slaves’ Hill ≠ demonstrated slavery;
  “奴隶山”现代昵称 ≠ 已发现奴隶制度；
- Large copper industry ≠ proven shipment to Jerusalem.
  大规模铜业 ≠ 铜已被证明运往耶路撒冷。

Operators more often linked to early Edomite groups; no direct royal Solomonic connection.<br>
经营者更常被研究者联系到早期以东群体；目前没有材料把矿山直接连接到所罗门王室。

**Evidence grade: A** for industry/radiocarbon/complexity; economic setting **B**; direct Solomon’s mines identification **D**.<br>
**证据等级：A**（工业遗址、碳十四与组织复杂性）；区域经济背景为 **B**；“所罗门矿山”直接认定为 **D**。

See [Ben-Yosef, Back to Solomon’s Era, BASOR 376](https://cris.iucc.ac.il/en/publications/back-to-solomons-era-results-of-the-first-excavations-at-slaves-h/) and [EU CORDIS report](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/334274/reporting).<br>
参考：[Ben-Yosef, “Back to Solomon’s Era,” *BASOR* 376](https://cris.iucc.ac.il/en/publications/back-to-solomons-era-results-of-the-first-excavations-at-slaves-h/)；[欧盟 CORDIS 项目总结](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/334274/reporting)。

## 14.5 Amenemope: Solomonic Tradition in International Wisdom | 14.5 《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》：所罗门传统属于国际智慧对话

British Museum EA 10474 recto preserves Egyptian Instruction of Amenemope: elder-to-younger teaching on vulnerable people, boundaries, greed, temper, banquets, with dense sequence/image/wording parallels in Proverbs 22:17–24:22.<br>
大英博物馆 EA 10474 的正面保存埃及《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》。它以师长教导子弟的形式处理贫弱者、边界、贪财、急躁和筵席礼仪，与箴言 22:17–24:22 形成密集的次序、意象和措辞平行。

Most relevant studies posit literary influence, not plagiarism of all Proverbs:<br>
多数相关研究认为两者存在文学影响，但这不等于整卷箴言“抄袭埃及”：

- Proverbs selects, reorders, rewrites;
  箴言自由选取、重排并重写材料；
- Similar counsel enters fear-of-the-Lord framework;
  相似劝诫被放进“敬畏耶和华”的整体框架；
- Surviving papyrus date differs from original composition, not a single Proverbs year;
  《教诲》现存纸草的日期不等于作品初创日期，也不能给箴言定出单一年份；
- Papyrus does not name Solomon; Proverbs itself has multiple headings/later collections.
  纸草没有提所罗门，箴言内部也明确包含多个标题和后期收集层。

Interpretively, biblical [wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) engages nations’ experience while reassessing wisdom through Creator, covenant ethics, fear of God.<br>
这项比较最重要的解读价值是：圣经[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)不怕与列国经验对话，却用创造主、圣约伦理和敬畏神重新判断何为真正的智慧。

**Evidence grade: A** for papyrus/text; literary relation **B+**; transmission/authorship/precise dates **C**.<br>
**证据等级：A**（纸草与文本）；两者文学关系为 **B+**；传播路线、作者和精确定年为 **C**。

See [British Museum EA 10474,2](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA10474-2) and [Emerton, Vetus Testamentum 51](https://doi.org/10.1163/15685330152939488).<br>
参考：[大英博物馆：EA 10474,2](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA10474-2)；[J. A. Emerton, *Vetus Testamentum* 51](https://doi.org/10.1163/15685330152939488)。

## 15. Shoshenq I / Shishak: Threshold of the Next Stage | 十五、舍顺克一世／“示撒”：下一阶段的门槛

1 Kings 14:25–26 recounts Shishak’s Jerusalem campaign/Temple-palace treasure seizure in Rehoboam’s fifth year.<br>
列王纪上 14:25–26 说罗波安第五年，埃及王示撒攻取耶路撒冷，夺去圣殿和王宫财宝。

Egyptian Twenty-Second Dynasty Shoshenq I left Karnak relief/place list; [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG55871) also identifies him with biblical Shishak.<br>
埃及第二十二王朝法老舍顺克一世在卡纳克留下迦南战役的胜利浮雕和地名表；大英博物馆也把[Sheshonq I](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/term/BIOG55871)列为圣经中的 Shishak。

<a id="t1-23e-1371"></a>
### Supports | 能支持

- Matching pharaonic name/date and southern-Levant campaign in late tenth century BCE;
  约主前 10 世纪末，埃及确有一位名字、年代相合的法老在南黎凡特发动战役；
- Many Israel/Judah/neighboring place names;
  其地名表包括许多以色列／犹大及邻近地区的地点；
- External setting shortly after Solomon.
  这为所罗门死后不久的区域政治提供外部锚点。

<a id="t1-23e-1377"></a>
### Does Not Directly Establish | 不能直接支持

- No undisputed Jerusalem in surviving legible names;
  现存可读地名中没有一个无争议的“耶路撒冷”；
- List does not mean every city captured/burned;
  地名表未必等于每座城都被攻破或焚毁；
- Royal propaganda, not neutral itinerary;
  卡纳克图像是王室胜利宣传，不是中立行军日志；
- One related destruction cannot automatically be Shishak’s.
  不能仅凭一个相关毁坏层就宣布“这一定是示撒所毁”。

The 2023 Gezer study accepts a southern-Levant campaign while warning against city-by-city destruction inference; see [peer-reviewed study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0293119).<br>
2023 年基色年代研究指出，多数学者接受舍顺克确曾进入南黎凡特，但也强调卡纳克表不能简单当作逐城攻击／毁灭清单。参见[原始同行评议研究](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0293119)。

**Evidence grade: A** for king/date/campaign; Jerusalem details related to 1 Kings 14 **B**.<br>
**证据等级：A**（法老、年代、南黎凡特战役）；与王上 14 的耶路撒冷细节对应为 **B**。

A storyline turn:<br>
这也是王国故事的转折：

> Solomon’s glory does not secure descendants; division, enemies, treasure loss reveal covenant-breaking monarchy’s fragility.<br>
> 所罗门的荣耀没有保证后代安全；分裂、外敌和圣殿财宝被夺，开始显出背约王国的脆弱。

<a id="t1-23f-1513"></a>
## 17. Key Scripture Notes | 十七、关键经文注释

<a id="t1-23f-1515"></a>
### Joshua 6: Fallen Walls | 约书亚记 6 章：城墙倒塌

- God delivers the firstfruits city, not a modern siege-engineering report.
  经文焦点是神把“初熟”的第一城交给以色列，不是提供现代攻城工程报告。
- Devoted things/Achan stress victory is not private plunder.
  “当灭之物”与亚干事件相连，强调胜利不属于私人掠夺。
- Fallen walls/fire/grain require chronological testing before Joshua attribution.
  遗址有倒塌、火毁和粮食材料，不代表不经年代检验即可归给约书亚。

<a id="t1-23f-1521"></a>
### Joshua 10:40/11:23: Whole Land | 约书亚记 10:40、11:23：夺了全地

- Read with13:1 and unexpelled peoples.
  应与书 13:1 和各支派未赶出清单同读。
- Summary of defeated coalitions/new promise stage, not compulsory claim of every inch occupied.
  是主要联盟被打败和应许进入新阶段的总结，不必强解成每寸土地已实际占领。

<a id="t1-23f-1526"></a>
### Joshua 11:10: Hazor’s Preeminence | 约书亚记 11:10：“夏琐素来在诸国中是为首的”

- Size, palace, regional standing give strong background.
  夏琐的巨大规模、宫殿和区域地位，为这句提供强历史背景。
- Accurate setting does not identify destroyer.
  背景准确不等于毁城者身份已由考古确定。

<a id="t1-23f-1531"></a>
### Judges 5: Deborah’s Song | 士师记 5 章：底波拉之歌

- Uneven mobilization, local leaders, chariot advantage.
  诗歌呈现支派动员不齐、地方领袖和战车优势。
- Plains suit chariots; highlands offer other advantages.
  平原城邦适合使用战车，山地村落在地形上有不同优势。
- Archaic language is debated; linguistic dating cannot give one precise year.
  诗歌的古老语言特征常被讨论，但语言年代不能精确到某一年。

<a id="t1-23f-1537"></a>
### Judges 17–21: No King | 士师记 17–21 章：“那时以色列中没有王”

- Not mere advocacy that any king is better.
  不是单纯宣传“任何君王都比没有好”。
- Samuel shows unrestrained kings reproduce judges-era sin.
  撒母耳记随后证明：不受神约束的王会复制士师时代的罪。
- Hope for righteous rule obedient to the Lord.
  真正盼望是既有公义治理、又顺服耶和华的王。

<a id="t1-23f-1543"></a>
### Ruth 4:17–22: Toward David | 路得记 4:17–22：通向大卫

- Ordinary family loyalty joins royal storyline.
  家谱把普通家庭的忠诚接到王国主线上。
- Behind major events, widows/harvest/marriage/kin obligation preserve redemption’s thread.
  历史书的大事件背后，神借寡妇、收割、婚姻和家族责任保存救赎线。

<a id="t1-23f-1548"></a>
### 1 Samuel 8: Kingship | 撒母耳记上 8 章：立王

- Wrong lies not merely in requesting a system, but imitation of nations/replacing trust in God.
  百姓的错误不只是请求制度，而是“像列国一样”并以王取代对神的信靠。
- Royal demands reflect ancient Near Eastern labor, land, livestock, taxation.
  王的征用清单反映古代近东王权会抽取劳力、土地、牲畜和税收。
- Deuteronomy 17 places king under law;1 Samuel 8 warns of unrestrained power.
  申命记 17 章要求王受律法约束；撒上 8 章警告王权不受约束的代价。

<a id="t1-23f-1554"></a>
### 2 Samuel 7: Davidic Covenant | 撒母耳记下 7 章：大卫之约

- David proposes God’s house/Temple; God promises David’s house/dynasty.
  大卫想为神建“殿／家”，神反而应许为大卫建立“家／王朝”。
- Partly realized in Solomon, but royal sin points toward a later Davidic son.
  应许先在所罗门身上部分实现，却因诸王犯罪继续指向更远的大卫之子。
- Tel Dan supports a real dynastic title; covenant theology comes from Scripture.
  特尔但碑可支持“这个王朝称谓真实存在”，但约的神学意义来自经文启示。

<a id="t1-23f-1560"></a>
### 1 Kings 9:15: Construction | 列王纪上 9:15：所罗门建筑工程

- Identify named cities, then assess each stratigraphy.
  先确认经文提到哪些城市，再分别判断各地地层。
- Three six-chamber gates do not predetermine king/year.
  不要因为三地都有六室门，就先假定同王同年。
- Cross-check form, pottery, layers, radiocarbon, inscription.
  建筑类型、陶器、地层、碳十四和铭文必须交叉验证。

<a id="t1-23f-1566"></a>
### 1 Kings 11: Cracks at the Peak | 列王纪上 11 章：王国高峰中的裂缝

- Wisdom, Temple, foreign relations are not the end.
  所罗门的智慧、圣殿和国际关系不是结局。
- Multiple wives, idols, labor, regional tension foreshadow division.
  多妻、偶像、重役与地区张力为分裂埋下伏笔。
- Archaeology asks state size; Scripture covenant loyalty. Neither replaces the other.
  考古争论王国“有多大”，经文则追问王“是否忠于约”；两种问题不能彼此替代。

<a id="t1-23f-1572"></a>
### Manuscript Note: Joshua 4Q47/48 and Kings 4Q54/5Q2 | 抄本注释：4Q47／4Q48《约书亚记》与 4Q54／5Q2《列王纪》

This study’s Late Bronze/Iron cities, people, state formation differ from the Second Temple copying layer.<br>
这一专题原先主要处理晚青铜—铁器时代的城市、人口与王国形成；昆兰残卷补的是另一个年代层：第二圣殿时期经文怎样抄传。

- 4Q47/48 are Hasmonean Hebrew leather Joshua. Common 4Q47 reconstruction places later 8:34–35 law reading after Jordan crossing/before chapter 5 circumcision;4Q48 nearer later Masoretic tradition.
  4Q47／4Q48 是两份哈斯摩尼时期《约书亚记》希伯来文皮卷。4Q47 的常见拼接把后来位于书 8:34–35 的读律法材料放在过约旦河之后、书 5 割礼之前；4Q48 大体更接近后来马所拉传统。
- Altar/law-reading location is a real text-history question; earlier sequence, joins, geography debated. Smoother narrative does not equal autograph.
  祭坛／读律法段落的不同位置是真实文本史问题；4Q47 是否保存较早次序、残片是否应如此拼接，以及地点应怎样理解，仍有争论。不能把“叙事更顺”直接等同“作者原稿”。
- 4Q54 preserves 1 Kings 7–8 vessels/ark/prayer;5Q2 preserves chapter 1 succession.4Q54’s 8:16 longer reading absent from Masoretic Kings occurs in 2 Chronicles 6:5b–6a/some Old Greek.
  4Q54 保存王上 7–8 的圣殿器具、约柜入殿与祷告段落；5Q2 保存王上 1 所罗门继位部分。4Q54 在王上 8:16 有一段马所拉《列王纪》缺失、却见于代下 6:5b–6a 与部分古希腊本的长读。
- 6Q4 extends Kings material to 1 Kings 3/12/22 and 2 Kings 5–10; see [divided kingdom study](./分裂王国至归回_帝国史料与逐事件对照.md).
  6Q4 还把《列王纪》文本实物延伸到王上 3、12、22 与王下 5–10，详见[分裂王国专题](./分裂王国至归回_帝国史料与逐事件对照.md)。
- These witnesses lie centuries/a millennium after events: Hebrew transmission/continuity/difference, not Joshua campaigns/Temple building/state size.
  四卷与本专题事件相隔数百至千年。它们能证明古代希伯来文本的流传、连续性与差异，不能证明约书亚战役、所罗门建殿或王国规模。

Keep two questions distinct:<br>
因此要把两类问题分开：

> Archaeology asks about period cities, settlement, destruction, organization; manuscripts ask later wording/order. Neither is an autograph or event-site record respectively.<br>
> 考古问“这一时期有哪些城、聚落、毁坏与政治组织”；抄本学问“数百年后这些书卷以哪些文字和次序流传”。前者不是经文原稿，后者也不是事件现场。

Melito/Origen catalogues (204–205) add third layer: Melito separates Judges/Ruth, combines modern Samuel/Kings as four Kings; Origen combines Judges–Ruth and Samuel/Kings/Chronicles pairs. Second/third-century CAT reception, not new conquest/judges/David-size/Solomon archaeology. See [ancient catalogue study](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md).<br>
梅利托、俄利根书目（锚点 204–205）再增加第三层：梅利托把《士师记》《路得记》分列，却用“列王四卷”合计现代《撒母耳记》与《列王纪》；俄利根则把《士师记—路得记》《撒母耳记》《列王纪》《历代志》分别按合卷计数。这是二、三世纪的 CAT（书目列名）接收，不能倒作约书亚征服、士师人物、大卫王朝规模或所罗门工程的新考古证据。计数详见[古代旧约书目专题](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md)。

<a id="t1-23f-1608"></a>
## 19. Research and Teaching Rules | 十九、研究与教学使用守则

<a id="t1-23f-1610"></a>
### Appropriate Claims | 可以说

- Material confirms related people, city, dynasty, or political setting in the period.
  “这件材料确认同一时期存在相关民族、城市、王朝或政治格局。”
- Destruction is chronologically/materially compatible with a narrative.
  “这个遗址的毁坏与经文事件在年代和性质上相容。”
- Discovery makes a reconstruction more plausible.
  “这项发现提高某种历史重建的可能性。”
- Mainstream reading with important dissent.
  “主流释读如此，但仍有重要异议。”

<a id="t1-23f-1617"></a>
### Inappropriate Claims | 不宜说

- Archaeology proves the Bible 100%.
  “圣经已经被考古百分之百证明。”
- Not found means never happened.
  “没有找到，所以一定没有发生。”
- Fire means Joshua/David/Shishak did it.
  “有火毁，所以一定是约书亚／大卫／示撒所为。”
- European-related DNA means all Philistines are European.
  “发现欧洲相关 DNA，所以非利士人都是欧洲人。”
- Six chambers means Solomon designed it.
  “找到六室门，所以这是所罗门亲自设计的。”
- Tel Dan proves every David verse.
  “特尔但碑证明了大卫故事的每一节。”

<a id="t1-23f-1626"></a>
### Six Questions for New Evidence | 每次新增证据先问六题

1. Reliable in-place stratigraphy?
   文物是否来自可靠原位地层？
2. Dating by pottery, script, radiocarbon, or biblical guess?
   年代靠什么确定：陶器、古文字、碳十四，还是经文猜测？
3. Secure location identification?
   地点认定有多稳？
4. Complete wording, or restored key words?
   文字是否完整，关键字是否需要复原？
5. Person, dynasty, setting, or actual event?
   它确认的是人物、王朝、背景，还是具体事件？
6. Alternative historical models explaining it equally well?
   是否存在同样能解释材料的其他历史模型？

Keep two commitments:<br>

> Honest historical research helps faith avoid unproved publicity presented as testimony.<br>
> 历史研究的诚实，不会削弱信仰；它帮助我们不把未经证明的宣传当作见证。
