## 0. The Story in One Overview | 0. 一句话总览

The Bible tells of God's good [creation](../Glossary/README.md#creation) and humanity's estrangement through sin. God chooses Abraham's family, forms [Israel](../Glossary/README.md#israel), and prepares [redemption](../Glossary/README.md#redemption) through law, [priests](../Glossary/README.md#priest), kings, [temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple), and [prophets](../Glossary/README.md#prophet). Israel fails and is exiled, yet God promises a new king, [covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant), heart, and creation. Jesus [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) fulfills these promises, opening [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation) through death and [resurrection](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection). Through the [church](../Glossary/README.md#church), the [gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel) reaches the nations, culminating in restored life with God in the new heaven and earth.<br>
圣经讲的是：神[创造](../Glossary/README.md#creation)美好的世界，人类因罪远离神；神拣选亚伯拉罕一家，形成[以色列](../Glossary/README.md#israel)，借着律法、[祭司](../Glossary/README.md#priest)、君王、[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)和[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)预备[救赎](../Glossary/README.md#redemption)；以色列失败并[被掳](../Glossary/README.md#exile)，但神[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)新的君王、新的约、新的心和新的创造；耶稣[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)来成全这一切，借着祂的死与[复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection)开启[救恩](../Glossary/README.md#salvation)，并借[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)把[福音](../Glossary/README.md#gospel)传向万国，最终在新天新地中完全恢复神与人同住。

## 2. The Shortest Timeline | 2. 最短时间线

First compress the whole Bible into twelve stages:<br>
先把整本圣经压缩成 12 段：

1. Creation: God creates heaven and earth; humans bear God's image.
   创造：神创造天地、人按神形象被造。
2. Fall: humans sin; sin, death, and curse enter the world.
   堕落：人犯罪，罪、死亡、咒诅进入世界。
3. Flood and Babel: corruption spreads; God judges and preserves a remnant.
   洪水与巴别：人类败坏扩散，神审判，也保守余民。
4. Abrahamic covenant: God chooses a family and promises descendants, land, and [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing) for all nations.
   亚伯拉罕之约：神拣选一个家族，应许后裔、土地、万国得福。
5. Exodus and Sinai: God rescues Israel, gives the law, and forms a covenant people.
   出埃及与西奈：神拯救以色列，赐律法，建立立约子民。
6. Wilderness and Canaan: unbelief leads to wandering; the next generation enters the promised land.
   旷野与进迦南：百姓不信，漂流；下一代进入应许之地。
7. Judges: repeated sin, oppression, cries for help, and rescue.
   士师时代：以色列反复犯罪、受压、呼求、蒙拯救。
8. Kingdom: Saul, David, Solomon; the Davidic covenant opens messianic hope.
   王国时代：扫罗、大卫、所罗门；大卫之约带出弥赛亚盼望。
9. Division and exile: Israel and [Judah](../Glossary/README.md#judah) break covenant; Assyria defeats the north and Babylon the south.
   分裂与被掳：以色列和犹大背约，北国亡于亚述，南国亡于巴比伦。
10. Return and waiting: temple and walls are rebuilt, but full restoration remains ahead.
    归回与等候：圣殿、城墙重建，但真正恢复还未完成。
11. Jesus Christ: [incarnation](../Glossary/README.md#incarnation), proclamation of God's kingdom, death, resurrection, ascension.
    耶稣基督：[道成肉身](../Glossary/README.md#incarnation)、传[神国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)、受死、复活、升天。
12. Church and new creation: the Spirit comes, the gospel reaches nations, and finally a new heaven and earth.
    教会与新创造：[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)降临、福音传万国，最终新天新地。

## 4. Detailed Chronology and Content Threads | 4. 详细时间线与内容线

<a id="t1-21b-141"></a>
### 4.1 Creation: God's World Begins Good | 4.1 创造：神的世界本来是好的

Main passage: Genesis 1–2<br>
主要经文：创世记 1-2

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- God creates heaven and earth.
  神创造天地。
- Humans are made in God's image.
  人按神形象被造。
- Humans are placed in the garden to steward, worship, and obey.
  人被放在园中，承担管理、敬拜、顺服的使命。
- God is present with humanity in a world of order, blessing, and life.
  神与人同在，世界有秩序、有[祝福](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)、有生命。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- God is the Creator, not part of the world.
  神是创造主，不是世界的一部分。
- Humans have dignity because they bear God's image.
  人有尊严，因为人按神形象被造。
- Their task is not to become God but to govern creation under God.
  人的使命不是自己作神，而是在神之下治理受造界。
- The Bible's final new creation restores and completes the original purpose rather than discarding creation.
  圣经最后的新创造不是废掉创造，而是恢复和完成神起初的目的。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> The Bible begins with God creating and giving life, rather than humanity searching for God.<br>
> 圣经不是从“人找神”开始，而是从“神创造并赐生命”开始。

<a id="t1-21b-165"></a>
### 4.2 Fall: Sin Breaks Every Relationship | 4.2 堕落：罪把关系全部打坏

Main passage: Genesis 3<br>
主要经文：创世记 3

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- The serpent tempts humans to doubt God's word and goodness.
  蛇引诱人怀疑神的话和良善。
- Adam and Eve disobey God.
  亚当夏娃违背神。
- Sin brings shame, fear, blame, death, curse, and expulsion.
  罪带来羞耻、惧怕、推责、死亡、咒诅和被逐出园。
- God announces judgment and hope together: the woman's offspring will overcome the serpent.
  神同时宣告审判和盼望：女人的后裔将来要胜过蛇。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Sin is more than a small error: humans seek independence from God and define good and evil for themselves.
  罪不是小错误，而是人想脱离神、自己定义善恶。
- Sin damages four relationships: with God, oneself, other people, and creation.
  罪破坏四重关系：人与神、人与自己、人与人、人与受造界。
- Expulsion from the garden patterns later exile: leaving the place of God's presence.
  “被赶出园”是后来“被掳”的原型：离开神同在之地。
- Genesis 3:15 seeds the promise of redemption.
  创 3:15 是救赎应许的种子。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> The later rescues, sacrifices, covenants, kings, prophets, and Jesus respond to Genesis 3's problem.<br>
> 后面所有拯救、献祭、圣约、君王、先知、耶稣，都在回应创世记 3 的问题。

### 4.3 Flood and Babel: Sin Spreads; God Preserves the Promise | 4.3 洪水与巴别：罪扩散，神保守应许线

Main passage: Genesis 4–11<br>
主要经文：创世记 4-11

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Cain kills Abel and violence spreads.
  该隐杀亚伯，暴力扩散。
- Human corruption deepens; God judges through the flood.
  人类败坏严重，神用洪水审判。
- Noah's household is preserved and God covenants with Noah.
  挪亚一家蒙保守，神与挪亚立约。
- At Babel, people unite in pride and self-glory; God confuses languages and scatters them.
  巴别塔事件中，人类以骄傲和自我荣耀联合，神变乱语言，使人分散。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Sin does not remain in individual hearts; it also shapes culture, cities, and structures of power.
  罪不是只停在个人心里，也会形成文化、城市、权力结构。
- God judges sin without abandoning the plan for creation.
  神审判罪，但不毁掉祂的创造计划。
- The nations scattered after Babel form the setting for Abraham's promise of blessing for all nations.
  巴别之后“万国”分散，为亚伯拉罕“万国得福”的应许做背景。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Genesis 1–11 concerns humanity's shared problem; from Genesis 12, God uses one family to bless all nations.<br>
> 创世记 1-11 是全人类问题；创世记 12 开始，神用一个家族来祝福万国。

### 4.5 Isaac, Jacob, Joseph: Promise Continues Through a Frail Family | 4.5 以撒、雅各、约瑟：应许在软弱家庭中继续

### 4.7 Sinai, Law, Tabernacle: The Holy God Dwells Among People | 4.7 西奈、律法、会幕：圣洁的神住在人中间

### 4.8 Joshua and Judges: Land Does Not Solve the Heart's Problem | 4.8 约书亚与士师：得地以后，人心问题仍在

Main books: Joshua, Judges, Ruth<br>
主要经文：约书亚记、士师记、路得记

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Joshua leads Israel into Canaan.
  约书亚带领以色列进入迦南。
- The land promise begins to be realized.
  土地应许开始实现。
- Israel is not wholly faithful; Judges cycles through sin, oppression, cries for help, rescue, and renewed sin.
  以色列没有完全忠心，士师时代进入循环：犯罪、受压、呼求、拯救、再犯罪。
- Ruth shows God's quiet faithfulness amid disorder and connects to David's genealogy.
  路得记在混乱时代中显出神安静的信实，并连接到大卫家谱。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Receiving land does not end the problem.
  得地不等于问题结束。
- The deepest human problem concerns the heart, not circumstances.
  人真正的问题不是环境，而是心。
- Judges' closing emphasis on the absence of a king prepares the monarchy.
  士师记最后强调“那时以色列中没有王”，为君王时代铺路。

Historical/archaeological notes:<br>
历史与考古说明：

- The late-thirteenth-century BCE Merneptah Stele already names Israel in Canaan but does not narrate the Exodus or Joshua's campaigns.
  主前 13 世纪末的梅尔内普塔碑已在迦南提到“以色列”，但没有叙述出埃及或约书亚战役。
- Jericho, Ai, and Hazor's strata do not support a simple, synchronized city-by-city destruction model. Hazor has major late-Late Bronze burning, but the destruction layer alone cannot identify its destroyers.
  耶利哥、艾城、夏琐的地层并不支持一次简单、逐城同步的毁灭模型；夏琐有明显的晚青铜时代末火毁，毁城者身份仍无法由烧毁层单独确定。
- Hundreds of small villages appeared in the central highlands in the twelfth–eleventh centuries BCE, compatible with Judges' decentralized tribal society. Individual artifacts are not ethnic identity cards.
  主前 12–11 世纪中央山地出现数百个小村落，与《士师记》的分散部落社会相容；村落器物不能逐件充当族群身份证。
- Coastal Philistine culture combines Aegean connections and local continuity; Ashkelon ancient DNA supports some migration around the Bronze–Iron transition.
  非利士沿海文化兼有爱琴海联系与本地连续性，亚实基伦古 DNA 支持青铜—铁器转折时确有部分外来人口迁入。
- 4Q47/4Q48 are two Hasmonean Hebrew Joshua fragments. A common reconstruction of 4Q47 places the law-reading later at Joshua 8:34–35 after the Jordan crossing and before circumcision in chapter 5, establishing ancient variation in sequence. It cannot prove conquest; priority and joins remain disputed (anchor 178).
  4Q47／4Q48 是哈斯摩尼时期两份《约书亚记》希伯来残卷；4Q47 的常见拼接把后来位于书 8:34–35 的读律法材料放在过约旦之后、书 5 割礼之前，证明古代段落次序并不完全一致。它不能证明征服事件，较早次序与拼片方案也仍有争论（锚点 178）。
- 1Q6 and 4Q49/4Q50 are Hebrew Judges fragments centuries later. 4Q49 lacks Judges 6:7–10, establishing local textual difference, but shorter alone cannot identify the whole original or prove Gideon's events.
  1Q6、4Q49／4Q50 是数百年后的《士师记》希伯来残卷；4Q49 没有士 6:7–10，证明局部文本曾有差异，却不能由“较短”直接判定全书原版或证明基甸事件。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Judges creates expectation for a truly righteous, faithful king leading the people back to God.<br>
> 士师记让你期待一个真正公义、忠心、能带领百姓归向神的王。

### 4.9 Saul, David, Solomon: Kingdom Growth and Emerging Crisis | 4.9 扫罗、大卫、所罗门：王国兴起，也埋下危机

Main passages: 1–2 Samuel; 1 Kings 1–11; 1 Chronicles; 2 Chronicles 1–9; selected [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm); Proverbs; Ecclesiastes; Song of Songs<br>
主要经文：撒母耳记上下、列王纪上 1-11、历代志上、历代志下 1-9、部分[诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)、箴言、传道书、雅歌

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Israel asks for a king.
  以色列要求立王。
- Saul rules but is rejected for disobedience.
  扫罗作王，但因不顺服被弃。
- David rises as a king after God's heart, yet also fails seriously.
  大卫兴起，成为合神心意的王，但他也有严重失败。
- God covenants with David concerning descendants and throne.
  神与大卫立约，应许他的后裔和国位。
- Solomon builds the temple and the kingdom peaks, but later declines through idolatry.
  所罗门建圣殿，王国达到高峰；晚年却因偶像崇拜衰败。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- The Davidic covenant is pivotal, clarifying messianic hope.
  大卫之约非常关键：弥赛亚盼望从这里更清楚。
- The temple centers divine presence and worship.
  圣殿成为神同在与敬拜中心。
- Even the best kings sin, revealing need for a king greater than David.
  但连最好的王也有罪，说明人需要比大卫更大的君王。

Historical/archaeological notes:<br>
历史与考古说明：

- The ninth-century BCE Tel Dan Stele uses the dynastic title House of David, crucial external evidence for David's dynasty, not every detail of his life or the united kingdom's whole territory.
  特尔但碑在主前 9 世纪使用“大卫之家”这一王朝称谓，是大卫王朝最重要的外证；它不能证明大卫生平的所有细节或联合王国的全部疆域。
- Khirbet Qeiyafa establishes a carefully planned fortified center around 1000 BCE, supporting early political integration without undisputed proof of direct Davidic control.
  基伯特·凯雅法显示约主前 1000 年已有规划严整的设防中心，支持早期政治整合，却不能无争议地证明该城由大卫直接管辖。
- Jerusalem's continuing occupation and later construction cause major disturbance. The tenth century is not archaeologically blank, but palace location and city size remain substantially disputed.
  耶路撒冷持续居住且后期工程扰动严重，10 世纪并非考古空白，但王宫位置和城市规模仍有实质争论。
- Gate shapes alone cannot label Hazor, Gezer, and Megiddo Solomonic. Each site's phases require independent stratigraphy, pottery, and radiocarbon assessment.
  夏琐、基色和米吉多的城门不能只凭形状一概署名“所罗门”；三地分期需分别由地层、陶器和碳十四判断。
- 1Q7 and 4Q51/4Q52/4Q53 are third–first-century BCE Samuel fragments. 4Q51's longer Nahash passage in 1 Samuel 10–11 establishes an ancient longer Hebrew text; narrative smoothness alone cannot settle the original or validate Saul's campaign.
  1Q7、4Q51／4Q52／4Q53 是主前三至一世纪的《撒母耳记》残卷；4Q51 保存撒上 10–11 的拿辖长读，证明一种古代较长希伯来文本存在，但不能单凭叙事更顺就裁定原文或验证扫罗战役。
- 4Q54, 5Q2, and 6Q4 are three second–first-century BCE Kings fragments. 4Q54 preserves 1 Kings 7–8, including a longer 8:16 agreeing with 2 Chronicles 6 and some Old Greek witnesses. They physically attest the Solomon–Jehu text, not royal archives or temple remains (anchor 179).
  4Q54、5Q2、6Q4 是三份主前二至一世纪《列王纪》残卷；4Q54 保存王上 7–8，并在 8:16 留下与代下 6、部分古希腊本相合的长读。它们给所罗门—耶户故事直接文本实物，却不是王室档案或圣殿遗物（锚点 179）。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> David is not the destination; he points toward the greater son of David, Christ.<br>
> 大卫不是终点，大卫指向更大的大卫之子：基督。

### 4.10 Division: Northern Israel and Southern Judah | 4.10 王国分裂：北国以色列与南国犹大

Main passages: 1 Kings 12–2 Kings; 2 Chronicles; prophets<br>
主要经文：列王纪上 12-列王纪下、历代志下、先知书

For sources, archaeology, and event-by-event detail, see [Divided Kingdom to Return](./分裂王国至归回_帝国史料与逐事件对照.md).<br>
史料、考古和逐事件详解：[分裂王国至归回：帝国史料与逐事件对照](./分裂王国至归回_帝国史料与逐事件对照.md)

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- The kingdom divides after Solomon's death.
  所罗门死后，王国分裂。
- The northern kingdom is Israel, eventually centered in Samaria.
  北国称以色列，首都后来在撒玛利亚。
- The southern kingdom is Judah, centered in Jerusalem.
  南国称犹大，首都耶路撒冷。
- The north largely persists in idolatry.
  北国基本持续偶像崇拜。
- A few good southern kings reform, but the overall course also declines.
  南国有少数好王改革，但整体也走向败坏。
- Prophets repeatedly call for [repentance](../Glossary/README.md#repentance), warn of judgment, and proclaim hope.
  先知不断呼召[悔改](../Glossary/README.md#repentance)，警告审判，也宣告盼望。
- Ahab in 853 BCE, Jehu in 841, Menahem around 738, Pekah, and Hoshea can be linked with Assyrian or neighboring inscriptions.
  主前 853 年的亚哈、841 年的耶户、约 738 年的米拿现，以及比加、何细亚，都可与亚述或邻国铭文相连。
- 6Q4 directly preserves Hebrew portions of 1 Kings 12, 22 and 2 Kings 5–10, with some short readings or ancient-version agreements in 2 Kings 7–8. This is later textual history, not event proof for division, miracles, or coups (anchor 179).
  6Q4 的纸草残片直接保存王上 12、22 与王下 5–10 的部分希伯来文字，其中王下 7–8 有若干较短或与古译本相合的读法；这是后期文本史，不是分裂、神迹或政变的事件证明（锚点 179）。
- 4Q82's Twelve scroll preserves identifiable Obadiah, Nahum, and Zephaniah fragments, supplying pre-Christian Hebrew physical witnesses for these companion prophets. The tiny remains, especially continuous Obadiah 1–15 lines, include modern bracketed restorations; they are not complete preservation or proof of prophetic dates/events (anchor 155).
  4Q82《十二先知》卷还保存俄巴底亚、那鸿和西番雅的可辨片段，使三卷“伴读小先知”也有前基督教希伯来文实物见证；残片非常零碎，俄 1–15 的连续行尤其含现代括号复原，不能写成完整保存，更不能由抄本反推先知日期或神谕事件（锚点 155）。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Kings evaluates rulers by faithfulness to YHWH rather than offering ordinary political history.
  列王纪不是普通政治史，而是用“是否忠于耶和华”评价王。
- Prophets do more than predict: as covenant prosecutors, they expose breach and announce judgment and restoration.
  先知不是单纯预测未来的人，而是约的检察官：指出背约，宣告审判和复兴。
- Idolatry and social injustice often go together.
  偶像崇拜和社会不义常常连在一起。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Read prophets within the divided kingdoms and their Assyrian/Babylonian historical settings.<br>
> 读先知书时，要把先知放回分裂王国、亚述、巴比伦这些历史背景中。

### 4.11 Exile: A Major Old Testament Crisis | 4.11 被掳：旧约最大危机之一

Main passages: 2 Kings 17, 25; Jeremiah; Lamentations; Ezekiel; Daniel<br>
主要经文：列王纪下 17、25；耶利米书；耶利米哀歌；以西结书；但以理书

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Assyria defeats northern Israel in 722 BCE.
  主前 722 年，亚述灭北国以色列。
- Research should express the northern ending as 722/720 BCE: 722 for the main fall, 720 for Sargon II's renewed suppression and provincialization.
  专题研究宜把北国结局写成主前 722/720 年：722 年为陷落主轴，720 年涉及撒珥根二世再次镇压和行省化。
- Babylon first captures Jerusalem in 597 BCE and deports Jehoiachin.
  主前 597 年，巴比伦首次攻陷耶路撒冷并掳走约雅斤。
- Babylon destroys Jerusalem and temple in 587/586 BCE. This guide's quick reference retains the common church date 586, while research notes both conversions.
  主前 587/586 年，巴比伦毁耶路撒冷和圣殿；本书速查沿用教会常见的 586 年，专题注明两种换算。
- Loss of land, king, and temple seems to empty both Abrahamic and Davidic promises.
  百姓失去土地、君王、圣殿，似乎亚伯拉罕之约和大卫之约都落空。
- The six Jeremiah fragments 2Q13 and 4Q70–72b show parallel Second Temple Hebrew readings close to the later longer Masoretic form and shorter Old Greek exemplar. Skin DNA identifies at least sheep and cattle. This is transmission history, not event proof for Jerusalem's fall or Jeremiah's authorship (anchor 180).
  2Q13、4Q70–72b 六份《耶利米书》残卷显示：到第二圣殿时期，接近后来较长马所拉型正文与接近较短古希腊底本的希伯来读法可以并行；羊皮 DNA 又确认其中至少有羊、牛两类材料。这是文本流传史，不是耶路撒冷陷落或耶利米执笔的事件证明（锚点 180）。
- The eight Daniel fragments 1Q71–72, 4Q112–116, and 6Q7 preserve chapters 1–11; 4Q174 separately quotes 12:10. 4Q114's new calibrated 2σ skin interval is 230–160 BCE, dating the carrier rather than a precise midpoint copying year, author, final composition, or prophecy's nature (anchor 181).
  1Q71–72、4Q112–116、6Q7 八份《但以理书》残卷现存文字覆盖 1–11 章，另有 4Q174 引但 12:10；4Q114 羊皮的新碳十四 2σ 范围为主前 230–160 年。这个范围测的是载体，不能取中点作精确抄写年，也不能单独决定作者、最终成书或预言性质（锚点 181）。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Exile climaxes judgment for covenant breach.
  被掳是“背约审判”的高潮。
- It also enlarges Eden's expulsion historically: departure from God's presence.
  这也是“出伊甸”的历史放大版：离开神同在之地。
- Yet prophets announce a remnant, new covenant, new heart/Spirit, and Davidic branch.
  但先知宣告：神会保留余民，赐新约、新心、新灵，兴起大卫的苗裔。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Exile does not end the story in failure. It reveals that people need renewed hearts as well as restored land.<br>
> 被掳不是故事失败，而是把人带到一个更深问题：人需要的不只是回到土地，而是心被更新。

### 4.12 Return: Home Again, Yet Restoration Incomplete | 4.12 归回：回来了，但还没完全恢复

Main books: Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi<br>
主要经文：以斯拉记、尼希米记、以斯帖记、哈该书、撒迦利亚书、玛拉基书

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Persian king Cyrus permits Jewish return.
  波斯王古列允许犹太人归回。
- Temple construction resumes under Darius and finishes around 516/515 BCE.
  圣殿在大流士时代重启工程，约主前 516/515 年完成。
- Ezra promotes law teaching.
  以斯拉推动律法教导。
- Nehemiah rebuilds the walls.
  尼希米重建城墙。
- Esther shows God's preservation of Jews dispersed among other peoples.
  以斯帖记显示散居外邦的犹太人仍被神保守。
- After Malachi, the Old Testament [canon](../Glossary/README.md#canon) enters a period of silence.
  玛拉基书之后，旧约正典进入沉默期。
- The Cyrus Cylinder supplies only a setting of restoring local shrines and resettling displaced populations. It does not name Jews, Jerusalem, or the temple.
  古列圆柱只提供恢复地方神庙和安置迁徙人口的政策背景，并没有直接提犹太人、耶路撒冷或圣殿。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Outward buildings recover, with less glory than before.
  外在建筑恢复了，但荣耀不如从前。
- Sin, priestly failures, and social injustice remain.
  百姓仍有罪，祭司仍失败，社会仍不完全公义。
- People keep waiting for the true king, cleansing, and new covenant.
  人们继续等候真正的王、真正的洁净、真正的新约。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> The Old Testament's close leaves problems unresolved and directs continued expectation toward the Messiah.<br>
> 旧约结束时，问题没有完全解决；它让人继续等候弥赛亚。

### 4.13 Between Testaments: Why Rome, Pharisees, and Synagogues at the New Testament's Opening? | 4.13 两约之间：为什么新约一开始有罗马、法利赛人、会堂？

Period: roughly 400 BCE to Jesus' birth<br>
时间：约主前 400 年到耶稣降生前

No Protestant Old Testament book continuously narrates this whole interval, but its history is not silent. Maccabean writings, Josephus, Dead Sea Scrolls, inscriptions, coins, and sites preserve rich material. Maccabean books have different canonical status in Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant traditions.<br>
新教旧约正典中没有一卷专门连续叙述这一整段，但这段历史并不“沉默”。《马加比书》、约瑟夫斯、死海古卷、铭文、钱币和遗址保存了丰富材料；《马加比书》在天主教、东正教与新教传统中的正典地位不同。

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Alexander's campaigns spread Greek culture and language. The Pentateuch was translated into Greek around the third century BCE; other books gradually formed Old Greek traditions.
  亚历山大东征后，希腊文化和希腊语广泛传播；摩西五经约于主前 3 世纪译成希腊文，其余书卷陆续形成古希腊译本传统。
- Judea passes from Ptolemaic to Seleucid control.
  犹太地先受托勒密王朝、后受塞琉古王朝控制。
- Temple desecration, religious prohibitions, and local power struggles under Antiochus IV prompt the Maccabean revolt beginning in 167 BCE.
  安条克四世时期的圣殿亵渎、宗教禁令和地方权力斗争，引发主前 167 年开始的马加比反抗。
- Slightly before this section's usual starting point, Elephantine Pap. Berlin P. 13464 gives an Egyptian Jewish garrison Nisan, seven-day unleavened-bread, rest, and leaven-removal instructions in 419–418 BCE. It connects Exodus festival memory to Persian diaspora life, without narrating the Exodus. Its so-called Passover Letter title alone cannot establish a complete Pentateuch, uniform regional practice, or the Last Supper's date (anchor 187).
  稍早于本段通常起点的主前 419–418 年，象岛 Pap. Berlin P. 13464 已给埃及犹太驻军尼散月、七日无酵、停工与除酵指令；它把出埃及节期记忆接到波斯时期散居生活，却没有叙述出埃及事件，也不能仅凭“所谓逾越节书信”题名证明完整五经、各地统一守节或福音书最后晚餐日期（锚点 187）。
- 4Q114 Daniel's skin interval of 230–160 BCE crosses the period around this crisis, with its later end overlapping the 160s BCE. It proves neither that the whole book was completed before the crisis nor that formation began only then (anchor 181).
  4Q114《但以理书》羊皮的主前 230–160 年碳十四范围跨入这场危机前后；晚端与主前 160 年代重合，因此它既不是“全卷必在危机前完成”的证明，也不是“只到危机时才开始形成”的证明（锚点 181）。
- 4Q175/4QTestimonia combines Deuteronomy 5/18's Moses-like prophet, Numbers 24's star/scepter, Deuteronomy 33's Levitical blessing, and Joshua 6:26's disaster in one short scroll. It shows first-century BCE Jewish combined reading of prophetic, royal, and priestly passages, without mentioning Jesus or establishing a uniform Jewish prophet–priest–king messianic formula (anchor 185).
  4Q175／4QTestimonia 把申 5／18 的“像摩西的先知”、民 24 的“星／杖”、申 33 的利未祝福及书 6:26 的灾祸放进同一短卷；它显示主前一世纪犹太人可以把先知、王权与祭司相关经文并读，却没有提耶稣，也不能据此制造一套全犹太教统一的“先知—祭司—君王弥赛亚”公式（锚点 185）。
- 11Q13/11QMelchizedek combines Leviticus 25's Jubilee, Deuteronomy 15's debt remission, Isaiah 52/61's good news/release, Psalm 82's judgment, and Daniel 9's periods into a final tenth Jubilee. It illuminates the Jewish interpretive world of Luke 4/Hebrews 7 without directly quoting Genesis 14 or Psalm 110, or proving those authors saw the scroll (anchor 186).
  11Q13／11QMelchizedek 把利 25 禧年、申 15 债务豁免、赛 52／61 的好消息与释放、诗 82 的审判和但 9 的时期语言组合成末世“第十禧年”；它帮助解释路 4、来 7 的语言为何处于可理解的犹太释经世界，却没有直接引用创 14 或诗 110，也不能证明路加或《希伯来书》作者见过该卷（锚点 186）。
- The temple's rededication in 164 BCE underlies the later Feast of Dedication, mentioned at John 10:22.
  主前 164 年圣殿重新奉献，成为后来修殿节的历史根源；约翰福音 10:22 提到这个节期。
- The Hasmonean family moves from revolt to priestly dynasty, autonomy, and expansion; concentrated power and civil war draw in Rome.
  哈斯摩尼家族由反抗者成为祭司王朝，取得自治并扩张，却因权力集中和内战把罗马引入。
- Pompey enters Jerusalem in 63 BCE. From 40/37 BCE, Herod the Great rules with Roman recognition and greatly expands the Second Temple.
  主前 63 年庞培进入耶路撒冷；主前 40/37 年起，大希律以罗马认可的王权统治并大规模扩建第二圣殿。
- After Herod's death, territories pass to Archelaus, Antipas, and Philip. Archelaus is removed in 6 CE and Judea comes under direct Roman governors.
  希律死后，领地分给亚基老、安提帕和腓力；主后 6 年亚基老被废，犹太地改由罗马长官直接治理。
- In New Testament times the temple remains central and synagogues provide local reading/teaching. Society includes Pharisees, Sadducees, scribes, priestly groups, Samaritans, Qumran-related communities, and diverse anti-Roman voices.
  到新约时，圣殿仍是中心，会堂承担地方诵读和教导；社会中有法利赛人、撒都该人、文士、祭司集团、撒玛利亚人、昆兰相关群体和多种反罗马声音。
- Josephus' Antiquities 18.116–119 (anchor 176) records influential John the Baptist being sent by Antipas to execution at Machaerus. The palace-fortress, halls, baths, and water systems (anchor 177) provide compatible material setting, without identifying John's cell, banquet, or Salome's dance floor.
  约瑟夫斯《犹太古史》18.116–119（锚点 176）另记一位有群众影响的施洗约翰被安提帕送到马凯鲁斯处死；当地宫堡、厅室、浴池和蓄水系统（锚点 177）给地点以相合物质背景，却不能认证约翰牢房、宴席或“撒罗米舞池”。
- The Pilate inscription/coins, Caiaphas ossuary, temple warning inscriptions, early synagogues, ritual baths, and ancient Galilee boat add evidence for the Gospels' governmental and material setting.
  彼拉多铭文、彼拉多钱币、该亚法骨棺、圣殿警告碑、早期会堂、礼仪浴池和加利利湖古船，为四福音的政治与物质背景提供其他旁证。
- Quirinius' registration in Luke 2 remains chronologically disputed. Common passion candidates are 30 or 33 CE; a safer broad placement is near Passover during Pilate's administration.
  路加福音 2 章的居里扭人口登记仍有年代争议；耶稣受难常见候选为主后 30 或 33 年，较稳妥的宽范围是彼拉多任内、逾越节附近。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Widespread Greek supplies conditions for gospel communication.
  希腊语普及为福音传播提供语言条件。
- Roman roads, shipping, cities, and imperial order supply later mission networks, while also representing oppression and imperial sovereignty.
  罗马道路、海运、城市和帝国秩序为后来宣教提供外在网络，却也代表压迫与皇帝主权。
- Expectations concerning Messiah, kingdom, temple, and law intensify.
  犹太人对弥赛亚、国度、圣殿、律法的期待变得更强烈。
- The Antiochene crisis brings martyrdom, resurrection, temple cleansing, and enduring faithfulness into sharper focus.
  安条克危机使殉道、复活、圣殿洁净和“忠心到底”的主题更加突出。
- Neither Hasmonean nor Herodian kingship fulfills David's covenant; expectation of the true king deepens.
  哈斯摩尼与希律王权都未成全大卫之约，真正君王的等待继续加深。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> The New Testament opens within unfinished Old Testament hope, rather than suddenly changing stories.<br>
> 新约不是突然换故事，而是在旧约未完成的盼望中开场。

For eight redemptive-history trunks plus wisdom/suffering/Spirit companion lines in each book, see the [Eleven-Theme Matrix](./52卷十一条主题线_逐卷追踪矩阵.md).<br>
完整政权表、宗派说明、史料边界和逐段经文注释见[《两约之间至四福音：第二圣殿、希腊化与罗马世界》](./两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md)；若要沿八条救赎历史主干及智慧、受苦、圣灵三条伴线直接追踪每卷，见[《52 卷十一条主题线：逐卷追踪矩阵》](./52卷十一条主题线_逐卷追踪矩阵.md)。

### 4.14 The Gospels: Jesus Fulfills the Old Testament | 4.14 四福音：耶稣成全旧约

Main books: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John<br>
主要书卷：马太、马可、路加、约翰

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Jesus is born.
  耶稣降生。
- John the Baptist prepares the way.
  施洗约翰预备道路。
- Jesus is baptized, tempted, proclaims God's kingdom, heals, expels demons, and calls [disciples](../Glossary/README.md#disciple).
  耶稣受洗、受试探、宣讲神国、医病赶鬼、呼召[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)。
- John is imprisoned and killed for speaking truth to Antipas; Jesus' path likewise emerges as suffering rather than seizure of power.
  约翰因向安提帕权力说真话被囚并被杀；耶稣的道路也逐步显明为受苦而非夺权。
- Jesus conflicts with religious leaders.
  耶稣与宗教领袖冲突。
- Last Supper, Gethsemane, arrest, trial, crucifixion, burial, resurrection.
  最后晚餐、客西马尼、被捕、受审、被钉十字架、埋葬、复活。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Jesus is true Israel, overcoming wilderness temptation and fully obeying the Father.
  耶稣是真以色列：祂在旷野胜过试探，完全顺服父。
- Jesus is true king, proclaiming and bringing God's kingdom.
  耶稣是真君王：祂宣讲并带来神的国。
- Jesus is true temple, concentrating God's presence in himself.
  耶稣是真圣殿：神的同在集中在祂身上。
- Jesus is true sacrifice and high priest, offering himself once.
  耶稣是真祭物和大祭司：祂一次献上自己。
- Jesus is David's son and Abraham's offspring, bringing blessing to all nations.
  耶稣是大卫之子，也是亚伯拉罕后裔，使万国得福。

Four Gospel emphases:<br>
四福音重点：

| Book / 书卷 | Emphasis / 重点 |
| --- | --- |
| Matthew | Promised Messiah and king, especially Old Testament fulfillment |
| 马太 | 耶稣是应许中的弥赛亚、君王，特别强调旧约应验 |
| Mark | Authoritative yet suffering Son of God; the fastest narrative pace |
| 马可 | 耶稣是有权柄却受苦的神子，节奏最快 |
| Luke | Salvation for sinners, the poor, and [Gentiles](../Glossary/README.md#gentile) |
| 路加 | 耶稣带来给罪人、穷人、[外邦人](../Glossary/README.md#gentile)的救恩 |
| John | The incarnate Son of God who gives life |
| 约翰 | 耶稣是道成肉身的神子，赐生命 |

Read external evidence through agreements and differences. Josephus (anchor 176) and the Gospels share John the Baptist, crowds, and execution by Antipas. Josephus emphasizes fear of unrest and names Machaerus; Matthew 14/Mark 6 emphasize marriage criticism, feast, and beheading. Machaerus archaeology (anchor 177) establishes a compatible Herodian palace-fortress, not independent proof of banquet details or the voice and Spirit at Jesus' baptism.<br>
历史旁证须按交集和差异一起读：约瑟夫斯（锚点 176）与福音书共同记得“施洗者约翰—群众—安提帕处死”，前者强调政治恐惧并点名马凯鲁斯，太 14／可 6 则强调婚姻谴责、宴席和斩首。马凯鲁斯遗址（锚点 177）确认一座年代相合的希律宫堡，却没有独立证明福音书宴会细节，更没有证明耶稣受洗时的天声与圣灵。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> The cross is not failure: Jesus redeems as a suffering king; resurrection begins new creation.<br>
> 十字架不是失败，是耶稣以受苦君王的方式完成救赎；复活是新创造的开始。

The resurrection narratives' common core/differences, women at the tomb and burial/testimony settings, empty-tomb models, 1 Corinthians 15's early tradition, Mark's endings, Holy Sepulchre remains, Nazareth Inscription, and Tacitus belong to distinct evidence layers. See [Resurrection and Early Witnesses](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md) for full grading.<br>
四福音复活叙事的共同核心和差异、妇女到墓的丧葬／证词语境、空墓研究模型、林前 15 的早期传统、马可福音结尾的抄本问题、圣墓教堂地下遗存、“拿撒勒敕令”和塔西佗分别属于不同证据层；完整分级见[《复活与早期见证：文本史、墓葬背景与历史研究边界》](./复活与早期见证_文本史与历史研究边界.md)。

### 4.15 Acts: Gospel from Jerusalem to the Ends of the Earth | 4.15 使徒行传：福音从耶路撒冷到地极

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Jesus ascends.
  耶稣升天。

  圣灵在[五旬节](../Glossary/README.md#pentecost)降临。

  教会在耶路撒冷兴起。

  福音向万国扩展，是亚伯拉罕之约“万国得福”的实现。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Acts moves from Jerusalem through all Judea and Samaria to the ends of the earth.<br>
> 使徒行传的方向是：耶路撒冷、犹太全地、撒玛利亚，直到地极。

For the three journeys, Jerusalem council, Roman trials, letter stages, and external evidence from Philippi, Thessalonica, Athens, Corinth, and Ephesus, see [Acts and Paul](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md). The Gallio inscription constrains Paul's Corinthian phase around 51–52 CE but does not name Paul.<br>
进一步把三次宣教旅程、耶路撒冷会议、罗马审讯、保罗书信写作阶段，以及腓立比、帖撒罗尼迦、雅典、哥林多和以弗所的外部资料放在一起，见[《使徒行传与保罗书信：旅程、年代与城市史料对照》](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md)。其中迦流铭文可把保罗在哥林多的阶段约束在主后 51–52 年附近；铭文本身不提保罗。

Third-century P30/P.Oxy. XIII 1598 separates journey chronology from later copying. Pages 207–208 continuously preserve 1 Thessalonians' ending and first-letter title, then 2 Thessalonians' title and opening, establishing paired copying then. Later than Paul, it cannot determine 2 Thessalonians' author, the composition interval, or end-time sequence (anchor 182).<br>
三世纪 P30／P.Oxy. XIII 1598 则把“旅程年代”和“后期传抄”明确分开：纸草的 207–208 页连续保存《帖撒罗尼迦前书》结尾、“第一封”标题、《帖撒罗尼迦后书》标题与开头，证明两封信当时已成对抄传；它晚于保罗时代，不能决定《帖后》作者、两封信写作间隔或末世时序（锚点 182）。

### 4.16 Letters: How the Gospel Shapes Church Life | 4.16 书信：解释福音如何塑造教会生活

Main books: Romans–Jude<br>
主要书卷：罗马书到犹大书

What happens:<br>
发生的事：

- Apostles write to different churches and coworkers.
  使徒写信给不同教会和同工。
- They address gospel truth, order, believers' lives, suffering, false teaching, and hope.
  处理福音真理、教会秩序、信徒生活、苦难、异端、盼望等问题。

Content thread:<br>
内容线：

- Romans: sin, [justification](../Glossary/README.md#justification), faith, Israel/Gentiles, and Christian life explained systematically.
  罗马书：系统解释罪、[称义](../Glossary/README.md#justification)、信心、以色列与外邦、基督徒生活。
- 1–2 Corinthians: the cross reshapes a disordered church.
  哥林多前后书：十字架如何重塑混乱教会。

  帖撒罗尼迦前后书：主再来与忠心生活。
- 1–2 Timothy and Titus: pastoral care and leaders.
  提摩太前后书、提多书：教会牧养与领袖。
- Hebrews: Christ surpasses old-covenant priests, sacrifices, and sanctuary.
  希伯来书：基督超越旧约祭司、祭物、圣所。
- James: genuine faith bears works.
  雅各书：真实信心必有行为。
- 1–2 Peter: sojourners hold hope amid suffering.
  彼得前后书：寄居者在苦难中持守盼望。
- Johannine letters: truth, love, life, and discernment.
  约翰书信：真理、爱、生命与分辨。
- Jude: contend for the faith.
  犹大书：为真道竭力争辩。

Takeaway:<br>
你要抓住：

> Letters explain how Jesus' completed work now shapes the church, rather than leaving the story for abstract doctrine.<br>
> 书信不是离开故事的道理，而是在解释“耶稣已经成就的事，现在怎样塑造教会”。

Canonical order is not composition order. More secure reconstructions place 1 Thessalonians at Corinth during journey two and 1–2 Corinthians/Romans during journey three. Galatians, prison letters, and Pastorals need parallel date/location models. See the [Pauline Letter Timeline and Certainty Notes](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md#pauline-letter-timeline).<br>
保罗书信的正典排列不等于写作顺序。较稳妥的重建把帖撒罗尼迦前书放在第二次旅程的哥林多阶段，把哥林多前后书与罗马书放在第三次旅程；加拉太书、监狱书信和教牧书信则需并列不同年代／地点方案。详见[保罗书信时间表与确定度说明](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md#pauline-letter-timeline)。

Philemon shows letters remaining within the story. P87/P139 directly preserve third-/fourth-century fragments; Oxyrhynchus slave-sale/manumission documents illuminate debt, price, ownership, and legal freedom in the same empire. These are transmission and comparative setting respectively, neither recording Onesimus. The letter does not explicitly say he fled, stole, or ultimately gained legal freedom, yet publicly addresses the house church and applies real ethical pressure through beloved brotherhood. See [P87/P139 and Slave Documents](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md#p87-p139-philemon-text).<br>
《腓利门书》尤其适合看见“书信怎样留在故事里”：P87 与 P139 直接保存三至四世纪经文残片，俄克喜林库斯奴隶买卖／解放文书另恢复同一帝国中欠债、价格、所有权和法律自由的社会重量；前者是文本史，后者是比较背景，都没有记录阿尼西母本人。经文没有明说他逃跑、偷窃或最终获得法律释放，却把请求公开写给家中教会，并以“亲爱弟兄”对既有权力关系施加实质伦理压力。详见[《使徒行传与保罗书信》专题中的 P87／P139 与奴隶文书分层](./使徒行传与保罗书信_旅程年代与城市史料对照.md#p87-p139-philemon-text)。

Hebrews, James, Petrine/Johannine letters, and Jude move into the apostolic age's later setting: the Second Temple's end, pressured diaspora groups, false teachers, apparent delay of the return, traveling workers, and conflicts with local leaders. For authors/date proposals, P72, and Pliny's Bithynian material, see [Hebrews to Revelation](./希伯来书至启示录_晚期新约与七教会背景.md).<br>
希伯来书、雅各书、彼得书信、约翰书信和犹大书则把故事带入使徒时代后半段：第二圣殿终结、散居群体受压、假教师、主再来似乎迟延、旅行同工和地方领袖冲突。各卷的作者／年代方案、P72 抄本及小普林尼的比提尼亚材料，见[《希伯来书至启示录：晚期新约、普通书信与七教会背景》](./希伯来书至启示录_晚期新约与七教会背景.md)。

Content thread:<br>

Takeaway:<br>

The most common composition proposal is around 95–96 CE; a late-60s date remains an important minority view. The seven churches are real city communities, not the later church buildings visible today. Imperial worship combines urban honor, sacrifice, guilds, and economy, not simply one empire-wide coercive decree. For Patmos, cities, 666/616, major millennium interpretations, and evidential limits, see [Later New Testament and Seven Churches](./希伯来书至启示录_晚期新约与七教会背景.md#revelation-genre).<br>

## 7. Common Sources of Confusion | 7. 最容易混乱的地方

<a id="t1-21f-1152"></a>
### 7.1 Why Do Kings and Chronicles Overlap? | 7.1 列王纪和历代志为什么重复？

- Kings emphasizes why Israel and Judah went into exile.
  列王纪更强调：以色列和犹大为什么走向被掳。
- The later Chronicles emphasizes how returnees rediscover identity through David, temple, and worship.
  历代志写作较晚，更强调：归回群体如何从大卫、圣殿、敬拜中重新认识身份。

Simply put:<br>
简单说：

> Kings explains failure; Chronicles rebuilds hope.<br>
> 列王纪解释失败，历代志重建盼望。

<a id="t1-21f-1161"></a>
### 7.2 Why Do Prophets Seem to Jump Around? | 7.2 先知书为什么读起来跳来跳去？

Prophetic books often include more than modern sequential storytelling:<br>
因为先知书常常不是按现代故事方式写，而是包含：

- Accusations against contemporary sin;
  对当时罪恶的控诉；
- Warnings of coming judgment;
  对将来审判的警告；
- Hope for remnant and restoration;
  对余民和复兴的盼望；
- Judgment on other nations;
  对外邦列国的审判；
- Visions of Messiah and new covenant.
  对弥赛亚和新约的远景。

How to read:<br>
读法：

1. Locate the prophet broadly within a kingdom period.
   先问这个先知大概在哪个王国时期。
2. Identify the main audience.
   再问他主要对谁说话。
3. Distinguish judgment and hope.
   再分辨审判和盼望。

<a id="t1-21f-1177"></a>
### 7.3 Why Gospel Repetitions and Differences? | 7.3 四福音为什么有重复又有差异？

The four Gospels concern one Jesus through interconnected but differing traditions, selections, and arrangements.<br>
因为四福音讲同一位耶稣，也经过彼此关联但不完全相同的传统、选材和编排。

- Repetition: core events matter to all.
  重复：说明核心事件共同重要。
- Difference: authors select and arrange for theological purposes.
  差异：作者按神学目的选择和安排材料。
- Relationship: Matthew/Luke likely use Mark; John's relationship with the Synoptics remains disputed. Four books cannot mechanically become four wholly independent testimonies.
  关系：马太、路加很可能使用马可；约翰与对观福音的关系仍有争论。因此“四卷书”不能机械换算成“四份完全独立口供”。

Do not treat the Gospels as rival biographies or erase differences into a minute-by-minute schedule the text never supplies. First hear each book's sequence and emphases; then compare common core, compatible differences, and genuinely open questions.<br>
不要把四福音当作四本互相竞争的传记，也不要先把差异抹成一张经文没有给出的分钟表；先让每卷按自己的次序和重点说话，再比较共同核心、可相容差异与真正需要保留的问题。

<a id="t1-21f-1187"></a>
### 7.4 How Do Paul's Letters Connect with Acts? | 7.4 保罗书信怎么和使徒行传对应？

A broad approach:<br>
粗略理解：

- Acts narrates mission history.
  使徒行传讲宣教历史。
- Paul writes to churches founded or cared for within that mission.
  保罗书信是写给这些宣教中建立或牧养的教会。
- Ask who received a letter, what problems they faced, and how the gospel responds.
  读保罗书信时，最好问：这封信写给谁？他们的问题是什么？福音怎样回应？

<a id="t1-21f-1195"></a>
### 7.5 Are Old and New Testaments Disconnected? | 7.5 旧约和新约是不是断开的？

No. The New Testament fulfills Old Testament promises rather than offering another religious story.<br>
不是。新约不是另一个宗教故事，而是旧约应许的成全。

Connections:<br>
对应关系：

| Old Testament problem/hope / 旧约问题/盼望 | New Testament fulfillment / 新约成全 |
| --- | --- |
| Sin and death | Jesus' cross and resurrection |

## 11. The Core to Remember | 11. 你真正要记住的核心

If you remember only seven statements:<br>
如果只能记 7 句话：

1. God created a good world; humans are made to dwell with and worship God and represent divine rule.
   神创造世界是好的，人被造是为了与神同住、敬拜神、代表神治理。
2. Sin estranges humanity from God and brings death, shame, violence, idols, and injustice.
   罪使人离开神，也使死亡、羞耻、暴力、偶像和不义进入世界。
3. God does not abandon the world, beginning through Abraham's family a plan to bless all nations.
   神没有放弃世界，而是借亚伯拉罕一家开始祝福万国的计划。
4. Israel's law, priests, sacrifices, temple, kings, and prophets prepare greater redemption.
   以色列的律法、祭司、献祭、圣殿、君王和先知都在预备更大的救赎。
5. Israel's failure and exile reveal the need for a new heart and covenant, beyond outward institutions.
   以色列失败并被掳，显明人需要的不只是外在制度，而是新心和新约。
6. Jesus is the promised Christ, fulfilling the Old Testament and bringing salvation through cross and resurrection.
   耶稣是应许中的基督，祂成全旧约，借十字架和复活带来救恩。
7. The church lives in God's inaugurated but incomplete kingdom, awaiting full divine–human dwelling in the new heaven and earth.
   教会活在已经开始但尚未完全的神国里，等候新天新地中神与人完全同住。
