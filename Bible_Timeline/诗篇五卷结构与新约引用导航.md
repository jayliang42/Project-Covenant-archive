<a id="psalms-guide-first-distinguish-four-evidential-layers"></a>
## First Distinguish Four Evidential Layers | 一、先把四层证据分开

| Layer / 层次 | Use in this guide / 本导航怎样使用 | Do not turn into / 不应越界成什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Textual facts** | Actual titles, book endings, verses cited in NT | Exact date from one preposition, title, or quotation |
| **文本事实** | 诗题实际写了什么；五卷在哪里收束；[新约](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament)引用了哪一节 | 不由一个介词、标题或引用自动推出精确年代 |
| **Compilation models** | Psalms 1–2 as entrance; royal crisis to divine kingship and final praise | One whole-collection model as sole scholarly consensus |
| **编集模型** | 诗篇 1–2 是否构成双重入口；五卷次序是否形成王权危机—耶和华作王—终末赞美的推进 | 不把一种整体阅读模型写成唯一学术共识 |
| **[Reception history](../Glossary/README.md#reception-history)** | Qumran and NT links with Davidic [covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant) and final hope | One group’s reading as all Second Temple Judaism |
| **接收历史** | 昆兰文本和新约作者怎样把诗篇与大卫之约、末世盼望相连 | 不把某个群体的读法等同于所有第二圣殿犹太人 |
| **Theological confession** | Christian reading through Jesus’ suffering, [resurrection](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection), exaltation, rule | [Faith](../Glossary/README.md#faith) conclusion as directly instrument-measured result |
| **神学认信** | 基督徒怎样在耶稣受苦、[复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection)、升高和掌权中读诗篇 | 不把信仰结论伪装成考古仪器可直接测量的结果 |

These layers illuminate but cannot replace one another. Avoid two opposite simplifications:<br>
这四层可以彼此照明，却不能互相代替。尤其要避免两种相反的简化：

- A royal original setting does not exclude later messianic interpretation.
  “诗篇原来有王室处境，所以后来不能作弥赛亚解释”；
- New Testament application to Jesus does not mean every line initially predicted only Jesus.
  “新约把某篇用于耶稣，所以原诗每一句起初都只在预测耶稣。”

A fuller reading traces original context, a new place within the collection, Second Temple reception, and New Testament application.<br>
较完整的读法会追踪同一段经文怎样经历原处境、诗集中的新位置、第二圣殿时期接收和新约应用。

<a id="psalms-guide-superscriptions-ancient-navigation-not-modern-copyright-pages"></a>
## Superscriptions: Ancient Navigation, Not Modern Copyright Pages | 二、诗题怎样读：是古代导航，不是现代版权页

<a id="psalms-guide-1-four-possible-functions"></a>
### 1. Four Possible Functions | 1. 诗题可能同时承担四种功能

| Function / 功能 | Examples / 常见例子 | First reading question / 研读时先问什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person/tradition | David, Asaph, Korah’s sons, Solomon, Moses | Authorship, collection, dedication, or associated tradition? |
| 人物／传统归属 | “大卫的”“亚萨的”“可拉后裔的”“所罗门的”“摩西的” | 是作者署名、所属诗集、献给某人，还是围绕此人的传统？ |
| Poetic genre | mizmor, shir, maskil, miktam | How securely is this ancient musical/genre term understood? |
| 诗歌类型 | *mizmor*（常译“诗”）、*shir*（歌）、*maskil*、*miktam* | 这个术语能确定到什么程度？有些古代音乐或文类词义已经不完全清楚 |
| Performance/liturgy | Choir direction, tunes, instruments, ascents | Performance context rather than composition date? |
| 演唱／礼仪说明 | “交与伶长”、某种曲调或乐器提示、上行之诗 | 它是否说明表演场景，而不是创作年代？ |
| Historical framing | Psalms 3, 18, 34, 51, 52, 54, 56, 57, 59, 60, 63, 142 connect to David | How does Samuel’s narrative reshape the first-person voice? |
| 历史性框架 | 诗篇 3、18、34、51、52、54、56、57、59、60、63、142 等把诗与大卫生平片段相连 | 标题怎样引导读者用《撒母耳记》的故事重读诗的第一人称声音？ |

The Hebrew preposition le- in leDavid can express belonging, dedication, purpose, or association. A Davidic superscription can carry an authorship tradition, but should not mechanically become a modern claim that David personally wrote this poem on an exact date. A title can also mark a collection, worship tradition, or later Davidic rereading.<br>
希伯来文 `leDavid` 的介词 `le-` 有“属于、给、为、关于”等语义范围。在具体诗题中，“大卫的”当然可以表达作者传统，但不能把每一处机械翻成现代意义的“本篇由大卫亲笔写于某日”。同一个标题还可能标示诗集归属、礼仪传统或后来的大卫式重读。

<a id="psalms-guide-2-the-value-of-historical-superscriptions"></a>
### 2. The Value of Historical Superscriptions | 2. 历史诗题的价值在哪里

Historical titles place general lament, gratitude, or confession within David’s story. For example:<br>
历史诗题把一般性的哀歌、感恩或认罪放进大卫故事。例如：

- Psalm 3’s connection to Absalom’s rebellion places its language of many enemies within royal crisis.
  诗篇 3 与押沙龙叛乱相连，使“许多人起来攻击我”进入王室危机；
- Psalm 51’s link to Nathan’s rebuke moves confession beyond private failure to the ruler’s sin.
  诗篇 51 与拿单责备大卫相连，使认罪不再只是私人失败，也触及掌权者的罪；
- Psalm 18’s near parallel with 2 Samuel 22 makes personal rescue part of Davidic royal memory.
  诗篇 18 与撒母耳记下 22 章几乎平行，使个人拯救成为大卫王权记忆的一部分。

These titles perform a real canonical interpretive role. They may preserve early information or reflect recontextualization through copying, compilation, and worship; they are not archaeological date stamps. Some [Septuagint](../Glossary/README.md#septuagint) titles, attributions, and numbering differ from the Masoretic tradition, making the transmission of titles itself a textual-history question.<br>
这些标题具有真实的[正典](../Glossary/README.md#canon)解释功能。与此同时，标题可能保存早期资料，也可能反映诗篇在传抄、编集和礼仪使用中的再语境化；不能把它们直接当成考古年代戳。希腊《[七十士译本](../Glossary/README.md#septuagint)》的部分诗题、归属和编号又与马所拉本不同，说明“标题怎样传下来”本身也属于文本史。

<a id="psalms-guide-3-a-repeatable-reading-process"></a>
### 3. A Repeatable Reading Process | 3. 一个可重复使用的读诗题步骤

1. Read the superscription as part of the textual tradition rather than skipping it.
   先把标题当作经文传统的一部分读出来，不跳过。
2. Identify whether it concerns person, genre, performance, tune, or historical scene.
   分辨它是在说人物、文类、演唱、曲调，还是历史场景。
3. Read the poem and ask how the title shapes its speaker, enemies, king, and sanctuary.
   再读诗正文，检查标题怎样改变“我、敌人、王、圣所”的指向。
4. Read the corresponding Samuel narrative when the title connects to David’s life.
   若标题连接大卫生平，同时阅读《撒母耳记》相应叙事。
5. Discuss authorship and date last, distinguishing certainty, possibility, and inability to decide.
   最后才讨论作者与年代，并明确结论是“确定、可能、无法判断”中的哪一级。

## The Five Books: Textual Divisions and Interpretive Story Models | 三、五卷诗篇：分卷是文本事实，故事推进是解释模型

<a id="psalms-guide-1-boundaries-of-the-five-books"></a>
### 1. Boundaries of the Five Books | 1. 五卷边界

| Book / 卷 | Range / 范围 | Closure or transition / 明显收束或转折 | Possible storyline reading / 可用的故事线阅读 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | Psalms 1–41 | Doxology at 41:13 | Law/[wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) and anointed king in 1–2; Davidic prayer amid hostility, sin, rescue |
| 第一卷 | 诗 1–41 | 41:13 的颂赞句 | 诗 1–2 以律法／[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)与受膏君王开门；随后大量大卫式个人祷告，把受膏者放在敌对、罪与拯救中 |
| II | Psalms 42–72 | Doxology 72:18–19; Davidic-prayer ending 72:20 | Korah, Zion longing, Davidic lament, Solomonic hope; ideal king’s justice, poor, universal [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing) in 72 |
| 第二卷 | 诗 42–72 | 72:18–19 的颂赞；72:20“耶西的儿子大卫的祈祷完毕” | 可拉诗、锡安渴慕、大卫哀歌与所罗门王权盼望并置；诗 72 以公义、穷人和万国[祝福](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)描写理想君王 |
| III | Psalms 73–89 | Doxology 89:52 | Asaph/Korah, sanctuary, national disaster; Psalm 89 climaxes dynastic crisis and questioned covenant |
| 第三卷 | 诗 73–89 | 89:52 的颂赞 | 亚萨与可拉传统突出圣所、全国灾难和困惑；诗 89 以“大卫之约为何像被废弃”把王朝危机推到顶点 |
| IV | Psalms 90–106 | Doxology 106:48 | Moses, [creation](../Glossary/README.md#creation), wilderness, failure; divine reign shifts hope from present Davidic king to enduring rule |
| 第四卷 | 诗 90–106 | 106:48 的颂赞 | 由“神人摩西的祈祷”重新回看[创造](../Glossary/README.md#creation)、旷野和以色列失败；多篇“耶和华作王”把盼望从现任大卫王转向神不动摇的统治 |
| V | Psalms 107–150 | No single ending identical to earlier books; 146–150 hallelujah finale, 150 closes collection | Gathering, pilgrimages, [Torah](../Glossary/README.md#law), Davidic voice, universal praise; suffering retained and directed toward all breathing creation’s praise |
| 第五卷 | 诗 107–150 | 没有与前四卷完全同式的单一卷尾；诗 146–150 连续“哈利路亚”，诗 150 为全诗集收束 | 被聚集者感恩、上行朝圣、妥拉默想、重新出现的大卫声音和普世赞美汇合；痛苦没有被删掉，却被带向“凡有气息的都要赞美” |

The doxologies ending books I–IV and Psalm 72:20 are directly observable markers; book V ends with a group finale. An editor’s exact historical metanarrative and the events corresponding to each stage are proposed compilation models needing argument, not a contents note on an excavated object.<br>
第一至第四卷末的颂赞句和诗篇 72:20 是可直接观察的文本标记；第五卷以整组终曲结束。至于编辑者是否有意设计一条精确的“历史元叙事”、每个阶段对应哪一事件，属于需要论证的编集模型，不是出土器物直接写明的目录说明。

<a id="psalms-guide-2-psalms-1-2-as-two-entrances"></a>
### 2. Psalms 1–2 as Two Entrances | 2. 诗篇 1–2：两扇入口

- **Psalm 1** asks who meditates on the LORD’s instruction day and night and walks the righteous path.
  **诗篇 1** 问：谁在耶和华的训诲中昼夜思想，走义人的道路？
- **Psalm 2** asks how nations respond to the LORD and his anointed king.
  **诗篇 2** 问：列国怎样面对耶和华和祂的受膏者？

Together they make Psalms more than collected emotions: it trains life in God’s word and hope under divine kingship. Their double-entrance function is a fruitful final-form reading, while the poems’ earlier formation and later placement at the opening remain separate questions.<br>
两篇并列，使整部诗篇不只是情绪选集，也成为“在神的话中生活、在神的王权下盼望”的操练。把两篇称为诗集的“双重入口”是很有解释力的最终形态阅读；但仍应承认，单篇较早的形成与后来被放在卷首不是同一个问题。

<a id="psalms-guide-3-a-brief-storyline-through-psalms"></a>
### 3. A Brief Storyline through Psalms | 3. 一条简明的诗篇故事线

| Stage / 阶段 | Psalms / 代表诗篇 | Developing question / 推进的问题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instruction and anointed one open collection | 1–2 | How do God’s people live and nations face God’s king? |
| 律法与受膏者开门 | 1–2 | 神的百姓怎样生活？万国怎样面对神的王？ |
| David prays in trouble | 3–41 | Why does the anointed one face enemies, sin, cry out, receive rescue? |
| 大卫在患难中祈祷 | 3–41 | 受膏者为何受敌、犯罪、呼求并蒙拯救？ |
| Expanded royal hope | 42–72 | How do sanctuary, Zion, nations, and righteous king connect? |
| 王权盼望扩展 | 42–72 | 圣所、锡安、万国和公义君王怎样相连？ |
| Temple/dynasty crisis | 73–89 | What becomes of promise after destroyed sanctuary, failed nation, fallen crown? |
| 圣殿与王朝危机 | 73–89 | 若圣所被毁、国家失败、大卫王冠落地，[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)怎么办？ |
| The LORD still reigns | 90–106 | Who remains eternal dwelling and king when people fade and generations fail? |
| 耶和华仍然作王 | 90–106 | 人会衰残、世代会失败，谁仍是永恒居所和君王？ |
| Return, pilgrimage, final praise | 107–150 | How do redeemed gather, travel, keep instruction, invite universal praise? |
| 归回、朝圣与终极赞美 | 107–150 | 被[救赎](../Glossary/README.md#redemption)者怎样重新聚集、行路、守道，并邀请万有赞美？ |

This model does not force every poem into one stage. Psalm 88 ends in darkness, and Psalm 89’s crisis resists cheap answers. The collection ends in praise, but its path retains confession, protest, imprecation, waiting, and tears.<br>
这条线不是要把每一首诗强塞进一个阶段。诗篇 88 甚至在黑暗中结束，诗篇 89 的危机也不会被廉价答案抹平。整部诗集的终点是赞美，但到达终点的路保留了认罪、抗议、咒诅、等候和哀哭。

## Royal Psalms: Historical Kings and Messianic Hope | 四、王权诗：从历史君王到弥赛亚盼望

<a id="psalms-guide-1-which-psalms-are-commonly-called-royal"></a>
### 1. Which Psalms Are Commonly Called Royal? | 1. 哪些通常称为王权诗

Psalms 2, 18, 20–21, 45, 72, 89, 101, 110, 132, and 144:1–11 commonly count as royal because they address king, anointed one, royal wedding, war, government, or Davidic covenant. Lists vary. Royal psalms are a genre/theme grouping, not an extra Masoretic book.<br>
因正文直接涉及王、受膏者、王室婚礼、战争、治理或大卫之约，诗篇 2、18、20–21、45、72、89、101、110、132、144:1–11 常被归入“王权诗”。学者的清单会略有不同；“王权诗”是文类和主题分类，不是马所拉本另设的一卷。

| Psalm / 诗篇 | Royal setting / 王权场景 | Question within the whole [Bible](../Glossary/README.md#bible) / 放入整本[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)时的关键问题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Enthronement, nations’ revolt, divine sonship language | How does 2 Samuel 7’s father/son dynasty language lead toward greater anointed hope? |
| 2 | 登基、列国反叛、神称王为“我的儿子” | 撒下 7 的父子—王朝语言怎样走向更大的受膏者盼望？ |
| 18 | King rescued and victorious | How does parallel 2 Samuel 22 turn personal rescue into dynastic witness? |
| 18 | 王蒙拯救并胜过仇敌 | 与撒下 22 并读时，个人拯救怎样成为王朝见证？ |
| 20–21 | Before-battle prayer, after-victory thanksgiving | Why pray for the king, and how does his outcome affect the people? |
| 20–21 | 出战前代祷与得胜后感恩 | 百姓为何为王祷告？王的胜败怎样牵动群体？ |
| 45 | Royal wedding and righteousness | How does Hebrews 1:8–9 apply historical wedding language to the Son? |
| 45 | 王室婚礼与王的公义 | 历史婚礼语言怎样在来 1:8–9 中被用于子的王权？ |
| 72 | Justice, poor, peace, nations’ blessing | How does the ideal king continue Abrahamic blessing and Davidic covenant? |
| 72 | 王的公义、穷人、和平与万国祝福 | 理想王怎样承接亚伯拉罕“万族得福”和大卫之约？ |
| 89 | Davidic covenant versus fallen crown | How does the post-collapse community question God about promise? |
| 89 | 大卫之约与王冠落地的冲突 | 经历亡国以后，群体怎样继续向神追问应许？ |
| 101 | Commitment to righteous rule | How does ideal rule judge actual kings? |
| 101 | 王对公义治理的承诺 | 理想治理为什么也成为对现实君王的审判标准？ |
| 110 | Right-hand lord and Melchizedek-order priest | How do kingship, priesthood, exaltation converge in Gospels, Acts, Hebrews? |
| 110 | 坐在右边的“我主”与照麦基洗德等次的祭司 | 王权、[祭司](../Glossary/README.md#priest)和升高怎样在[福音书](../Glossary/README.md#gospels)、使徒行传、希伯来书会合？ |
| 132 | David’s oath, Zion, offspring promise | How are dynasty, temple, and divine Zion choice reconnected? |
| 132 | 大卫誓言、锡安与后裔应许 | 王朝、圣殿和神选择锡安怎样被重新连在一起？ |
| 144:1–11 | Royal warfare/rescue | How is war language placed under divine rescue before the finale? |
| 144:1–11 | 王的争战与拯救 | 战争语言怎样在整本诗集终曲前被置于神的救援之下？ |

<a id="psalms-guide-2-four-layers-of-reading"></a>
### 2. Four Layers of Reading | 2. 四层阅读次序

1. **Original/historical:** ask how the poem served actual Israelite or Judaean royalty, worship, and crisis.
   **原初／历史层：** 先问诗怎样服务以色列或犹大的真实王室、敬拜与危机。
2. **Collection/canonical:** after kingdom and dynasty failed, ask how placement within five-book Psalms creates renewed tension and hope.
   **诗集／正典层：** 亡国和王朝中断以后，这首诗被放在五卷诗篇中怎样获得新的盼望张力。
3. **Second Temple reception:** ask how different Jewish groups recombine Davidic covenant, anointed figure, and final [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation).
   **第二圣殿接收层：** 不同犹太群体怎样把大卫之约、受膏者和末世拯救重新组合。
4. **New Testament/Christological:** ask how Jesus and New Testament authors proclaim fulfillment in his suffering, resurrection, exaltation, and return.
   **新约／基督论层：** 耶稣和新约作者怎样宣告这些盼望在耶稣受苦、复活、升高与再临中达到目标。

The four layers need not cancel each other. Christians can confess fulfillment in [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) while recognizing that fulfillment often gathers a whole royal and covenant pattern. The original hearers need not have known Nazareth, Pilate, or every first-century detail.<br>
这四层不是互相取消。基督徒可以认信诗篇在基督里成全，同时诚实承认“成全”常是整条王权与[圣约](../Glossary/README.md#covenant)模式的汇聚，不必假装原诗的第一批听众已经知道拿撒勒、彼拉多或主后一世纪的全部细节。

<a id="psalms-guide-3-4q174-an-actual-second-temple-combination"></a>
### 3. 4Q174: An Actual Second Temple Combination | 3. 4Q174：一份第二圣殿时期的实际组合实例

The IAA calls Cave 4’s 4Q174 **4Q Eschatological Commentary A**, formerly 4QFlorilegium. It combines 2 Samuel 7’s Davidic offspring promise, Psalms 1–2, and prophetic texts within an eschatological/Davidic framework. Rather than proving Psalm 2 originally meant only the end time, it physically attests Jewish interpreters before or near New Testament times combining covenant and royal psalm language.<br>
昆兰第 4 洞的 4Q174，现由以色列文物局题为 **4Q Eschatological Commentary A**，旧称 4QFlorilegium。残卷把撒母耳记下 7 章的大卫后裔应许、诗篇 1–2 和其他[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)经文放进末世／大卫式解释框架。它的重要性不在于“证明诗篇 2 原来只讲末世”，而在于留下物质见证：新约以前或同时代的犹太释经世界中，确实有人把大卫之约与诗篇的王权语言组合起来阅读。

It cannot establish:<br>
它不能证明：

- One messianic view shared by all Jews.
  所有犹太人都有同一种[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)观；
- Identity between Qumran interpretation and New Testament Christology.
  昆兰解释与新约基督论完全相同；
- Direct copying of 4Q174 by Hebrews.
  希伯来书直接抄用了 4Q174；
- The original date of Psalm 1, Psalm 2, or 2 Samuel 7 from this fragment alone.
  这份残片能够单独决定诗篇 1、2 或撒母耳记下 7 章的原始日期。

Related evidence anchor: **113**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**113**。

<a id="psalms-guide-four-checks-for-reading-new-testament-citations"></a>
### Four Checks for Reading New Testament Citations | 读新约引用时的四个检查

1. Is the author quoting, adapting, alluding, or using shared biblical language?
   新约是在**引用**、**改写**、**暗引**，还是共享相同圣经语言？
2. Is the wording nearer the Masoretic Hebrew tradition, Septuagint Greek, or a mixed/unknown early text?
   它依据的文字较接近希伯来马所拉传统、希腊《七十士译本》，还是混合／未知的早期文本形态？
3. Which pattern remains: kingship, righteous suffering, [exodus](../Glossary/README.md#exodus), sanctuary, creation, wisdom, or worship?
   它保留了原诗的哪一条结构：王权、受苦义人、[出埃及](../Glossary/README.md#exodus)、圣所、创造、智慧或敬拜？
4. How is that pattern concentrated in Jesus and extended to church and [new creation](../Glossary/README.md#new-creation)?
   它把这条结构怎样集中到耶稣，又怎样延伸到教会和[新创造](../Glossary/README.md#new-creation)？

诗篇在整本圣经中的独特作用，是把历史和教义变成神子民可以说出口的话。它既让读者回忆神做过什么，也训练读者在尚未看见结局时怎样祷告。
