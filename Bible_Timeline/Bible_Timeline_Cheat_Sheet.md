<a id="big-story"></a>

## Big Story | 大故事

- [Creation](../Glossary/README.md#creation): God made a good world.
  [创造](../Glossary/README.md#creation)：神创造了美好的世界。
- Fall: sin and death entered.
  堕落：罪和死亡进入历史。
- Covenant: God chose a people and made promises.
  立约：神拣选子民并赐下[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)。
- Kingdom: [Israel](../Glossary/README.md#israel) received land, kings, [temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple), and law.
  国度：[以色列](../Glossary/README.md#israel)得地、得王、得殿、得律法。
- [Exile](../Glossary/README.md#exile): judgment came because of covenant breaking.
  [被掳](../Glossary/README.md#exile)：背约带来审判。
- Promise: [prophets](../Glossary/README.md#prophet) announced return, [Messiah](../Glossary/README.md#messiah), Spirit, and new covenant.
  应许：[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)宣告归回、[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)、[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)和新约。
- Jesus: the promised Messiah fulfilled the story.
  耶稣：应许中的弥赛亚成全故事。
- [Church](../Glossary/README.md#church): the [gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel) spread to the nations.
  [教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)：[福音](../Glossary/README.md#gospel)向万国扩展。
- [New Creation](../Glossary/README.md#new-creation): God will fully restore all things.
  [新创造](../Glossary/README.md#new-creation)：神最终完全恢复万有。

<a id="fast-timeline"></a>

## Fast Timeline | 快速时间线

1. Genesis 1-11: creation, fall, flood, Babel
   创世记 1-11：创造、堕落、洪水、巴别
2. Genesis 12-50: Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph
   创世记 12-50：亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各、约瑟
3. [Exodus](../Glossary/README.md#exodus)-Deuteronomy: Moses, exodus, Sinai, wilderness
   出埃及记到申命记：摩西、[出埃及](../Glossary/README.md#exodus)、西奈、旷野
4. Joshua-Judges-Ruth: land, instability, need for a king
   约书亚记-士师记-路得记：得地、混乱、需要君王
5. 1-2 Samuel; 1 Kings 1-11: Saul, David, Solomon
   撒母耳记上下；列王纪上 1-11：扫罗、大卫、所罗门
6. 1 Kings 12-2 Kings 25: divided kingdom to exile
   列王纪上 12 到列王纪下 25：分裂王国直到被掳
7. Ezra-Nehemiah-Esther: return and rebuilding
   以斯拉记-尼希米记-以斯帖记：归回与重建
8. Intertestamental / Second Temple period: Hellenistic kingdoms, Maccabees, Hasmoneans, Rome, Herod
   两约之间／第二[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)时期：希腊化王国、马加比、哈斯摩尼、罗马与希律
9. Gospels: Jesus
   四福音：耶稣
10. Acts-Letters: church and mission
    [使徒](../Glossary/README.md#apostle)行传-书信：教会与宣教
11. Revelation: final judgment and new creation
    启示录：最终审判与新创造

详见[《两约之间至四福音：第二圣殿、希腊化与罗马世界》](./两约之间至四福音_第二圣殿与罗马背景.md)。

## Main Covenants | 主要圣约

- Adam / creation themes: life, rule, [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)
  亚当 / 创造主题：生命、治理、[祝福](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)
- Noah: preservation of the world
  挪亚：世界被保守
- Abraham: offspring, land, blessing to nations
  亚伯拉罕：后裔、土地、万国得福
- Moses: law and covenant people
  摩西：律法与立约子民
- David: eternal kingly line
  大卫：永远的君王血脉
- New Covenant: [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness), Spirit, new heart, final restoration
  新约：赦罪、圣灵、新心、最终恢复

## Where People Get Confused | 最常混乱的地方

- Kings and Chronicles overlap.
  列王纪和历代志有大量重叠。
- Prophets often belong inside Kings-era history.
  先知书常常要放回列王时代去理解。
- [Psalms](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) come from many periods.
  [诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)来自不同年代。
- The four Gospels overlap in time.
  四福音在时间上是重叠的。
- Jesus is the center, not just one section near the end.
  耶稣是整本圣经的中心，不只是快到结尾才出现的人物。

## One-Sentence Connection | 一句话连接

The Bible is one story of God creating, promising, rescuing, dwelling, judging, restoring, and finally bringing his people into full life through Jesus [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ).<br>
圣经是一个完整故事：神创造、应许、拯救、同住、审判、恢复，并最终借着耶稣[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)把祂的子民带入完全的生命。
