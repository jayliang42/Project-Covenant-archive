## How to Use the Crosswalk | 一、怎样使用

<a id="crosswalk-weeks-starting-with-the-week-s-reading"></a>
### Starting with the Week’s Reading | 从每周读经出发

Find the week in section two, then follow evidence numbers. Ask:<br>
先在第二部分找到本周，再按编号进入史料索引。编号锚点用于回答：

- Which external sources concern this week’s people, cities, dynasties, events?
  这周涉及的人物、城市、王朝或事件，有哪些外部材料？
- How far can each support a conclusion?
  材料能支持到哪一步？
- Which popular claims overreach?
  哪些流行说法超过了证据？

<a id="crosswalk-weeks-starting-with-a-source"></a>
### Starting with a Source | 从某项史料出发

Find the anchor in section three for suitable weeks/books. An anchor can serve several weeks without proving all their Scripture content.<br>
在第三部分找到锚点，可看到它最适合放进哪一周、对应哪些书卷。一个锚点可以服务多个周次，但这不表示它能证明相关经文的全部内容。

<a id="crosswalk-weeks-reading-boundaries"></a>
### Reading Boundaries | 三个边界

1. **No numbered anchor does not mean no background.** Theology, literature, everyday settings need different sources; never invent evidence to fill cells.
   **没有直接编号锚点，不等于没有历史背景。** 神学宣告、文学作品和日常处境需要不同类型的外部材料，不能为了填表制造伪证。
2. **One anchor can support different strength grades.** Site dating may be A, identification with a narrated event only C.
   **同一锚点可有不同证据等级。** 例如遗址年代为 A，认定它就是经文中的某一事件可能只有 C。
3. **Manuscripts attest transmission, not narrated events.** Apply this to P46, P72, P9, Sinaiticus, P115, Dead Sea Scrolls.
   **抄本证明文本流传，不直接证明卷内事件。** P46、P72、P9、西奈抄本、P115 和死海古卷必须按这一规则使用。
4. **Versions, lectionaries, commentaries, catalogues differ.** Version inclusion does not mean local liturgical selection; commentary does not mean a universal canon ruling.
   **译本、读经本、注释和明确书目回答不同问题。** 某译本有一卷，不等于地方礼仪实际选读；某注释家解释一卷，也不等于全教会已作统一正典判决。
5. **Strength and type are different axes.** A–D grades judgment; EV/BG/MS/RX/UT concerns event/person, context, copying, reception, or limits on testing. UT is a limit, not evidence.
   **强度与类型是两条轴。** A–D 表示判断有多强；EV／BG／MS／RX／UT 表示材料实际回答事件人物、制度环境、文本抄传、解释接收或不可由考古直接检验中的哪一类问题。UT 是边界，不是证据。

> Anchors 204–205 are cross-book CAT/named-catalogue RX: second/third-century naming and joining, not commentary on every book or event proof. <a id="coverage-weeks-11-and-15-17-sites-inscriptions-and-biblical-fragments"></a>
### Weeks 11 and 15–17: sites, inscriptions, and biblical fragments | 第 11、15–17 周怎样把遗址／铭文与经卷残片分层

1. Jericho, Ai, Hazor, and highland settlements address Late Bronze–Iron Age sites, destruction, and population formation. 4Q47/4Q48 address the copying of Joshua centuries later. These layers are not interchangeable.
   耶利哥、艾城、夏琐和山地聚落回答晚青铜—铁器时代的城址、毁坏与人口形成；4Q47／4Q48 回答数百年后《约书亚记》怎样抄传，两层不可互换。
2. A common reconstruction of 4Q47 places the law-reading material later found at Joshua 8:34–35 after the Jordan crossing and before the circumcision in Joshua 5. This observable sequence difference is not contemporary evidence for Joshua's altar-building event.
   4Q47 的常见拼接把后来位于书 8:34–35 的读律法材料放到过约旦之后、书 5 割礼之前；这是可观察的次序差异，不是约书亚筑坛事件的同期证明。
3. The joins in 4Q47, the altar location, and the earlier sequence remain disputed. A smoother or shorter text, or an earlier carrier, is insufficient to establish an author's autograph.
   4Q47 拼片、祭坛地点及“哪一种次序更早”均有争论；较顺、较短或较早载体都不是作者原稿的充分条件。
4. 4Q48 preserves very little from Joshua 2–4 and 17, broadly close to the Masoretic tradition. Broad similarity does not mean word-for-word identity throughout the book, or permit treating missing chapters as evidence.
   4Q48 只存书 2–4、17 的极少文字，大体接近马所拉传统；“大体相近”不等于整卷逐字相同，也不允许把未保存章节补成证据。
5. 5Q2 preserves Solomon's accession in 1 Kings 1; 4Q54 preserves 1 Kings 7–8; 6Q4 preserves portions of 1 Kings 3, 12, 22 and 2 Kings 5–10. Together they remain a small part of Kings.
   5Q2 保存王上 1 的所罗门继位，4Q54 保存王上 7–8，6Q4 保存王上 3、12、22 与王下 5–10；三卷合计仍只是《列王纪》的很小部分。
6. 4Q54 is broadly proto-Masoretic, yet at 1 Kings 8:16 it preserves a longer reading agreeing with 2 Chronicles 6:5b–6a and some Old Greek witnesses. This establishes ancient variation in Hebrew readings, not the overall priority of Chronicles.
   4Q54 整体接近原马所拉型，却在王上 8:16 保存与代下 6:5b–6a、部分古希腊本相合的长读；最强结论是古代希伯来文本已有不同读法，不是《历代志》整体更早的证明。
7. Earlier portions of 6Q4 are mostly close to the Masoretic text; parts of 2 Kings 7–8 have shorter readings or agreements with ancient versions. The papyrus is badly damaged and many fragments cannot be located; do not force a chapter assignment for every piece.
   6Q4 的前段多近马所拉本，王下 7–8 有部分较短或与古译本相合的读法；纸草破损严重，大量碎片无法定位，不能为每片强配章节。
8. The Mesha Stele, Kurkh Monolith, Black Obelisk, and Assyrian royal inscriptions can identify particular kings, wars, or tribute. The three Kings fragments address textual history. Event evidence and physical manuscript witnesses both matter, but neither replaces the other.
   米沙碑、库尔赫碑、黑色方尖碑和亚述王室铭文可确认某些王、战争或贡赋；三份《列王纪》残卷则处理文本史。事件外证与抄本实物都重要，但一方不能替代另一方。
9. Having Kings manuscripts does not establish Solomon's temple, Elisha's miracles, or Jehu's coup. Inscriptions naming people likewise do not establish that every sentence of Kings has remained unchanged.
   有《列王纪》抄本不证明所罗门圣殿、以利沙神迹或耶户政变；同样，有人物铭文也不证明《列王纪》的每一句文字从未变化。
10. For weeks 11 and 15–17, consistently ask: how far is this material from the story in time, and does it support an ancient event or the later text carrying that story?
    第 11、15–17 周应固定问两次：“这件材料与故事相隔多久？”“它证明的是古代事件，还是后来承载这个故事的文本？”

<a id="coverage-weeks-23-24-26-and-34-events-textual-forms-and-scientific-dating"></a>
### Weeks 23–24, 26, and 34: events, textual forms, and scientific dating | 第 23–24、26、34 周怎样区分事件史、文本形态与科学测年

1. The Babylonian Chronicle, Lachish letters, Jerusalem destruction layers, and exile administrative archives address sixth-century BCE war, political change, and displacement. 2Q13 and 4Q70–72b address Jeremiah's copying centuries later. The layers are not interchangeable.
   巴比伦编年史、拉吉书信、耶路撒冷火毁层和被掳行政档案处理主前六世纪的战争、政权更替与流亡处境；2Q13、4Q70–72b 处理数百年后《耶利米书》怎样抄传，两层不可互换。
2. Some shorter readings in Jeremiah 9–10 in 4Q71 and Jeremiah 43 in 4Q72a resemble the Old Greek's underlying text; 4Q70, 4Q72, 4Q72b, and 2Q13 often resemble the later, longer Masoretic form. The strongest conclusion is the ancient existence of differing Hebrew textual forms.
   4Q71 的耶 9–10 与 4Q72a 的耶 43 有若干短读接近古希腊底本；4Q70、4Q72、4Q72b 和 2Q13 则多处接近后来较长的马所拉型正文。最强结论是古代已有不完全相同的希伯来文本形态。
3. Two locally shorter witnesses cannot establish every omission or the ordering of the nations oracles throughout the Old Greek book. A shorter form may approach an earlier stage, but is not automatically the author's autograph.
   两份局部短本不能独自证明古希腊本全卷每一处省略及列国神谕次序；“较短”可能接近较早阶段，却不是“作者原稿”的自动同义词。
4. Sheepskin in 4Q71/4Q72a and cattle-skin DNA in 4Q72b distinguish these groups from mistaken pieces of one skin. Animal species cannot reveal words or by itself identify the copying city.
   4Q71／4Q72a 的羊皮与 4Q72b 的牛皮 DNA 说明三组材料不是同一张皮被误拆；动物种属不能读出经文，也不能仅凭牛皮确定抄写城市。
5. The burning and rewriting in Jeremiah 36 provide internal literary clues. Six later fragments establish real textual diversity, but are not Baruch's rewritten original. Interpreting the new covenant in Jeremiah 31 still requires the whole Bible; skin experiments cannot decide its fulfillment.
   耶 36 的烧卷与重写给书内文学线索；六份后期残卷证明文本多样性真实存在，却不是巴录重写时留下的原件。耶 31 的新约实现仍须从整本圣经解释，不能交给羊皮实验裁决。
6. 1Q71–72, 4Q112–116, and 6Q7 are eight Daniel manuscripts with identifiable surviving text from chapters 1–11. The quotation of 12:10 in 4Q174 belongs to a separate interpretive work and should not be counted as a ninth manuscript of Daniel.
   1Q71–72、4Q112–116、6Q7 是八份《但以理书》抄本；现存可辨文字覆盖 1–11 章。4Q174 引 12:10 是另一个解释作品中的经文引用，不应计成第九份本卷抄本。
7. 1Q71 preserves the Hebrew–Aramaic seam at Daniel 2:4; 4Q112/113 preserve the Aramaic–Hebrew seam at 7:28–8:1. The bilingual structure therefore appears in early manuscripts, without establishing that one person wrote the whole book on one day.
   1Q71 保存但 2:4 的希伯来文—亚兰文接缝，4Q112／113 保存 7:28–8:1 的亚兰文—希伯来文接缝；这表明双语结构很早已在抄本中存在，不证明整卷由一人在同一天写成。
8. The 2025 calibrated 2σ interval for 4Q114's skin is 230–160 BCE. This probability range is not a uniform ruler: 195 BCE cannot be treated as the actual copying year, and the later end overlaps the Antiochene crisis.
   2025 年 4Q114 羊皮的 2σ 校准范围为主前 230–160 年；概率范围不是均匀直尺，不能取主前 195 年作真实抄写年，晚端也与安条克危机重合。
9. Radiocarbon dates the skin carrying the words, not directly the conception of a work, delivery of a vision, or author's lifetime. A carrier may postdate a work or belong to a tradition still forming or just completed.
   碳十四测的是承载文字的羊皮，不直接测作品构思、异象发出或作者生活；载体可能晚于作品，也可能属于仍在形成或刚完成的文本传统。
10. For weeks 26 and 34, retain three questions: when is the internal story set, when was the surviving copy produced, and how do scholars reconstruct composition from language, historical correspondences, and textual development? These are related, but no single object automatically supplies one date for all three.
    第 26、34 周应同时保留三问：“卷内故事设在哪个时代？”“现存副本何时制作？”“现代学者怎样由语言、历史对应和文本发展重建成书？”三问相关，却没有一件实物能自动把答案合成一个日期。

<a id="coverage-weeks-36-38-josephus-the-gospels-and-machaerus"></a>
### Weeks 36–38: Josephus, the Gospels, and Machaerus | 第 36–38 周怎样比较约瑟夫斯、福音书与马凯鲁斯遗址

1. Josephus' Antiquities 18.116–119 was written around 93/94 CE. It is a first-century Jewish historian's work, not an official contemporary record from John the Baptist's ministry.
   约瑟夫斯《犹太古史》18.116–119 约在主后 93／94 年写成；它是第一世纪犹太史家作品，不是施洗约翰活动时的同期官府记录。
2. The passage calls John the Baptist and a good man, describing righteousness, piety, water baptism, influence over crowds, Antipas' fear of unrest, imprisonment at Machaerus, and execution. It does not mention Jesus.
   本段把约翰称为“施洗者”和“好人”，记他的义、敬虔、水洗、群众影响、安提帕的政治恐惧、马凯鲁斯囚禁与处死；它完全不提耶稣。
3. Matthew 3, Mark 1, Luke 3, and John 1 connect John to preparing the Lord's way and Jesus' baptism. Josephus cannot independently support the opened heavens, Spirit, heavenly voice, messianic identity, or prophetic fulfillment.
   太 3／可 1／路 3／约 1 把约翰接到预备主路和耶稣受洗；约瑟夫斯不能外证天开、圣灵、天声、弥赛亚身份或先知应验。
4. Matthew 14 and Mark 6 place the fatal conflict after John's criticism of Antipas' marriage to Herodias and include a birthday feast, Herodias' daughter, an oath, and beheading. Josephus emphasizes possible unrest among the crowds and says only that John was executed.
   太 14／可 6 把死亡冲突放在约翰谴责安提帕与希罗底婚姻之后，并有生日宴、希罗底之女、誓言和斩首；约瑟夫斯则强调群众可能引发变乱，只说处死。
5. Moral criticism and fear of unrest could both affect Antipas, but possible reconciliation does not establish a common source or double attestation for every detail.
   道德批评和政治恐惧可以同时作用于安提帕，却不能因“可协调”就假定两套记载使用同一份资料或每一细节均获双重证明。
6. The name Salome does not occur in this passage of Matthew 14 or Mark 6; it comes from Josephus' Herodian genealogy elsewhere. Identify that source when adding the name rather than presenting it as the Gospels' wording.
   “撒罗米”不见于太 14／可 6 本段；这个名字来自约瑟夫斯另处的希律家谱。把它补入福音叙事时，应说明来源而非放进引号冒充经文用名。
7. Most researchers accept Josephus' John passage as authentic, citing all surviving manuscripts, wording, context, clear differences from the Gospels, and Origen's early quotation. Minority interpolation arguments still leave its formation history at grade C.
   多数研究者接受约瑟夫斯约翰段落为真文，理由包括全部现存抄本、用语、上下文、与福音书的明显差异及俄利根早期引用；少数插补论仍使段落形成史维持 C 级。
8. Machaerus excavation establishes Hasmonean foundations, Herod the Great's palace-fortress, halls, courtyards, baths, water storage, gates, and Roman military layers: grade-A site facts.
   马凯鲁斯发掘确认哈斯摩尼基础、大希律宫堡、厅室、庭院、浴池、蓄水、城门和罗马军事层；这些是 A 级遗址事实。
9. Josephus names Machaerus and archaeology establishes a palace-fortress of compatible date; together they support the imprisonment location at B+. The site itself has no John's name, identified cell, execution instrument, remains, or execution inscription.
   约瑟夫斯点名马凯鲁斯，考古确认年代相合的宫堡，二者共同使囚禁地点达到 B+；遗址本身却没有约翰姓名、牢房、刑具、遗骸或行刑铭文。
10. Identifying the semicircular niche as Antipas' throne and the neighboring courtyard as the banquet site or Salome's dance floor is the excavator's interpretation. Archaeologists have questioned dimensions and function; do not call it discovery of the exact execution scene.
    半圆壁龛为安提帕王座、相邻庭院为宴会／“撒罗米舞池”的说法属于发掘者解释，已有考古学者质疑尺寸和功能；不可写成已发现精确处决现场。

<a id="coverage-weeks-12-14-iron-age-settings-judges-samuel-transmission-and-claims-about-originals"></a>
### Weeks 12–14: Iron Age settings, Judges/Samuel transmission, and claims about originals | 第 12–14 周怎样区分铁器时代背景、《士师记》《撒母耳记》文本史与“原版”宣称

1. Hazor, highland settlements, Philistine material culture, and Qeiyafa address Late Bronze–Iron Age cities, populations, and political transitions. They are not manuscripts of Judges or Samuel.
   夏琐、山地聚落、非利士物质文化和凯雅法处理晚青铜—铁器时代的城市、人口与政治转型；它们不是《士师记》或《撒母耳记》的抄本。
2. 1Q6 and 4Q49/4Q50 are three Hebrew Judges fragments from around the first century BCE. Centuries later than the internal setting, they address transmission rather than directly establishing Gideon, Abimelech, or the Benjaminite war.
   1Q6、4Q49／4Q50 则是三份主前一世纪前后的《士师记》希伯来残卷；它们晚于卷内时代数百年，回答文本怎样流传，不直接证明基甸、亚比米勒或便雅悯战争。
3. 4Q49 moves from Judges 6:6 directly to 6:11, without the unnamed prophet's rebuke in Masoretic 6:7–10. This is an observable shorter reading, not a modern scholar's invented deletion.
   4Q49 从士 6:6 直接接到 6:11，没有马所拉文本 6:7–10 的无名先知责备；这是可观察的短读，不是由现代学者凭空删节。
4. The shorter reading may preserve an earlier literary form, an accidental omission, or intentional abbreviation. Shorter is insufficient to establish earlier, and four verses cannot reconstruct an entire short edition.
   短读可能保存较早文学形态，也可能来自意外遗漏或有意缩略；“较短”不是“较原始”的充分条件，更不能由四节扩展成一套已恢复的整卷短版。
5. 4Q50 is broadly close to the later Masoretic tradition, but line length, word forms, and local long/short readings in Judges 21 remain discussed. Broad agreement is not word-for-word absence of variants.
   4Q50 整体接近后来马所拉传统，但士 21 的行长、词形和局部长短读法仍有讨论；“总体相近”与“逐字无异文”不能互换。
6. 1Q7 and 4Q51/4Q52/4Q53 are four Hebrew Samuel manuscripts spanning roughly the third–first centuries BCE. Even the earliest carrier is not a royal archive from Samuel's, Saul's, or David's era.
   1Q7、4Q51／4Q52／4Q53 是四份《撒母耳记》希伯来抄本，约跨主前三至一世纪；最早的载体仍不是撒母耳、扫罗或大卫时代的王室档案。
7. 4Q51 ranges from 1 Samuel 1 to 2 Samuel 24, yet the official edition estimates only about 15% survives. Coverage across the book describes the distribution of passages, not a complete scroll.
   4Q51 覆盖撒上 1 至撒下 24，正式版本却估计只保存约 15%；“横跨全书”只说现存章节分布，不等于“完整经卷”。
8. At 1 Samuel 10:27–11:1, 4Q51 preserves a longer account of Nahash oppressing Gadites and Reubenites, similar to Josephus. It establishes the existence of an ancient longer Hebrew reading and helps explain the demand to gouge out right eyes.
   4Q51 在撒上 10:27–11:1 保存拿辖压迫迦得、流便人的较长段落，与约瑟夫斯叙述近似；最强结论是古代较长希伯来读本确实存在，并使剜右眼要求的背景更易理解。
9. The Nahash passage may be earlier text lost through a scribal skip in the Masoretic line or an early editorial expansion. Similar material in Josephus does not automatically become independent Iron Age event evidence.
   拿辖段既可能是马所拉传本因跳行失落的较早正文，也可能是早期编辑扩充；约瑟夫斯的近似材料不自动成为独立铁器时代事件外证。
10. Weeks 12–14 therefore retain three layers: Iron Age social/political setting, Second Temple copying, and modern judgments on long/short texts and book formation. None can be substituted for archaeological proof of the judges', Saul's, or David's events.
    第 12–14 周因此同时保留三层：铁器时代的社会／政治背景，第二圣殿时期的经卷抄传，现代对长短文本及书卷形成的判断；任何一层都不能偷换成士师、扫罗或大卫事件已经被考古证明。

<a id="coverage-weeks-18-23-and-42-northern-settings-joel-transmission-and-pentecost"></a>
### Weeks 18, 23, and 42: northern settings, Joel transmission, and Pentecost | 第 18、23、42 周怎样把北国背景、《约珥书》抄传与五旬节分层

1. The Samaria ostraca directly establish standardized administrative writing, local networks, and high-quality wine/oil circulation in the northern kingdom around Joash/Jeroboam II. They do not record the bribery, sale of the poor, or false measures condemned by Amos.
   撒马利亚陶片直接证明约阿施／耶罗波安二世时代北国有标准化行政文字、地方网络和优质酒油流动；它没有记录阿摩司所控诉的收贿、卖穷人或缺斤少两。
2. Kuntillet Ajrud 3.1 directly preserves YHWH of Samaria and `ʾšrth`. The latter's identity and relationship to jar images remain disputed; do not abbreviate this as discovery of portraits of YHWH and his wife.
   昆提拉·阿杰鲁德 3.1 直接保存“撒马利亚的耶和华”和 `ʾšrth`；后一个词的身份及其与罐上图像的关系仍有争论，不能简写成“考古发现耶和华和妻子的画像”。
3. These two Iron Age sources illuminate socioeconomic life and religious practice respectively. Nearness in date does not automatically make them records of Amos', Hosea's, or Jonah's activities.
   两项铁器时代材料分别照明社会经济与宗教实践；它们不因年代接近，就自动变成阿摩司、何西阿或约拿本人活动的记录。
4. 4Q78 and 4Q82 are distinct Hebrew Twelve scrolls, both preserving portions of Joel and other minor prophets. They establish late Second Temple copying of a collection, not that four prophets lived when these copies were made.
   4Q78、4Q82 是不同的希伯来文《十二先知》卷，都保存《约珥书》及其他小先知残段；它们证明第二圣殿后期的合集抄传，不证明四位先知生活在抄本时代。
5. 4Q78/4Q82 do not preserve Joel 2:28–32, so do not claim a Qumran scroll contains the complete Pentecost quotation. Roman-period Mur88 actually preserves that passage.
   4Q78／4Q82 没有保存约珥 2:28–32，不能声称“昆兰卷已有完整五旬节引文”；实际保存该段的是罗马时期 Mur88。
6. Mur88 supplies a physical Hebrew witness to Joel's promise of the Spirit. Its broad Roman-period date and lack of use history do not identify it as Peter's scroll or Luke's direct exemplar.
   Mur88 证明约珥圣灵应许有希伯来文实物见证；“罗马时期”的宽定年和缺少使用史，不能把它认作彼得手中的卷轴或路加的直接底本。
7. Acts 2 primarily quotes Joel through the Greek tradition and interprets the event with wording such as the last days. The prophetic text, Greek translation, New Testament rereading, and theological judgment about Pentecost are four different questions.
   徒 2 主要通过希腊文传统引用约珥，并以“末后的日子”等措辞解释事件；原先知文本、希腊译本、新约重读和五旬节神学判断是四层问题。
8. Joel's manuscripts can be dated; proposals for the prophet and book formation still range from early dates through the exile to the post-return era. Placing Joel in week 23 and last in this route's Old Testament books is thematic sequencing, not a dating conclusion.
   《约珥书》的抄本可以定年，先知活动与全书形成仍有早期、被掳前后和归回后方案；本课程把约珥放在第 23 周及 52 卷旧约末位，都是主题接力，不冒充定年结论。

<a id="coverage-weeks-30-33-and-43-strengthening-thin-coverage-without-manufacturing-archaeological-proof"></a>
### Weeks 30, 33, and 43: strengthening thin coverage without manufacturing archaeological proof | 第 30、33、43 周怎样补强低密度书卷而不制造“考古证明”

1. Week 30's existing Yehud administrative anchor only establishes a Persian provincial setting. 4Q76 adds copying of Malachi's latter half in the second century BCE; together these do not prove priestly apathy archaeologically.
   第 30 周原有 Yehud 行政锚点只说明波斯行省背景；4Q76 新增的是主前二世纪《玛拉基书》后半抄传，两者不应合并成“考古证明祭司冷淡”。
2. 4Q76 preserves the messenger, remembering Moses, and Elijah passages, letting weeks 34–35 trace the prehistory of Gospel quotations. It is not an identifiable exemplar for Mark or Luke and does not decide John the Baptist's identity.
   4Q76 保存“我的使者”“记念摩西”和以利亚段落，使第 34–35 周能追踪四福音引用前史；它不是马可或路加的可识别底本，也不裁决施洗约翰身份。
3. Whether the material catalogued as 4Q78 fragment 35 belongs to that scroll, and so makes 4Q78 a Malachi witness, is disputed. This table does not increase its count on that attribution; anchor 158 centers on the clearly identified 4Q76.
   被列作 4Q78 第 35 号残片的材料是否真属该卷、能否据此把 4Q78 计作《玛拉基书》见证，存在研究争议；本表不以这项归属增加计数，锚点 158 只以内容明确的 4Q76 为核心。
4. Week 33's existing Instruction of Amenemope–Proverbs 22–24 connection is relatively concentrated. Ahiqar adds a Persian-period Aramaic court narrative, instructions, and shared rhetorical world, with a different relation and evidential strength.
   第 33 周原有《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》与箴 22–24 的关系较集中；《阿希卡尔》新增的是波斯时期亚兰语宫廷故事、训诲和共同修辞世界，证据强度与关系类型不同。
5. Finding Ahiqar at Elephantine does not establish Jewish authorship or synagogue use. Common motifs do not automatically establish direct copying into Proverbs.
   《阿希卡尔》出土于象岛不等于犹太作者或会堂教材；共同母题也不能自动变成《箴言》直接抄写的箭头。
6. Week 43's existing Josephus material addresses the death of James in Jerusalem. P20/P23 address third-century copying of James. Personal history and textual history cannot replace each other.
   第 43 周原有约瑟夫斯材料处理耶路撒冷雅各之死；P20／P23 处理三世纪《雅各书》抄传，人物史与文本史不能互相替代。
7. P20/P23 are fragments that establish circulation of different passages, not a recovered whole book, complete Catholic Epistles collection, or universal acceptance.
   P20／P23 都是残片，只能确认不同段落已经流传；不能据两片恢复全卷、完整普通书信合集或普世接纳。
8. James and 2 Peter previously appeared to have single anchors in the 52-book table partly because navigation rows were too narrow. P74, versions, lectionaries, commentaries, and explicit lists already supplied reception evidence.
   《雅各书》和《彼得后书》此前在 52 卷表看似“单锚点”，部分是导航行过窄造成的统计假象：P74、版本、读经本、注释和明确书目早已提供接收史材料。
9. This batch therefore adds one early physical anchor for James and reconnects existing reception anchors. It does not invent another number for 2 Peter, instead reconnecting P72, Sinaiticus, P74, Syriac versions/liturgy/commentary, and lists.
   因此本批为《雅各书》增一项早期实物锚点，同时把既有接收锚点回挂；《彼得后书》不重复造新编号，而回挂 P72、西奈、P74、叙利亚版本／礼仪／注释与书目。
10. Increased evidence density only means fuller layering, not simultaneous increases in certainty about authorship, composition date, events, or theology.
    “旁证密度增加”只表示证据层次更完整，不表示作者、成书日期、事件或神学判断的确定度同步上升。

<a id="coverage-weeks-12-19-23-and-27-story-settings-copying-and-later-interpretation"></a>
### Weeks 12, 19, 23, and 27: story settings, copying, and later interpretation | 第 12、19、23、27 周怎样区分故事背景、文本抄传与后期释经

1. Week 12's highland settlements, Hazor, and Philistine material illuminate the judges' setting. 2Q16/2Q17 and 4Q104/4Q105 add multiple late Second Temple Ruth manuscripts; they cannot serve retrospectively as personal archives from the judges' period.
   第 12 周原有山地聚落、夏琐与非利士材料只照明士师时代环境；2Q16／2Q17、4Q104／4Q105 新增的是第二圣殿后期《路得记》多份抄本，不能反向充当士师时代人物档案。
2. The four Ruth fragments cover the opening, gleaning, threshing floor, and land redemption, but not the whole book. Multiple copies strengthen textual history without deciding authorship, initial composition date, David's genealogy as history, or Weeks-festival use.
   四份《路得记》残卷覆盖开场、拾穗、禾场和赎地，却仍非全卷；多份副本只加强文本史，不能决定作者、初写年代、大卫家谱史实或七七节礼仪。
3. Week 19's Assyrian inscriptions establish pressures in Ahaz's era; 4Q81 preserves Micah 5's ruler-from-Bethlehem passage. Prophetic speech amid a crisis and copying in the first century BCE are distinct dating questions.
   第 19 周的亚述铭文固定亚哈斯时代政治压力，4Q81 则保存弥 5 的伯利恒治理者段落；“预言在危机中发声”与“经文在主前一世纪被抄写”是两个年代问题。
4. 4Q81 establishes that the passage predates Christianity, not that Qumran readers already interpreted it as Matthew 2 does. It also rules out the claim that Christians invented the Bethlehem wording for the occasion.
   4Q81 证明经文先于基督教，不能证明昆兰读者已作太 2 的解释；反过来，它也排除“伯利恒经句由基督徒临时创造”的说法。
5. Week 23's Babylonian material supplies Habakkuk's problem setting; 1QpHab is a Jewish community's actualizing commentary on Habakkuk 1–2 centuries later. Setting, text, and interpretation must remain separate.
   第 23 周的巴比伦材料说明哈巴谷的问题背景，1QpHab 则是数百年后犹太群体对哈 1–2 的现实化注释；历史背景、文本和解释三层不可合并。
6. 1QpHab applies Habakkuk 2:4 to law-observers and the Teacher of Righteousness. Galatians 3, Romans 1, and Hebrews 10 use the same verse for Gentile justification, gospel righteousness, and endurance in suffering respectively. Shared wording is not identical theology or direct dependence.
   1QpHab 把哈 2:4 应用于遵行律法者及公义教师；加 3、罗 1、来 10 使用同一经节，却分别服务于外邦人称义、福音之义和受苦忍耐。共同经句不等于相同神学或直接依赖。
7. The Teacher of Righteousness, Wicked Priest, and Kittim in 1QpHab remain disputed identifications. One identification scheme cannot precisely date the whole commentary.
   1QpHab 中公义教师、邪恶祭司、“基提人”的具体身份仍有争论；不能以某一人物对号方案给整卷注释精确定年。
8. Week 27's Persian administrative material supplies Haggai/Zechariah's setting; 4Q80 supplies later copying of Haggai 2 and Zechariah 1–6, 8, 12. The manuscript neither validates visions nor settles formation of Zechariah's parts.
   第 27 周的波斯行政材料给哈该／撒迦利亚时代背景，4Q80 给该 2 与亚 1–6、8、12 的后期抄传；抄本不验证异象发生，也不解决撒迦利亚前后部分形成史。
9. Zechariah 12:10 in 4Q80 has gaps and restorations, while Hebrew syntax itself contains pronominal tension. Comparison with John 19 and Revelation 1 must distinguish original context, textual traditions, and New Testament rereading.
   4Q80 的撒 12:10 有缺损与复原，希伯来句法本身又有代词张力；与约 19、启 1 比较时，必须把原先语境、不同文本传统和新约重读分别陈述。
10. These additions give four thinly covered core books direct textual/interpretive material. A physical object still does not equal archaeological proof of people, events, fulfillment, or theology.
    本批让四卷低密度主读书卷获得直接文本／释经材料，但仍坚持“有实物”不等于人物、事件、应验或神学已经被考古证明。

<a id="coverage-weeks-28-29-names-imperial-settings-events-and-textual-history"></a>
### Weeks 28–29: names, imperial settings, events, and textual history | 第 28–29 周怎样区分姓名外证、帝国背景、事件与文本史

1. TAD A.4.7/4.8 are copies/drafts of a 407 BCE petition to rebuild Elephantine's YHW temple; A.4.9 is a related memorandum. The three documents are not one decree personally signed by a Persian king.
   TAD A.4.7／4.8 是主前 407 年象岛 YHW 圣殿重建请愿的抄件／草稿，A.4.9 是相关备忘录；三件文书不是一份波斯王亲署诏令。
2. The petition names two sons of Sanballat, governor of Samaria, while contacting Judah's governor and Jerusalem's high priest. This provides a strong external comparison for Nehemiah's names, titles, and interprovincial relationships.
   请愿直接点名“撒玛利亚省长参巴拉”的两个儿子，并同时联系犹大省长和耶路撒冷大祭司；这使《尼希米记》的姓名、职衔与省际政治获得很强外部参照。
3. The documents are decades later than Nehemiah's usual activity dates and mention neither Nehemiah, the wall, false prophets, Tobiah, nor Geshem. Identifying the same Sanballat/family requires argument; a shared name does not establish it automatically.
   文书比尼希米通常活动年代晚数十年，没有尼希米、城墙、假先知、多比雅或基善；同一参巴拉／同一家族是需论证的身份方案，不是由同名自动得出的事实。
4. Elephantine's YHW temple and sacrificial requests directly illuminate one diaspora community's practice, not uniform rules for Jerusalem, Samaria, or all Persian-period Jews.
   象岛 YHW 圣殿及其祭祀请求直接照明一个侨民群体的实践；它不能代表耶路撒冷、撒玛利亚或全部波斯犹太人的统一规范。
5. The Persepolis Fortification archive records supplies, rations, travel authorization, and multilingual administration under Darius; the Treasury archive extends into Xerxes' reign. They establish a real institutional setting comparable to Esther's imperial bureaucracy.
   波斯堡城防档案记录大流士时期的物资、口粮、旅行授权和多语言行政，国库档案跨到薛西斯时期；两者说明《以斯帖记》的帝国官僚世界具有真实制度背景。
6. `Marduka/Marduku` occurs on several tablets, establishing the name form's existence. Several namesakes may be involved, and Susa, Jewish identity, and the book's relationship chain are missing; do not claim the tablets discovered Mordecai.
   `Marduka／Marduku` 姓名出现在多件泥版中，证明该姓名形式存在；文本可能涉及多位同名者，缺少书珊、犹太身份和书中关系链，所以不能写成“泥版发现末底改”。
7. The Fortification archive is mainly earlier than Xerxes and concentrated around Persepolis. Local institutions can be compared, but cannot be extended without argument to identical practice in Susa or every one of 127 provinces.
   城防档案主要早于薛西斯，又集中在波斯堡地区；地方制度可以比较，不能未经论证就外推为书珊或 127 省每一地完全相同。
8. P.Oxy. 4443 is a late-first/early-second-century Greek Esther roll; P967 places Esther in a multi-book codex around 200 CE. They address textual transmission, not whether Persian events occurred.
   P.Oxy. 4443 是晚一／早二世纪希腊文《以斯帖记》卷轴，P967 约主后 200 年把本卷置于多卷册本；它们回答的是文本怎样流传，不是波斯事件是否发生。
9. P.Oxy. 4443 preserves the latter part of Greek addition E, establishing early circulation of expanded text. This neither establishes a Hebrew original for the addition nor identifies the copyist as Jewish merely from roll format.
   P.Oxy. 4443 保存希腊增补 E 后段，证明扩展文本很早流传；这不等于增补原用希伯来文，也不能单凭卷轴形式确认抄写者为犹太人。
10. Week 28 must retain four layers: Susa's location, Persian bureaucracy, questions about the book's people/events, and varied Hebrew/Greek textual forms. None substitutes for proof of another.
    第 28 周最终必须同时保留四层：书珊地点、波斯官僚背景、书中人物／事件史问题、希伯来／希腊多种文本形态；其中任何一层都不能偷换成另一层的证明。

<a id="coverage-weeks-21-25-27-and-29-chronicles-ezra-resident-diaspora-and-events"></a>
### Weeks 21, 25, 27, and 29: Chronicles, Ezra, resident diaspora, and events | 第 21、25、27、29 周怎样连接《历代志》《以斯拉记》、留居侨民与事件史

1. Column two of 4Q118 closely agrees with 2 Chronicles 28:27–29:3. It establishes a late Second Temple Hebrew transmission/rewriting witness to the Ahaz–Hezekiah narrative.
   4Q118 第二栏保存与代下 28:27–29:3 高度相合的文字，最强结论是第二圣殿晚期已有这段亚哈斯—希西家叙事的希伯来传抄／改写见证。
2. Column one of 4Q118 cannot securely be matched to Masoretic or Greek Chronicles and may parallel 4Q381 31. The catalogue label 4Q Chronicles does not settle whether the object is an ordinary copy, rewriting, or parallel work.
   4Q118 第一栏不能稳妥对上马所拉或希腊《历代志》，并可能与 4Q381 31 平行；目录名“4Q Chronicles”不能取消整件属于普通抄本、改写或平行作品的争议。
3. Week 21 should therefore distinguish event evidence from Assyrian inscriptions/Lachish archaeology from 4Q118's textual evidence: the former illuminates the 701 BCE crisis, while the latter is centuries later and preserves only portions of 2 Chronicles 28–29.
   因此第 21 周应把亚述铭文／拉吉考古的事件层与 4Q118 的文本层分开：前者照明主前 701 年危机，后者晚数百年且只保存代下 28–29。
4. 4Q117 preserves Hebrew from Ezra 4 and Aramaic from Ezra 4–6 within one proposed scroll, but not the switch at 4:7–8 itself. Say that passages in both languages survive, not that a complete language-switch page survives.
   4Q117 在同一候选卷中保存拉 4 的希伯来文与拉 4–6 的亚兰文，但没有直接留下 4:7–8 的转换点；应说“两种语言的段落均有残存”，不夸成语言切换整页完整保存。
5. Proposals have revised 4Q117's paleographic dating from late Hasmonean/transitional to early Herodian. Around 50 BCE is an estimate, not a copyist's dated signature.
   4Q117 的字形断代已有从晚哈斯摩尼／过渡期改列早期希律时期的建议；约主前 50 年前后是估计，不是抄写员署年。
6. The fragments establish circulation of the temple-building conflict, officials' letters, and archive-search narrative, not discovery of Cyrus' or Darius' original decrees in Persian archives. A manuscript's story content and a document's external authenticity are separate questions.
   残卷证明建殿冲突、官员来函与查档叙事已经流传，不等于在波斯档案中找到古列或大流士诏令原件；抄本的“故事内容”与公文的“域外真实性”是两个问题。
7. 4Q117 lacks Ezra 7–10 and Nehemiah, so cannot decide Ezra's absolute date, mixed-marriage measures, an Ezra–Nehemiah collection, or authorship.
   4Q117 没有拉 7–10 或《尼希米记》文字，不能用来决定以斯拉绝对年代、混合婚姻措施、以斯拉—尼希米合卷状态或作者身份。
8. The Murashu archive, roughly 454–404 BCE, directly shows Judean farmers and a few lower-level officials remaining in Persian-period Babylonia. It places the coming up from Babylon in Ezra 7–8 within a diaspora world where many stayed.
   穆拉舒档案约主前 454–404 年，直接显示波斯时期巴比伦仍有犹大农民和少数基层官员；它让拉 7–8 的“从巴比伦上来”被放回一个许多人仍留下的侨居世界。
9. Identification of 61 Judeans in 64 documents uses names, relatives, and networks, not a self-reported census. The archive records economic activity from a business family's perspective and cannot represent women, the poor, religious practice, or every return decision.
   “61 位犹大人／64 件文书”依姓名、亲属和网络识别，不是自报人口普查；档案又从经营家族角度记录经济活动，不能代表妇女、贫民、宗教实践或所有归回选择。
10. The three sources remain distinct: 4Q118 addresses Chronicles' transmission/rewriting, 4Q117 Ezra's bilingual transmission, and Murashu those still living in Babylonia during the return period. None directly establishes every person, decree, reform, or theological interpretation in these books.
    三项材料最终形成清楚分层：4Q118 回答《历代志》怎样流传／改写，4Q117 回答《以斯拉记》怎样以双语文本流传，穆拉舒回答归回年代谁仍留在巴比伦；没有一项直接证明书中全部人物、诏令、改革或神学解释。

<a id="coverage-weeks-24-25-destruction-exile-diaspora-society-and-book-transmission"></a>
### Weeks 24–25: destruction/exile, diaspora society, and book transmission | 第 24–25 周怎样区分毁城／被掳事件、侨民社会与本卷文本史

1. The Babylonian Chronicle and Jerusalem's burn layers address the 597 BCE capture and 587/586 BCE catastrophe, not authorial signatures for Lamentations or Ezekiel.
   巴比伦编年史和耶路撒冷火毁层处理主前 597 年陷落与 587／586 年城市灾变；它们不是《哀歌》或《以西结书》的作者题署。
2. 3Q3, 4Q111, 5Q6, and 5Q7 are four Hebrew Lamentations fragments collectively touching all five poems. Text from all five chapters does not mean any scroll is complete or that eyewitnesses wrote all five poems during the destruction.
   3Q3、4Q111、5Q6、5Q7 是四份《哀歌》希伯来残卷，合计接触五首诗；“五章都有文字”不等于任一卷完整，更不等于五首诗均由毁城目击者当场写成。
3. In Lamentations 1, 4Q111 places the pe stanza before ayin, unlike the Masoretic chapter. Real variants require textual criticism; they neither invalidate the whole book nor permit claims of word-for-word invariance.
   4Q111 第一章把 pe 诗节置于 ayin 诗节之前，与马所拉本第一章次序不同；真实异文要求文本批判，不证明全书失真，也不允许宣称逐字从无变化。
4. The four fragments lack authorial signatures. Traditional attribution to Jeremiah, formation, compilation, and later liturgical use each require separate arguments.
   四份残卷没有作者题署；传统耶利米归属、作品形成、编集和后来礼仪使用都须另行论证。
5. Jehoiachin's ration tablets, Al-Yahudu, and Murashu illuminate royal provision, early exiles' society, and Persian-period diaspora continuity respectively. They cannot test Ezekiel's visions.
   约雅斤配给表、亚胡杜与穆拉舒档案分别说明王室供给、早期被掳者社会和波斯时期侨民延续；它们不能检验以西结的异象。
6. 4Q73–75 and 11Q4 are four representative Hebrew Ezekiel fragments preserving parts of judgment visions, symbolic acts, departing glory, and new-temple measurements. They are direct textual history, not four new physical proofs of visions.
   4Q73–75、11Q4 是四份代表性希伯来文《以西结书》残卷，保存审判异象、象征行动、荣耀离殿和新殿量度的部分文字；这是本卷直接文本史，不是新添四件异象物证。
7. The four scrolls are generally close to the later Masoretic tradition, with some short readings and minor variants. 4Q75 preserves only Ezekiel 24:2–3 and especially cannot establish stability of the whole book by itself.
   四卷总体接近后来马所拉传统，却有少数短读和细部异文；4Q75 仅存结 24:2–3，尤其不能独自承担整卷稳定性论证。
8. The shorter Greek text and differing order of P967 keep the relationship between long and short forms open. Not all differences are copying mistakes, and shorter alone does not establish earlier.
   希腊 P967 的较短文本与不同次序使长短文本关系保持开放；不能把所有差异都归为抄错，也不能仅凭“短”就判定必然更早。
9. Lamentations fragments postdate the city's destruction by centuries; Ezekiel fragments likewise postdate its internal sixth-century BCE dates by centuries. Manuscript dates date surviving carriers, not automatically initial composition or authors.
   《哀歌》残卷晚于毁城数百年，《以西结书》残卷也晚于书中主前六世纪日期数百年；抄本日期只给现存文字载体的年代，不自动给作品初写或作者年代。
10. Weeks 24–25 retain destruction/exile event history, the social history of exiles and descendants, and transmission of both books. Poetic theology and prophetic visions still require interpretation by text and genre.
    第 24–25 周最终保留三层：毁城／被掳事件史、被掳者与后裔的社会史、两卷书的文本抄传史；诗歌神学与先知异象仍须按经文和文类解释。

<a id="coverage-weeks-31-and-33-comparative-literature-hebrew-manuscripts-and-aramaic-translation"></a>
### Weeks 31 and 33: comparative literature, Hebrew manuscripts, and Aramaic translation | 第 31、33 周怎样区分比较文学、希伯来抄本与亚兰译本

1. *Ludlul bēl nēmeqi* and the Babylonian Theodicy establish that righteous suffering and sufferer–friend debate were ancient wisdom concerns. They are not Job manuscripts.
   *Ludlul bēl nēmeqi* 和《巴比伦神义论》是美索不达米亚智慧作品，证明“义人为何受苦”及受苦者—朋友辩论属于古代问题域；它们不是《约伯记》抄本。
2. 4Q99–101 are three Hebrew Job fragments; 11Q10 is an Aramaic translation. They address Hebrew transmission and translation into another language respectively, and cannot replace each other.
   4Q99–101 是三份希伯来文《约伯记》残卷；11Q10 是亚兰译本。前者回答希伯来文本如何抄传，后者回答经文如何进入另一语言，两者不能互相替代。
3. 4Q101 uses paleo-Hebrew script and is broadly assigned to the early Hellenistic period. An older-looking script neither dates the scroll to the Iron Age nor establishes Mosaic composition of Job in the patriarchal era.
   4Q101 用古希伯来字体且宽归早期希腊化时期；“字体较古”不等于卷轴写于铁器时代，更不等于《约伯记》由摩西在列祖时代写成。
4. 4Q99 preserves Elihu's speeches, establishing at most their presence in one Hasmonean copy. It does not establish when they were first composed or entered the book.
   4Q99 保存以利户讲话，最多把这部分在一份传本中的存在推到哈斯摩尼时期；它没有说明以利户段落最初何时写成或怎样进入全书。
5. Dense parallels between the Instruction of Amenemope and Proverbs 22–24 support discussion of a particular literary relationship. Ahiqar's relation to Proverbs is more appropriately broad genre, rhetorical, and social-wisdom background.
   《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》与箴 22–24 的密集平行，可支持具体文学关系讨论；《阿希卡尔》与《箴言》的联系更适合作广泛文类、修辞与社会智慧背景。
6. 4Q102/103 are direct Hebrew textual witnesses to Proverbs itself, preserving small portions of chapters 1–2 and 13–15. Comparative works and biblical manuscripts are distinct evidence types.
   4Q102／103 则属于《箴言》自身的直接希伯来文本史，保存 1–2、13–15 章小部分；比较文本与经文抄本是两类证据。
7. 4Q102 crosses neighboring instruction units and is written in poetic lines, making identification as a Proverbs manuscript reasonable. Too little survives to project the whole textual form, all 31 chapters, or canonical status backward.
   4Q102 跨过相邻训诲单元并按诗行抄写，使《箴言》抄本鉴定较合理；残文仍太少，不能把整卷文本形态、31 章范围或正典地位全部推回去。
8. The two Proverbs fragments mostly resemble the later Masoretic tradition with some variants. Similarity in surviving excerpts is not whole-book word-for-word invariance, nor an explanation of all Greek Proverbs order and addition/omission differences.
   两件《箴言》残卷多与后来马所拉传统相近并有少量异文；“现存小段相近”不等于“整卷逐字从无变化”，也不能单独解释希腊《箴言》的全部次序和增删差异。
9. 4Q99–103 are far later than the wisdom traditions or Solomonic titles potentially involved. They date attested textual circulation, not authors' identities or initial composition.
   4Q99–103 都远晚于书中可能涉及的智慧传统与所罗门题署；它们给文本流传的下限，不给作者身份证或初写日期。
10. Weeks 31 and 33 retain ancient wisdom questions/genres, Hebrew transmission, translation/reception, and the books' theological arguments. None becomes proof of internal people or events.
    第 31、33 周最终应保留四层：古代智慧问题／文类、希伯来文本抄传、翻译与接收、书卷内神学论证；任何一层都不能偷换成卷内人物或事件证明。

<a id="coverage-week-49-institutions-literature-manuscripts-inscriptions-and-lists"></a>
### Week 49: institutions, literature, manuscripts, inscriptions, and lists | 第 49 周的制度、文学、抄本、碑铭与书目怎样分层

1. Acts ends after Paul's two years imprisoned in Rome and does not narrate the traditional Pastoral travels. Narrative silence neither proves later journeys nor proves that none occurred.
   《使徒行传》在保罗被囚罗马两年处结束，没有叙述教牧书信传统行程；叙事沉默既不证明后来旅行，也不能证明绝无后来旅行。
2. 1 Clement 42–44 shows overseers/elders, deacons, character, recognition, and succession as early community concerns, without identifying a quotation of the Pastorals.
   《革利免一书》42–44 显示监督／长老、执事、品格、认可和继任已经是很早的群体问题，却没有注明引用教牧书信。
3. Polycarp combines 1 Timothy 6:10 and 6:7 in reverse order within one short passage: highly probable literary use, directly strengthening only 1 Timothy's early reception.
   坡旅甲把提前 6:10、6:7 两句在同一小段倒序连用，属于高概率文学使用；它只直接加强《提前》的早期接收。
4. The Ignatian middle recension repeatedly distinguishes one bishop, a council of elders, and deacons, establishing further second-century development of offices. Normative exhortations do not directly supply a survey of actual practice in every city.
   伊格那丢中卷本反复区分单一监督、长老团和执事，证明二世纪职分结构进一步发展；规范性劝告不能直接换算成各城实践调查。
5. Irenaeus directly uses each Pastoral twice with introductory formulas and attributes the words to the apostle/Paul. This is explicit reception and authorial attribution, not an automatic answer to modern authorship questions.
   爱任纽对三封教牧书信各有两处带引介公式的直接使用，并把话语归给使徒／保罗；这是明确接收和作者判断，不是现代作者问题的自动答案。
6. P46's final leaves are missing: only the absence of Pastorals from its surviving part can be stated. P32 and P133 positively attest Titus and 1 Timothy respectively on papyrus.
   P46 末叶缺失，只能说现存部分没有教牧书信；P32、P133 则分别给《提多书》《提摩太前书》的正面纸草见证。
7. Sinaiticus copies all three Pastorals completely and continuously and supplies the earliest currently known complete Greek 2 Timothy. Earliest surviving complete copy does not date composition as late as the fourth century.
   西奈抄本连续完整抄录三封教牧书信，并给目前所知最早完整希腊文《提后》；“现存最早完整”不等于写作最晚到四世纪。
8. Eleutherna MR245 and IEph 543 put elders/deacons on stone in Crete and Ephesus, but date to the fourth–sixth centuries CE. An object's directness cannot erase the chronological gap.
   Eleutherna MR245 与 IEph 543 把“长老／执事”留在克里特和以弗所石头上，却晚至主后四至六世纪；实物的直接性不能抵消年代距离。
9. The Muratorian fragment explicitly lists one to Titus and two to Timothy, addressing catalogue and public-reading boundaries. Its eighth-century carrier and disputed second-/fourth-century underlying date must both be stated.
   穆拉多利残篇明确列“一封给提多、两封给提摩太”，回答书目和公共诵读边界；现存八世纪载体及二／四世纪底本争议必须同时注明。
10. Together these sources trace concerns, institutional differentiation, literary use, explicit attribution, papyrus fragments, a complete collection, local inscriptions, and catalogue judgment. None alone proves Pauline authorship or travels after Acts 28.
    上述材料共同勾勒“问题出现—制度分化—文学使用—明确归属—纸草残片—完整文集—地方碑铭—书目判断”的接收史，仍没有任何一项能单独证明保罗作者或徒 28 后行程。

<a id="coverage-why-week-41-still-needs-a-separate-caution"></a>
### Why week 41 still needs a separate caution | 第 41 周为何仍须单独提醒

Week 41 now includes anchors for early tradition, manuscripts, burials, and Roman writings, but their evidence types differ from ordinary inscriptions naming people:<br>
第 41 周现已补入早期传统、抄本、墓葬和罗马文献锚点，但证据类型与一般人物铭文不同：

1. 1 Corinthians 15 illuminates the very early resurrection belief and witness chain; P46 and Mark-ending material illuminate transmission.
   林前 15 可说明复活信仰非常早及其见证链，P46 和马可结尾材料说明文本流传；
2. Yehohanan and remains beneath the Church of the Holy Sepulchre illuminate only punishment, burial, and cemetery settings.
   约哈难与圣墓教堂地下遗存只能说明刑罚、埋葬和墓地环境；
3. Philo repeats witness numbers without a gender qualification; Josephus explicitly excludes women; the Mishnah preserves both formal restrictions and exceptions for a husband's death. These cannot be reduced to one timeless rule.
   斐洛只重申人数而没有性别附加，约瑟夫斯明确排除妇女，《米示拿》则同时保存正式限制和丈夫死亡案件的例外；三者不能压成一条跨时代单一规则；
4. The Mishnah was compiled around 200 CE; projecting individual traditions back into the 30s CE requires further argument.
   《米示拿》约主后 200 年编定，个别传统若要倒推到主后 30 年代，必须另有论证；
5. The Nazareth Inscription corrects a misconception; Tacitus reaches only execution and the movement's spread.
   “拿撒勒敕令”是误传纠正，塔西佗只到处决及运动扩展；
6. Justin preserves the stolen-body accusation, while Celsus/Origen preserve criticism of female testimony and appearance experiences. They show continuing second-century disputes, not contemporary first-century external evidence.
   游斯丁保存“门徒盗尸”指控，克理索／俄利根保存对女性见证及显现经验的批评；两者说明第二世纪争论怎样延续，不是第一世纪同期外证；
7. Ignatius argues against merely apparent suffering by affirming real suffering, bodily resurrection, touching, and shared eating/drinking. This is early second-century positive reception, not another first-century eyewitness.
   伊格那丢以真实受苦、身体复活、触摸和同吃同喝反驳“只是表象”的主张；这是二世纪很早的正面接收，不是新增一名第一世纪目击者；
8. The Dura baptistery painting establishes female-procession salvation imagery in Christian ritual space around 240–245 CE. Women at the tomb, wise virgins, and baptismal candidates remain competing readings; do not first label it an empty-tomb picture and then use that label as proof.
   杜拉洗礼堂壁画证明约主后 240–245 年基督徒礼仪空间已有女性行列救恩图像；墓前妇女、聪明童女和洗礼入门者释读未定，不能先叫作“空墓图”再拿名称反证自己；
9. Irenaeus and Tertullian arrange creation, prophets, Christ's events, Spirit, return, and bodily resurrection into full outlines. Their rules show a stable narrative framework with variable expression, not two drafts of today's Apostles' Creed or wholly independent regional surveys.
   爱任纽与特土良把创造、先知、基督事件、圣灵、再来和身体复活排成完整纲要；两份规则显示稳定故事骨架与可变表达，不是今天《使徒信经》的两份原稿，也不是完全独立的地区问卷；
10. Apostolic Tradition 21 is reconstructed from multilingual versions, with composite layers in the work and chapter. The triple questions' story order can be identified, but the Hippolytus/about 215 CE label alone cannot make it a verbatim Roman rite from that year.
    《使徒传统》21 由多语版本重建，作品和本章都有复合层次；可确认三重问答所传的故事次序，不能只凭“希坡律陀／约主后 215 年”标签就称为当年罗马逐字礼文；
11. Marcellus' confession around 340/341 CE is the earliest complete continuous witness to the Old Roman Creed. Earlier Roman precursors' dates/wording and his use remain disputed; do not project the whole text into the second century or treat it as the original of today's Apostles' Creed.
    马尔凯鲁斯约主后 340／341 年认信是古罗马信条最早完整连续见证；更早罗马前身的年代、措辞及马尔凯鲁斯如何使用文本仍有争论，不能把它整篇倒推成二世纪或今天通行《使徒信经》的原稿；
12. Rufinus around 404 CE explicitly compares Roman and Aquileian forms, showing fixed recitation alongside local variants. His twelve-apostle composition story establishes only the existence of that origin tradition in his time.
    鲁非努约主后 404 年明确比较罗马与阿奎莱亚形式，说明固定背诵和地方变体并存；他的“十二使徒合写”叙述只能证明当时已有这项起源传说；
13. The 325 Nicene Creed N is a conciliar definition addressing particular Christological disputes. It is not word-for-word today's commonly recited C2 and did not determine the biblical book list.
    325 年尼西亚信经 N 是针对特定基督论争议的会议定义；它与今天礼仪中常诵的 C2 不是逐字同一文本，也没有决定圣经书目；
14. C2 first receives a complete, explicit attestation attributed to the 150 fathers in the 451 Chalcedonian records. Its exact relation to 381, C1/C2 reconstruction, and earliest liturgical use still need graded discussion.
    C2 在 451 年迦克墩会议记录中首次得到完整、明确且归于“150 位教父”的见证；它与 381 年会议的精确文本关系、C1／C2 重建和最早礼仪使用仍须分级讨论；
15. None of these sources can directly test the theological judgment that God raised Jesus.
    没有一项材料可直接检验“神使耶稣复活”这一神学判断。

<a id="coverage-week-51-letter-manuscripts-and-reception"></a>
### Week 51: letter manuscripts and reception | 第 51 周的书信抄本与接收史怎样分层

1. Polycarp dates as early as the first half of the second century but preserves only a formula closely parallel to 1/2 John, without identifying a book title.
   坡旅甲早至二世纪前半，却只保存与约一／约二高度平行的公式，没有注明所引书名；
2. Irenaeus more clearly uses 1 and 2 John, but this passage neither quotes 3 John nor lists all three books.
   爱任纽较清楚使用约一、约二，仍没有在该段引用约三或列出三卷书目；
3. Origen/Eusebius show broad recognition of 1 John and knowledge and substantial acceptance of 2/3 John alongside authorship/authenticity disputes. Disputed does not mean unanimously rejected.
   俄利根／优西比乌显示约一广获承认，约二、约三已知且被许多人接纳，却仍有作者与真实性争议；“有争议”不等于“被一致拒绝”；
4. P9 is a small fragment of 1 John 4. It establishes Egyptian textual circulation, not an existing three-letter collection.
   P9 是约一 4 章的小残片；它证明文本在埃及流传，不证明三书当时已经合编；
5. Fourth-century Sinaiticus displays all three letters completely; seventh-century papyrus P74 preserves fragments of all three. Material cannot replace dating: P74 is not earlier than Sinaiticus.
   西奈抄本在四世纪完整展示三书，P74 则在七世纪纸草文集中保存三书残片；载体不能替代定年，P74 并不比西奈抄本早；
6. Surviving Old Syriac witnesses are Gospel codices and cannot determine translation of the Catholic Epistles. The 22-book Peshitta includes 1 John but not 2/3 John: a version's scope, not a region-wide rejection declaration.
   古叙利亚文现存见证本来是福音书册，不足以回答普通书信是否被译；别西大 22 卷新约收约一、不收约二约三，是版本范围，不是全地区拒绝声明；
7. The 507/508 Philoxenian original is not fully preserved, so attribution of short Catholic letters needs caution. The 615/616 Harklean revision explicitly supplies a complete 27-book Syriac version. Do not conflate them.
   507／508 年菲罗克塞努译本原稿未完整保存，短普通书信归属须保留；615／616 年哈克尔修订则明确提供完整 27 卷叙利亚文版本，二者不能混称；
8. Early-sixth-century Edessene BL Add. 14,528 selects 1 Peter and 1 John but omits James, already in the Peshitta. This counterexample shows absence from a cycle is not directly noncanonical status.
   六世纪初埃德萨 BL Add. 14,528 选彼前、约一，却未选已经进入别西大的雅各；这个反例表明“某循环未读”不能直接等于“非正典”；
9. The 1255 West Syriac BL Add. 14,686 reads 2 Peter 3:8–15, absent from the Peshitta, but apparently not 2/3 John. Twelfth-century Bar Salibi explicitly comments on both short letters. Liturgical and exegetical use do not proceed together.
   1255 年西叙利亚 BL Add. 14,686 选读别西大缺少的彼后 3:8–15，却未见约二、约三；十二世纪巴尔·萨利比则明确注释两封短书，礼仪和释经使用并不同步；
10. The apostolic-letter column of the 1889 Urmia East Syriac table takes only the Pauline tradition. Commemorating James the Lord's brother in its calendar does not mean James was read that day. This late source cannot be projected back as the ancient Kokhe system.
    乌尔米 1889 东叙利亚读经表的“使徒书信”栏只取保罗传统；即使历书纪念主的兄弟雅各，也不等于当日选读《雅各书》。这份晚期资料不能倒作古科赫系统；
11. Ninth-/tenth-century BL Add. 14,491 selects Philemon twice; the 1889 Urmia table does not. This directly shows variation by system and period within the broader East Syriac tradition.
    九／十世纪 BL Add. 14,491 两次选读《腓利门书》，1889 年乌尔米表却不选；这项差异直接证明同一东叙利亚大传统的礼仪选择会随系统与时代变化；
12. The colophon of eleventh-century BL Add. 17,923 locates copying at the monastery of Hormizd, while full passage comparison assigns it to the Upper Monastery system. Copying location and liturgical tradition are compatible questions, but an eleventh-century copy is not a seventh-century original table.
    十一世纪 BL Add. 17,923 的题记证明它抄于霍尔米兹德修道院，完整逐段比较又把它归入上修道院系统；抄写地与礼仪传统并非二选一，但十一世纪抄本仍不是七世纪原表；
13. Kannookadan's annual Upper Monastery table has two Old Testament/Acts readings, Pauline tradition, and a Gospel in its main cycle, without Philemon, the Catholic Epistles, or Revelation. This establishes selective liturgical boundaries, not rejection of their canonicity.
    Kannookadan 的上修道院全年表以两段旧约／使徒行传、保罗传统和福音构成主循环，未见《腓利门书》、普通书信或《启示录》；这说明礼仪选择有边界，不是正典否决；
14. Camb. 3291 binds Acts and three Catholic letters with a Pauline lectionary, illustrating that presence in one carrier and inclusion in the annual letter-reading column remain separate facts.
    Camb. 3291 把《使徒行传》和三封普通书信与保罗读经本合订，恰好说明“同一载体有某卷”和“某卷进入年度书信栏”仍是两件事；
15. The late-fourth/early-fifth-century Doctrine of Addai genuinely uses exclusive reading language, stronger than a silent lectionary. Yet it is a later literary work placing instructions in an apostolic foundation story, not a first-century archive.
    晚四／早五世纪《阿代教义》真的使用了排他诵读语句，比读经表沉默更强；但它仍是把训令放进使徒奠基故事的后期文学作品，不是第一世纪档案；
16. The anonymous list in Sinai Syr. 10 is textually dated to the fourth century, while its carrier is around 700 CE. It lacks the Catholic Epistles and is not identical in scope to the later 22-book Peshitta containing James, 1 Peter, and 1 John.
    Sinai Syr. 10 所存匿名清单文字定于四世纪，承载抄本却约主后 700 年；它不列普通书信，不能与后来含雅、彼前、约一的别西大 22 卷混称为同一范围；
17. ʿAbdishoʿ's 1298 catalogue supplies a named East Syriac list explicitly naming James, 1 Peter, 1 John, and fourteen Pauline letters. Its many other ecclesiastical works do not turn the entire catalogue into a biblical canon.
    ʿAbdishoʿ 1298 年目录提供具名东叙利亚书目，逐名列雅、彼前、约一和十四封保罗书信；全书另列大量教会著作，不能因此把整部目录都叫作圣经正典；
18. These sources address verbal echoes, explicit use, reception categories, fragmentary copying, collection format, version scope, local liturgy, commentary use, and explicit catalogues respectively. They cannot replace each other or become first-century community archives.
    上述材料分别回答“语言回响”“明确使用”“接收分类”“残片传抄”“合集形态”“译本范围”“地方礼仪”“注释使用”和“明确书目”，不能互相偷换，也不能倒作一世纪群体档案。
