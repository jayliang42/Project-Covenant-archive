> Different book counts can reflect joining or splitting books rather than changing their contents. This guide compares ancient lists with later Jewish and Protestant counting. > Why do modern Protestant Old Testaments usually count 39 books, Jewish tradition often 24, Origen 22, and this project 52 core plus 14 bridge books? Different numbers need not mean extensive added/removed content: **joining books, splitting books, and arranging a course** often explain the difference.<br>
> 这份专题解决一个容易混乱的问题：为什么现代新教旧约通常说 39 卷，犹太传统常说 24 卷，俄利根却说 22 卷，而本项目又使用“52 卷主读＋14 卷桥接”？数字不同，未必表示经文内容被大量增删；很多时候，首先改变的是**合卷、分卷和课程编排方式**。
>

所以，本项目的 52 是**研读路线**，不是古代书目数字；22、24、39 主要是**书卷计数与集合边界问题**，也不是故事发生年代。

<a id="canon-lists-melito-a-named-old-testament-list-in-the-late-second-century"></a>
## Melito: A Named Old Testament List in the Late Second Century | 二、梅利托：第二世纪后期的逐名“旧约书卷”

Writing to Onesimus, Melito says he went east, to where the events were proclaimed and occurred, to investigate the [Old Testament](../Glossary/README.md#old-testament) books’ number and order. His Extracts is lost; Eusebius preserves its preface fragment in Ecclesiastical History 4.26.13–14.<br>
梅利托写给俄尼西母时说，他曾往“东方／这些事宣讲和发生之地”查考[旧约](../Glossary/README.md#old-testament)书卷的数目和次序。原《摘录集》已经失传，现由优西比乌《[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)史》4.26.13–14 保存序言片段。

His list includes the following; English labels are paired with the retained Chinese diagram.<br>
他的名单包括：

```text
Five books of Moses
摩西五卷
→ Joshua
→ 约书亚
→ Judges, Ruth
→ 士师、路得
→ Four books of Kingdoms
→ 列王四卷
→ Two books of Chronicles
→ 历代志两卷
→ Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, Job
→ 诗篇、箴言、传道书、雅歌、约伯
→ Isaiah, Jeremiah
→ 以赛亚、耶利米
→ The Twelve as one
→ 十二先知一卷
→ Daniel, Ezekiel, Ezra
→ 但以理、以西结、以斯拉
```

<a id="canon-lists-connecting-ancient-labels-to-modern-book-names"></a>
### Connecting Ancient Labels to Modern Book Names | 怎样接回现代书名

- Four books of Kingdoms follow Greek counting, broadly modern 1–2 Samuel and 1–2 Kings.
  “列王四卷”采用希腊传统的王国书计数，大体对应现代《撒母耳记上、下》与《列王纪上、下》。
- Two Chronicles books correspond to modern 1–2 Chronicles.
  “历代志两卷”对应现代《历代志上、下》。
- The Twelve, Hosea to Malachi, form one collection counted as a single unit.
  “十二先知一卷”把何西阿至玛拉基视作一个卷集，而不是十二个独立计数单位。
- Ezra probably includes Ezra–Nehemiah by ancient convention, but one title cannot determine the exact Greek text form.
  “以斯拉”很可能按古代习惯包含以斯拉—尼希米，但具体希腊文本形态不能只凭一个书名决定。
- Proverbs also called [Wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) is usually an alternative title, not a separate Wisdom of Solomon.
  “箴言，也称智慧”通常是《箴言》的别称，不宜直接拆成另一部《所罗门智训》。

<a id="canon-lists-gaps-that-must-remain-visible"></a>
### Gaps That Must Remain Visible | 必须保留的空白

- Esther is absent; deliberate omission, source variation, or copying loss cannot be decided.
  名单没有《以斯帖记》；这是有意、资料来源差异还是传抄遗漏，不能确定。
- Only Jeremiah is named, not explicitly Lamentations. Do not insert Origen’s later joining into Melito’s wording.
  名单只写“耶利米”，没有明确写“哀歌”；不能靠后来俄利根的合卷方式替梅利托补字。
- Melito does not claim a Jewish council decree or identical use in all churches.
  梅利托没有说自己取得犹太会议决议，也没有说所有教会已经使用完全一致的范围。

Its strongest support is that a late-second-century Christian investigated law, history, wisdom, and [prophets](../Glossary/README.md#prophet) by name as Old Testament books. It is not external evidence for events within those books.<br>
这份名单最强能证明的是：第二世纪后期已有基督徒作者把律法、历史、[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)和先知书当作一组“旧约书卷”逐名查考。它不是这些书卷事件的外证。

<a id="canon-lists-origen-how-twenty-two-books-are-joined"></a>
## Origen: How Twenty-Two Books Are Joined | 三、俄利根：二十二卷是怎样“合”出来的

In his early-third-century Commentary on [Psalm](../Glossary/README.md#psalm) 1, Origen reports twenty-two canonical books transmitted by Hebrews, corresponding to Hebrew letters. That commentary fragment is lost; Eusebius preserves the list in Ecclesiastical History 6.25.1–2.<br>
俄利根在第三世纪前半的《[诗篇](../Glossary/README.md#psalm)第一篇注释》中说，按希伯来人传下的正典书卷有二十二卷，与希伯来字母数对应。原注释片段已经失传，优西比乌在《教会史》6.25.1–2 中保存了名单。

| Named Origen group or conventional reconstruction / 俄利根明列或“22 卷”常用重建中的合卷单位 | Modern divisions / 对应现代分卷 | Storyline effect / 对故事线的影响 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Judges + Ruth | Judges, Ruth | Decline and Davidic genealogy remain continuous, counted once |
| 士师＋路得 | 士师记、路得记 | 黑暗循环与大卫家谱仍连续，只是按一卷计数 |
| Samuel as one | 1–2 Samuel | Saul’s failure, David’s rise, [covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant) remain one royal line |
| 撒母耳一卷 | 撒母耳记上、下 | 扫罗失败、大卫兴起与大卫之约仍是一条王权线 |
| Kings as one | 1–2 Kings | Solomon, division, both kingdoms’ fall remain kingdom-to-exile line |
| 列王一卷 | 列王纪上、下 | 所罗门、分裂、两国灭亡仍是一条王国—[被掳](../Glossary/README.md#exile)线 |
| Chronicles as one | 1–2 Chronicles | One retelling from Adam’s genealogy to Cyrus’ decree |
| 历代志一卷 | 历代志上、下 | 从亚当家谱到古列诏令仍是一部重述 |
| Ezra I and II as one | Ezra, Nehemiah | [Temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple), walls, law, communal reform in one restoration |
| 以斯拉第一、第二一卷 | 以斯拉记、尼希米记 | [圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)、城墙、律法和群体改革仍属同一归回工程 |
| Jeremiah + Lamentations + Letter | Jeremiah, Lamentations, Letter of Jeremiah | Warning, mourning, later letter tradition joined; not identical contents to modern Hebrew/Protestant Old Testament |
| 耶利米＋哀歌＋书信一卷 | 耶利米书、耶利米哀歌，另含《耶利米书信》 | 警告、毁城哀悼和后续书信传统被合计；集合内容不完全等同现代希伯来／新教旧约 |
| Twelve as one; missing in surviving enumeration, explicitly reconstructed here | Hosea through Malachi | Northern kingdom, Assyria, Babylon, return, Day of the LORD connected in one collection |
| 十二先知一卷（现传俄利根枚举缺此项；此处标作重建） | 何西阿至玛拉基十二卷 | 北国、亚述、巴比伦、归回与“耶和华的日子”仍按卷集相连 |

Origen explicitly names Esther, providing a witness where Melito’s list is silent.<br>
俄利根还明确逐名列《以斯帖记》，所以它能补上梅利托名单的沉默。

<a id="canon-lists-the-surviving-list-s-arithmetic-problem"></a>
### The Surviving List’s Arithmetic Problem | 现传名单的算术问题

Origen twice gives twenty-two, but Eusebius’ surviving enumeration has twenty-one entries, lacking the Twelve. Most research treats this as an omission somewhere in Origen, Eusebius’ excerpt, or later copying. This project follows two rules:<br>
俄利根两次说“二十二卷”，但优西比乌现传的逐项枚举只有二十一项，因为《十二先知》整卷没有出现。多数研究认为这是俄利根原文、优西比乌引录或后来抄传某一环节的遗漏；本项目采用两条纪律：

1. Explain that adding the conventionally expected Twelve brings the count to twenty-two.
   可以说明“补入通常预期的《十二先知》后，计数才达到二十二”；
2. Do not silently insert a reconstruction and then claim the surviving ancient sentence explicitly contains it.
   不能把现代重建静默塞进古文，再声称现传句子本来就逐字写着《十二先知》。

Accordingly, Melito’s explicit text supplies the Twelve’s CAT-type RX entry; the Origen anchor does not duplicate a reconstructed entry.<br>
因此，小先知的 CAT 型 RX 由梅利托明文承担；俄利根锚点不重复补列。

<a id="canon-lists-from-39-to-24-and-22"></a>
## From 39 to 24 and 22 | 四、39 怎样变成 24，又怎样变成 22

The following is a **counting explanation**, not a claim that every ancient list has exactly this arrangement:<br>
下面是一个**计数说明**，不是声称每一份古代名单都完全照此排列：

```text
Modern Protestant Old Testament: 39
现代新教旧约 39 卷

Join:
合并：
  1–2 Samuel         -1
  撒母耳上下        -1
  1–2 Kings          -1
  列王纪上下        -1
  1–2 Chronicles     -1
  历代志上下        -1
  Ezra–Nehemiah      -1
  以斯拉—尼希米     -1
  The Twelve         -11
  十二小先知合一    -11
                     ───
Common Jewish count: 24
犹太传统常见计数      24

Join again:
再合并：
  Judges–Ruth        -1
  士师—路得         -1
  Jeremiah–Lamentations -1
  耶利米—哀歌       -1
                     ───
Some ancient lists: 22
某些古代书目计数      22
```

The model explains numbers without erasing differences:<br>
这个模型帮助读者理解数字，但不能抹掉差异：

- Origen’s Jeremiah grouping includes the Letter of Jeremiah, not a purely mechanical compression of the modern Protestant 39.
  俄利根的耶利米组合还含《耶利米书信》，并不等同现代新教 39 卷的纯机械压缩。
- Melito omits Esther and does not explicitly join Lamentations to Jeremiah.
  梅利托没有列《以斯帖记》，也没有明确把《哀歌》并入《耶利米书》。
- Lists show what an author knew, named, or recommended. Listing, quotation, copying within one codex, and liturgical reading remain different kinds of evidence.
  古代列表回答的是某位作者所知、所列或所建议的书卷；“列名”“引用”“抄在同一 codex”“礼仪宣读”仍是不同层次。

<a id="canon-lists-how-these-lists-connect-the-52-book-route"></a>
## How These Lists Connect the 52-Book Route | 五、两份名单怎样帮助串联 52 卷

Many Old Testament books on the 52-book route already had MS [manuscript](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript) entries but lacked RX ancient-reception entries. Melito and Origen add a cross-book connection, not new evidence for narrated events:<br>
在 52 卷主读路线中，许多旧约书卷此前已有 MS（[抄本](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript)）却缺少 RX（古代接收）入口。梅利托与俄利根补的不是新的事件证据，而是一个跨卷连接：

```text
Pentateuch
五经
  ↓
Joshua–Judges–Ruth
约书亚—士师—路得
  ↓
Samuel–Kings–Chronicles
撒母耳—列王—历代志
  ↓
Poetry and wisdom
诗歌与智慧
  ↓
Isaiah–Jeremiah/Lamentations–Ezekiel–Daniel
以赛亚—耶利米／哀歌—以西结—但以理
  ↓
The Twelve
十二先知
  ↓
Ezra–Nehemiah–Esther
以斯拉—尼希米—以斯帖
```

Ancient readers could investigate diverse genres as one historical/[faith](../Glossary/README.md#faith) collection. The lists do not explain each book or establish equal reception depth across them.<br>
这条链显示，古代读者已经会把不同文类当作一组历史—信仰文献来查考；但名单本身不告诉我们每卷如何解释，也不表示每卷接收深度相同。

<a id="canon-lists-what-remains-after-rx-coverage-closes"></a>
### What Remains after RX Coverage Closes? | RX 覆盖闭合后还缺什么

After research batch 71, every canonical book had at least one RX entry: **binary coverage closure** only. Further work distinguishes:<br>
第 71 批后，66 卷都至少有一个 RX 入口；这只是**二元覆盖闭合**。后续仍要区分：

| RX subtype / RX 子型 | Question / 回答什么 | Example / 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAT: catalogue | Which books an author explicitly groups | Melito, Origen, Muratorian fragment |
| CAT｜书目列名 | 某作者明确把哪些书列入一组 | 梅利托、俄利根、穆拉多利残篇 |
| USE: literary use | Whether author quotes, adapts, or densely uses a book | Polycarp uses 1 Peter and Ephesians |
| USE｜文学使用 | 某作者是否引用、改写或密集使用一卷 | 坡旅甲使用《彼得前书》《以弗所书》 |
| COM: continuous commentary | How a group explains text sequentially | 1QpHab, 4Q169, 4Q252 |
| COM｜连续注释 | 某群体怎样逐句解释经文 | 1QpHab、4Q169、4Q252 |
| LEC: liturgical selection | What passages a time/place actually reads in worship | Syriac lectionary |
| LEC｜礼仪选读 | 某时某地实际在崇拜中读哪些段落 | 叙利亚读经表 |
| VER: version/collection scope | Contents in a language version or bound collection | Peshitta, Codex Sinaiticus |
| VER｜译本／合集范围 | 某语言版本或合订本实际收录什么 | 别西大、西奈抄本 |

CAT establishes naming, not automatically USE, COM, or LEC. The next question is no longer merely whether RX exists but its earliest subtype, named-book status, physical witness, and geographical scope.<br>
CAT 能证明列名，却不自动证明 USE、COM 或 LEC。下一阶段不再追求“有没有 RX”，而要追问“最早是哪一种 RX、是否具名、是否有实体载体、地区范围多大”。

<a id="canon-lists-four-questions-for-group-study"></a>
## Four Questions for Group Study | 六、查经时可直接使用的四个问题

1. Does an ancient list count this book separately or joined to neighboring books?
   这卷在古代名单里是单独计算，还是与前后卷合在一起？
2. Does joining clarify a handoff, such as Judges to Ruth or Jeremiah’s warning to Lamentations?
   合卷是否让故事接力更明显，例如《士师记》到《路得记》、耶利米警告到《哀歌》？
3. Does the material merely name books, or quote, comment, or read them liturgically?
   现有材料只是列书名，还是已经引用、注释或礼仪宣读？
4. Am I confusing ancient reception of a book with proof that its narrated events happened?
   我是否误把“古代有人接收这卷”写成“古代资料证明卷内事件发生”？
