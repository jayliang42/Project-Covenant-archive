<a id="wisdom-first-distinguish-four-kinds-of-date"></a>
## First Distinguish Four Kinds of Date | 一、先分清四种“年代”

[Wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) and poetry especially invite collapsing different questions into one date. Keep these distinct:<br>
[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)与诗歌书尤其容易把不同问题压成一个日期。研读时应分开：

1. **Narrative setting:** the period in which the book places its characters or scenes.
   **叙事年代**：书中人物或场景被放在哪个时代；
2. **Formation:** when oral tradition, individual compositions, or earlier materials arose.
   **作品形成**：口传、单篇写作或较早材料何时出现；
3. **Compilation:** when the book or collection took its present shape.
   **编集年代**：现有书卷或诗集何时被整理成目前形态；
4. **Manuscript date:** when the surviving physical copy was written.
   **抄本年代**：今天实际保存下来的那一份物质载体何时写成。

Job’s household wealth, [sacrifices](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice), and lifespan often evoke the patriarchal world, but literary features alone cannot date its protagonist or composition. Psalms contains voices across kingdoms, temples, [exile](../Glossary/README.md#exile), and return; a first-century CE copy attests how poems were preserved, arranged, or used then, not automatically their authors’ dates. Qumran fragments of Ecclesiastes and Song of Songs similarly establish circulation by their copying dates, not Solomonic authorship, first composition, or final compilation.<br>
《约伯记》的家族财富、[献祭](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice)和寿数常使人联想到列祖时代，但这些文学特征不能单独锁定主人公或成书年代。《诗篇》则明确包含跨越王国、[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)、[被掳](../Glossary/README.md#exile)与归回的声音；一份主后一世纪抄本只能证明当时怎样保存、排列或使用诗歌，不能自动成为原作者的年代。《传道书》和《雅歌》的昆兰残卷也只给出“最迟当时已经流传”的下限，不能直接解决所罗门题署、作品初创或最后编集的日期。

<a id="wisdom-job-similar-questions-do-not-mean-identical-answers"></a>
## Job: Similar Questions Do Not Mean Identical Answers | 二、《约伯记》：问题相近，不等于答案相同

<a id="wisdom-1-the-book-s-own-argument"></a>
### 1. The Book’s Own Argument | 1. 经文自己的论证

Readers know about Job’s heavenly-council scene, but Job and his friends do not. The friends absolutize general wisdom: if God is just, a sufferer must have sinned in proportion to the suffering. Job refuses a false confession and keeps seeking an encounter with God.<br>
《约伯记》先让读者知道天上议会的场景，约伯和朋友却不知道。朋友把一般性的智慧原则绝对化：既然神公义，受苦者必定犯了与苦难相称的罪。约伯拒绝虚假认罪，也不断要求与神相遇。

God’s whirlwind response does not disclose the first two chapters’ hidden events. The breadth, order, and uncontrollability of [creation](../Glossary/README.md#creation) instead correct human claims to knowledge. Job 42:7 rebukes the friends and contrasts what they said about God unfavorably with Job’s words; their pious-sounding statements therefore cannot all be treated as the book’s own conclusion. The original Chinese quotation is retained below.<br>
神从旋风中回答时，没有把第一、二章的幕后情节告诉约伯，而是以受造界的广阔、秩序与不可控制性校正人的知识边界。结尾明确责备朋友“议论我不如我的仆人约伯说的是”（伯 42:7），因此不能把朋友每一句听起来敬虔的话都当作本书结论。

<a id="wisdom-2-ludlul-b-l-n-meqi-and-the-modern-label-babylonian-job"></a>
### 2. Ludlul bēl nēmeqi and the Modern Label Babylonian Job | 2. 《我要赞美智慧之主》：常被称为“巴比伦约伯”

The Akkadian Ludlul bēl nēmeqi describes a protagonist with status, family, and religious life who suffers disease, humiliation, estranged friends, and failed divination before Marduk restores him. Assyrian and Babylonian tablet witnesses survive from Nineveh, Assur, Babylon, Sippar, and elsewhere. Babylonian Job is a modern comparative nickname, not the tablet’s original title.<br>
阿卡德文作品 *Ludlul bēl nēmeqi* 的主人公原有地位、家庭与宗教生活，后来经历疾病、社会羞辱、朋友疏远和占卜失效，最终由玛尔杜克恢复。现存见证来自尼尼微、亚述、巴比伦、锡帕等地的亚述与巴比伦泥版；“巴比伦约伯”是现代比较用昵称，不是泥版原题。

- **Similarities:** an apparently devout person experiences unexplained disaster; ordinary religious explanations fail; human knowledge is limited before divine intention; restoration concludes the story.
  **相似处**：敬虔者遭受难以解释的灾祸；常规宗教解释失效；人在神意面前承认知识有限；故事最后出现恢复。
- **Differences:** rescue comes from Marduk within a world of many gods, divination, exorcism, and personal protectors. Job has the one Creator speak and criticizes condemning sufferers through mechanical retribution.
  **关键差别**：作品把解救归给玛尔杜克，并处在多神、占卜、驱邪和个人保护神的世界；《约伯记》则让独一创造主亲自发言，并批判用机械报应替受苦者定罪。
- **Limit:** the righteous sufferer was already an ancient wisdom question. This proves neither that Job copied the poem nor the dates of Job’s person or biblical book.
  **解释边界**：相似问题证明“义人为何受苦”早已属于古代智慧讨论；它不证明《约伯记》抄自这首诗，也不能给约伯本人或[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)书卷定年。

Related evidence anchor: **98**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**98**。

<a id="wisdom-3-the-babylonian-theodicy-sufferer-and-friend-in-debate"></a>
### 3. The Babylonian Theodicy: Sufferer and Friend in Debate | 3. 《巴比伦神义论》：受苦者与朋友的辩论

The work now called the Babylonian Theodicy is a dialogue between sufferer and friend. Its surviving form is an acrostic of 27 stanzas of 11 lines. The sufferer questions poverty, injustice, and divine government; the friend defends traditional order. Its ending is not Job’s ending.<br>
现代称为《巴比伦神义论》的作品以受苦者和朋友对话，现有形式为二十七节、每节十一行的离合诗。受苦者质疑贫穷、社会不义和诸神的治理，朋友尝试维护传统秩序；文本本身没有给出与《约伯记》相同的结局。

- **Similarities:** dialogue, a friend’s role, tension between righteousness and suffering, and incomplete knowledge of divine intention.
  **相似处**：对话形式、朋友角色、义与苦难的张力，以及人无法完全知道神意。
- **Differences:** its gods, social argument, poetic form, and conclusion cannot simply be transferred into Job.
  **关键差别**：它的众神世界、社会论证、诗体结构和结论都不能直接套进《约伯记》。
- **Limit:** the works address comparable ancient wisdom questions. This does not make a friend the prototype of Eliphaz or establish borrowing from form alone.
  **解释边界**：可说两部作品属于可比较的古代智慧问题域；不能说其中一位朋友就是以利法的原型，也不能仅凭形式相似断定直接借用。

Related evidence anchor: **99**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**99**。

<a id="wisdom-4-11q10-aramaic-job-as-transmission-not-event-evidence"></a>
### 4. 11Q10: Aramaic Job as Transmission, Not Event Evidence | 4. 11Q10《约伯记亚兰文译本》：传播史，不是事件证物

Qumran Cave 11’s 11Q10 is a Herodian-period parchment translation of Job in square Aramaic script, surviving mainly from the book’s latter half. It attests Second Temple readers translating difficult Hebrew Job into Aramaic and continuing to read and interpret it.<br>
昆兰第 11 洞的 11Q10 是羊皮纸、希律时期、方体字亚兰文的《约伯记》翻译，现存部分主要来自本书后半。它说明第二圣殿时期已有群体把艰深的《约伯记》翻成亚兰文，并持续诵读、解释这卷书。

The manuscript helps investigate:<br>
这项材料能帮助研究：

- Job’s circulation and translation in the Second Temple period.
  《约伯记》在第二圣殿时期的流传和翻译；
- How Aramaic readers understood the Hebrew text.
  亚兰语读者怎样理解希伯来文本；
- Translation as both communication and interpretive choice.
  经文翻译既传达内容，也会留下解释选择。

It cannot establish Job’s historical identity, story location, or initial composition date. Separate manuscript date, translation formation, and Hebrew composition.<br>
它不能证明约伯的历史身份、故事发生地点或《约伯记》最初写作年代。抄本日期、译本形成日期和希伯来原作年代必须分开。

Related evidence anchor: **100**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**100**。

<a id="wisdom-5-4q99-4q100-4q101-hebrew-copies-are-distinct-from-aramaic-translation"></a>
### 5. 4Q99/4Q100/4Q101: Hebrew Copies Are Distinct from Aramaic Translation | 5. 4Q99／4Q100／4Q101：先分开希伯来原文抄本与亚兰译本

Qumran Cave 4 preserves three Hebrew Job manuscripts. 4Q101 has chapters 13–14 in paleo-Hebrew, broadly catalogued by the IAA as early Hellenistic. Hasmonean 4Q99 uses square script and preserves parts of chapters 31–37, including Elihu. Herodian 4Q100 is a square-script fragment preserving small portions of chapters 8–14 and 31.<br>
昆兰第 4 洞另存三份希伯来文《约伯记》残卷。4Q101 用古希伯来字体保存 13–14 章，文物局宽归早期希腊化时期；4Q99 是哈斯摩尼时期方体字抄本，现存 31–37 章多段，包含以利户讲话；4Q100 是希律时期方体字残卷，保存 8–14、31 章若干短段。

These manuscripts and 11Q10 answer different questions:<br>
这三件材料和 11Q10 回答不同问题：

- 4Q99–101 directly attest Hebrew copying of Job.
  4Q99–101 是《约伯记》希伯来文的直接抄传见证；
- 11Q10 attests reception through translation into Aramaic.
  11Q10 是把希伯来经文译成亚兰文的接收见证；
- Ludlul and the Babylonian Theodicy are comparative suffering literature, not Job manuscripts.
  *Ludlul bēl nēmeqi* 与《巴比伦神义论》则是可比较的古代受苦文学，不是《约伯记》抄本。

The archaic appearance of 4Q101’s script does not date Job to patriarchal or Mosaic times. Paleo-Hebrew could be a deliberate Second Temple choice. Keep script form, copying date, composition, and narrative setting separate. Elihu in 4Q99 shows those speeches in one Hasmonean copy, not when they first entered the book.<br>
4Q101 看起来“字体古老”，却不能因此把本书写作推到列祖或摩西时代。古希伯来字体可以是第二圣殿时期的书写选择；抄本字形、抄写日期、作品形成和叙事场景必须分开。4Q99 保存以利户讲话，最多说明这部分到哈斯摩尼时期已在一份传本中，不能独自告诉我们它最初何时进入全书。

Related evidence anchor: **171**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**171**。

## Psalms: A Collection across Generations, Not One Author’s Single Composition | 三、《诗篇》：跨世代诗集，不是一位作者的一次写作

<a id="wisdom-1-using-superscriptions-and-the-five-book-structure"></a>
### 1. Using Superscriptions and the Five-Book Structure | 1. 标题与五卷结构怎样使用

Superscriptions preserve traditions associated with David, Asaph, Korah’s sons, Solomon, Moses, and others. Hebrew Psalms also has five books, often closed by doxologies. A careful reading recognizes all of the following:<br>
诗篇标题保存“大卫的”“亚萨的”“可拉后裔的”“所罗门的”“摩西的”等传统线索；希伯来诗篇本身又分成五卷，并在若干位置以颂赞句收束。较稳妥的读法是同时承认：

- David’s central place in [Israel](../Glossary/README.md#israel)’s poetic and worship memory.
  大卫在[以色列](../Glossary/README.md#israel)诗歌和敬拜记忆中的核心地位；
- Multiple authors, historical settings, and extended compilation.
  多位作者、不同历史处境和长期编集；
- Superscriptions’ interpretive value without equating them to modern proof of authorship.
  标题有真实解释价值，却不一定等于现代版权页式的作者证明；
- Meaning arising both from a poem’s original setting and its later place in the collection.
  单篇原始处境与整部诗集中的新位置可以同时产生意义。

The linked Psalms navigation guide explains four functions of superscriptions, five-book endings, layers of royal interpretation, and major [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament) quotations. It distinguishes observable text from editorial models, [reception history](../Glossary/README.md#reception-history), and Christian confession.<br>
诗题的四种功能、五卷收束、王权诗的分层解释和主要[新约](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament)引用，已经另建[《诗篇五卷结构、王权诗与新约引用导航》](./诗篇五卷结构与新约引用导航.md)。该导航把可观察的文本事实与编集模型、接收历史和基督教神学认信分开。

<a id="wisdom-2-4q174-rereading-davidic-covenant-and-royal-psalms"></a>
### 2. 4Q174: Rereading Davidic Covenant and Royal Psalms | 2. 4Q174：大卫之约与诗篇王权语言的第二圣殿重读

Qumran Cave 4’s 4Q174, now called 4Q Eschatological Commentary A and formerly 4QFlorilegium, is a Hebrew interpretive fragment around the Herodian period. It combines 2 Samuel 7’s Davidic offspring promise with Psalms 1–2 and prophetic texts in an eschatological, Davidic framework.<br>
昆兰第 4 洞的 4Q174，现题为 4Q Eschatological Commentary A，旧称 4QFlorilegium，是希律时期前后的希伯来文释经残卷。它把撒母耳记下 7 章的大卫后裔[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)、诗篇 1–2 和其他[先知](../Glossary/README.md#prophet)经文放进末世／大卫式解释框架。

It directly attests Jewish readers before or around New Testament times combining Davidic [covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant) and anointed-king language from Psalms. It does not establish one messianic scheme for all Jews, identify Qumran interpretation with New Testament Christology, show Hebrews copying this scroll, or restrict the original poems to this one meaning.<br>
这份残卷能直接说明：新约以前或同时代的犹太解释世界中，确实有人把大卫之约和诗篇受膏君王语言组合阅读。它不能证明所有犹太人采用同一[弥赛亚](../Glossary/README.md#messiah)图式，也不能证明昆兰解释等同新约基督论、希伯来书直接抄用此卷，或原诗只有这一层意义。

对应旁证锚点：**113**。

### 3. 11Q5: Second Temple Arrangement and Use of Psalms | 3. 11Q5《大诗篇卷》：第二圣殿时期的排列与使用

11Q5/11QPsᵃ from Cave 11 is a Herodian Hebrew parchment dated within 1–68 CE. The IAA describes 48 poems, seven absent from the standard Masoretic collection, as well as prose portraying David as a prolific author.<br>
11Q5（11QPsᵃ）出自昆兰第 11 洞，是主后 1–68 年间、希律时期的希伯来文羊皮纸抄本。以色列文物局称其保存四十八首诗，其中七首不见于标准马所拉本；卷中也有把大卫描述为大量诗歌作者的散文段落。

Its importance includes:<br>
这份抄本的重要性在于：

- Physical evidence of Second Temple copying, reading, and Davidic association.
  它实物见证诗篇在第二圣殿时期被抄写、诵读并与大卫传统相连；
- An order and selection in its latter part that differ from the Masoretic Text.
  它后半部诗歌的次序和收录范围与马所拉本不完全相同；
- The question whether it represents a Psalms [canon](../Glossary/README.md#canon) or a particular community’s liturgical collection.
  它迫使研究者讨论这是一种诗篇[正典](../Glossary/README.md#canon)形态，还是为特定群体整理的礼仪／敬拜选集。

It proves neither that no canon existed nor that every Jewish group used the same arrangement. It is not David’s autograph.<br>
因此，11Q5 既不能简单证明“当时完全没有正典”，也不能证明所有犹太群体都使用同一排列；更不是大卫亲笔卷。

<a id="wisdom-4-the-great-hymn-to-the-aten-and-psalm-104"></a>
### 4. The Great Hymn to the Aten and Psalm 104 | 4. 《阿吞大神颂》与诗篇 104：相似自然图景，不同敬拜中心

The Great Hymn to the Aten appears in 13 hieroglyphic columns on the west wall of the court in Ay’s Amarna tomb, from Akhenaten’s period. It describes darkness and wild animals after sunset, work after sunrise, plants, birds, boats, fish, and life. Psalm 104 similarly praises the Creator through night, lions, dawn, work, sea, creatures, and provision.<br>
《阿吞大神颂》刻在阿玛尔那大臣阿伊墓室庭院西墙的十三列象形文字中，属于阿肯那顿时期。它描写日落后的黑暗与野兽、日出后的工作、植物、鸟、船只、鱼和万物得生命。诗篇 104 也以黑夜、狮子、日出、劳作、海洋、生物和受造物得供应来赞美创造主。

Comparison reveals shared Egyptian and Levantine cultural language for observing creation and praising deity. More specific literary transmission is possible, but its direction and route remain disputed.<br>
比较可以说明埃及与黎凡特共享一套观察受造世界、以诗歌赞美神明的文化语言，也可能涉及更具体的文学传播；但具体方向和路径仍有争论。

Their theological centers must remain distinct:<br>
两者的神学中心不能抹平：

- The hymn links life-giving light, Aten, and royal mediation.
  《大神颂》把生命之光、阿吞与王室中介紧密相连；
- Psalm 104 addresses the LORD; the sun is a created light, and sea and life are under his rule.
  诗篇 104 向耶和华祷告，太阳只是受造秩序中的光体，海与一切生命都由祂治理；
- Parallels alone neither date Psalm 104 nor reduce it to copying an Egyptian hymn.
  相似段落不能单独给诗篇 104 定年，更不能缩成“诗篇只是抄袭埃及颂歌”。

Related evidence anchor: **102**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**102**。

<a id="wisdom-5-ugaritic-baal-texts-and-storm-and-sea-poetry"></a>
### 5. Ugaritic Baal Texts and Storm-and-Sea Poetry | 5. 乌加列巴力史诗与风暴—海洋诗歌

Alphabetic cuneiform tablets from Ras Shamra/Ugarit preserve Baal’s conflict with the sea deity Yam and others. Louvre AO 16640, excavation RS 3.367/3.346, catalogued KTU 1.2, is a Late Bronze II witness to the Baal Cycle.<br>
拉斯沙姆拉古乌加列出土的字母楔形文字泥版保存巴力与海神雅姆等神祇的争战。卢浮宫 AO 16640（发掘号 RS 3.367／3.346，目录 KTU 1.2）属于晚青铜时代 II，是巴力循环的重要见证。

Psalm 29’s thunder, waters, Lebanon, and divine kingship, together with sea, monsters, and kingship in Psalms 74 and 89, invite comparison with Northwest Semitic poetry. The parallels help recover language of storm, sea, chaos, and kingship familiar to ancient audiences.<br>
诗篇 29 的雷声、众水、黎巴嫩和神作王，诗篇 74、89 的海、怪兽与王权，都可放在西北闪族诗歌传统中比较。这些平行帮助读者听见古代受众熟悉的“风暴、海、混沌、君王”语言。

Comparison does not identify the deities:<br>
但比较不等于神祇等同：

- Ugaritic texts narrate Baal gaining kingship among multiple gods.
  乌加列文本在多神叙事中描写巴力争取王权；
- Psalms places sea, storm, and every chaotic power under the LORD’s existing sovereignty.
  诗篇把海、雷暴和一切混沌力量置于耶和华已有的主权之下；
- Shared poetic resources, reuse, or polemical adaptation can be considered. They do not establish Baal worship in Psalms or unsupported claims of copying a particular tablet.
  可讨论共同诗歌库存、重用或论战性改写，却不能因此说诗篇在敬拜巴力，也不能无证据地宣称某篇直接抄某块泥版。

Related evidence anchor: **103**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**103**。

<a id="wisdom-proverbs-ecclesiastes-and-song-of-songs-order-limits-and-created-love"></a>
## Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and Song of Songs: Order, Limits, and Created Love | 四、《箴言》《传道书》《雅歌》：秩序、限制与受造之爱

<a id="wisdom-1-the-three-books-qualify-rather-than-cancel-one-another"></a>
### 1. The Three Books Qualify Rather than Cancel One Another | 1. 三卷书不是互相取消，而是互相校正

Proverbs trains observation of created order: reverence for the LORD, diligence, honesty, and teachability generally bring life, while folly and oppression undermine it. Ecclesiastes asks whether time, chance, injustice, and death allow these principles to become formulas controlling outcomes. Song of Songs situates wisdom within bodies, desire, mutual praise, searching, boundaries, and love’s intensity.<br>
《箴言》训练读者观察受造秩序：敬畏耶和华、勤劳、诚实和受教通常带来生命，愚昧和欺压通常自毁。《传道书》追问人在时间、偶然、不义与死亡之下，能否把这些原则变成控制结果的公式。《雅歌》则把智慧放进身体、渴望、彼此赞美、寻找、边界和爱情强度中。

Together they offer a fuller wisdom:<br>
三卷书放在一起，形成较完整的智慧：

- Principles are real without becoming mechanical guarantees or ways of manipulating God.
  原则是真实的，却不是操纵神或保证即时结果的机械法则；
- Although tomorrow is beyond control, food, work, relationships, and present joy may be received as God’s gift.
  人无法掌控明天，仍可把食物、工作、关系和当下喜乐当作神所赐的分；
- Bodies and love can be celebrated, while love must not be forced, purchased, or casually possessed.
  身体和爱情可以被正面歌咏，但爱也不可被催逼、购买或随意占有；
- Solomonic attributions must be read separately through the genre and editorial clues of Proverbs 1:1, 10:1, 25:1, Ecclesiastes 1:1, 12, and Song 1:1, rather than as modern copyright pages.
  “所罗门的”或“与所罗门有关”必须分别按箴言 1:1、10:1、25:1，传道书 1:1、12，以及雅歌 1:1 的文体和编集线索处理，不能一概当作现代版权页。

The dense parallels between the Instruction of Amenemope and Proverbs 22:17–24:22 are anchor 97. They show international wisdom exchange and Proverbs’ reframing within reverence for the LORD.<br>
《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》与箴言 22:17–24:22 的密集平行已列为锚点 **97**：它显示以色列智慧参与国际交流，也显示箴言怎样把材料重置于敬畏耶和华的伦理中。

<a id="wisdom-2-elephantine-ahiqar-shared-wisdom-is-not-one-way-copying"></a>
### 2. Elephantine Ahiqar: Shared Wisdom Is Not One-Way Copying | 2. 象岛《阿希卡尔》：共同智慧世界不等于单向抄写

The Ahiqar papyrus from Elephantine (Berlin P. 13446) preserves an Imperial Aramaic court story and sayings. Ahiqar is falsely accused by his adopted son and later restored; instruction concerns secrets, speech, obedience, childrearing, wealth, servants, and power. Berlin’s museum broadly dates it to the Persian period, about 525–404 BCE; scholarship commonly places the surviving copy in the later fifth century.<br>
象岛出土的 Berlin P. 13446 保存一部帝国亚兰文宫廷故事和格言集：智者阿希卡尔遭养子陷害，后来复位；接续的训诲处理秘密、言语、顺服、亲子管教、财富、仆役和权势。柏林博物馆把纸草宽定于波斯时期约主前 525–404 年，研究通常把现存抄写放在主前五世纪后期。

Its value for Proverbs is not an original draft but evidence that:<br>
这份材料对《箴言》的价值不在于找到“原稿”，而在于证明：

- Court story and practical sayings circulated together in the Persian-period Aramaic world.
  波斯时期的亚兰语世界真实流传“宫廷故事＋生活格言”的智慧作品；
- Parental or teaching instruction, cautious speech, discipline, friends, wealth, and rank were not uniquely Israelite topics.
  父／师对年轻人的训诲、谨慎言语、管教、朋友、财富与地位并非以色列独有议题；
- Proverbs participates in international wisdom while reorganizing knowledge, ethics, and communal responsibility around reverence for the LORD.
  《箴言》既可参与国际智慧传统，又以“敬畏耶和华”重新安排知识、伦理和群体责任；
- Amenemope’s dense passage-level relation to Proverbs 22–24 is not the same strength of evidence as Ahiqar’s broader genre and rhetorical comparison.
  《阿蒙尼莫普教诲》与箴 22–24 的密集段落关系，和《阿希卡尔》的广泛文类／修辞比较，不应写成同一强度。

Finding the papyrus where Jews lived does not establish a Jewish author, synagogue curriculum, or direct acquisition by Proverbs. Its story claims an Assyrian court setting, while the copy belongs to Persian Egypt. Separate narrated period, tradition formation, and manuscript date.<br>
纸草发现于有犹太社群的象岛，也不能据此宣布作者是犹太人、作品是当地会堂教材，或《箴言》直接从象岛取得。故事自称亚述宫廷，现存副本属于波斯时期埃及；故事时代、传统形成和抄本年代必须分开。

Related evidence anchor: **159**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**159**。

<a id="wisdom-3-4q102-4q103-direct-but-small-proverbs-witnesses"></a>
### 3. 4Q102/4Q103: Direct but Small Proverbs Witnesses | 3. 4Q102／4Q103《箴言》：直接文本见证仍然很小

4Q102 and 4Q103 are Herodian Hebrew parchment fragments from Cave 4. 4Q102 has Proverbs 1:27–2:1, crossing from wisdom’s rebuke into a new parental address. 4Q103 mainly preserves sayings from chapters 13–15. They attest both longer instruction and short sayings in Herodian Proverbs copies, although narrower paleographic dates differ.<br>
昆兰第 4 洞的 4Q102、4Q103 是两份希律时期希伯来文皮质残卷。4Q102 保存箴言 1:27–2:1，刚好跨越智慧责备的结尾和新一段“我儿”训诲的开头；4Q103 主要保存 13–15 章的格言。它们说明到希律时期，较长训诲与短句格言都已在《箴言》传本中被抄写；两卷更窄的古文字断年并不完全一致。

Their small surviving scope requires small conclusions:<br>
两件抄本的范围很小，所以结论也必须很小：

- They directly support Hebrew Proverbs transmission, beyond Egyptian and Aramaic parallels.
  它们补上《箴言》自身的希伯来文本史，不再只靠埃及或亚兰智慧平行；
- Most readings resemble the later Masoretic tradition, with some variants.
  现存文字多与后来马所拉传统相近，也有少量异文；
- 4Q102’s line layout supports identifying it as Proverbs, but tiny fragments are not a complete book.
  4Q102 的诗行布局支持把它看作《箴言》抄本，但极小残片仍不等于完整书卷；
- Greek Proverbs differs in order, additions, omissions, and interpretation. These fragments cover too little to decide every difference’s origin in a Hebrew source or translation method.
  希腊《箴言》存在次序、增删和诠释差异，4Q102／103 覆盖太少，不能替整卷判定每一差异来自不同希伯来底本还是翻译方法。

They do not establish Solomon’s authorship, final compilation, one identical 31-chapter text across all groups, or every proverb as an immediate promise without exception.<br>
因此，它们不能证明所罗门作者、全书最后编集年代、当时所有群体已有完全相同的 31 章文本，或《箴言》每句话都是没有例外的即时应许。

Related evidence anchor: **170**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**170**。

<a id="wisdom-4-hebel-is-not-simply-meaninglessness"></a>
### 4. Hebel Is Not Simply Meaninglessness | 4. “虚空”不只等于“毫无意义”

Ecclesiastes repeatedly uses Hebrew hebel, literally evoking breath, mist, or vapor. Different contexts emphasize transience, elusiveness, unreliable outcomes, opacity, or frustration. Using meaninglessness everywhere makes the book more nihilistic than its text.<br>
传道书反复使用希伯来词 *hebel*，字面图像是气息、雾气或蒸汽。不同上下文可强调短暂、难以抓住、结果不可靠、认识不透明或令人挫折；用一个“毫无意义”包办所有出现，会把本书读成比经文更彻底的虚无主义。

Its tension is not that death makes every action unnecessary, but that:<br>
本书的张力不是“既然会死，什么都不必做”，而是：

1. Human work cannot produce permanent gain under one’s own control.
   人的劳碌不能产生永久、可由自己掌握的“益处”；
2. Wisdom retains value over folly without eliminating death or chance.
   智慧相对愚昧仍有真实价值，却不能取消死亡和偶然；
3. Eating, drinking, working, and sharing days with loved ones can be received as divine gifts in an uncontrollable world.
   在无法控制的世界里，吃喝、工作与所爱之人共享日子，可以被领受为神的礼物；
4. The ending keeps reverence for God and judgment on the human horizon.
   结尾仍把敬畏神与审判放在人的生活地平线上。

The enjoyment of Ecclesiastes 9:7–10 is not a slogan for unethical indulgence, but receiving everyday gifts and acting faithfully within mortality’s limits.<br>
因此，传道书 9:7–10 的“享受”不是逃避伦理的纵欲口号，而是在死亡限制下领受日常恩赐并忠实行动。

<a id="wisdom-5-4q109-4q110-ecclesiastes-circulated-by-their-copying-dates"></a>
### 5. 4Q109/4Q110: Ecclesiastes Circulated by Their Copying Dates | 5. 4Q109／4Q110《传道书》残卷：给出流传下限

Cave 4 preserves two Hebrew Ecclesiastes manuscripts:<br>
昆兰第 4 洞保存两份希伯来文《传道书》残卷：

- **4Q109/4QQohᵃ** contains portions of chapters 5–7 and is commonly paleographically dated around 175–150 BCE.
  **4Q109（4QQohᵃ）** 保存第 5–7 章的若干段落，古文字学常定为约主前 175–150 年；
- **4Q110/4QQohᵇ** has a very small chapter 1 fragment, usually placed in the later Herodian period. Its tiny survival does not justify overly precise paleographic dating.
  **4Q110（4QQohᵇ）** 只保存第 1 章的很小片段，通常置于较晚的希律时期；残存太少，具体古文字日期不宜说得过窄。

4Q109 is among the earliest surviving Ecclesiastes witnesses, showing formation and copying by the first half of the second century BCE. It limits how late composition could be, not how much earlier it was, and alone neither proves nor disproves Solomonic authorship. Its variants and 4Q110’s fragmentary state justify neither zero-variant claims nor denial of textual stability.<br>
4Q109 是现存最早的《传道书》见证之一，说明本书最迟在主前二世纪前半已经成形并被抄写。它为成书日期提供一个“不会晚于此抄本”的上限，却不能告诉我们作品究竟早了多少年，也不能只凭抄本否定或证明所罗门作者说。4Q109 与马所拉本有细部差异，4Q110 又极残缺；两者不能支持“每个字毫无异文”或反过来声称当时没有稳定文本。

Related evidence anchor: **109**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**109**。

<a id="wisdom-6-siduri-and-ecclesiastes-9-7-9-real-parallels-real-differences"></a>
### 6. Siduri and Ecclesiastes 9:7–9: Real Parallels, Real Differences | 6. 西杜丽的劝告与传道书 9:7–9：真正平行，也真正不同

The Old Babylonian Gilgamesh Meissner–Millard tablet, OB VA+BM, joins Berlin VAT 4105 and British Museum BM 96974. Dealers claimed a Sippar origin, without controlled modern excavation. Siduri’s advice to the immortality-seeking Gilgamesh combines mortality, enjoyment of food and drink, clean clothes, children, and a partner’s embrace. Ecclesiastes 9:7–9 similarly groups food, wine, white clothing, and a beloved companion.<br>
古巴比伦《吉尔伽美什》“梅斯纳—米拉德泥版”（学术缩写 OB VA+BM）由柏林 VAT 4105 与大英博物馆 BM 96974 两片直接拼合，来源商称来自锡帕，但没有现代控制发掘语境。泥版中的酒馆女神西杜丽告诉寻找永生的吉尔伽美什：人免不了死亡，所以应吃喝欢喜、保持衣服洁净，并在孩子和伴侣的拥抱中得乐。它与传道书 9:7–9 的食物、饮酒、洁白衣服和所爱伴侣构成醒目的意象组合。

Keep three differences in view:<br>
比较时必须保留三层差别：

- Siduri’s advice survives only in Old Babylonian fragments; the standard Babylonian version rewrites the passage. Do not merge every Gilgamesh version into one text.
  西杜丽的话只见于古巴比伦残片，后来的标准巴比伦版已重新编写该段，不能把所有《吉尔伽美什》版本混成一个文本；
- Both face mortality but belong to different theological and literary structures. Ecclesiastes regards everyday enjoyment as a divinely approved share while retaining work, reverence, and judgment.
  两部作品都面对死亡，却属于不同神学与文学结构；传道书把日常享受称为神认可、赐给人的分，并继续谈工作、敬畏与审判；
- Shared wisdom themes, literary contact, or a more specific relationship may explain parallels. These lines alone do not prove direct translation, a transmission route, or Ecclesiastes’s date.
  相似可以来自共同智慧母题、文学接触或更具体的传统关系，但不能仅凭这几行证明传道书直接翻译泥版、确定传播路线或给本书定年。

Related evidence anchor: **110**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**110**。

<a id="wisdom-7-first-hear-song-of-songs-as-love-poetry"></a>
### 7. First Hear Song of Songs as Love Poetry | 7. 《雅歌》首先应当作为爱情诗被听见

Male and female voices alternate in Song of Songs, with women’s voices especially prominent. Bodies, fragrance, gardens, fields, cities, searching, and mutual attention form its poetic world. Its safest first reading recognizes human love and embodiment rather than immediately turning each detail into a hidden code.<br>
《雅歌》让男女声音轮流出现，女性声音尤其突出；身体、香气、园子、田野、城市、寻找和互相凝视构成它的诗歌世界。最稳妥的第一层阅读是承认它在歌咏人类爱情与身体，而不是把每个部位、香料或地名立即改写成隐藏密码。

This does not prevent canonical or communal readings connecting marriage with God and Israel or [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) and [church](../Glossary/README.md#church). A useful order is:<br>
这并不禁止正典或信仰群体进一步把婚约语言与神—以色列、基督—[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)相连。较好的顺序是：

1. Identify speakers, settings, repetition, and poetic metaphor first.
   先辨认说话者、场景、重复句和诗歌隐喻；
2. Then consider love’s timing, strength compared with death, and resistance to overwhelming waters or purchase. For the Chinese phrases retained below, read Song of Songs 2:7 and 8:6–7 in an authorized translation; this sentence explains their themes rather than supplying a new [Bible](../Glossary/README.md#bible) translation.
   再读“不要惊动爱情”“爱情如死之坚强”“众水不能熄灭”等关于时机、力量和不可购买性的智慧；
3. Finally discuss Jewish and Christian reception without erasing human love in theological rereading.
   最后讨论犹太与基督教[接收史](../Glossary/README.md#reception-history)怎样在不抹去人类之爱的前提下作神学重读。

Some ancient Near Eastern love poetry concerns deities, kingship, or ritual; other poems clearly depict human lovers. Similar imagery alone cannot classify the whole Song as an Inanna–Dumuzi sacred-marriage ritual script.<br>
古代近东的爱情诗有时来自神祇、王权或祭仪世界，有时明显描写人类恋人。仅凭意象相似，不能把《雅歌》整体定为伊南娜—杜穆兹式“神婚礼仪剧本”。

<a id="wisdom-8-4q106-4q107-4q108-and-6q6-second-temple-song-of-songs"></a>
### 8. 4Q106/4Q107/4Q108 and 6Q6: Second Temple Song of Songs | 8. 4Q106、4Q107、4Q108 与 6Q6：第二圣殿时期的《雅歌》抄传

Four Hebrew Song of Songs manuscripts come from Qumran: 4Q106, 4Q107, 4Q108, and 6Q6. They directly attest late-Second-Temple copying and circulation. Length, spacing, and variants prompt debate over complete-book fragments, selections, or editorial stages; scholarship has not settled this in one sentence.<br>
昆兰洞穴发现四份希伯来文《雅歌》残卷：第 4 洞的 4Q106、4Q107、4Q108，以及第 6 洞的 6Q6。它们共同直接见证《雅歌》在第二圣殿晚期被抄写和流传。部分残卷的篇幅、空白和异文又引发讨论：它们是完整书卷的残片、节选文本，还是反映不同编集阶段？学界并未用一句话解决。

They establish contemporary textual witnesses to Song of Songs, but their Qumran location does not independently establish:<br>
因此，这四份抄本能证明“当时已有《雅歌》文本见证”，却不能由“在昆兰发现”直接推出：

- How the Qumran community interpreted the love content.
  昆兰群体如何解释爱情内容；
- An identical book form across all Jewish groups.
  所有犹太群体已使用完全相同的书卷形态；
- Archaeological proof of Solomonic authorship.
  所罗门作者身份已经得到考古证明；
- That missing text must represent editing rather than damage, selection, or another textual form.
  某处缺文一定表示删改，而不是残损、节选或另一种文本形态。

Related evidence anchor: **111**.<br>
对应旁证锚点：**111**。

<a id="wisdom-9-harris-500-egyptian-love-poetry-as-real-comparative-context"></a>
### 9. Harris 500: Egyptian Love Poetry as Real Comparative Context | 9. Harris 500 纸草：埃及爱情诗的真实比较语境

British Museum EA 10060, Papyrus Harris 500, is a hieratic manuscript. Its recto has four love-song groups and the Harpist’s Song from Intef’s tomb; its verso has stories including the Capture of Joppa and the Doomed Prince. The museum catalogue places the papyrus in the Eighteenth Dynasty. Keep the copy’s date distinct from the poems’ formation and Song of Songs’ composition.<br>
大英博物馆 EA 10060（Papyrus Harris 500）是一份僧侣体纸草。其正面保存四组爱情歌与《安特夫墓竖琴师之歌》，背面保存《夺取约帕》和《注定的王子》等故事；大英博物馆目录把纸草列在埃及第十八王朝。纸草抄写年代、其中诗歌的形成年代和《雅歌》的形成年代仍须分开。

Egyptian love songs often alternate lovers’ voices, call them brother and sister, and express desire through bodies, scents, gardens, rivers, and nature. They place Song of Songs within a real ancient Near Eastern love-song genre rather than an isolated modern invention.<br>
埃及爱情歌常以恋人交替发声，使用“兄弟／姊妹”作爱称，并以身体、香气、花园、河流和自然景物描写渴望。这些材料说明《雅歌》属于古代近东真实存在的爱情诗世界，而不是孤立、现代才出现的文类。

Shared genre and imagery do not automatically mean direct copying:<br>
但“共享文类与意象”不自动等于“直接抄袭”：

- Harris 500 names neither Solomon, Israel, nor the LORD.
  Harris 500 没有提所罗门、以色列或耶和华；
- Body-and-nature metaphors span Egyptian and Levantine traditions; a specific transmission chain is difficult to reconstruct.
  相似的身体—自然[比喻](../Glossary/README.md#parable)可跨越多份埃及和黎凡特传统，具体传播链难以重建；
- Human love poetry is not the same as divine-marriage ritual. Egyptian parallels alone cannot classify Song as secular, ritual, or a record of a wedding performance.
  人类恋人的诗歌不等于神婚祭仪，《雅歌》也不能仅凭埃及平行被定成世俗、礼仪或婚礼现场记录。

## Placing the Five Books within the Bible’s Story | 五、怎样把五卷智慧与诗歌书放回整本圣经

| Story question / 故事线问题 | Job and Psalms / 《约伯记》与《诗篇》 | Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song / 《箴言》《传道书》《雅歌》 | Toward the New Testament / 进入新约 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is the Creator trustworthy? | Suffering does not remove divine rule; creation becomes praise | Learnable order with uncontrollable timing and limits | All things created and sustained through Christ |
| 创造主是否可信 | 苦难未使神失去受造界主权；[创造](../Glossary/README.md#creation)成为敬拜语言 | 世界有可学习的秩序，也有不可掌控的时机与边界 | 万有借基督受造并在祂里面维系 |

| 义人为何受苦 | 拒绝“受苦必等于个人隐罪”；哀歌让人向神呼求 | 原则不等于即时保证；死亡与偶然暴露人的有限 | 耶稣走过义人受苦，十字架颠覆世俗智慧 |
| How should people live? | Honest argument, praise, confession, and hope belong within relationship with God | Reverence, teachability, faithful work, present gifts, and respect for love | Christ as God’s wisdom; practical life bears fruit in the Spirit |
| 人如何生活 | 诚实争辩、赞美、认罪和盼望都进入与神的关系 | 敬畏、受教、忠实工作、领受当下与尊重爱情同时成立 | 基督成为神的智慧，[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)在[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)里结出实际生活 |

| 身体与爱有何位置 | 身体会受苦，也能以气息和声音赞美创造主 | 爱与身体属于受造的善，却有时机、边界和不可购买性 | 婚姻与教会获得正典重读，身体等候[复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection) |
| Kingship and [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation) | Davidic covenant, anointed one, rejection, and victory shape the collection | Solomonic wisdom and kingship reach heights without conquering death | The New Testament proclaims a greater-than-Solomon Christ and interprets him through Psalms |
| 王权与拯救 | 大卫之约、受膏者、被弃与得胜成为诗集主轴 | 所罗门传统显示智慧与王权的高峰，也暴露王无法胜过死亡 | 新约宣告比所罗门更大的基督，并以诗篇解释祂 |

Christians may discern how these books point to Christ within the canon without making every detail a one-to-one code. First hear the texts within Israel and their ancient world; then examine actual New Testament quotation and rereading.<br>
基督徒可以在整本正典中看见这些书怎样指向基督，却不必把每个细节都变成一对一密码。先听见文本在以色列和古代世界中的声音，再观察新约作者实际怎样引用和重读。

Melito’s and Origen’s catalogues, anchors 204–205, supply later CAT named-list reception evidence. Both explicitly list Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs, and Job. Grouping the books does not give them one author, date, genre, or interpretation, and cannot replace Qumran manuscripts, commentaries, versions, or literary use. The linked ancient-catalogue study explains 22/24/39-book counts and the 52-book course.<br>
梅利托、俄利根旧约书目（锚点 204–205）又提供一个较晚的 CAT（书目列名）时钟：两份都明确列《诗篇》《箴言》《传道书》《雅歌》《约伯记》，但“被列入同一组”不等于五卷具有相同作者、年代、文体或解释方式。它也不能替代昆兰经卷、注释、古代译本和实际文学使用。22／24／39 卷的合并算法与 52 卷课程关系，见[古代旧约书目专题](./古代旧约书目_22卷24卷39卷与52卷路线桥接.md)。

<a id="wisdom-five-reading-notes"></a>
## Five Reading Notes | 七、研读时可用的五条注释

1. **Do not answer on sufferers’ behalf too quickly.** Job permits lament, protest, and silence. Even correct doctrine can become the friends’ error when used to condemn wounded people.
   **不要替受苦者抢答。** 《约伯记》允许哀哭、抗议和沉默；正确教义若被用来给伤者定罪，也可能成为朋友式的错误。
2. **Do not read poetry as a news release.** Hyperbole, parallelism, metaphor, and ancient imagery are ways of communicating truth, not automatically factual errors.
   **不要把诗歌读成新闻稿。** 夸张、平行、隐喻和古代图像不是事实错误，而是诗歌传达真实的方式。
3. **Ask how parallels differ first.** Their value is not just shared culture, but how biblical texts reframe creation, divine rule, human dignity, and hope.
   **相似材料先问“怎样不同”。** 平行文本最有价值的地方，不只是证明古代文化相通，也在于显出圣经怎样重置创造、神权、人类尊严和盼望。
4. **Do not make principles into control formulas.** Proverbs identifies normal order; Job and Ecclesiastes remind us of suffering, chance, time, and death.
   **不要把原则变成控制公式。** 箴言教人辨认通常秩序，约伯记和传道书提醒人仍活在苦难、偶然、时间和死亡之下。
5. **Do not erase love by allegory or close the canon by love poetry alone.** Hear the lovers’ bodies and voices first; Jewish and Christian theological rereading should build on that meaning rather than replace it.
   **不要用寓意抹去爱情，也不要用爱情封死正典。** 《雅歌》首先让人听见恋人的身体与声音；犹太和基督教的神学重读应建立在这层意义之上，而不是取代它。
