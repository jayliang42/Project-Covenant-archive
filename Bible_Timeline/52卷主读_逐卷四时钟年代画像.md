## Reading the Four Clocks | 一、怎样读“四只时钟”

| Clock / 时钟 | Question / 回答什么 | Appropriate conclusion / 正确结论句式 | Do not substitute / 不能偷换成什么 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story | Era of people/events in book | Narrative proceeds from one setting to another | Author wrote at event time |
| 故事时钟 | 卷内人物和事件被放在哪个时代 | “本卷叙事从……推进到……” | 作者就在故事发生时写成 |
| Composition/compilation | Formation of work, materials, present form | Traditional attribution and current models distinguished | One model archaeologically certified |
| 写作／编集时钟 | 作品、材料或现有书卷形态何时形成 | “传统归属为……；现有研究仍有……模型” | 某个模型已经被考古盖章 |
| Physical text | Production of currently verified copies, translations, inscriptions | Text/tradition circulated by this date | Autograph or narrated events proved |
| 文本实物时钟 | 项目目前已核实的抄本、译本或经文刻文何时制作 | “至迟在此时，这段文本／传统已经流传” | 这是原稿，或卷内事件已获证明 |
| Reception | Later quotation, combination, translation, liturgy, catalogue | A community read/practiced this by then | Original author shared reading or all early places identical |
| 接收时钟 | 后人何时引用、组合解释、翻译、礼仪使用或编目 | “到此时，已有群体这样阅读／实践” | 原作者必然采用同一解释，或早期各地都相同 |

Four labels:<br>
四个标签：

- **Representative witness:** an appropriately verified, numbered item in the original research set, not necessarily the absolutely earliest witness worldwide.
  **代表实物：** 当前项目已编号并核实、适合承担这一卷文本时钟的材料；不保证它是全球所有已知见证中绝对最早的一件。
- **Partial tradition:** inscription, anthology, or another book’s quotation containing limited language, not this book’s scroll.
  **片段传统：** 刻文、选集或别卷引用只接触本卷少量语言，不是本卷卷轴。
- **Reception witness:** commentary, anthology, adaptation preserves Scripture while primarily attesting later interpretation/use, not an ordinary text copy.
  **接收实物：** 注释、选集或改写作品保存本卷经文，却首先见证后来的解释与使用；不能冒充普通经文副本。
- **Evidence pending:** explicitly records a project gap when no numbered anchor supports a direct MS clock. This is not ancient absence. Following batch 67, none of the 52 currently carries this label.
  **待补证：** 若当前编号锚点尚无适合承担某卷直接 MS 时钟的材料，就用此标签显式登记；缺口只表示项目待补资料，不表示古代没有该卷抄本。第 67 批补证后，52 卷目前已无此标签。

<a id="four-clocks-sixteen-conclusions-to-retain-after-completing-the-52-profiles"></a>
## Sixteen Conclusions to Retain after Completing the 52 Profiles | 三、52 卷完成后必须保留的十六个结论

1. **Clear narrative order does not mean equally clear absolute chronology or final formation.** Pentateuch, land, kingdom, prophets, exile, return form a stable textual handoff; authors, source layers, final compilation need individual study.
   **故事顺序清楚，不表示绝对年代和最后形成也同样清楚。** 从五经、土地、王国到先知、被掳与归回，可以按文本建立稳定接力；作者、资料层次和最后编集仍须逐卷讨论。
2. **A story’s setting is not its composition date.** Ruth’s Judges setting, Jonah’s Jeroboam II connection, Daniel’s sixth-century courts each leave an independent formation question.
   **“故事设在某时代”不能直接变成“作品写于该时代”。** 《路得记》明说士师时代，《约拿书》把人物接到耶罗波安二世，《但以理书》把宫廷故事置于主前六世纪；三卷都仍有独立的文学形成问题。
3. **A surviving copy establishes existence by its date, not an author’s signature.** 4Q109 shows copyable Ecclesiastes by the earlier second century BCE; 4Q76 shows later Malachi material by that century, neither initial composition dates.
   **现存副本给作品已存在的下限，不给原稿落款。** 4Q109 能说明《传道书》最迟主前二世纪前半已有可抄文本；4Q76 能说明《玛拉基书》后半至迟主前二世纪已流传；都不能把副本年代当初写年。
4. **Strong event anchors are distinct from composition.** Second Kings, Isaiah, Jeremiah relate to 701, 597, 587/586 BCE without acquiring single composition years.
   **强事件锚点与成书问题彼此正交。** 《列王纪下》《以赛亚书》《耶利米书》可以接到 701、597、587／586 等固定点，却不会因此各自得到一个单一写作年。
5. **Twelve scrolls attest both text and collection form.** 4Q76, 4Q78, 4Q80–82, Mur88 show several prophets copied together, not autographs or every word of all twelve surviving in each item.
   **《十二先知》卷既是直接文本实物，也是合集形态证据。** 4Q76、4Q78、4Q80–82 与 Mur88 可以证明多卷小先知已经相邻抄传；它们不是十二位先知的原稿，也不保证每件残卷实际保存十二卷全部文字。
6. **Commentary is not an ordinary Scripture copy.** 1QpHab preserves/interprets Habakkuk 1–2 as reception; 8ḤevXIIgr/Mur88 supply ordinary witnesses spanning 1–3. Neither type is a complete autograph.
   **注释卷不能冒充普通经文副本。** 1QpHab 逐段保存并解释《哈巴谷书》1–2 章，因而首先属于接收史；8ḤevXIIgr／Mur88 才为本表补上涵盖 1–3 章的普通经文卷见证。两类实物都不是完整原稿。
7. **Reception does not mechanically reproduce original meaning.** Compare contexts, text forms, interpretive actions in 1QpHab/Paul/Hebrews on Habakkuk 2:4, Acts 2 on Joel 2, Matthew 2 on Micah 5; identical phrases do not guarantee identical meanings.
   **接收不是原意的机械复印。** 1QpHab、罗马书／加拉太书／希伯来书对哈 2:4 的使用，徒 2 对珥 2 的解释，以及太 2 对弥 5 的重读，都需要比较语境、文本形态和解释动作，不能只对齐一句话就宣布含义完全相同。
8. **Physical-text coverage is not closure of every evidence question.** Anchors 193–197 give all 52 ordinary MS entries, but 4Q7, 4Q22, 4Q27, 8ḤevXIIgr/Mur88, Sinaiticus postdate settings by centuries or more. They attest transmission, not persons/events, author, final date.
   **文本实物覆盖闭合，不表示证据问题闭合。** 锚点 193–197 使 52 卷目前都拥有普通经文 MS 入口；但 4Q7、4Q22、4Q27、8ḤevXIIgr／Mur88 和《西奈抄本》仍分别晚于卷内时代数百年到更久，只给抄传下限，不给人物事件、作者或定稿日期签字。
9. **Gospel events and Gospel formation use different clocks.** Herod, high priests, Pilate, 30/33 CE candidates concern narrative history; 60–90 CE and later formation schemes concern works. Neither clock silently certifies the other.
   **福音事件时钟与福音书形成时钟必须分开。** 希律、大祭司、彼拉多及主后 30／33 年受难候选属于叙事历史坐标；主后 60–90 年以上的形成方案回答作品何时成形。任何一只时钟都不能偷偷替另一只签字。
10. **A narrative endpoint is not automatically composition day.** Acts ends at Roman house arrest around 60–62; open ending may contribute to argument, not exclude 60s, 80–90, or later schemes alone.
    **叙事停笔处不是自动成书日。** 《使徒行传》在保罗约主后 60–62 年罗马软禁处结束，只能固定故事终点；开放结尾可能参与日期论证，却不能单独排除 60 年代、80–90 年或更晚的形成方案。
11. **Pauline letters can fit journeys while later collections remain another physical layer.** Coworkers, places, Gallio relatively locate Thessalonians, Corinthians, Romans; P46 around 200 attests collected circulation, not original dispatched letters.
    **保罗书信可贴近旅程链，后期文集仍是另一层实物。** 《帖撒罗尼迦前书》《哥林多前后书》《罗马书》等可由同工、地点与迦流锚点相对定位；约主后 200 年前后的 P46 证明多封书信已经合册流传，不是各信初写时的邮递原件。
12. **Creeds and patristic material are reception, not event-scene testimony.** Early resurrection formula, Justin’s counterstory, Ignatius’ bodily language, later creeds show transmission/clarification/controversy. Assess independence and named citation individually, not as undifferentiated contemporary corroboration.
    **信经与教父材料属于接收史，不是事件现场证词。** 早期死亡—复活公式、游斯丁的盗尸反叙事、伊格那丢的身体复活语言和后期信经可显示信仰怎样被传递、澄清与争辩；必须逐件判断是否独立、是否具名引用，不能把它们统称为同期外证。
13. **Pastoral narrative setting and author model must remain distinct.** Post-Acts travels, second Roman imprisonment, deathbed frame belong to traditional reconstruction; successor schemes around 80–100 concern formation. P133, Sinaiticus, second-century reception set later bounds, not a sole verdict.
    **教牧书信的故事场景与作者方案不能互相偷换。** 徒 28 后再旅行、第二次罗马监禁和临终遗言构成传统叙事重建；主后 80–100 年的后继者方案回答作品形成。P133、西奈抄本和二世纪接收只能提供后续下限与上限，不能单独裁决。
14. **Survival gaps do not prove ancient absence.** Missing P46 leaves prevent claims of original pastoral exclusion. Earliest known complete 2 Timothy in Sinaiticus does not mean fourth-century origin.
    **幸存材料的缺口不是古代不存在的证明。** P46 末叶缺失，不能据现存没有教牧书信断言原册排除它们；西奈抄本是目前所知最早完整《提摩太后书》见证，也不表示本卷到四世纪才产生。
15. **Versions, liturgy, commentary, catalogues are different reception actions.** Peshitta omission of 2 Peter/Revelation can be followed by inclusion; no lection selection need not mean formal rejection. Ask when, where, and in which material, not one global canon switch.
    **版本、礼仪、注释与书目是四种不同接收动作。** 别西大缺《彼得后书》《启示录》，后续译修可以补入；某读经循环没有选某卷，也不等于正式拒绝。问“何时、何地、在哪种材料中被使用”比压成一次全球正典开关更准确。
16. **City archaeology restores readers’ world, not visionary fulfillment.** Patmos and seven cities are real; later churches, water, guilds, imperial cult illuminate imagery without authenticating first-century churches, a unique beast-number person, or end date.
    **城市考古能恢复原读者世界，不能挖出异象应验。** 拔摩、以弗所、士每拿、别迦摩、推雅推喇、撒狄、非拉铁非与老底嘉都是真实地点；晚期教堂、供水、行会和帝国崇拜可照明图像，却不能认证七个一世纪教堂、兽数唯一人物或末世日期。
