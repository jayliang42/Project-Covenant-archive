## Historical Coordinates for Twelve Stages | 二、十二段历史坐标层

<a id="combined-guide-first-distinguish-four-clocks"></a>
### First Distinguish Four Clocks | 先分清四只时钟

1. **Story:** era assigned to characters/events; Daniel moves from Babylon to Persia.
   **故事时钟：** 经文把人物和事件放在哪个时代。例如《但以理书》的故事从巴比伦转到波斯。
2. **Composition/compilation:** formation of current literary work, possibly near story setting or after extended transmission; retain disputed ranges.
   **写作／编集时钟：** 书卷何时形成现有文学形态。它可能与故事时代接近，也可能经过较长传承；有争议就保留范围。
3. **Surviving copy:** when a discovered copy was made. Earliest survival attests existence by then, not initial composition.
   **幸存[抄本](../Glossary/README.md#manuscript)时钟：** 目前实际发现的副本何时制作。最早幸存抄本只给“至迟已有这段文本”的下限，不等于原作年份。
4. **Reception:** later quotation, translation, reading, commentary, cataloguing. Later use is not a contemporary event archive.
   **接收时钟：** 后来的群体何时引用、翻译、诵读、注释或编目这卷书。它能证明后期使用，不能倒作原事件的同期档案。

Fixed coordinates below are representative, not full topic chronologies. A Second Temple transition bridges Malachi’s world and the Gospels without adding a Protestant [Old Testament](../Glossary/README.md#old-testament) core book. Smaller BCE numbers are later; 1 BCE is followed by 1 CE with no year zero.<br>
下表的“可固定坐标”只列代表点，不替代各专题的完整年表。十二段之间另列“第二圣殿世界”转场，因为它没有新增一卷新教旧约主读书，却是从《玛拉基书》世界走到四福音不可省略的历史桥。主前／BCE 年份数字越小越晚；主前 1 年之后直接进入主后 1 年，没有第 0 年。

<a id="combined-guide-three-chronology-corrections"></a>
### Three Chronology Corrections | 三个读年表的校正

- **Fixed event does not date whole book:** the 597 BCE Babylonian chronicle fixes one siege, not Jeremiah’s final completion.
  **固定事件不等于整卷定年：** 主前 597 年的巴比伦编年史可固定一次攻城，却不能仅凭这一点决定《耶利米书》全卷何时完成。
- **Earliest copy is not original:** a second-century BCE fragment attests text then, not author/story originating then.
  **最早副本不等于原作：** 一份主前二世纪残卷能证明当时已有该段文本，不能把作者和故事都改写成主前二世纪。
- **Contextual attestation does not date the event:** Israel in Canaan around 1208 BCE does not calculate an exodus year.
  **背景下限不等于事件日期：** 梅尔内普塔碑证明约主前 1208 年迦南已有“以色列”，不能倒算出埃及发生在哪一年。

<a id="combined-guide-four-essential-interpretive-checkpoints"></a>
## Four Essential Interpretive Checkpoints | 四、四个不能跳过的解释关口

<a id="combined-guide-1-wisdom-is-integral-to-the-timeline"></a>
### 1. Wisdom Is Integral to the Timeline | 1. 智慧书不是时间线的岔路

Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Job deliberately interrupt Kings: even with law, king, temple, stable society, how can people remain faithful in daily choices, mortality, unresolved suffering? They do not add a national period; they train people to inhabit the whole story.<br>
《箴言》《传道书》《约伯记》被放在《列王纪》之间，是有意让读者停下来追问：即使有律法、王、圣殿和看似稳定的社会，人究竟怎样在日常选择、死亡限制和无解苦难中忠心？它们不新增一段国家年代，却训练能活在整条故事中的人。

<a id="combined-guide-2-return-is-not-the-complete-ending"></a>
### 2. Return Is Not the Complete Ending | 2. 归回不是故事的圆满结局

Temple, walls, communal order genuinely advance. Yet Ezra, Nehemiah, Malachi end with recurring failures of heart, worship, justice. Persian homecoming cannot exhaust prophetic promise: new covenant, heart, Spirit, king, divine dwelling remain awaited.<br>
圣殿、城墙和群体制度的恢复都是真实进展；《以斯拉记》《尼希米记》《玛拉基书》的结尾却显示人心、敬拜和公义仍会反复。因此归回必须继续等待新约、新心、圣灵、君王和神亲自同住，不能把波斯时期返乡直接等同于先知全部应许已经完成。

<a id="combined-guide-3-letters-are-integral-to-the-gospel-story"></a>
### 3. Letters Are Integral to the Gospel Story | 3. 书信不是福音故事之后的附录

Gospels proclaim Jesus’ identity/action; Acts and letters test whether good news forms a multiethnic community addressing money, sexuality, authority, suffering, division, hope. Skipping letters hides how the Spirit makes [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation) communal life.<br>
四福音宣告耶稣是谁、做了什么；《使徒行传》和书信则检验这好消息能否形成一个跨民族、处理金钱、性、权柄、受苦、分裂与盼望的真实群体。若只读福音事件而跳过书信，故事会停在“耶稣完成救恩”，却看不见圣灵怎样把救恩塑造成教会生活。

<a id="combined-guide-4-revelation-continues-the-story-rather-than-a-separate-code"></a>
### 4. Revelation Continues the Story Rather Than a Separate Code | 4. 《启示录》不是另开一条末世密码线

Revelation regathers creation, exodus, Lamb, temple, Babylon, life river/tree. Its goal matters because divine dwelling, death’s end, nations’ healing answer Genesis’ losses, beyond merely predicting future events.<br>
最后一卷重新收回创世、出埃及、羔羊、圣殿、巴比伦、生命河和生命树。它的终点之所以重要，不只是“将来发生什么”，而是因为神与人同住、死亡消失、列国得医治，逐项回答了《创世记》以来的失落。

<a id="combined-guide-evidence-gates-for-reading"></a>
## Evidence Gates for Reading | 五、使用时的证据闸门

1. **EV:** specify the person, regime, event actually fixed; local correspondence is not proof of every detail.
   **看见 EV：** 先说清它具体固定了哪个人物、政权或事件坐标；不要把局部相合扩成全卷每个细节都获外证。
2. **BG:** say the setting becomes clearer, not that the story is proved.
   **看见 BG：** 结论写成“这使经文处境更可理解”；不要改写成“这证明故事已经发生”。
3. **MS:** say the text circulated by a time/place; copying, composition, events have different dates.
   **看见 MS：** 结论写成“这段文本至迟在某时某地已经流传”；抄本年代不自动等于写作年代，更不等于卷内事件年代。
4. **RX:** later reading, controversy, worship, catalogue; not original-event contemporary archive.
   **看见 RX：** 它回答后人怎样阅读、争论、敬拜或编目；后期接收不能倒作原事件的同期档案。
5. **Every book has UT:** historical study remains possible while objects alone cannot prove miracle, revelation, atonement, resurrection, theological prophecy fulfillment.
   **每卷都有 UT：** 这不是说历史不能研究，而是承认器物不能独自证明神迹、启示、赎罪、复活及预言的神学实现。
