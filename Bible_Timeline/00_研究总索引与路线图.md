## Scope and Reading Models | 范围与阅读模型

The research base follows the common Protestant arrangement of **66 books**: 39 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament. The project offers two course-shaped derivatives without changing that base.<br>
研究底稿按新教常用的 **66 卷**结构编排：旧约 39 卷，新约 27 卷。项目在这份完整底稿之上提供两种课程化路线，但不改变底稿范围。

| Model<br>模型 | Structure<br>结构 | Best use<br>最适合的用途 | Completeness rule<br>完整性规则 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **66-book research base**<br>**66 卷研究底稿** | 39 Old Testament books plus 27 New Testament books<br>旧约 39 卷＋新约 27 卷 | Book-by-book study, research, and long-term reference<br>逐卷研读、专题研究与长期查考 | This is the complete base from which both course routes are derived.<br>这是两种课程路线共同派生的完整底稿。 |
| **52-week course**<br>**52 周课程** | Fifty-two weekly study units cover all 66 books.<br>52 个每周研读单元覆盖全部 66 卷。 | A one-year chronological and thematic reading plan<br>一年制的时间线与主题式阅读计划 | “52” counts weeks, not books; no biblical book is removed.<br>这里的“52”指周次，不指书卷；没有删去任何书卷。 |
| **52-book core plus 14-book bridge**<br>**52 卷主读＋14 卷桥接** | Fifty-two core books and fourteen bridge books are disjoint, and together they equal all 66 books.<br>52 卷主读与 14 卷桥接彼此不重叠，合并后正好覆盖全部 66 卷。 | A compressed core-reading route that still shows where every other book joins the story<br>压缩主读量，同时明确其余每卷怎样插入故事线 | It is a course design, not an alternative canon; the fourteen bridge books remain visible and linked.<br>这是课程设计，不是另立正典；14 卷桥接书仍明确保留并链接。 |

Ancient Jewish and Christian sources may count or combine Old Testament books as 22, 24, or 39. Those numbers describe different grouping conventions and reception histories; they do not authorize silently deleting books from this project’s 66-book base.<br>
古代犹太与基督教资料可能把旧约计为 22、24 或 39 卷。这些数字反映不同的合卷方式与接收史，不表示本项目可以从 66 卷底稿中静默删书。

<a id="research-framework"></a>

## Six-Layer Research Framework | 六层研究框架

Every historical stage and long-form module should answer the same six questions. Keeping the layers separate prevents a manuscript, inscription, ruin, or later interpretation from being asked to prove more than it can.<br>
每个历史阶段与长篇专题都应回答同样的六个问题。把这些层次分开，才能避免要求抄本、铭文、遗址或后期解释证明它们本来无法证明的事。

1. **Biblical narrative | 经文叙事:** What does the biblical text say happened? / 圣经文本说发生了什么？
2. **Book placement | 书卷位置:** Which books narrate this stage, and which later books look back on it? / 哪些书卷叙述这段历史，哪些后来的书卷回望这段历史？
3. **Historical coordinates | 历史坐标:** What approximate dates, empires, rulers, geography, and social settings are relevant? / 相关的大致年代、帝国、君王、地理与社会背景是什么？
4. **External corroboration | 外部旁证:** What contemporary or later inscriptions, texts, sites, objects, or manuscripts can establish? / 同期或后期的铭文、文献、遗址、器物与抄本能够建立什么？
5. **Evidence boundary | 证据边界:** What can the material not establish, and where are dating or interpretation disputed? / 材料不能建立什么，哪些年代或释读仍有争议？
6. **Theological interpretation | 神学解读:** How does the passage advance creation, fall, covenant, kingdom, presence, redemption, Messiah, and new creation? / 这段怎样推进创造、堕落、圣约、国度、同在、救赎、弥赛亚与新创造？

## Evidence Anchors, Types, and Grades | 旁证锚点、类型与等级

The current public register contains **205 numbered evidence anchors**. An anchor is a stable research record, not a claim that archaeology has proved a whole biblical passage. One anchor may map to several weeks or books, and anchors 204–205 are ancient book-list reception evidence rather than archaeological event evidence.<br>
当前公开总表包含 **205 个编号旁证锚点**。锚点是稳定的研究记录，不表示考古已经证明整段经文。同一锚点可以映射到多个周次或书卷；第 204–205 项属于古代书目接收史，并非考古事件证据。

Evidence **type** and evidence **strength** are separate axes. Type tells you what question a source can answer; the A–D grade evaluates the strength of a specific claim made from that source.<br>
证据**类型**与证据**强度**是两条不同的轴。类型说明资料能够回答哪类问题；A–D 等级评估的是根据该资料提出的某一项具体判断有多强。

- **EV — event/person | 事件／人物:** External material directly touches a person, political action, war, exile, execution, or another historical coordinate. / 外部材料直接触及人物、政权行动、战争、流亡、处决或其他历史坐标。
- **BG — background | 制度／环境:** Cities, law, labor, administration, worship, or comparative literature make the setting more intelligible. / 城市、法律、劳动、行政、敬拜或比较文学帮助理解处境。
- **MS — manuscript transmission | 文本抄传:** Manuscripts, fragments, or versions establish that text circulated at a certain time and place. / 抄本、残卷或译本说明文本在某时某地已经流传。
- **RX — reception | 解释／接收:** Interpretation, practice, controversy, liturgy, book lists, or creeds show how texts were received and used. / 释经、实践、争论、礼仪、书目或信经说明文本后来怎样被接收和使用。
- **UT — not directly testable | 不可直接检验:** Miracles, revelation, atonement, resurrection, and theological fulfillment cannot be adjudicated by an object alone; UT is a stop line, not evidence. / 神迹、启示、赎罪、复活与神学成全不能由器物本身裁定；UT 是停止外推的边界，不是一类证据。

| Grade<br>等级 | Meaning<br>定义 | Appropriate use<br>合理用途 | Stop line<br>停止线 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — direct and strong**<br>**A—直接且强** | Provenance, date, reading, and relevance to the stated subclaim are comparatively secure.<br>出土语境、年代、释读及其与具体子主张的关系相对稳固。 | Establish a reliable historical, material, textual, or reception anchor for that specific point.<br>为该项具体历史、物质、文本或接收史判断建立可靠锚点。 | A strong object or text still does not automatically prove every surrounding biblical detail or theological claim.<br>再强的器物或文本也不会自动证明周围全部经文细节或神学宣告。 |
| **B — strong but indirect**<br>**B—有力但间接** | The material fits the period, institution, setting, or relationship well but does not uniquely identify the biblical claim.<br>材料与时代、制度、处境或关系高度吻合，但不能唯一指向该经文主张。 | Illuminate background, constrain plausible reconstruction, or increase historical confidence in a limited claim.<br>照明背景、限制合理重建范围，或提高某项有限历史判断的可信度。 | Do not turn contextual fit into direct identification, dependence, or event proof.<br>不要把处境吻合升级为直接认定、文学依赖或事件证明。 |
| **C — disputed**<br>**C—有争议** | A key reading, identity, date, provenance, site identification, or inference remains materially contested.<br>关键释读、身份、年代、来源、遗址认定或推论仍有实质争议。 | Present the main competing views and state what evidence would change the assessment.<br>并列主要观点，并说明什么新证据可能改变判断。 | Do not write a disputed reconstruction as settled fact.<br>不要把有争议的重建写成定论。 |
| **D — not core support**<br>**D—不纳入核心支持** | The source is unprovenanced, suspect, probably forged, mainly promotional, or the proposed conclusion exceeds what the material can bear.<br>来源不明、材料可疑、可能伪造、主要来自宣传性转述，或所下结论超过资料承载能力。 | Discuss only as a boundary case, misinformation example, or rejected inference, with the reason stated.<br>只作为证据边界、误传案例或被拒绝的推论讨论，并明确说明原因。 | Do not cite it as affirmative support for the historical or theological claim.<br>不要把它当作历史或神学主张的正面支持。 |

The project does use **plus/minus modifiers**. They refine confidence within a letter grade for a stated subclaim: **A−** is slightly less secure than a standard A, while **B+** is stronger than a standard B but still indirect. A notation such as **A/B+** means different aspects of the same item receive different grades; it is not a new grade and must be explained in context.<br>
本项目确实使用**加减修饰符**。它们只在某项具体子主张内细分同一字母等级：**A−** 比标准 A 略弱，**B+** 比标准 B 更强，但仍属于间接证据。像 **A/B+** 这样的写法表示同一材料的不同层面分别得到不同等级；它不是新的等级，必须结合上下文说明。

Three writing rules remain non-negotiable.<br>
以下三条写作纪律不可省略。

1. Archaeology can confirm some people, places, polities, wars, and lived settings, but it normally cannot test the theological cause of a miracle or the revelatory nature of a claim. / 考古可以确认某些人、地、政权、战争和生活背景，但通常不能检验神迹的神学原因或宣告的启示性质。
2. Royal inscriptions are interested political texts; both external sources and biblical narratives must be read according to genre and purpose. / 王室铭文是带立场的政治文本；外部文献与圣经叙事都必须按其文类和目的阅读。
3. “No evidence has been found” does not automatically mean “it did not happen,” but an undiscovered source cannot be used to fill a conclusion. / “尚未发现证据”不自动等于“事情没有发生”，但也不能拿尚未发现的材料填补结论。

## Fourteen Storyline Research Units | 整条故事线的 14 个研究单元

这些单元是研究容器，不表示每卷书都写成于其叙述事件发生之时。需要区分事件时间、写作／编集、现存抄本与后期接收时，应配合“四只时钟”画像使用。
