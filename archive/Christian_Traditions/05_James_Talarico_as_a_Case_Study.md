## Why Use a Case Study? | 为什么采用个案研究？

**English:** James Talarico is useful as a case study because descriptions of him often combine four different things: Presbyterian institutional connections, a local congregation that calls itself progressive, policy positions associated with the political left, and a public theology centered on Jesus' commands to love God and neighbor. Those facts are related, but they are not interchangeable.<br>
**中文：** James Talarico 适合作为个案，是因为外界对他的描述常把四件不同的事放在一起：长老会制度背景、一个自称进步的地方堂会、通常被归入政治左翼的政策立场，以及以耶稣“爱神、爱邻舍”诫命为中心的公共神学。这些事实彼此有关，却不能互相替代。

**English:** A case study can document what one person and the institutions around him publicly say. It cannot make him representative of the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.), every member of St. Andrew's, all progressive Christians, all Democrats, or Christians of any race.<br>
**中文：** 个案研究可以记录一个人及其周围机构公开说过什么，却不能让他代表美国长老会（PC(USA)）、St. Andrew's 的所有成员、全部进步基督徒、全部民主党人，或任何族裔的基督徒。

**English:** The institutional line is **sixteenth-century Reformed Christianity → Scottish and Anglo-American Presbyterianism → multiple American Presbyterian divisions and reunions → the 1983 reunion that formed the PC(USA) → St. Andrew's as a local PC(USA) congregation**. “Progressive” enters at a different level: it is the local congregation's current theological and public-life self-description, not the name of a denomination created by the Reformation.<br>

**English:** This lineage explains where the labels came from; it does not prove that Reformed theology mechanically produces Talarico's politics, or that every PC(USA) congregation shares St. Andrew's profile. ## Four Different Kinds of Labels | 四种不同性质的标签

<a id="formal-facts"></a>
### 1. Formal and Institutional Facts | 正式与制度性事实

**English:** “MATS '25,” “Austin Seminary,” and “St. Andrew's is a PC(USA) congregation” are externally verifiable institutional facts. Even here, the conclusion must remain narrow: a degree is not ordination, attendance is not necessarily membership, and a congregational connection is not denominational authority.<br>
**中文：** “MATS '25”“Austin Seminary”以及“St. Andrew's 是 PC(USA) 堂会”都属于可以从机构资料核实的事实。即使如此，结论也必须保持有限：学位不等于按立，参加聚会不一定等于正式会籍，与堂会有关联也不等于拥有宗派权威。

<a id="congregational-description"></a>
### 2. Congregational Self-Description | 堂会自述

**English:** “Presbyterian & Progressive” is language chosen by St. Andrew's for itself. It tells readers how this local congregation locates its mission, but it should not be silently converted into Talarico's formal denominational title or the official identity of PC(USA) as a whole.<br>
**中文：** “Presbyterian & Progressive”是 St. Andrew's 为自己选择的表述。它帮助读者理解这个地方堂会如何定位自身使命，却不能被悄悄转换成 Talarico 的正式宗派头衔，也不能当作整个 PC(USA) 的官方身份。

<a id="media-framing"></a>
### 3. Media Framing | 媒体框架

**English:** News and commentary frequently place Talarico within “progressive Christianity” or progressive politics. For example, [*The Atlantic*](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/2026/04/progressive-christianity-texas-talarico/686914/) framed him through the progressive-Christian debate. Such descriptions can locate a public controversy, but they remain journalistic categories rather than church membership records.<br>
**中文：** 新闻与评论常把 Talarico 放进“进步基督教”或进步政治的框架。例如，[《大西洋月刊》](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/2026/04/progressive-christianity-texas-talarico/686914/)就用进步基督教之争来解释他。这类说法可以定位公共争议，却仍是媒体分类，不是教会会籍记录。

<a id="self-description"></a>
### 4. His Own Qualification of the Label | 他本人对标签的限定

**English:** In a written statement reported by *The Atlantic*, Talarico rejected a choice between progressive Christianity and conservative Christianity and instead called his faith “biblical Christianity,” rooted in Scripture and Jesus' teaching. In a [2025 full PBS interview](https://www.pbs.org/video/the-full-conversation-with-rep-james-talarico-bfmdng/), he also said conservative and progressive Christians can belong to the same church and should not deny one another a place within the faith.<br>
**中文：** 《大西洋月刊》刊出的书面回应显示，Talarico 不接受必须在进步基督教与保守基督教之间二选一，而把自己的信仰称为扎根圣经和耶稣教导的“合乎圣经的基督教”。在 [PBS 2025 年完整采访](https://www.pbs.org/video/the-full-conversation-with-rep-james-talarico-bfmdng/)中，他还表示，保守与进步基督徒可以同属一个教会，不应把另一方逐出基督信仰的范围。

**English:** A careful article may therefore say that his congregation self-identifies as progressive and that media often use a progressive-Christian frame, while also recording that he resists making “progressive” or “conservative” the controlling name for his Christianity.<br>
**中文：** 因此，严谨的文章可以说他的堂会自称进步，媒体也经常采用进步基督教框架；同时还必须记录，他本人反对让“进步”或“保守”成为界定其基督信仰的首要名称。

## Public Theology in His Own Record | 公开记录中的个人神学

<a id="love-of-god-and-neighbor"></a>
### Love of God and Neighbor | 爱神与爱邻舍

**English:** The most consistent center in the reviewed record is Jesus' commands to love God and neighbor. In the Austin Seminary profile, Talarico described theological study as nourishment for loving God and public service as one arena for loving neighbors. His [campaign narrative](https://jamestalarico.com/why-im-running/) similarly presents every person as bearing sacred worth and applies neighbor-love across differences of prayer, party, race, and gender.<br>
**中文：** 在已复核材料中，最稳定的中心是耶稣“爱神、爱邻舍”的诫命。在 Austin Seminary 的人物专访中，Talarico 把神学学习描述为爱神的滋养，并把公共服务视为爱邻舍的一种场域；他的[竞选自述](https://jamestalarico.com/why-im-running/)也强调每个人都具有神圣价值，并把爱邻舍延伸到祷告方式、党派、族裔与性别差异之上。

**English:** This produces a public theology in which care for schoolchildren, prisoners, sick people, poor families, and other vulnerable neighbors can become matters of policy rather than private charity alone. The source establishes how Talarico connects faith and public action; it does not prove that every Christian must endorse his preferred legislation.<br>
**中文：** 由此形成的公共神学认为，对学生、囚犯、病患、贫困家庭及其他弱势邻舍的关怀，可以成为公共政策议题，而不只停留在私人慈善。来源能够证明 Talarico 怎样连接信仰与公共行动，却不能证明每位基督徒都必须支持他偏好的具体法案。

<a id="interfaith-language"></a>
### Interfaith and God-as-Love Language | 宗教间语言与“神是爱”

**English:** In an official [Texas House Journal entry from 2021](https://journals.house.texas.gov/HJRNL/87R/HTML/87RDAY13FINAL.HTM), Talarico's recorded invocation addressed God as holy mystery and used a sequence of descriptions associated with the Torah, Quran, Gita, the concept of *dharma*, and 1 John. He then located his own faith in Jesus and the commands to love God and neighbor. The prayer connected faith with feeding, housing, healing, welcoming, forgiving, protecting creation, and pursuing public justice.<br>
**中文：** 得州众议院 [2021 年正式议事录](https://journals.house.texas.gov/HJRNL/87R/HTML/87RDAY13FINAL.HTM)所载的 Talarico 祷告，称神为神圣奥秘，并使用一系列与《妥拉》、《古兰经》、《薄伽梵歌》、*dharma* 这一概念及《约翰一书》相关的描述；随后，他把自己的信仰定位在耶稣以及爱神、爱邻舍的诫命上。该祷告又把信仰与供给食物、住处、医治、接待、饶恕、保护受造界和追求公共公义相连。

**English:** This text supports describing his public language as interfaith-open and centered on God as love. It does **not** by itself establish a complete theology of revelation, Christology, salvation, heaven and hell, or the final destiny of every person. This guide therefore does not label him a universalist.<br>
**中文：** 这份文本足以说明他的公共宗教语言向宗教间对话开放，并以“神是爱”为中心；它却**不能**单独建立他对启示、基督论、救恩、天堂地狱或所有人最终归宿的完整主张。因此，本导读不会把他标为普救论者。

<a id="church-and-state"></a>
## Church and State | 教会与国家

**English:** In the PBS interview, Talarico argued that church-state separation protects the church as well as the state: pastors and faith communities, not politicians and bureaucrats, should teach a church's faith. He distinguished teaching *about* religions in public schools from using government power to preach or elevate one tradition above others.<br>
**中文：** 在 PBS 采访中，Talarico 主张政教分离既保护国家，也保护教会：教会信仰应由牧者和信仰群体教导，而不是由政治人物与官僚代教。他区分公立学校“介绍各种宗教”与政府运用权力“宣讲或抬高某一种宗教传统”。

**English:** He uses “Christian nationalism” for projects that join Christian identity to coercive state preference, such as government-mandated religious displays or replacing neutral public functions with sectarian authority. His [campaign policy page](https://jamestalarico.com/issue/freedom-family-faith/) explicitly rejects Christian nationalism, supports church-state separation, opposes religious discrimination, and rejects forced religion in public institutions.<br>
**中文：** 他用“基督教民族主义”描述把基督教身份与国家强制性偏爱相结合的项目，例如由政府强制展示宗教内容，或以宗派权威取代中立公共职能。他的[竞选政策页面](https://jamestalarico.com/issue/freedom-family-faith/)明确反对基督教民族主义、支持政教分离、反对宗教歧视，并反对在公共机构中强制宗教实践。

**English:** Opposing Christian nationalism does not mean demanding that Christians keep faith out of public reasoning. Talarico's own model is almost the opposite: faith motivates public action, while the state must not establish, privilege, or administer the faith itself.<br>
**中文：** 反对基督教民族主义不等于要求基督徒把信仰排除在公共论证之外。Talarico 自己的模式几乎正好相反：信仰可以推动公共行动，但国家不得设立、偏爱或管理信仰本身。

<a id="scripture-and-inclusion"></a>
## Scripture, Gender, and Inclusion | 圣经、性别与接纳

**English:** In the official [Texas House debate on HB 229 of May 12, 2025](https://journals.house.texas.gov/HJRNL/89R/HTML/89RDAY62SUPPLEMENT.HTM), Talarico responded to an appeal to Genesis 1:27 in legislation affecting transgender people. He observed that Genesis also presents day and night, land and sea, and birds and fish as paired categories even though creation includes dawn, estuaries, penguins, and other realities between or across those pairs. He concluded that Genesis' poetry should not be used to bully or discriminate against people with less power.<br>
**中文：** 在 [2025 年 5 月 12 日得州众议院对 HB 229 的正式辩论记录](https://journals.house.texas.gov/HJRNL/89R/HTML/89RDAY62SUPPLEMENT.HTM)中，面对有人在影响跨性别者的立法讨论中引用《创世记》1:27，Talarico 指出，《创世记》也把昼夜、陆海、飞鸟与游鱼成对描述，但受造界仍有黎明、河口、企鹅等位于两端之间或跨越两端的现实；他由此主张，不应使用《创世记》的诗歌去欺凌或歧视权力较少的人。

**English:** That exchange documents one contextual interpretation and the ethical use Talarico made of it. It does not, without further evidence, define his entire doctrine of creation, biblical inspiration, theological anthropology, marriage, or gender. Nearby remarks by other representatives must also not be attributed to him.<br>
**中文：** 这段辩论记录的是 Talarico 在特定语境中的一种解释及其伦理应用；若无进一步证据，不能据此定义他对创造、圣经默示、神学人论、婚姻或性别的全部教义。议事录中附近由其他议员说的话，也不能误记在他名下。

<a id="intra-christian-dialogue"></a>
### Disagreement Inside Christianity | 基督教内部的分歧

**English:** The PBS interview is especially important because Talarico did not claim that a progressive reading alone counts as Christian. He described conserving inherited traditions and improving institutions as a continuing conversation, and said conservative and progressive Christians can share a church while debating how to embody God's kingdom.<br>
**中文：** PBS 采访特别重要，因为 Talarico 并没有宣称只有进步解释才算基督教。他把保存继承传统与改善制度描述为一场持续对话，并认为保守与进步基督徒可以同属一个教会，同时争论怎样活出神的国度。

**English:** Readers may still judge particular claims more or less faithful to Scripture or historic creeds. The evidence-based task is first to quote the right speaker, identify the type of source, and distinguish an individual's argument from a denomination's confession.<br>
**中文：** 读者仍然可以判断某项主张在多大程度上忠于圣经或历史信经；但以证据为基础的第一步，是确认发言者、辨明来源类型，并把个人论证与宗派信条区分开。

<a id="policy-boundary"></a>
## Public Policy Is Not a Complete Theology | 公共政策不等于完整神学

**English:** Talarico's campaign platform supports reproductive freedom, legal same-sex marriage, LGBTQ protections, contraception, and IVF. These are verified **public-policy positions** stated by a political campaign. They should be reported plainly, neither hidden nor upgraded into a complete doctrinal system.<br>
**中文：** Talarico 的竞选纲领支持生育自主、同性婚姻合法性、LGBTQ 权利保护、避孕与试管婴儿技术。这些是政治竞选组织公开陈述、可以核实的**公共政策立场**；既不应隐去，也不应被扩大成一套完整教义体系。

**English:** A policy position does not by itself answer questions about the Trinity, incarnation, atonement, justification, sacraments, biblical authority, ordination, or eschatology. Conversely, Christians who share a creed may still disagree about which law best protects life, liberty, equality, conscience, or vulnerable neighbors.<br>
**中文：** 一项政策立场本身不会回答三一论、道成肉身、赎罪、称义、圣礼、圣经权威、按立或末世论等问题；反过来，共享同一信经的基督徒，也可能对哪种法律最能保护生命、自由、平等、良心或弱势邻舍持不同意见。

## What This Case Cannot Establish | 本个案不能证明什么
