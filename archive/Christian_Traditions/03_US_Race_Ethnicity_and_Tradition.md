## What the Survey Measures | 调查测量什么

**English:** The figures below come from Pew Research Center's 2023–24 Religious Landscape Study (RLS), a nationally representative survey of 36,908 U.S. adults conducted from July 17, 2023, through March 4, 2024. It used an address-based sample and offered online, paper, and telephone response modes in English and Spanish. Pew reports a margin of sampling error of plus or minus 0.8 percentage points at the 95% confidence level for estimates based on the full national sample. That figure does not apply to individual subgroup cells in Table A or Table B; each subgroup estimate has its own sampling uncertainty, which must be assessed separately.<br>
**中文：** 以下数字来自 Pew Research Center 的 2023–24 年美国宗教景观调查（RLS）。该调查在 2023 年 7 月 17 日至 2024 年 3 月 4 日间完成，以地址抽样方式取得 36,908 名具有全国代表性的美国成年受访者，并提供英语或西班牙语的网络、纸本和电话作答方式。Pew 报告的 95% 置信水平下正负 0.8 个百分点抽样误差，只适用于以全体全国样本为基础的估计，不能套用于表 A 或表 B 的单个子群格；每项子群体估计都有自己的抽样不确定性，必须分别评估。

**English:** In these tables, “White,” “Black,” and “Asian” refer to non-Hispanic respondents who selected only that race. “Hispanic” includes people of any race. Pew states that its RLS estimates for Asian adults are representative of English-speaking Asian adults only. This limitation matters because language, immigration history, and national origin vary greatly within the broad Asian category.<br>
**中文：** 在这些表中，“白人”“黑人”和“亚裔”指的是非西语裔、并且只选择该单一种族的受访者；“西语裔”可以属于任何种族。Pew 明确说明，RLS 对亚裔成年人的估计只代表会说英语的亚裔成年人。这个限制很重要，因为宽泛的“亚裔”类别内部，在语言、移民经历和原籍来源方面差异很大。

**English:** All percentages are rounded. Rows may not add to 100%, and a Christian total may not equal the visible Christian subgroups exactly. The Christian total also includes Orthodox Christians, Latter-day Saints, Jehovah's Witnesses, and smaller Christian categories that are not displayed separately in the compact tables below.<br>
**中文：** 所有百分比都经过四舍五入，因此各行相加未必正好是 100%，基督徒总数也未必与表中可见的各基督徒子项精确相加。基督徒总数还包括东正教、耶稣基督后期圣徒教会、耶和华见证人及其他较小的基督教类别；为保持表格简洁，以下没有逐项展开。

**English:** This follows Pew's survey taxonomy of self-reported religious affiliation. It is not a theological judgment that every group included in the “Christian” total belongs within Nicene Christianity; for that distinction, see [Shared Center Before Disagreement](./02_Where_Christians_Disagree.md#shared-center).<br>
**中文：** 这里遵循 Pew 对受访者自报宗教归属的调查分类，并不是神学判定“基督徒”总项中的每个群体都属于尼西亚基督教；相关区别见[先看共同中心，再看分歧](./02_Where_Christians_Disagree.md#shared-center)。

## Table A: Within Each Racial or Ethnic Group | 表 A：各族裔内部的分布

**English:** The denominator in Table A is all U.S. adults in the racial or ethnic group named in the first column, whether Christian, non-Christian, or religiously unaffiliated. It answers questions such as, “What share of White adults are evangelical Protestants?”<br>
**中文：** 表 A 的分母是第一栏所列族裔中的全体美国成年人，包括基督徒、非基督徒及无宗教归属者。它回答的是“白人成年人中有多少是福音派新教徒”这一类问题。

**English:** Pew's report page 95 separately displays only the four racial or ethnic groups reproduced in Table A. The absence of separate rows for populations outside those four reported categories, including Native American and multiracial adults, is a reporting and sample boundary—not evidence that those populations do not exist or have no Christian traditions.<br>
**中文：** Pew 报告第 95 页只把表 A 复录的这四个种族或族裔群体单独列行。美国原住民、多种族成年人等不在这四个已公布类别中的人口没有单独成行，反映的是报告与样本边界，并不表示这些人口不存在或没有基督教传统。

| Racial or ethnic group / 种族或族裔群体 | Christian / 基督徒 | Evangelical Protestant / 福音派新教 | Mainline Protestant / 主流新教 | Historically Black Protestant / 历史黑人新教 | Catholic / 天主教 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| White, non-Hispanic / 非西语裔白人 | 62% | 27% | 15% | &lt;1% | 17% |
| Black, non-Hispanic / 非西语裔黑人 | 73% | 15% | 6% | 44% | 4% |
| Asian, non-Hispanic; English-speaking adults only / 非西语裔亚裔；仅代表会说英语的成年人 | 33% | 12% | 6% | &lt;1% | 14% |
| Hispanic, any race / 西语裔；可属于任何种族 | 67% | 17% | 4% | 1% | 42% |

**English:** The historically Black Protestant percentage must also be read with Pew's classification method in view. For Protestants who provide only a vague denominational identity, Pew may use race together with born-again or evangelical self-identification to assign one of the three Protestant traditions. The observed concentration therefore reflects both denominational history and the survey's rules for classifying vague answers.<br>
**中文：** 阅读历史黑人新教比例时，还必须考虑 Pew 的分类方法。对只提供模糊宗派身份的新教受访者，Pew 可能结合种族及其重生或福音派自我认同，把他们归入三种新教传统之一。因此这种集中度既反映宗派历史，也受到调查如何归类模糊回答的规则影响。

**English:** Read across a row, not down a religious column, when answering a question about one racial or ethnic population. For example, 42% of Hispanic adults are Catholic and 17% are evangelical Protestant; these percentages use all Hispanic adults as their denominator, not Hispanic Christians alone.<br>
**中文：** 回答某一个族裔人口的问题时，应横向阅读一整行，而不是沿着某个宗教栏目向下读。例如，西语裔成年人中 42% 是天主教徒、17% 是福音派新教徒；这里的分母是全体西语裔成年人，并非只计算西语裔基督徒。

**English:** Table A supports a limited group-level description: historically Black Protestant churches are especially prominent among Black adults, while Catholicism is especially prominent among Hispanic adults. It does not show that every Black or Hispanic Christian belongs to the largest category, nor does it explain why the pattern exists.<br>
**中文：** 表 A 可以支持有限的群体描述：历史黑人新教在黑人成年人中格外突出，而天主教在西语裔成年人中格外突出。但它不能说明每一位黑人或西语裔基督徒都属于最大的类别，也不能单靠这些数字解释这种分布为何形成。

## Table B: Within Each Christian Tradition | 表 B：各基督教传统内部的构成

**English:** Table B reverses the denominator. Each row begins with all members of the named religious tradition, then reports that tradition's racial and ethnic composition. It answers questions such as, “What share of evangelical Protestants are White?”<br>
**中文：** 表 B 把分母反转过来。每一行以该宗教传统的全体成员为分母，再列出这个传统内部的种族与族裔构成。它回答的是“福音派新教徒中有多少是白人”这一类问题。

| Religious group or tradition / 宗教群体或传统 | White / 白人 | Black / 黑人 | Hispanic / 西语裔 | Asian / 亚裔 | Other or multiracial / 其他或多种族 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All Christians / 全体基督徒 | 61% | 13% | 18% | 3% | 4% |
| Evangelical Protestant / 福音派新教 | 70% | 7% | 12% | 3% | 6% |
| Mainline Protestant / 主流新教 | 79% | 6% | 6% | 3% | 5% |
| Historically Black Protestant / 历史黑人新教 | 3% | 92% | 4% | &lt;1% | 1% |
| Catholic / 天主教 | 54% | 2% | 36% | 4% | 2% |

**English:** The race and ethnicity definitions and the English-speaking limitation for Asian respondents are the same as in Table A. Because nonresponses are not shown and values are rounded, the displayed percentages may not total exactly 100%.<br>
**中文：** 表 B 采用与表 A 相同的种族、族裔定义，也同样受到亚裔受访者仅代表英语使用者的限制。由于没有显示未作答者，而且数值经过四舍五入，各行显示的百分比未必精确合计为 100%。

**English:** The phrase “historically Black Protestant” names a historical and analytic church tradition. It is not another word for all Black Protestants or all Black Christians, and membership is not absolutely limited to Black adults, as the row itself demonstrates.<br>
**中文：** “历史黑人新教”指的是一个具有历史背景的教会传统和调查分析类别，并不是“所有黑人新教徒”或“所有黑人基督徒”的同义词；正如表中数字所示，这个传统的成员也并非绝对只限于黑人。

## The 70% Versus 27% Denominator Trap | 70% 与 27% 的分母陷阱

**English:** Both of the following statements are supported by the same Pew survey, but they answer different questions: 70% of evangelical Protestants are White, while 27% of White adults are evangelical Protestant. In probability notation, the first is the share who are White given that they are evangelical; the second is the share who are evangelical given that they are White. Reversing the condition changes the denominator and therefore changes the percentage.<br>
**中文：** 以下两个说法都来自同一份 Pew 调查，却回答不同问题：福音派新教徒中 70% 是白人；白人成年人中 27% 是福音派新教徒。用条件概率表示，前者是在“已经属于福音派”的条件下计算白人占比，后者是在“已经属于白人”的条件下计算福音派占比。把条件反过来，就改变了分母，百分比也随之改变。

| Question / 问题 | Numerator / 分子 | Denominator / 分母 | Result / 结果 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| What share of evangelical Protestants are White? / 福音派新教徒中有多少是白人？ | White evangelical Protestants / 白人福音派新教徒 | All evangelical Protestants / 全体福音派新教徒 | 70% |
| What share of White adults are evangelical Protestant? / 白人成年人中有多少是福音派新教徒？ | White evangelical Protestants / 白人福音派新教徒 | All White adults / 全体白人成年人 | 27% |

**English:** Therefore, “most U.S. evangelicals are White” is consistent with the survey, but “most White U.S. adults are evangelical” is not. Dividing the rounded Table A values, 27% by 62%, suggests that evangelical Protestants are roughly 44% of White Christians—an approximate derived figure, not a direct Pew estimate. That is the largest listed single tradition, but it is still below a majority.<br>
**中文：** 因此，“美国福音派中多数是白人”符合这份调查，但“多数美国白人成年人都是福音派”并不符合。若用表 A 已取整的 27% 除以 62%，可以粗略推算福音派约占白人基督徒的 44%；这只是衍生的近似值，不是 Pew 直接公布的估计。它是表中最大的单一传统，却仍未超过半数。

## How Pew Constructs the Categories | Pew 如何建立分类

**English:** “Evangelical,” “mainline,” and “historically Black” are three analytic traditions Pew uses to organize U.S. Protestants. Pew normally begins with the respondent's specific denomination. When a respondent supplies only a vague identity such as “Baptist,” “Methodist,” or “just Christian,” Pew may also use the person's born-again or evangelical self-identification and race to make the classification. The labels in these tables therefore are research categories, not necessarily the exact names individuals use for themselves.<br>
**中文：** “福音派”“主流新教”和“历史黑人新教”是 Pew 用来整理美国新教徒的三种分析传统。Pew 通常先根据受访者所报的具体宗派归类；若受访者只回答“浸信会”“卫理宗”或“只是基督徒”等模糊身份，Pew 还可能参考他是否自认为重生或福音派基督徒，以及他的种族资料。这些表中的标签因此是研究分类，不一定是每个人称呼自己的原话。

**English:** “Fundamentalist” is not a separate tradition in these RLS tables. Pew places some respondents who explicitly give a fundamentalist identity within the broader evangelical classification, but evangelical Protestantism also includes many denominations and people who do not use the fundamentalist label. The two terms overlap but cannot be substituted for one another.<br>
**中文：** “基要主义者”在这些 RLS 表格中不是一个独立传统。Pew 会把部分明确自称基要主义者的受访者归入更宽泛的福音派类别，但福音派新教还包括许多并不使用“基要主义”标签的宗派与个人。因此两者虽然有重叠，却不能互换。

**English:** “Progressive Christianity” or “progressive theology” is also not one of the RLS religious-tradition categories. It is a cross-denominational theological and public-life orientation. Mainline Protestant cannot simply be replaced with “progressive”: mainline denominations contain progressive, centrist, and conservative congregations and members, while progressive Christians may also be found outside mainline bodies.<br>
**中文：** “进步基督教”或“进步神学”也不是 RLS 的宗教传统类别，而是跨宗派的神学与公共生活取向。不能直接把“主流新教”替换成“进步派”：主流宗派内部有进步、中间和保守的堂会及成员，主流宗派之外也可能有进步基督徒。

## What the Data Can and Cannot Show | 数据可以与不可以说明什么

| The evidence can support… / 证据可以支持…… | The evidence cannot establish… / 证据不能证明…… |
| --- | --- |
| One survey's estimated distribution for a defined U.S. adult population at a stated time / 某项调查在特定时间对明确定义的美国成年人口所作的分布估计 | The faith, doctrine, politics, morality, or spiritual life of a particular person / 某个具体个人的信仰、教义、政治、道德或属灵生命 |
| A comparison between groups when the denominator and method are held constant / 在分母与方法一致时比较群体 | That race caused the religious pattern / 种族造成了该宗教分布 |
| A reason to investigate history, migration, region, language, class, institutions, and local congregations / 进一步研究历史、移民、地区、语言、阶层、制度与地方堂会的理由 | One timeless profile for all White, Black, Hispanic, Asian, or multiracial Christians / 一套永远适用于所有白人、黑人、西语裔、亚裔或多种族基督徒的画像 |

**English:** Racial and religious identities can be statistically associated without race determining belief. A responsible description says that a tradition is “more common in this surveyed group” or that a group “made up a larger share under this survey's definitions.” It should not say that a person probably holds a doctrine merely because of appearance or ancestry.<br>
**中文：** 种族身份与宗教身份可以在统计上相关，但这不代表种族决定信仰。负责任的写法应当说某传统“在这个受调查群体中较常见”，或某群体“按本调查定义占比较高”；不能只凭外貌或祖源，就推测一个人很可能持守某项教义。

**English:** These national estimates also should not be transferred automatically to China, to all Chinese-speaking churches, or even to every U.S. city. The RLS did not publish a Chinese-origin subgroup in these tables, and its broad Asian estimate covers English-speaking adults only. Claims about Chinese or Chinese American Christians require a separate population, survey design, date, and denominator.<br>
**中文：** 这些美国全国估计也不能自动套用到中国境内、所有华语教会，甚至美国的每一座城市。RLS 在这些表中没有单独公布华裔来源群体，而且宽泛的亚裔估计只涵盖会说英语的成年人。讨论中国基督徒或美国华裔基督徒时，需要另行确定人口范围、调查设计、年份与分母。
