<a id="short-answer"></a>
## The Short Answer: Five Pressures Recur | 简短答案：五种力量反复出现

**English:** Christian traditions did not arise for one reason. At different times, Christians disagreed about doctrine, biblical interpretation, sacraments, authority, and church government. Language, geography, empire, nation-building, migration, revival, race, and state power often determined whether a disagreement could be contained or became a lasting institutional separation.<br>
**中文：** 基督教传统并不是因为一个原因而产生。不同年代的基督徒会在教义、圣经解释、圣礼、权柄与教会治理上发生分歧；语言、地理、帝国、民族国家建构、移民、复兴、族裔与国家权力，又常常决定一场分歧能否留在同一制度中，还是变成持久的组织分立。

| Recurring pressure / 反复出现的力量 | Historical examples / 历史例子 | Reading boundary / 阅读边界 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doctrine and interpretation / 教义与解释 | Christology after Ephesus and Chalcedon; justification and biblical authority in the Reformation; modern debates over criticism and science / 以弗所与迦克墩会议后的基督论；宗教改革中的称义与圣经权威；近代历史批判与科学争议 | Doctrine was often sincere and consequential, not merely a cover for politics. / 教义分歧往往真实而且后果重大，不能一概当作政治借口。 |
| Authority and governance / 权柄与治理 | Papal primacy, councils, bishops, presbyteries, congregational autonomy, and the authority of civil rulers / 教宗首席权、大公会议、主教、长老会、地方堂会自治与世俗统治者的权力 | Similar doctrine does not guarantee shared government or communion. / 教义相近并不保证治理制度一致或彼此共融。 |
| Language, geography, and empire / 语言、地理与帝国 | Syriac, Greek, and Latin worlds; Roman-Persian rivalry; the Latin West and Byzantine East / 叙利亚语、希腊语与拉丁语世界；罗马与波斯竞争；拉丁西方与拜占庭东方 | A date such as 431, 451, or 1054 is usually a milestone in a longer estrangement. / 431、451 或 1054 这类年份通常只是长期疏离中的节点。 |
| Reform, revival, and mission / 改革、复兴与宣教 | Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Anabaptist, Methodist, Holiness, Pentecostal, and evangelical networks / 路德宗、改革宗、圣公宗、再洗礼派、卫理宗、圣洁运动、五旬节运动与福音派网络 | A renewal movement may remain inside older churches, cross them, or later organize as a denomination. / 更新运动可能留在旧宗派内、跨越多个宗派，或后来组织成新宗派。 |
| Social exclusion, migration, and public conflict / 社会排斥、移民与公共冲突 | Independent Black churches under racism; immigrant and language congregations; modern disputes over civil rights, gender, sexuality, and church-state relations / 种族主义下独立形成的黑人教会；移民与语言堂会；民权、性别、性取向与政教关系争议 | Social history shapes institutions, but race, class, or party does not predetermine an individual's theology. / 社会历史会塑造制度，但族裔、阶层或党派不能预先决定个人神学。 |

**English:** These forces usually interacted. The English Reformation, for example, involved royal authority, an annulment dispute, nationalism, inherited English church life, Protestant theology, liturgical reform, and later political settlement. “It was only theology” and “it was only power” are both inadequate explanations.<br>
**中文：** 这些力量通常彼此交织。例如，英格兰宗教改革同时涉及王权、婚姻无效争议、民族国家意识、英格兰既有教会生活、新教神学、礼仪改革与后来的政治安排。无论说“纯粹是神学”还是“纯粹是权力”，都不足以解释完整历史。

## How to Read the Family Tree | 怎样阅读这张谱系图

Solid historical descent does not mean doctrinal identity, and the arrows do not imply that the later body is the only legitimate heir of the earlier one. Traditions also reunite, borrow from one another, develop internal parties, and spread into cultural settings that reshape them.<br>
**中文：** 下面的谱系是有意简化的。历史继承关系不等于教义完全相同，箭头也不表示后来的群体是早期教会唯一合法的继承者。各传统还会重新联合、彼此借鉴、在内部形成不同派别，并在传播到新文化后继续改变。

They should not be placed in one flat list as though they were interchangeable choices.<br>
**中文：** “天主教”“东正教”“东方正统教会”通常指教会共融体；“浸信会”“长老会”指宗派家族或治理传统；“福音派”“灵恩派”“进步基督教”可能跨越宗派；“主流新教”则常是美国调查与制度史中的分类。不能把它们放进同一张平面清单，当成彼此可替换的选项。

<a id="ancient-communions"></a>
## Before Denominations: Ancient Communions and Councils | 宗派以前：古代共融体与大公会议

**English:** Early Christianity was already geographically and linguistically diverse. Local churches were connected through bishops, letters, councils, shared worship, and creeds, but they did not yet form one modern centralized denomination. Councils attempted to preserve common confession while settling disputes; the reception or rejection of their formulas could also harden regional separation.<br>
**中文：** 早期基督教本来就具有地理与语言上的多样性。各地教会通过主教、书信、会议、共同敬拜与信经相连，却还不是一个现代式中央集权宗派。大公会议试图在处理争议时保存共同信仰，但对会议用语的接受或拒绝，也可能使区域分离固定下来。

**English:** Nicaea in 325 and Constantinople in 381 supplied a Trinitarian creedal center still shared by major Christian communions. At the same time, the regional authority of Rome, Constantinople, Alexandria, Antioch, Jerusalem, and churches beyond the Roman Empire developed gradually. The later “five patriarchates” should not be projected backward as five first-century denominations.<br>
**中文：** 325 年尼西亚会议与 381 年君士坦丁堡会议形成了主要基督教共融体至今共享的三一信经中心。与此同时，罗马、君士坦丁堡、亚历山大、安提阿、耶路撒冷，以及罗马帝国以外教会的区域权柄，都是逐渐发展出来的。后来所谓“五大牧首区”，不能倒推成第一世纪就存在的五个宗派。

<a id="east-431"></a>
### The Church of the East and 431 | 东方教会与 431 年

**English:** Christian communities east of the Roman frontier developed within the Persian Sasanian Empire and used East Syriac language and institutions. They organized under a catholicos in 410, and in 424 their bishops barred appeals to churches in the Roman West. Their ecclesial independence therefore preceded the Council of Ephesus in 431; later reception of Antiochene Christology and political distance from Roman churches deepened separation. The Church of the East was not simply “created in 431,” and the polemical nickname “Nestorian” should not substitute for its own name or for modern dialogue.<br>
**中文：** 罗马边境以东的基督徒群体在波斯萨珊帝国内发展，使用东叙利亚语及其制度；410 年在一位大公牧首之下组织起来，424 年其主教禁止向罗马帝国西方教会提出上诉。因此，它们的教会独立性早于 431 年以弗所会议；后来对安提阿基督论传统的接收，以及与罗马帝国教会在政治上的距离，使分离进一步加深。不能简单说东方教会“创建于 431 年”，也不应以争议性的“聂斯托留派”称呼取代其自称或现代对话成果。

**English:** Ephesus condemned Nestorius while rival sessions and subsequent negotiations exposed divisions among the bishops; Cyril of Alexandria and John of Antioch restored communion in 433. The later Church of the East should not be confused with every bishop associated with the Antiochene theological tradition.<br>
**中文：** 以弗所会议定罪聂斯托留，但相互竞争的会议程序及后续谈判也显出主教之间的分歧；亚历山大的居里禄与安提阿的约翰在 433 年恢复共融。后来的东方教会不能与所有采用安提阿神学传统的主教混为一谈。

**English:** A 1994 Catholic-Assyrian common declaration confessed Christ together as true God and true man and said past divisions were due in large part to misunderstandings. That agreement does not erase every ecclesial difference, but it warns readers against treating a fifth-century label as a complete account of present belief.<br>
**中文：** 1994 年天主教与亚述东方教会的共同声明，一同承认基督是真神也是真人，并指出过去的分裂很大程度上源于误解。该声明没有消除全部教会差异，却提醒读者：不能用一个五世纪标签概括对方今天的全部信仰。

<a id="chalcedon-451"></a>
### The Oriental Orthodox Churches and 451 | 东方正统教会与 451 年

**English:** The Council of Chalcedon in 451 confessed one Christ “in two natures.” Many Christians especially in Egypt, Syria, and Armenia did not receive the council's formula; over the following centuries, parallel hierarchies and related communions developed into the Oriental Orthodox family now including Coptic, Syriac, Armenian, Ethiopian, Eritrean, and Indian churches. Theology mattered, but Greek terminology, imperial enforcement, regional identity, and later persecution also widened the breach.<br>
**中文：** 451 年迦克墩会议以“在两性之中”的表达承认同一位基督。特别是在埃及、叙利亚与亚美尼亚，许多基督徒没有接收该会议的表述；此后数百年间，平行的教阶与相关共融逐渐形成今天包括科普特、叙利亚、亚美尼亚、埃塞俄比亚、厄立特里亚与印度教会在内的东方正统教会家族。神学确实重要，但希腊语术语、帝国强制、区域身份与后来的逼迫，也使裂痕扩大。

**English:** It is misleading to say that Oriental Orthodox Christians simply deny either Christ's divinity or humanity. Catholic-Coptic and Eastern-Oriental Orthodox dialogues have found substantial Christological agreement while full communion remains unresolved. “Non-Chalcedonian” describes conciliar reception more accurately than a caricature of their faith.<br>
**中文：** 若说东方正统教会简单否认基督的神性或人性，就会造成误导。天主教—科普特及东正教—东方正统教会的现代对话已经确认相当程度的基督论共识，但完全共融仍未恢复。“非迦克墩派”较准确地描述其对会议的接收立场，不应把它变成对其信仰的漫画式标签。

<a id="east-west-1054"></a>
### The Eastern and Western Churches: 1054 Is a Symbolic Date | 东西教会：1054 年是象征性节点

**English:** Greek-speaking Eastern and Latin-speaking Western churches became estranged over centuries. Disputes included Roman papal claims, conciliar authority, the *filioque* addition to the creed, liturgical customs, language, and rivalry between political worlds. The exchange of excommunications in 1054 concerned named leaders and later became a symbol of the wider Catholic-Orthodox schism; it did not divide every Christian everywhere in a single day.<br>
**中文：** 使用希腊语的东方教会与使用拉丁语的西方教会经历了数百年疏离。争议包括罗马教宗权柄、大公会议权威、信经中的 *filioque*（“和子”）增句、礼仪习惯、语言，以及不同政治世界之间的竞争。1054 年的相互绝罚针对具体领袖，后来成为更广泛天主教—东正教分裂的象征；它并没有在一天之内把所有地方的基督徒全部分开。

**English:** The 1204 Latin sack of Constantinople made the rupture much harder to heal. In 1965, Rome and Constantinople removed the 1054 excommunications from memory, but that reconciliation gesture did not restore full sacramental communion. The history is therefore a process: growing distance, symbolic rupture, later trauma, and continuing dialogue.<br>
**中文：** 1204 年拉丁十字军洗劫君士坦丁堡，使裂痕更难修复。1965 年，罗马与君士坦丁堡共同从记忆中撤去 1054 年的绝罚，但这一和好行动并未恢复完全圣礼共融。因此，更准确的历史线是：距离逐步扩大、出现象征性决裂、后来创伤加深，并持续展开对话。

<a id="reformation-families"></a>
## The Sixteenth-Century Reformation Families | 十六世纪宗教改革各支

**English:** By the early sixteenth century, Western Christians were debating abuses in church practice, indulgences, salvation, sacramental theology, the authority of Scripture and tradition, papal jurisdiction, clerical formation, and the relation between church and rulers. Printing, universities, urban networks, and territorial states helped controversies travel and become institutional. Catholic reform, including the Council of Trent, also addressed discipline and doctrine rather than leaving the Roman church unchanged.<br>
**中文：** 到十六世纪初，西方基督徒围绕教会实践中的弊端、赎罪券、救恩、圣礼神学、圣经与传统的权威、教宗管辖权、圣职人员培育，以及教会与统治者的关系展开争论。印刷术、大学、城市网络与领地国家，使争议迅速传播并制度化。包括特伦托会议在内的天主教内部改革也处理了纪律与教义问题，因此罗马教会并非完全没有改变。

| Family / 家族 | Why it emerged / 为何形成 | Lasting emphasis / 持久重点 | Important boundary / 重要边界 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lutheran / 路德宗 | Martin Luther's 1517 indulgence protest expanded into conflict over justification, authority, sacraments, and church reform. / 马丁·路德 1517 年反对赎罪券的行动，扩大为称义、权威、圣礼与教会改革之争。 | Justification by grace through faith, the preached Word, baptism and Eucharist, and liturgical continuity reformed around the gospel / 因恩典借信称义、宣讲圣道、洗礼与圣餐，以及围绕福音更新的礼仪延续 | Lutheranism did not discard all pre-Reformation worship or sacramental life. / 路德宗并没有抛弃宗教改革前的一切敬拜与圣礼生活。 |
| Reformed and Presbyterian / 改革宗与长老会 | Swiss reforms associated with Zwingli, Calvin, and others developed a distinct account of worship, covenant, sacraments, discipline, and ordered church life; John Knox carried Reformed theology and polity into Scotland. / 与慈运理、加尔文等人有关的瑞士改革，对敬拜、圣约、圣礼、纪律与教会秩序形成独特理解；约翰·诺克斯把改革宗神学与治理带回苏格兰。 | God's sovereignty, covenant, confessions, and representative rule by elders in graded assemblies / 神的主权、圣约、信条，以及由长老参与分层议会的代表性治理 | “Reformed” is the wider theological family; “Presbyterian” is one prominent form of Reformed polity and denominational life. / “改革宗”是更广的神学家族；“长老会”是改革宗治理与宗派生活的重要形式之一。 |
| Anglican / 圣公宗 | Henry VIII's annulment conflict and claims of royal authority catalyzed the break with Rome; more explicitly Protestant theology and liturgy developed under Edward VI, followed by Mary's reversal and Elizabeth's settlement. / 亨利八世的婚姻无效争议与王权主张促成同罗马决裂；爱德华六世时期发展出更明确的新教神学与礼仪，随后经历玛丽一世的逆转与伊丽莎白一世的宗教安排。 | Episcopal government, historic creeds, common prayer, and a church that describes itself as both catholic and reformed / 主教制、历史信经、共同祈祷，以及自称兼具大公传统与改革性质的教会 | “Henry wanted a divorce” names one catalyst, not the whole theological, political, and liturgical history of Anglicanism. / “亨利想离婚”只指出一个诱因，不能概括圣公宗全部神学、政治与礼仪历史。 |
| Anabaptist / 再洗礼派 | Radical reformers beginning symbolically at Zurich in 1525 rejected infant baptism and the state-managed church, emphasizing a voluntary community of disciples. / 以 1525 年苏黎世为象征起点的激进改革者，拒绝婴儿洗礼与国家管理的教会，强调自愿加入的门徒群体。 | Believers' baptism, visible discipleship, congregational discipline, and in many branches nonviolence and refusal of oaths / 信徒洗礼、可见的门徒生活、堂会纪律，以及许多支派中的非暴力与拒绝宣誓 | Anabaptists were persecuted by both Catholic and Protestant authorities and are not simply “more extreme Baptists.” / 再洗礼派曾受天主教与新教政权逼迫，也不能简单视为“更激进的浸信会”。 |
| Baptist / 浸信会 | Seventeenth-century English Separatists formed churches around believers' baptism, congregational liberty, and freedom of conscience. Some had contact with Mennonites, while General and Particular Baptists had distinguishable English beginnings. / 十七世纪英格兰分离派围绕信徒洗礼、堂会自主与良心自由建立教会；其中一些人与门诺派有接触，而普遍救赎派浸信会与特定救赎派浸信会在英格兰各有可区分的源流。 | Believers' baptism, congregational governance, religious liberty, mission, and diverse Arminian or Calvinist theologies / 信徒洗礼、堂会治理、宗教自由、宣教，以及亚米念主义或加尔文主义等多种神学 | Modern Baptists are historically related to, but not simply a renamed continuation of, the continental Anabaptists. / 现代浸信会与欧洲大陆再洗礼派有历史关联，却不是后者简单改名后的延续。 |

**English:** The Reformation therefore did not produce one “Protestant church.” It produced overlapping confessional, national, and governmental settlements. Even churches descended from the same branch later divided over revival, slavery, biblical interpretation, worship, mission, ordination, and social questions.<br>
**中文：** 因此，宗教改革并没有产生一个统一的“新教会”，而是形成相互交叠的信条、民族国家与治理安排。即使出自同一支的教会，后来也会因复兴、奴隶制、圣经解释、敬拜、宣教、按立与社会议题再次分开。

<a id="revival-age"></a>
## Revival, Mission, and Voluntary Churches | 复兴、宣教与自愿结社教会

<a id="methodist-holiness"></a>
### Methodist and Holiness Traditions | 卫理宗与圣洁运动

**English:** John and Charles Wesley led an eighteenth-century renewal movement while remaining priests of the Church of England. Methodical prayer, small groups, preaching, works of mercy, conversion, and growth in holiness gave the movement its name and shape. The American Revolution and pastoral need accelerated independent Methodist organization in the United States; Methodism then became a worldwide denominational family.<br>
**中文：** 约翰与查尔斯·卫斯理在仍是英格兰教会牧师时，带领了十八世纪更新运动。规律祷告、小组、讲道、怜悯行动、归信与在圣洁中成长，使这个运动得到“循道／卫理”的名称与形态。美国革命后的制度处境和牧养需要，加快了美国卫理公会独立组织的进程；此后卫理宗成为全球宗派家族。

**English:** In the nineteenth century, the Holiness movement renewed Wesley's teaching about sanctification and often stressed a definite experience of entire sanctification. Some adherents remained Methodist; others formed bodies such as the Church of the Nazarene. The later Pentecostal movement inherited much of this revival culture while adding distinctive teaching about Spirit baptism and spiritual gifts.<br>
**中文：** 十九世纪圣洁运动重新强调卫斯理有关成圣的教导，并常突出“完全成圣”的明确经历。有人留在卫理宗内，也有人建立拿撒勒人会等宗派。后来的五旬节运动继承了许多复兴文化，同时加入有关圣灵洗与属灵恩赐的独特教导。

<a id="evangelical-awakenings"></a>
### Evangelical Awakenings | 福音派大觉醒

**English:** “Evangelical” originally referred broadly to the gospel and was used in Reformation settings, but modern English-speaking evangelicalism was strongly shaped by eighteenth- and nineteenth-century awakenings. It emphasized personal conversion, biblical proclamation, Christ's saving cross, evangelism, voluntary societies, and mission across Anglican, Baptist, Congregational, Methodist, Presbyterian, and other churches.<br>
**中文：** “福音派”一词原本广泛指向福音，也用于宗教改革语境；但现代英语世界的福音派特别受十八、十九世纪大觉醒塑造，强调个人归信、圣经宣讲、基督救赎的十字架、传福音、自愿结社与宣教，并跨越圣公宗、浸信会、公理会、卫理宗、长老会等教会。

**English:** Evangelicalism is therefore a movement and network as well as a survey identity; it is not one denomination. The same denominational family may contain an evangelical body, a mainline body, and people who use neither label.<br>
**中文：** 因此，福音派既是运动与网络，也可能是调查中的身份类别，却不是一个单一宗派。同一个宗派家族可能同时包含福音派宗派、主流新教宗派，以及不使用任何一项标签的人。

<a id="black-church-independence"></a>
### Black Church Independence | 黑人教会的制度独立

**English:** Some United States denominations were created or divided not because Black Christians invented a different creed, but because slavery, segregation, exclusion from leadership, and racist control made equal life inside White-governed churches impossible. Richard Allen and other Black Methodists built independent institutions after discriminatory treatment; the African Methodist Episcopal Church organized as a denomination in 1816. Black Baptist conventions and other historically Black bodies developed through related struggles for worship, leadership, education, mutual aid, abolition, and civil rights.<br>
**中文：** 美国一些宗派的形成或分裂，并不是因为黑人基督徒另造了一套信经，而是奴隶制、隔离、领导权排斥与种族主义控制，使他们无法在白人治理的教会中获得平等生活。Richard Allen 等黑人卫理宗信徒在遭受歧视后建立独立机构，非洲卫理公会于 1816 年正式组成宗派。黑人浸信会大会及其他历史性黑人宗派，也在争取敬拜、领导、教育、互助、废奴与民权的相关历程中形成。

**English:** “Historically Black Protestant” is consequently both a history and a survey category, not a statement that all Black Christians share one theology or politics. Black Christians are also Catholic, Orthodox, evangelical, mainline, Pentecostal, and unaffiliated with historically Black denominations.<br>
**中文：** 所以，“历史性黑人新教”既指一段制度史，也是一种调查分类，并不表示所有黑人基督徒共享同一种神学或政治。黑人基督徒也可能属于天主教、东正教、福音派、主流新教、五旬节派，或并不属于历史性黑人宗派。

<a id="pentecostal-charismatic"></a>
### Pentecostal and Charismatic Renewal | 五旬节与灵恩更新

**English:** Modern Pentecostalism emerged from several revival and Holiness settings around the turn of the twentieth century. Events in Topeka at the century's opening and the interracial Azusa Street revival of 1906–1909 became influential centers, but no single meeting explains all global Pentecostal origins. Pentecostals emphasized the present work of the Holy Spirit, Spirit baptism, gifts, healing, mission, and—within many classical bodies—speaking in tongues as initial evidence.<br>
**中文：** 现代五旬节运动在二十世纪之交从多个复兴与圣洁运动环境中兴起。1901 年托皮卡事件与 1906—1909 年跨族裔的亚苏撒街复兴成为重要中心，但没有一场聚会能够解释全球五旬节运动的全部起源。五旬节派强调圣灵当下的工作、圣灵洗、恩赐、医治与宣教；许多古典五旬节宗派还把方言视为最初证据。

**English:** Trinitarian and Oneness Pentecostal bodies later separated over the doctrine of God and baptismal formula. From the mid-twentieth century, charismatic renewal carried practices associated with Pentecostalism into Catholic, Anglican, mainline Protestant, evangelical, and independent churches without always creating a new denomination. “Pentecostal” and “charismatic” therefore overlap but are not identical.<br>
**中文：** 后来，三一论五旬节派与独一神格五旬节派因上帝论和洗礼公式分开。二十世纪中期以后，灵恩更新把与五旬节运动相关的实践带进天主教、圣公宗、主流新教、福音派与独立教会，却不一定另立宗派。因此，“五旬节派”与“灵恩派”彼此重叠，但并不完全相同。

<a id="nondenominational"></a>
### Nondenominational Churches | 非宗派教会

**English:** Revival meetings, Bible conferences, parachurch ministries, independent missions, church-growth methods, migration, and later megachurch networks all helped nondenominational churches expand. The absence of a denominational name does not mean the absence of inherited theology: a congregation may function like a Baptist, Reformed, Wesleyan, Pentecostal, or restorationist church while using only “Christian” or “community church” in its name.<br>
**中文：** 复兴聚会、圣经大会、超宗派机构、独立差会、教会增长方法、移民，以及后来的大型教会网络，都推动了非宗派教会扩展。没有宗派名称，不等于没有继承神学；一间只使用“基督教会”或“社区教会”名称的堂会，实际运作仍可能接近浸信会、改革宗、卫斯理宗、五旬节派或复原主义传统。

<a id="modern-labels"></a>
## Modern U.S. Labels: Not Four Parallel Denominations | 美国近代标签：不是四个并列宗派

<a id="fundamentalism"></a>
### Fundamentalism | 基要主义

**English:** Protestant fundamentalism arose in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries as a coalition defending doctrines regarded as essential against theological liberalism, historical criticism, evolutionary accounts, and perceived cultural secularization. *The Fundamentals* pamphlets of 1910–1915 helped supply the movement's vocabulary, and Baptist editor Curtis Lee Laws used “fundamentalists” as a coalition label in 1920. The controversies of the 1920s and 1930s then fought over seminaries, mission boards, ordination, denominational control, and Christianity's public role.<br>
**中文：** 新教基要主义兴起于十九世纪末至二十世纪初，是一个为抵抗神学自由主义、历史批判、进化论解释及所感受到的文化世俗化，而捍卫核心教义的联盟。1910—1915 年出版的《基要真理》系列小册子为运动提供了用语，浸信会编辑 Curtis Lee Laws 又在 1920 年用“基要派”称呼这一联盟。1920—1930 年代的冲突随后围绕神学院、差会、按立、宗派控制权与基督教公共角色展开。

**English:** Fundamentalism is not one denomination. Some fundamentalists left existing bodies and built separate churches, schools, and mission agencies; others remained within broader evangelical or confessional churches. In current Chinese usage, “holding to fundamental truths” may simply mean Nicene or evangelical orthodoxy and should not automatically be equated with the historical American movement, its separatist strategy, or its later political associations.<br>
**中文：** 基要主义不是一个宗派。有些基要派离开原有宗派，建立独立教会、学校与差会；也有人留在更广泛的福音派或信条型教会中。今天中文所说的“持守基要真理”，有时只表示坚持尼西亚或福音派正统，不能自动等同于美国历史基要主义、其分离主义策略或后来的政治关联。

<a id="neo-evangelicalism"></a>
### Neo-Evangelicalism and the Broader Evangelical Movement | 新福音派与更广泛的福音派

**English:** In the 1940s and 1950s, leaders later called neo-evangelicals sought to retain conservative doctrines while rejecting what they criticized as fundamentalist isolation and anti-intellectualism. The National Association of Evangelicals was formed in 1942, Fuller Theological Seminary in 1947, and *Christianity Today* in 1956. Their program favored evangelism, scholarship, cooperation, and engagement with culture rather than complete withdrawal.<br>
**中文：** 1940—1950 年代，后来被称为“新福音派”的领袖试图保留保守教义，同时拒绝他们所批评的基要主义孤立与反智倾向。全国福音派协会成立于 1942 年，富勒神学院成立于 1947 年，《今日基督教》创刊于 1956 年。他们强调传福音、学术、合作与文化参与，而不是全面退出社会。

**English:** Modern evangelicalism is broader than neo-evangelical institutions and far broader than one political coalition. Fundamentalists may sit at one edge of the evangelical world, but the terms are not synonyms; evangelical Protestants also differ over creation, biblical interpretation, charismatic gifts, race, gender, economics, and party politics.<br>
**中文：** 现代福音派比新福音派机构的范围更广，也远远超出某个政治联盟。基要派可以位于福音派世界的一端，但两词并非同义；福音派新教徒在创造论、圣经解释、灵恩恩赐、族裔、性别、经济与党派政治上也有分歧。

<a id="mainline"></a>
### Mainline Protestantism | 主流新教

**English:** “Mainline” names a historically prominent United States institutional family, and in surveys it is a classification assigned to denominations such as the PC(USA), Episcopal Church, ELCA, UMC, UCC, and American Baptist Churches USA. Many of these bodies participated strongly in ecumenical institutions, universities, social reform, and public life. The category is not itself a creed and does not mean that every member is liberal or progressive.<br>
**中文：** “主流新教”指美国历史上具有重要制度地位的一组教会；在调查中，PC(USA)、美国圣公会、ELCA、UMC、UCC 与美国浸信会等会被归入此类。许多宗派积极参与普世教会机构、大学、社会改革与公共生活。这个分类本身不是信经，也不表示每位成员都是自由派或进步派。

**English:** A single family name can cross survey traditions. Pew, for example, classifies the Southern Baptist Convention as evangelical, American Baptist Churches USA as mainline, and the National Baptist Convention USA as historically Black. “Baptist,” “evangelical,” “mainline,” and “historically Black” therefore answer different questions.<br>
**中文：** 同一个宗派家族名称可以跨越调查传统。例如，Pew 把美南浸信会归入福音派，把美国浸信会归入主流新教，把全美浸信会归入历史性黑人新教。因此，“浸信会”“福音派”“主流新教”与“历史性黑人新教”回答的是不同层面的问题。

<a id="progressive-christianity"></a>
### Progressive Christianity | 进步基督教

**English:** Progressive Christianity has no single founder, start date, confession, or denominational headquarters. Its modern streams draw in different proportions from Protestant liberal theology, historical-critical biblical study, the late nineteenth-century Social Gospel, ecumenism, civil-rights activism, Black and liberation theologies, feminist theology, LGBTQ inclusion, ecological concern, and interfaith dialogue. Some adherents remain strongly creedal; others substantially revise inherited doctrines. The label therefore identifies a family resemblance, not one doctrinal package. **This is this guide's historical synthesis, not a universally agreed definition or a genealogy every progressive Christian would adopt.**<br>
**中文：** 进步基督教没有单一创始人、起始年份、信条或宗派总部。它的现代脉络以不同比例吸收新教自由主义神学、历史批判式圣经研究、十九世纪末社会福音、普世教会运动、民权行动、黑人神学与解放神学、女性主义神学、LGBTQ 接纳、生态关怀与宗教间对话。有人仍坚定认信历史信经，也有人大幅修订传统教义。因此，这个标签描述的是一种“家族相似性”，而不是一套统一教义。**这是本导读采用的历史综合框架，不是普遍一致的定义，也不是每位进步基督徒都会接受的谱系。**

**English:** Progressive Christianity is especially visible in parts of mainline Protestantism, but “mainline” and “progressive” are not synonyms. Progressive Catholics, evangelicals, and independent Christians also exist, while conservative and centrist congregations remain inside mainline denominations. Political progressivism may overlap with theological progressivism without proving it.<br>
**中文：** 进步基督教在部分主流新教中尤其可见，但“主流新教”与“进步”不是同义词。天主教、福音派与独立教会中也有人自认进步；主流宗派内部同样保留保守与中间立场的堂会。政治进步主义可能与神学进步主义重叠，却不能互相证明。

<a id="race-and-history"></a>
## Why Race and Denomination Became Associated in the United States | 美国族裔与宗派为何产生关联

**English:** Race does not carry a denomination in human biology. The association arose through immigration routes, mission networks, language, neighborhood settlement, slavery, segregation, unequal access to seminaries and leadership, church property, marriage patterns, and later suburbanization. Institutions reproduce social networks across generations, so a historically contingent pattern can look “natural” long after its causes are forgotten.<br>
**中文：** 宗派并不写在人类族裔的生物属性中。美国的关联来自移民路线、差会网络、语言、社区定居、奴隶制、隔离、神学院与领导岗位的不平等准入、教产、婚姻模式及后来的郊区化。制度会跨代复制社会网络，于是一个历史偶然形成的格局，在起因被遗忘后可能看起来像“天生如此”。

**English:** That history explains why surveys still detect racial concentration, but it does not license statements such as “White people are evangelical,” “Black people are progressive,” or “Chinese people are fundamentalist.” The correct question fixes both denominator and date: for example, “What share of this surveyed racial group belonged to this defined religious tradition in this year?” See the [U.S. demographic guide](./03_US_Race_Ethnicity_and_Tradition.md#denominator-trap) for the measured percentages and denominator rules.<br>
**中文：** 这段历史能够解释为什么调查仍会发现族裔集中，却不能据此断言“白人都是福音派”“黑人都是进步派”或“华人都是基要派”。正确问题必须固定分母与年份，例如：“在这一年，这个受调查族裔群体中有多少比例属于这项明确定义的宗教传统？”具体比例与分母规则见[美国人口资料导读](./03_US_Race_Ethnicity_and_Tradition.md#denominator-trap)。

<a id="chinese-pathways"></a>
## Chinese and Chinese American Pathways | 中国大陆与美国华人教会路径

**English:** From the nineteenth century into the twentieth, Anglican, Methodist, Presbyterian, Reformed, Baptist, Lutheran, Holiness, Pentecostal, Catholic, and independent mission bodies established institutions in China. Chinese Christians also created indigenous movements, union institutions, and local networks that did not simply reproduce Western denominational boundaries. War, revolution, state regulation, and migration repeatedly interrupted or recombined those inheritances.<br>
**中文：** 从十九世纪到二十世纪，圣公宗、卫理宗、长老会、改革宗、浸信会、路德宗、圣洁运动、五旬节派、天主教与独立差会都曾在中国建立机构。中国基督徒也形成了本土运动、联合机构与地方网络，并不只是照搬西方宗派边界。战争、革命、国家规制与移民又多次中断或重组这些传承。

**English:** Within mainland China's registered Protestant system, union worship began in 1958 and the China Christian Council was established in 1980; its institutional account describes a post-denominational period. That description does not cover every unregistered house church and does not erase theological differences or Catholic institutional history. In the diaspora, migration, bilingual ministry, campus conversion, interdenominational seminaries, parachurch ministries, and independent evangelical networks can make one congregation combine several inherited patterns.<br>
**中文：** 在中国大陆注册新教体系内，1958 年开始联合礼拜，1980 年成立中国基督教协会；该体系的制度叙事把此后称为后宗派时期。这一说法不涵盖每一间未登记家庭教会，也不会消除神学差异或天主教独立的制度史。在海外，移民、双语事工、校园归信、跨宗派神学院、超宗派机构与独立福音派网络，会使同一堂会组合多种传统特征。

**English:** This helps explain why a Chinese-language church may teach Reformed theology, practice Baptist baptism and congregational membership, use charismatic prayer, and call itself nondenominational. It does not establish a reliable national percentage for “Chinese fundamentalists.” The [Chinese church guide](./04_Chinese_and_Chinese_American_Churches.md#bottom-line) separates mainland, diaspora, and survey evidence in detail.<br>
**中文：** 这能解释为什么一间华语教会可能教导改革宗神学、实行浸信会式洗礼与会籍、采用灵恩祷告，又自称非宗派；却不能据此计算“华人基要派”的可靠全国比例。[华人教会导读](./04_Chinese_and_Chinese_American_Churches.md#bottom-line)对大陆、海外与调查证据作了更详细区分。

<a id="talarico-lineage"></a>
## Where James Talarico Fits in the Genealogy | James Talarico 位于哪条谱系

**English:** Talarico's institutional setting can be traced carefully as follows: sixteenth-century Reformed Christianity → Scottish and Anglo-American Presbyterianism → multiple American Presbyterian divisions and reunions → the 1983 reunion that formed the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) → St. Andrew's, a PC(USA) congregation in Mission Presbytery. A 2023 Austin Seminary profile documented his attendance there, and Austin Seminary lists his completed MATS in 2025.<br>
**中文：** Talarico 的制度背景可以谨慎地追溯为：十六世纪改革宗 → 苏格兰及英美长老会传统 → 美国长老会多次分裂与重组 → 1983 年联合成立的美国长老会（PC(USA)）→ 隶属 Mission Presbytery 的 PC(USA) 堂会 St. Andrew's。Austin Seminary 2023 年人物专访记录了他当时参加该堂会，该校也列出他于 2025 年完成 MATS 学位。

| Layer / 层级 | Historically accurate description / 符合历史的描述 | What must not be inferred / 不应推断什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Christian family / 基督教家族 | Western Christianity → Protestant → Reformed → Presbyterian / 西方基督教 → 新教 → 改革宗 → 长老会 | This broad descent does not determine every later doctrine or policy. / 这一大致继承线不能决定其全部后期教义或政策。 |
| Denomination / 宗派 | St. Andrew's appears in the official PC(USA) database; PC(USA) is classified as mainline Protestant in major U.S. surveys. / St. Andrew's 列于 PC(USA) 官方数据库；美国主要调查把 PC(USA) 归入主流新教。 | Attendance does not make Talarico a denominational spokesperson or prove formal membership. / 参加聚会不会使 Talarico 成为宗派发言人，也不能证明正式会籍。 |
| Local congregation / 地方堂会 | St. Andrew's describes itself as “Presbyterian & Progressive.” / St. Andrew's 自称“Presbyterian & Progressive”。 | A local self-description is not the formal name or binding creed of the whole PC(USA). / 地方堂会自述不是整个 PC(USA) 的正式名称或约束性信经。 |
| Theological and public orientation / 神学与公共取向 | His public record overlaps with progressive concerns such as inclusion, social justice, interfaith openness, and church-state separation. / 他的公开记录与接纳、社会公义、宗教间开放及政教分离等进步关切重叠。 | Policy positions do not establish his complete Christology, doctrine of salvation, Scripture, sacraments, or eschatology. / 政策立场不能建立其完整基督论、救恩论、圣经论、圣礼论或末世论。 |
| Self-description / 本人自述 | He has resisted choosing between “progressive Christianity” and “conservative Christianity,” instead using “biblical Christianity.” / 他曾拒绝在“进步基督教”与“保守基督教”之间二选一，而使用“合乎圣经的基督教”自称。 | Media labels and this historical map must not override his documented qualification of the label. / 媒体标签与本历史地图都不能盖过他对该标签已有记录的限定。 |

**English:** The historical point is not that Reformed theology mechanically produces progressive politics. Rather, one branch of American Presbyterianism developed inside the mainline institutional world, participated in modern ecumenical and public-theology debates, and contains local congregations that explicitly identify as progressive. Other Presbyterian branches and PC(USA) congregations can reach different conclusions while sharing older Reformed ancestry.<br>
**中文：** 这里的历史结论不是“改革宗神学必然产生进步政治”，而是美国长老会传统中的一支，在主流新教制度环境中发展，参与现代普世教会与公共神学争论，其中有地方堂会明确自认进步。其他长老会支派乃至 PC(USA) 内部堂会，可以共享更早的改革宗祖源，却得出不同结论。

**English:** For verified institutional and first-person evidence, continue to [James Talarico as a Case Study](./05_James_Talarico_as_a_Case_Study.md#verified-background).<br>
**中文：** 若要查看已经核实的机构资料与本人表达，请继续阅读 [James Talarico 个案研究](./05_James_Talarico_as_a_Case_Study.md#verified-background)。

<a id="living-inheritance"></a>
## How Old Disputes Remain Visible Today | 古代争议怎样延续到今天

| Present question / 今日问题 | Historical inheritance / 历史遗产 | Why Christians still differ / 为何仍有分歧 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who has final teaching authority? / 谁拥有最终教导权？ | Scripture, apostolic tradition, councils, pope, bishops, confessions, elders, and congregation received different institutional weights. / 圣经、使徒传统、大公会议、教宗、主教、信条、长老与地方堂会在各传统中权重不同。 | A disagreement about one doctrine may actually rest on different rules for deciding doctrine. / 一项教义分歧的底层，可能是决定教义的方法不同。 |
| What is the church? / 教会是什么？ | Ancient communion, territorial state church, voluntary gathered church, and independent network embody different answers. / 古代共融体、领地国家教会、自愿加入的信徒教会与独立网络，各自体现不同答案。 | Governance determines ordination, discipline, property, mission, and who may reverse a decision. / 治理制度决定按立、纪律、教产、宣教，以及谁有权改变决定。 |
| What happens in baptism and Eucharist? / 洗礼与圣餐中发生什么？ | Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Anabaptist, and Baptist histories preserve different sacramental accounts. / 天主教、东正教、路德宗、改革宗、圣公宗、再洗礼派与浸信会保留不同圣礼理解。 | The dispute concerns grace, church membership, Christ's presence, and not merely ritual preference. / 争议涉及恩典、教会成员身份与基督临在，并非单纯礼仪偏好。 |
| How should Scripture meet modern knowledge? / 圣经怎样面对现代知识？ | Historical criticism, science, fundamentalist resistance, evangelical scholarship, and contextual theologies developed in conflict and dialogue. / 历史批判、科学、基要主义抵抗、福音派学术与处境神学在冲突和对话中发展。 | Christians differ over genre, inspiration, historical claims, tradition, and the interpreter's social location. / 基督徒在文体、默示、历史主张、传统与解释者处境上看法不同。 |
| Is salvation mainly personal, social, or both? / 救恩主要是个人、社会，还是两者兼有？ | Revivalism stressed conversion; the Social Gospel stressed institutions; Black, liberation, and evangelical movements recombined both in different ways. / 复兴主义强调归信；社会福音强调制度；黑人神学、解放神学与福音派运动又以不同方式重组两者。 | Most traditions affirm both dimensions in some form but disagree about priority, method, and public policy. / 多数传统以某种方式同时承认两面，却在优先次序、方法与公共政策上分歧。 |
| How should Christians use political power? / 基督徒应怎样使用政治权力？ | Established churches, persecuted free churches, civil-rights churches, and modern political coalitions carry different memories. / 国教、受逼迫的自由教会、民权教会与现代政治联盟带着不同历史记忆。 | The same Bible can be read through memories of rule, exclusion, resistance, or minority protection. / 同一本圣经可能透过统治、排斥、抵抗或少数群体保护的不同记忆来阅读。 |

**English:** A historical explanation does not settle which position is true. It does make the disagreement more intelligible: people are not approaching a blank page, but inherited confessions, wounds, institutions, and reading habits. Good comparison asks both “What does this tradition teach?” and “What history made that question urgent?”<br>
**中文：** 历史解释不会自动裁定哪一方为真，却能使分歧更容易理解：人们面对的不是一张白纸，而是继承下来的信条、创伤、制度与阅读习惯。好的比较既问“这个传统教导什么”，也问“哪段历史使这个问题变得紧迫”。

## Sources and Interpretation Boundaries | 来源与解释边界

### Ancient Churches | 古代教会

- [Richard Price and Thomas Graumann, eds., *The Council of Ephesus of 431*](https://www.jstor.org/stable/jj.38238602) — a modern scholarly translation and study supporting the council's contested proceedings and later reconciliation; access may depend on a library subscription. / 现代学术译注，用于核实会议中相互竞争的程序及后来的和解；全文访问可能需要图书馆订阅。
- [World Council of Churches, Oriental Orthodox churches](https://www.oikoumene.org/church-families/orthodox-churches-oriental) — supports the 451 breach, the present church family, and later Christological dialogue; this is an ecumenical institutional overview. / 支持 451 年分裂、今日教会家族及后来的基督论对话；属于普世教会机构概述。
- [World Council of Churches, *Dictionary of the Ecumenical Movement*](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/2022-11/Dictionary%20of%20the%20Ecumenical%20Movement_Web.pdf) — the Assyrian Church of the East entry explains its Sasanian setting, East Syriac identity, gradual independence, and objection to the “Nestorian” label. / 其中亚述东方教会词条说明其萨珊帝国处境、东叙利亚语身份、逐步独立及对“聂斯托留派”称呼的异议。
- [Catholic–Assyrian Common Christological Declaration (1994)](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/speeches/1994/november/documents/hf_jp-ii_spe_19941111_dichiarazione-cristologica.html) and [Catholic–Coptic Common Declaration (1973)](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/speeches/1973/may/documents/hf_p-vi_spe_19730510_dichiarazione-comune.html) — primary ecumenical documents showing substantial shared Christological confession and the role of historical misunderstanding and non-theological factors; neither restores full communion. / 两份一手普世对话文件显示相当程度的共同基督论认信，以及历史误解和非神学因素的作用；均不等于已经恢复完全共融。
- [Orthodox Church in America, “The Great Schism”](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/church-history/eleventh-century/the-great-schism) and the [1965 Catholic-Orthodox declaration](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/speeches/1965/documents/hf_p-vi_spe_19651207_common-declaration.html) — an Orthodox historical account and a joint primary document supporting the long process, the limited scope of the 1054 censures, the 1204 trauma, and the 1965 removal of the excommunications. / 一份东正教历史说明与一份共同一手文件，支持长期分离过程、1054 年处分的有限范围、1204 年创伤及 1965 年撤去绝罚。

### Reformation and Revival | 宗教改革与复兴

- [Lutheran World Federation, Reformation milestones](https://lutheranworld.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/DTPW-2017_Reformation_Flyer_Map-EN.pdf), [PC(USA) Historical Society, *Foundations of the Faith*](https://pcusa.org/historical-society/exhibits/foundations-faith), and [Church of Scotland history](https://www.churchofscotland.org.uk/about-us/our-faith/history) — institutional introductions to Lutheran and Reformed milestones and the Calvin-Knox-Presbyterian line. / 介绍路德宗与改革宗节点，以及加尔文—诺克斯—长老会谱系的机构资料。
- [Church of England, history](https://www.churchofengland.org/about/history-church-england) — the church's account of pre-Reformation continuity, Henry VIII's catalyst, Edwardian theological reform, and the Elizabethan settlement. / 英格兰教会对改革前延续、亨利八世诱因、爱德华时期神学改革与伊丽莎白宗教安排的自述。
- [Mennonite World Conference, Anabaptist history](https://mwc-cmm.org/en/resources/history-mwc/) and the [Baptist World Alliance–Mennonite World Conference dialogue report](https://www.baptistworld.org/wp-content/uploads/dialogues/Final-Report-of-the-International-Dialogue-between-BWA-and-WMC.pdf) — support the 1525 Anabaptist milestone and the related but distinct beginnings of English Baptists. / 支持 1525 年再洗礼派节点，以及与其有关联却可区分的英格兰浸信会起源。
- [United Methodist Church, Wesleyan heritage](https://www.umc.org/en/who-we-are/what-we-believe/our-wesleyan-heritage) and [Church of the Nazarene, history](https://nazarene.org/our-history/) — first-party accounts of Methodist renewal and the nineteenth-century Holiness movement. / 卫理更新与十九世纪圣洁运动的第一方历史说明。
- [Assemblies of God, history](https://ag.org/About/About-the-AG/History) — a classical Pentecostal denomination's account of Holiness roots, Topeka, Azusa Street, and its 1914 organization; it represents that tradition's institutional perspective, not every Pentecostal origin story. / 古典五旬节宗派对圣洁运动根源、托皮卡、亚苏撒街及 1914 年组织化的说明；代表该宗派视角，不是全球所有五旬节起源的唯一叙事。

### Modern United States Categories | 美国近代分类

- [Presbyterian Historical Society, “The Fundamentalist-Modernist Conflict”](https://history.pcusa.org/sites/default/files/06Longfield.pdf) — scholarly denominational history showing that theology, ecclesiology, culture, science, urbanization, and institutional control interacted in the 1920s–1930s controversy. / 宗派史学术文章，说明神学、教会论、文化、科学、城市化与制度控制如何共同塑造 1920—1930 年代冲突。
- [George M. Marsden, “The Fundamentalist Offensive on Two Fronts: 1920–1921”](https://academic.oup.com/book/38935/chapter-abstract/338118904) and [“Fundamentalism as an American Phenomenon”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/church-history/article/abs/fundamentalism-as-an-american-phenomenon-a-comparison-with-english-evangelicalism/708CF096739F0DD966597AA238057752) — scholarly historical studies supporting the 1920 coalition label and distinguishing the early American fundamentalist coalition from the broader evangelical tradition. / 两项学术历史研究用于核实 1920 年的联盟称呼，并区分美国早期基要主义联盟与更广泛的福音派传统。
- [National Association of Evangelicals, *For the Health of the Nation*](https://www.nae.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/For-the-Health-of-the-Nation_spreads.pdf) and [*Christianity Today*, history](https://www.christianitytoday.com/about/history/) — first-party records for the 1942 NAE and 1956 magazine, including the effort to engage culture without choosing theological liberalism or reactionary fundamentalism. / 记录 1942 年 NAE 与 1956 年杂志创办的一手机构资料，包括既不走神学自由主义、也不走反应式基要主义而参与文化的目标。
- [Harvard Pluralism Project, “The Social Gospel”](https://pluralism.org/the-social-gospel), Oxford Research Encyclopedia's [“Liberalism in American Religious History”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62249/chapter-abstract/551413467), and [“Liberation Theologies in America”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62249/chapter-abstract/551408580) — academic overviews supporting the historical streams summarized under progressive Christianity; together they connect industrialization, religious liberalism, modern scholarship, social reform, civil rights, and contextual liberation theologies, but they do not define every progressive Christian. / 三项学术概述用于支持“进步基督教”一节所综合的历史脉络，把工业化、宗教自由主义、现代学术、社会改革、民权及处境化解放神学联系起来；它们仍不能定义每一位进步基督徒。
- [Pew Research Center, 2023–24 denominational classification](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/02/26/appendix-b-classification-of-protestant-denominations/) — the exact survey rules for evangelical, mainline, and historically Black Protestant categories; these are analytic classifications, not complete creeds. / 福音派、主流新教与历史性黑人新教的具体调查归类规则；它们是分析分类，不是完整信经。
- [African Methodist Episcopal Church, history](https://www.ame-church.com/our-church/our-history/) — the denomination's account of the Free African Society, discriminatory treatment, legal independence, and the organization of Black Methodist life. / 该宗派对自由非洲人协会、歧视经历、法律独立与黑人卫理宗组织化的历史说明。

### Chinese Pathways and the Talarico Case | 华人路径与 Talarico 个案

- [World Council of Churches, *The Future of Mission Cooperation*](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/TheFutureOfMissionCooperation_Web.pdf) — includes the Nanjing Union Theological Seminary account of 1958 union worship, the 1980 China Christian Council, and the registered Protestant post-denominational system; it does not describe every house church. / 包含金陵协和神学院对 1958 年联合礼拜、1980 年中国基督教协会与注册新教后宗派体制的说明；不代表所有家庭教会。
- [PC(USA), Presbyterian history](https://pcusa.org/about-pcusa/who-we-are/history), [PC(USA) Church Trends: St Andrews](https://church-trends.pcusa.org/church/8634/overview/), [St. Andrew's About Us](https://www.staopen.org/aboutus), [Austin Seminary's 2023 Talarico profile](https://www.austinseminary.edu/faces-of-austin-seminary/~board/facesofaustinseminary/post/presbyterian-church-inspires-action-in-the-public-arena), and [Austin Seminary Alumni News](https://www.austinseminary.edu/alumni/alumni-news) — separate the 1983 denominational reunion, official congregational affiliation, local progressive self-description, Talarico's documented attendance, and his MATS '25 degree record. / 分别核实 1983 年宗派联合、堂会正式隶属、地方堂会进步自述、Talarico 当时有记录的参与及其 MATS '25 学位记录。

**English:** Institutional sources are best for official self-description and dates but can present history from their own tradition's viewpoint. Ecumenical agreements establish what their signatories jointly said, not universal scholarly consensus. Survey categories describe populations under stated coding rules, not the inner faith of individuals. This guide therefore pairs sources from more than one tradition and marks where interpretation must stop.<br>
**中文：** 机构来源最适合核实官方自述与日期，却可能从本传统视角讲述历史；普世对话协议能够证明签署方共同说了什么，却不等于所有学者都同意；调查分类按既定编码规则描述人口，也不能进入个人内心。因此，本导读搭配多个传统的来源，并标明解释必须停在哪里。
