<a id="bottom-line"></a>
## Bottom Line | 核心结论

**English:** None of the national surveys discussed on this page asks enough questions to show that most Chinese people or most Chinese Christians are historical fundamentalists. Historical and ethnographic research nevertheless shows that congregations with evangelical, theologically conservative, and/or nondenominational or independent characteristics have been influential in parts of Chinese American Protestant history. These are overlapping descriptions, not mutually exclusive groups. Chinese Christians also worship in Catholic communities and, within Protestantism, in mainline, Baptist, Presbyterian/Reformed, Pentecostal, independent or nondenominational, and other church bodies; evangelical, charismatic, conservative, and progressive orientations may cross several of those structures.<br>
**中文：** 本页讨论的全国调查都没有提出足够问题，无法证明“多数华人”或“多数华人基督徒”属于历史基要主义。历史研究与民族志仍显示：兼具福音派、神学保守和／或非宗派／独立特征的堂会，在美国华人新教历史的部分生态中具有显著影响。这些是彼此重叠的描述，不是互斥群体。华人基督徒也在天主教群体中敬拜；在新教内部，则分布于主流新教、浸信会、长老会／改革宗、五旬节、独立／非宗派及其他教会组织，而福音派、灵恩、保守或进步取向又可能跨越其中多个组织结构。

**English:** “Evangelical,” “theologically conservative,” “nondenominational,” and “fundamentalist” are not interchangeable. Race or language also does not establish a person’s doctrine, political affiliation, character, or spiritual life.<br>
**中文：** “福音派”“神学保守”“非宗派”与“基要派”不能互换；族裔或语言也不能证明某个人的教义、政治归属、品格或灵命状况。

## Four Different Populations and Denominators | 四个不同人群与分母

| Population / 人群 | Correct denominator / 正确分母 | What it does not represent / 不能代表什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adults in mainland China / 中国大陆成年人 | Adults sampled by a named mainland survey in a stated year / 某年某项大陆调查覆盖的成年人 | Chinese citizens abroad, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, or the global diaspora / 海外中国公民、港澳台或全球华人移民 |
| U.S. Chinese-origin adults / 美国华裔成年人 | U.S. adults who self-identify as ethnically Chinese under the survey’s rules / 按调查规则自认华人族裔的美国成年人 | PRC nationals only, immigrants only, or churchgoers only / 仅中国大陆国籍者、仅移民或仅教会参与者 |
| Chinese American Protestants / 美国华裔新教徒 | The Protestant subset of a U.S. Chinese-origin sample / 美国华裔样本中的新教子样本 | All Chinese Americans or all Chinese Christians / 全体美国华裔或全体华人基督徒 |
| Global Chinese-speaking congregations / 全球华语堂会 | Congregations defined by a stated country, language, network, or membership rule / 按明确国家、语言、网络或会员规则界定的堂会 | A single worldwide ethnic or denominational population / 一个可统一计算的全球族裔或宗派人口 |

**English:** A Mandarin- or Cantonese-speaking congregation may include people from mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, North America, and mixed-language families. Language of worship is therefore not a census category for nationality, ethnicity, denomination, or theology.<br>
**中文：** 一间普通话或粤语堂会可能同时包括中国大陆、台湾、香港、东南亚、北美背景的人和多语家庭。因此，敬拜语言不是国籍、族裔、宗派或神学的人口统计类别。

**English:** None of the sources reviewed for this guide supplies a worldwide denominational distribution for “Chinese-speaking Christians.” A global percentage without a defined country, year, sampling frame, and denominator should be treated as an estimate or advocacy claim, not as a measured fact.<br>
**中文：** 本导读复核的资料中，没有一项给出“全球华语基督徒”的统一宗派分布。如果一个全球比例没有界定国家、年份、抽样框和分母，就应把它视为估算或倡议性说法，而非已测量事实。

## Current U.S. Chinese-Origin Data | 当前美国华裔数据

**English:** The strongest current national evidence is Pew Research Center’s 2022–23 Survey of Asian Americans. It was fielded from July 5, 2022, through January 27, 2023, and included 7,006 Asian American adults, of whom 1,777 were classified as Chinese. The 95% margin of sampling error for the Chinese estimates is plus or minus 3.6 percentage points.<br>
**中文：** 当前最强的全国性证据来自 Pew Research Center 的 2022–23 年美国亚裔调查。调查于 2022 年 7 月 5 日至 2023 年 1 月 27 日进行，共访问 7,006 名美国亚裔成年人，其中 1,777 人被归为华裔。华裔结果在 95% 置信水平下的抽样误差为 ±3.6 个百分点。

| Religious identity among all U.S. Chinese-origin adults / 全体美国华裔成年人的宗教身份 | Share / 比例 |
| --- | ---: |
| Christian, net / 基督徒合计 | 23% |
| Born-again or evangelical Protestant / 重生或福音派新教徒 | 13% |
| Nonevangelical Protestant / 非福音派新教徒 | 6% |
| Catholic / 天主教徒 | 5% |
| Religiously unaffiliated / 无宗教归属 | 56% |

**English:** Every percentage in this table uses all U.S. Chinese-origin adults as the denominator, not Chinese Christians or Chinese Protestants. “Net Christian” includes small Christian groups that Pew could not show separately, and rows may not add exactly because of rounding.<br>
**中文：** 表中每个比例的分母都是全体美国华裔成年人，不是华人基督徒或华人新教徒。“基督徒合计”还包括 Pew 因样本不足而未单列的小型基督教群体，各行也可能因四舍五入而无法精确相加。

<a id="survey-method"></a>
### Survey Method and the Meaning of “Chinese” | 调查方法与“华裔”的含义

**English:** Pew describes this as a nationally representative, multilingual survey. It used a national residential address sample, supplemented by surname-list samples, and offered online or paper questionnaires in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Hindi, Korean, Tagalog, and Vietnamese.<br>
**中文：** Pew 将这项研究定义为全国代表性的多语调查。它使用全国住宅地址样本，并用姓氏名单样本补充；受访者可在线或使用纸本问卷，语言包括英语、简体中文、繁体中文、印地语、韩语、他加禄语和越南语。

**English:** “Chinese” is an ethnic self-identification, not nationality or citizenship. It does not mean only current or former citizens of the People’s Republic of China, and Pew explicitly includes respondents who identify as Taiwanese in this category.<br>
**中文：** “华裔”来自族裔自我认定，不是国籍或公民身份。它不仅指现任或曾经的中华人民共和国公民，而且 Pew 明确把自认台湾背景的受访者纳入此类。

**English:** Pew’s table labels the 13% category “born-again or evangelical Protestant.” That is a survey identity category, not a measurement of historical Protestant fundamentalism, biblical interpretation, separatism, or adherence to a particular statement of faith.<br>
**中文：** Pew 在表格中把 13% 这一类称为“重生或福音派新教徒”。它是调查身份类别，不是对历史新教基要主义、释经方法、分离主义或特定信仰告白认同度的测量。

<a id="christian-connection"></a>
## What the 42% “Christian Connection” Means | 42% “与基督教有联系”是什么口径

**English:** Pew reports that 42% of U.S. Chinese-origin adults had a connection to Christianity under a broader measure: 23% identified Christianity as their religion, 4% identified with another religion or did not answer the religion item and nevertheless felt close to Christianity, and 15% were religiously unaffiliated but felt close to Christianity for reasons such as family background or culture.<br>
**中文：** Pew 在一个更广的口径下报告，42% 的美国华裔成年人与基督教有某种联系：23% 把基督教视为自己的宗教；4% 认同其他宗教或没有回答宗教身份问题，却感到与基督教亲近；15% 没有宗教归属，但因家庭背景或文化等原因感到与基督教亲近。

**English:** The 42% figure is not the Christian share. “Feeling close” does not by itself establish Christian belief, baptism, church attendance, conversion, or willingness to receive evangelism. It is best read as religious or cultural affinity under Pew’s stated question.<br>
**中文：** 42% 不是基督徒比例。“感到亲近”本身不能证明基督教信仰、受洗、聚会、归信或接受传福音的意愿；它最适合被理解为 Pew 在特定问法下测得的宗教或文化亲近感。

<a id="historical-snapshot"></a>
## The 2012 Detail Is a Historical Snapshot | 2012 年细分只是历史快照

**English:** Pew’s 2012 Asian-American survey gives more denominational detail, but it is too old and its Chinese Protestant subgroup is too small to describe the current national distribution. The full survey interviewed 3,511 Asian American adults, including 728 Chinese Americans; the Chinese-origin estimate had a margin of error of plus or minus 5.4 percentage points. The denominational-family table was based on only 171 Chinese American Protestants.<br>
**中文：** Pew 的 2012 年亚裔调查提供了更细的宗派资料，但它年代已久，华裔新教子样本也过小，不能用来描述当前全美分布。全部调查访问了 3,511 名美国亚裔成年人，其中华裔 728 人；华裔估计的误差为 ±5.4 个百分点。宗派家族表仅以 171 名华裔新教徒为基础。

| 2012 result among Chinese American Protestants / 2012 年华裔新教徒结果 | Reported share / 报告比例 |
| --- | ---: |
| Described themselves as born-again or evangelical / 自认重生或福音派 | 58% |
| Nondenominational or independent / 非宗派或独立教会 | 25% |
| Baptist / 浸信会 | 15% |
| Presbyterian / 长老会 | 10% |
| Pentecostal and/or charismatic self-description / 自认五旬节和／或灵恩背景 | 21% |

**English:** These rows are not mutually exclusive: the evangelical and Pentecostal or charismatic figures are self-descriptions, while Baptist, Presbyterian, and nondenominational are denominational-family answers. Pew’s 2012 method also placed Protestants who answered “no” or did not answer the born-again or evangelical question into a “mainline” analytic category; that does not prove membership in a historically mainline denomination.<br>
**中文：** 这些行不是互斥类别：福音派与五旬节／灵恩比例来自自我描述，浸信会、长老会与非宗派则是宗派家族回答。Pew 的 2012 年方法还把对“重生或福音派”问题回答“否”或未作答的新教徒归入“主流新教”分析类别；这不能证明他们属于历史上的主流新教宗派。

**English:** The appropriate use of these numbers is to document that evangelical self-identification, independent churches, Baptists, Presbyterians, and charismatic currents were all visible in a 2012 national sample. They should not be presented as current percentages or as evidence that Chinese Christians were uniformly conservative.<br>
**中文：** 这组数据的合适用法，是说明福音派自我认定、独立教会、浸信会、长老会和灵恩脉络都出现在 2012 年全国样本中；不应把它们当成当前比例，也不能用它们证明华人基督徒的神学整齐地保守。

<a id="mainland-china"></a>
## Mainland China: What National Surveys Support | 中国大陆：全国调查能支持什么

**English:** Pew’s 2023 synthesis of mainland surveys reports that roughly 2% of Chinese adults in the 2018 Chinese General Social Survey identified Christianity as their formal religious belief. Among that Christian-identified group, roughly 90% were Protestant and the remainder were mostly Catholic. Pew translated the weighted estimates into approximately 20 million Christian adults, including approximately 18 million Protestants.<br>
**中文：** Pew 在 2023 年对大陆调查的综合分析中报告，2018 年中国综合社会调查约有 2% 的中国成年人把基督教认定为自己的正式宗教信仰。在这些自认基督徒中，约 90% 是新教徒，其余主要是天主教徒。Pew 将加权估计换算为约 2,000 万名成年基督徒，其中约 1,800 万人是新教徒。

**English:** Different question wording produces different measures. Pew notes that the 2018 World Values Survey found 2% who said they believed in Christianity, while the 2016 China Family Panel Studies found 3% who said they belonged to Christianity. Formal identity, belonging, belief, practice, and attendance are related but not interchangeable.<br>
**中文：** 问题措辞不同会产生不同指标。Pew 指出，2018 年世界价值观调查有 2% 表示信仰基督教，2016 年中国家庭追踪调查则有 3% 表示属于基督教。正式身份、归属、信念、实践与聚会参与彼此相关，却不能互换。

**English:** Each regular CGSS wave has about 12,000 respondents and usually samples at least 28 of mainland China’s 31 provincial-level units; the 2018 wave included additional religion measures. Pew warns that the 2021 wave covered only 19 units during the pandemic and is not directly comparable with earlier waves.<br>
**中文：** 常规每轮 CGSS 约有 12,000 名受访者，通常覆盖中国大陆 31 个省级行政区中的至少 28 个；2018 年轮还加入了额外宗教指标。Pew 提醒，2021 年轮在疫情期间仅覆盖 19 个行政区，不宜与早期轮次直接比较。

**English:** These surveys support a broad Protestant-versus-Catholic distinction among adults who formally identify as Christian. They do not provide nationally reliable shares for theological or cross-denominational labels such as evangelical, fundamentalist, Reformed, or charismatic. Nor do they measure organizational and legal settings such as registered congregations versus house churches as if those were theological traditions.<br>
**中文：** 这些调查能支持的，是正式自认基督徒中新教与天主教的宽泛区分；它们没有给出福音派、基要派、改革宗或灵恩等神学与跨宗派标签的全国可靠比例，也没有把注册堂会与家庭教会等组织及法律处境当作神学传统来测量。

<a id="measurement-limits"></a>
### Measurement Limits | 测量边界

**English:** Formal-religion questions may miss people who participate in unregistered congregations, understand religion outside the survey’s institutional categories, or hesitate to disclose religious identity. Pew cites evidence that house-church participants may be less willing to participate in surveys or disclose Christian identity, but the size and direction of any resulting national undercount cannot be measured precisely from the available data.<br>
**中文：** “正式宗教”问题可能遗漏参加未注册堂会、不按调查制度类别理解宗教，或对披露宗教身份有顾虑的人。Pew 引用证据指出，家庭教会参与者可能更不愿参加调查或披露基督徒身份，但现有资料无法精确测量因此造成的全国漏计规模与方向。

**English:** A 2025 peer-reviewed analysis of 19 nationally representative surveys since the early 2000s likewise found that recent surveys place formal Christian identification near 2% and do not show clear continued growth. This trend finding still does not supply an evangelical or fundamentalist denominator.<br>
**中文：** 2025 年一项同行评议研究综合了 2000 年代初以来的 19 项全国代表性调查，同样发现近年调查中正式自认基督徒的比例约为 2%，而且没有显示继续增长的清楚证据。这个趋势结论仍然没有提供福音派或基要派的分母。

<a id="post-denominational"></a>
## The Registered Protestant Post-Denominational System | 注册新教的后宗派体制

**English:** Western denominational labels do not map neatly onto mainland China’s registered Protestant structure. A report published by the World Council of Churches, including a contribution from Nanjing Union Theological Seminary, describes union worship beginning in 1958, the establishment of the China Christian Council in 1980, the ending of the former denominations’ organizational structures inside that system, and entry into a “post-denominational” period.<br>
**中文：** 西方宗派标签不能整齐套用到中国大陆的注册新教结构。世界基督教教会联合会出版的一份报告——其中包含金陵协和神学院的报告——将该体制的发展描述为：1958 年开始联合礼拜，1980 年成立中国基督教协会，原有宗派的组织结构在该体制内停止运作，并进入“后宗派”时期。

**English:** This is an institutional self-description of the registered Protestant system associated with the China Christian Council and Three-Self Patriotic Movement. It does not describe every unregistered or house church, and it does not apply to the distinct institutional history of Catholicism in China.<br>
**中文：** 这是中国基督教协会与三自爱国运动相关注册新教体制的制度性自我描述，不能代表每一间未注册或家庭教会，也不适用于中国天主教不同的制度历史。

**English:** Pew separately distinguishes officially sanctioned Protestant churches and meeting points from unauthorized Protestant gatherings. Official venue counts exclude at least some house churches and unauthorized meeting points, so venue statistics cannot be converted into a complete denominational census.<br>
**中文：** Pew 另行区分了获官方认可的新教教堂、聚会点与未获认可的新教聚会。官方场所数据至少排除部分家庭教会与未注册聚会点，因此不能把场所数量换算为完整的宗派普查。

<a id="north-american-history"></a>
## Why Evangelical and Independent Churches Became Influential in North America | 福音派与独立教会为何在北美具有影响

**English:** Historical and ethnographic studies describe Chinese immigrant congregations as both religious communities and institutions of migration. They may provide worship, evangelism, language continuity, friendship, family support, youth programs, and help navigating a new society. These functions can make the congregation an important site of adult conversion and community formation without proving that all members share one theology.<br>
**中文：** 历史与民族志研究把华人移民堂会同时视为宗教群体与移民机构。堂会可以提供敬拜、传福音、语言延续、友谊、家庭支持、青少年项目，以及适应新社会的帮助。这些功能可使堂会成为成年人归信与建立社群的重要场所，却不能证明所有成员共享一种神学。

**English:** [Fenggang Yang's 1999 study of Chinese Christians in America](https://www.psupress.org/books/titles/0-271-01916-6.html) emphasizes adult conversion, immigrant adaptation, ethnic identity, and the prominence of evangelical Protestant settings. Carolyn Chen's later ethnography of Taiwanese immigrants likewise examines conversion after migration. These works explain mechanisms and lived experience; neither is a current national denominational census.<br>
**中文：** 杨凤岗 1999 年对美国华人基督徒的研究，重点讨论成年归信、移民适应、族裔身份与福音派新教环境的突出影响。Carolyn Chen 后来对台湾移民的民族志也研究了移民后归信。这些作品用来解释机制与生活经验，却都不是当前的全国宗派普查。

**English:** [Russell Jeung's study of more than fifty Chinese and Japanese congregations in the San Francisco Bay Area](https://www.ubcpress.ca/faithful-generations) found multiple evangelical and mainline ways of forming Asian American Christian identity. Its regional evidence is valuable precisely because it shows diversity, but it should not be projected onto every Chinese congregation in North America.<br>
**中文：** Russell Jeung 2005 年对旧金山湾区五十多间华裔与日裔堂会的研究，发现福音派与主流新教会用不同方式建构亚裔美国人的基督徒身份。这项区域证据的价值正在于它显示了多样性，但不应被推广到北美每一间华人堂会。

**English:** Transnational origins also matter. Chinese-language churches may carry influences from mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, earlier Western missions, campus ministries, denominational agencies, and independent evangelical networks. These pathways help explain why Baptist, Presbyterian, Reformed, charismatic, and nondenominational patterns may coexist in one metropolitan area.<br>
**中文：** 跨国来源也很重要。华语教会可能承载中国大陆、台湾、香港、东南亚、早期西方宣教、校园事工、宗派机构与独立福音派网络的影响。这些路径可以解释为何浸信会、长老会、改革宗、灵恩派与非宗派模式可在同一大都会区并存。

<a id="evidence-strength"></a>
### Evidence Strength | 证据强度

| Evidence type / 证据类型 | Appropriate use / 合适用法 | Limit / 限制 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| National probability survey / 全国概率抽样 | Estimate a defined population under stated questions / 估计特定问法下的明确人口 | Does not reveal every member’s theology / 不能显示每位成员的神学 |
| Regional congregational study / 区域堂会研究 | Show local institutions, variation, and mechanisms / 展示地方机构、差异与机制 | Cannot produce a national percentage / 不能产生全国比例 |
| Ethnography or case study / 民族志或个案研究 | Explain lived experience and conversion pathways / 解释生活经验与归信路径 | Cannot represent all Chinese Christians / 不能代表全体华人基督徒 |
| Official institutional statement / 机构官方说明 | Establish how that institution describes its structure / 确立该机构如何描述自身结构 | Does not describe every unaffiliated congregation / 不能描述每个未隶属堂会 |

<a id="fundamentalism-boundary"></a>
## Why “Most Chinese Christians Are Fundamentalists” Is Unsupported | 为何不能说“多数华人基督徒是基要派”

**English:** Historical Protestant fundamentalism arose from early twentieth-century conflicts with modernist theology and often included institutional separation from churches judged to have compromised. The Chinese phrase “持守基要真理” must not be translated automatically as “fundamentalist”: the phrase alone does not establish identification with that historical movement, its separatism, or its institutional culture. The speaker’s own confession and context have to supply that evidence.<br>
**中文：** 历史上的新教基要主义形成于二十世纪初与现代主义神学的冲突，并常包含与被认为已妥协教会保持制度分离的立场。不可把“持守基要真理”自动译成 Fundamentalist：单凭这个短语，不能证明说话者认同该历史运动、其分离主义或制度文化。这需要从说话者自己的信仰告白与语境中找证据。

**English:** None of the national surveys discussed on this page asks enough questions to classify Chinese respondents as historical fundamentalists. The 2022–23 Pew survey supports the statement that 13% of all U.S. Chinese-origin adults fall in its born-again or evangelical Protestant category; it does not support relabeling that group as fundamentalist.<br>
**中文：** 本页讨论的全国调查都没有提出足够问题，无法把华裔受访者归为历史基要主义者。2022–23 年 Pew 调查可以支持“全体美国华裔成年人中有 13% 属于其重生或福音派新教类别”，却不支持把该类别改称为“基要派”。

**English:** The mainland surveys support only broad Christian, Protestant, and Catholic identities. The 2012 U.S. denominational breakdown is a dated, small-subgroup snapshot. Global Chinese-speaking congregations have no single representative denominator. These three evidence limits block any responsible calculation of “the percentage of Chinese Christians who are fundamentalist.”<br>
**中文：** 大陆调查只能支持宽泛的基督教、新教与天主教身份；2012 年美国宗派细分是过时的小子样本快照；全球华语堂会也没有一个统一代表性分母。这三项证据限制，使我们无法负责任地计算“华人基督徒中有多少是基要派”。

<a id="careful-language"></a>
### Recommended Wording | 建议用语

> **English:** Historical studies describe some influential Chinese American Protestant congregations as evangelical, theologically conservative, and/or nondenominational or independent. Those labels overlap and do not describe every Chinese church. None of the national surveys discussed here shows that most Chinese people or most Chinese Christians are historical fundamentalists.<br>
> **中文：** 历史研究把美国华人新教中一些具有影响力的堂会描述为福音派、神学保守和／或非宗派／独立教会。这些标签会彼此重叠，也不能代表所有华人教会。本页讨论的全国调查都不能证明多数华人或多数华人基督徒属于历史基要主义。

**English:** Theology, denominational membership, ethnicity, worship language, and political behavior should be treated as separate variables. If a claim crosses from one variable to another, it needs direct evidence rather than an ethnic stereotype.<br>
**中文：** 神学、宗派会员身份、族裔、敬拜语言与政治行为应当被视为不同变量。从一个变量推断另一个变量时，需要直接证据，不能依靠族裔刻板印象。
