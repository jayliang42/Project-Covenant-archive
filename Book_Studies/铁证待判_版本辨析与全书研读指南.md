<a id="purpose"></a>
## Purpose and Why It Is Recommended | 用途与推荐理由

This book family asks whether Christian claims about Scripture, Jesus, and the resurrection have public reasons that can be examined, rather than requiring a leap with no evidence. Its great strength is breadth: it gathers manuscript, canon, archaeology, prophecy, historical-Jesus, resurrection, worldview, and objection material into one reference work.

这一书系追问：基督教关于圣经、耶稣与复活的宣称，是否有可以公开查考的理由，而不是要求人毫无根据地跳进信仰。它最大的长处是范围广，把抄本、正典、考古、预言、历史耶稣、复活、世界观与异议汇入一部参考书。

**Why recommended:** it gives a reader a map of the major apologetic questions, names sources worth tracing, and is useful when preparing a focused discussion or lesson. It is best treated as a starting reference, not as the final verdict of every academic debate.

**推荐理由：** 它能给读者一张主要护教问题的地图，指出值得继续追踪的资料，也适合专题讨论或教学预备。最好的用法是把它当作查考起点，而不是每一场学术争论的最终判决。

It is not the best first book for every reader. Someone who has never read a Gospel should normally read a Gospel alongside it; someone facing grief, coercion, or a personal crisis needs patient care, not a pile of arguments. Evidence serves love and truth when it clarifies a real question without pressuring the person asking it.

它并不是每位读者都应先读的第一本书。尚未读过福音书的人，通常应同时直接阅读一卷福音书；正经历悲伤、压力或个人危机的人，需要耐心陪伴，而不是一叠论证。证据若能澄清真实问题，又不向提问者施压，才是在服事爱与真理。

<a id="argument-map"></a>
## Whole-Book Argument Map | 全书论证地图

Across editions, the broad movement is from the possibility of rational faith to the trustworthiness of biblical sources, then to Jesus' identity and resurrection, and finally to the reader's judgment and response. Later editions expand, rearrange, and update this movement.

不同版本虽有增删与重排，大体都从“信仰是否可以有理性根据”出发，进入圣经来源是否可信，再聚焦耶稣的身份与复活，最后把判断与回应交给读者。后续版本扩充并更新了这条路线。

```text
Worldview and truth | 世界观与真理
        ↓
Text, canon, and transmission | 文本、正典与传抄
        ↓
Jesus in history and the resurrection claim | 历史中的耶稣与复活宣称
        ↓
Old Testament context, archaeology, and prophecy | 旧约处境、考古与预言
        ↓
Judgment, trust, and response | 判断、信靠与回应
```

The word “evidence” covers different kinds of reasoning in this chain. A manuscript is physical evidence of a copied text; an inscription may establish a name or institution; a philosophical argument tests coherence; a confession expresses theological conviction. These are related, but they are not interchangeable.

这条链中的“证据”包含不同类型的推理。抄本是某段文字曾被复制的物质见证；铭文可能确认人名或制度；哲学论证检验观点是否一致；信仰告白表达神学确信。它们彼此有关，却不能互相替代。

<a id="bible-evidence"></a>
## Theme 1: Bible, Canon, and Manuscripts | 主题一：圣经、正典与抄本

The book gathers arguments about the Bible's literary character, canon formation, manuscript transmission, historical setting, and influence. Reading responsibly requires separating these questions instead of treating “the Bible is reliable” as one indivisible claim.

本书汇集了圣经的文学特征、正典形成、抄本传递、历史处境与影响等论证。负责任的读法应把这些问题拆开，而不是把“圣经可靠”当成一项不可分割的单一主张。

**Canon:** historical sources can show which communities read, disputed, listed, and copied particular books at particular times. They do not turn one early list into a universal decision, nor do they make inspiration an archaeological conclusion.

**正典：** 历史材料可以说明某一时期的某些群体阅读、争议、列举和抄写了哪些书卷，却不能把一份早期清单变成全教会统一决议，也不能把“默示”化成考古学结论。

**Manuscripts:** numerous and early witnesses give textual critics more data for comparing readings and reconstructing earlier forms of a text. A large witness count does not mean there are no variants, and a variant count is not the number of corrupted doctrines. Always ask what languages, dates, fragments, and counting method are included.

**抄本：** 数量较多、年代较早的见证，让文本批判学者能比较异读并重建更早的文本形态。见证多不等于没有异文；异文数量也不等于“被破坏的教义数量”。引用数字时，应问它包含哪些语言、年代、残片及计数方法。

**Historical reliability:** a text can accurately preserve some names, places, customs, or events while other claims remain debated. Reliability is assessed passage by passage and source by source; it is not an all-or-nothing switch.

**历史可靠性：** 一份文本可能准确保存部分人名、地名、习俗或事件，而其他主张仍有争议。可靠性应按段落与来源逐项评估，不是只能全盘接受或全盘拒绝的开关。

Audit questions: What is the oldest relevant witness? Is it a whole book or a fragment? What reading differs? Does the cited catalog still use the same date? What theological conclusion is added after the textual observation?

审计问题：最早的相关见证是什么？它是整卷还是残片？异读究竟在哪里？当前馆藏目录是否仍采用同一定年？文本观察之后又加入了什么神学结论？

<a id="jesus-evidence"></a>
## Theme 2: Historical Jesus and Resurrection | 主题二：历史耶稣与复活

The book moves from the historical existence and claims of Jesus to his deity and resurrection. The key discipline is to distinguish the data, the historical inference, and the theological interpretation.

本书从耶稣的历史存在与宣称推进到祂的神性与复活。这里最重要的纪律，是区分材料、历史推论与神学解释。

Historical inquiry can compare the dates, genres, dependence, social setting, and transmission of Christian and non-Christian sources. It can ask how early resurrection traditions appear, what the sources claim, how followers changed, and which alternative explanations fit the data. It cannot reproduce a unique ancient event in a laboratory.

历史研究可以比较基督教与非基督教来源的年代、文类、依赖关系、社会处境与传递过程；也可以追问复活传统出现多早、来源声称什么、跟随者发生了什么改变，以及各种替代解释如何契合材料。它不能在实验室里重演一件独特的古代事件。

“God raised Jesus from the dead” is not merely another archaeological label. It joins historical claims to a theological explanation of God's action. A careful argument should therefore say which facts it considers best established, why it prefers one explanation, and where faith and worldview enter the judgment.

“神使耶稣从死里复活”不只是又一个考古标签，而是把历史宣称与神行动的神学解释连接起来。因此严谨论证应说明：哪些事实被认为最稳固、为何偏好某个解释，以及信仰与世界观从哪里进入判断。

Audit questions: How close is the source to the events? Is “independent attestation” demonstrated or merely counted? Are empty-tomb, appearance, and conversion traditions being distinguished? Has the strongest alternative explanation been addressed fairly?

审计问题：来源距离事件有多近？所谓“独立见证”是经过证明，还是只按数量累计？空墓、显现与归信传统有没有分开讨论？是否公平回应了最强的替代解释？

<a id="old-testament-evidence"></a>
## Theme 3: Old Testament, Archaeology, and Prophecy | 主题三：旧约、考古与预言

Archaeology can illuminate settlements, rulers, languages, warfare, trade, worship, administration, and textual cultures. An artifact that confirms a king, city, or institution strengthens that point of historical context; it does not automatically confirm every event connected with the same name or place.

考古可以照明聚落、君王、语言、战争、贸易、敬拜、行政与文本文化。某件器物若确认一位君王、一座城或一种制度，就加强了该项历史处境；它不会自动确认与同一人名或地名相连的所有事件。

Prophecy arguments require more than a verbal resemblance. Check the date and textual form of the earlier passage, its meaning in its own context, the specificity of the proposed prediction, the independence and date of the fulfillment source, and whether several “predictions” are being counted from one literary unit.

预言论证不能只靠字面相似。应核对早期经文的年代与文本形态、它在原处境中的意义、所谓预告是否具体、应验来源是否独立及成于何时，以及多个“预言”是否其实来自同一个文学单元。

Christian canonical interpretation may legitimately read an Old Testament passage again in light of Christ. That is a theological and literary claim about the unity of Scripture. It should not be mislabeled as though an excavated object independently supplied the Christian interpretation.

基督教正典诠释可以在基督的光中重新阅读旧约经文；这是关于整本圣经统一性的神学与文学主张，不应被误标成“某件出土器物独立给出了基督教解释”。

Audit questions: What is the artifact number and current catalog? Was it found in a controlled excavation? Does the inscription contain the claimed name? What exact biblical claim does it illuminate, and where must the inference stop?

审计问题：器物编号与当前目录是什么？是否出自可控发掘？铭文是否真的包含所声称的人名？它具体照明了哪一项圣经主张，推论必须停在哪里？

<a id="truth-evidence"></a>
## Theme 4: Truth, Worldview, and Objections | 主题四：真理、世界观与异议

Later editions explicitly address truth, worldview assumptions, difficult passages, and modern skeptical objections. This matters because no one evaluates evidence without prior beliefs about what kinds of events are possible, what counts as explanation, and how testimony should be weighed.

后续版本明确讨论真理、世界观前提、难解经文与现代怀疑论异议。这很重要，因为每个人评估证据时，都带着关于“什么事件可能发生、什么算解释、证言应如何衡量”的先在看法。

Worldview analysis should expose assumptions on every side, including Christian assumptions. It should not dismiss an objection merely by labeling the critic “naturalist,” nor claim victory because one skeptical argument fails. A positive Christian case still needs its own reasons.

世界观分析应揭示各方的前提，也包括基督徒自己的前提。不能只给批评者贴上“自然主义者”标签便取消异议，也不能因一个怀疑论论证失败就宣布胜利；基督教的正面主张仍需给出自己的理由。

The tone also belongs to the argument. First Peter 3:15–16 joins a reasoned answer with gentleness and respect. A technically correct claim used to shame, corner, or misrepresent a person has failed the book's evangelistic purpose.

论证也包含态度。彼得前书 3:15–16 把有理由的回答与温柔、敬重连在一起。即使某句话技术上正确，若被用来羞辱、逼迫或歪曲他人，也已经偏离本书的福音目的。

<a id="evidence-audit"></a>
## Evidence Audit Method | 证据审计法

Use the following layers whenever the book presents an impressive quotation, number, artifact, or conclusion.

当书中出现看似有力的引文、数字、器物或结论时，可以逐层检查：

| Layer | Record this | Stop line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Claim / 主张** | Write the exact proposition in your own words.<br>用自己的话写出可以判断真假的具体命题。 | Do not audit a vague slogan.<br>不要审计一个模糊口号。 |
| **2. Source / 来源** | Record author, work, edition, page, artifact or manuscript ID, and link.<br>记录作者、作品、版本、页码、器物或抄本编号及链接。 | A quotation without a traceable source remains unverified.<br>无法追溯的引文仍属未核实。 |
| **3. Date / 年代** | Separate event date, composition date, copy date, excavation date, and publication date.<br>区分事件、成文、抄写、发掘与研究发表年代。 | One date must not silently replace another.<br>不能让一种年代暗中替代另一种年代。 |
| **4. Direct support / 直接支持** | State the narrowest fact the source directly establishes.<br>写出来源直接建立的最窄事实。 | Do not begin with the desired theological conclusion.<br>不要从预期的神学结论倒推。 |
| **5. Inference / 推论** | Name assumptions, competing explanations, and confidence level.<br>列出前提、竞争解释与置信程度。 | Correlation is not identity, causation, or miracle proof.<br>对应不等于同一、因果或神迹证明。 |
| **6. Theological step / 神学步骤** | Explain how Scripture and Christian doctrine interpret the evidence.<br>说明圣经与基督教教义如何解释材料。 | Label confession as confession; do not disguise it as raw data.<br>把信仰告白明确标为告白，不伪装成原始数据。 |

For each major claim, finish one sentence: “This source supports ___; it does not by itself establish ___.” If that sentence cannot be completed, the claim is not ready to teach.

每项主要主张都要完成一句话：“这项来源支持 ___；它本身不能单独建立 ___。”若这句话还写不出来，该主张就还不适合拿来教学。

<a id="reasoning-mistakes"></a>
## Common Reasoning Mistakes | 常见推理误区

| Shortcut | Better reading |
| --- | --- |
| “Many manuscripts mean no textual variants.”<br>“抄本很多，所以没有异文。” | More witnesses give more comparison data; variants still require classification and evaluation.<br>见证越多，比较资料越多；异文仍需分类和评估。 |
| “Archaeology confirms this place, so the whole narrative is proven.”<br>“考古确认这座城，所以整段叙事都被证明。” | The find supports the place or context actually attested; other claims need their own evidence.<br>发现只支持实际见证的地点或处境；其他主张各需证据。 |
| “The wording matches, so prophecy is independently proven.”<br>“措辞相似，所以预言已被独立证明。” | Check dating, textual form, original context, specificity, and source dependence.<br>核对年代、文本形态、原处境、具体程度与来源依赖。 |
| “One skeptical objection failed, so Christianity is true.”<br>“一个怀疑论异议失败，所以基督教就是真的。” | Removing an objection is not the same as establishing the whole positive case.<br>移除一个异议，不等于建立完整的正面论证。 |
| “The book changed a life, so every historical claim is correct.”<br>“这本书改变生命，所以每项史实都正确。” | Testimony matters as testimony; factual claims still require independent checking.<br>见证作为见证很重要；事实主张仍需独立核查。 |
| “One example is outdated, so the entire case collapses.”<br>“一个例证过时，所以全书论证都倒塌。” | Correct the example, then reassess the argument it was meant to support.<br>先修正例证，再重新评估它原本要支持的论证。 |

<a id="discussion"></a>
## Questions for Personal or Group Discussion | 个人或小组讨论问题

1. Which question brought you to this book: Scripture, Jesus, resurrection, science, morality, or trust?<br>是哪一类问题把你带到本书：圣经、耶稣、复活、科学、道德，还是信任？
2. Which argument in your edition depends most heavily on a date, quotation, or number that should be checked?<br>你所读版本中的哪项论证最依赖一个需要核查的年代、引文或数字？
3. Where does the book move from a direct observation to a historical inference, and then to a theological conclusion?<br>书中哪里从直接观察推进到历史推论，再推进到神学结论？
4. What is the strongest objection to the chapter you read, and does the book state it fairly?<br>你所读部分面对的最强异议是什么？本书是否公平表述了它？
5. What would change your confidence upward or downward?<br>什么新材料会提高或降低你的确信程度？
6. How can you explain the argument without exaggeration, mockery, or pressure?<br>怎样在不夸大、不嘲讽、不施压的情况下说明这个论证？
7. After examining evidence, have you also read the biblical passage in its full literary context?<br>查考证据以后，你是否也在完整文学上下文中读过相应经文？
8. What remains a historical question, and what has become a personal question of trust and response?<br>哪些仍是历史问题，哪些已经成为关于信靠与回应的个人问题？

## Sources and Copyright Boundary | 来源与版权边界

This page is an original thematic guide and evidence-audit framework. It neither reproduces the commercial book nor reconstructs an unavailable table of contents. Readers should obtain a lawful copy and use its own chapter titles and pagination.

本页是原创主题导读与证据审计框架，既不复制商业书正文，也不重建尚未取得的目录。读者应合法取得原书，并以手中版本的章节标题与页码为准。

The current English four-part structure, 32-chapter count, and 880-page length come from the authors' [official description](https://store.josh.org/product/evidence-all-in-one-pack-50th-year-celebration/). The official [“What's New?” overview](https://www.josh.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/607/2017/06/CHARTOFNEW-3.pdf) also shows that the revision contains new, substantially revised, revised, and retained material; this is why older and newer chapter references must not be merged silently.

当前英文版的四部分结构、32 章与 880 页信息来自作者机构的[官方说明](https://store.josh.org/product/evidence-all-in-one-pack-50th-year-celebration/)。官方[“What's New?” 概览](https://www.josh.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/607/2017/06/CHARTOFNEW-3.pdf)也显示修订版同时包含新增、大幅修订、修订及保留材料；因此不能暗中混用新旧版本章号。

Historical and archaeological conclusions in this guide are deliberately narrower than a devotional or evangelistic conclusion. Sources can clarify texts, contexts, people, and arguments; they cannot mechanically produce faith, prove inspiration, or compel a miracle interpretation.

本导读中的历史与考古结论有意比灵修或布道结论更窄。来源可以澄清文本、处境、人物与论证，却不能机械地产生信心、证明默示，或强迫人接受神迹解释。
