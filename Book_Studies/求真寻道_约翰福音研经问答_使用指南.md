<a id="central-question"></a>
## The Gospel's Central Question | 约翰福音的总问题

John repeatedly asks: **Who is Jesus, and how do people receive life by believing in him?**<br>
约翰福音反复追问：**耶稣是谁？人如何因信祂得生命？**

The Gospel is not simply a collection of miracles or moral lessons. Its narrative confronts readers with Jesus’ words and actions, witnesses, signs, conflicts, cross, and resurrection, and calls for a response. John 20:30–31 provides a reading key: signs point to Jesus’ identity, and trust points to life in his name.<br>
全书不是单纯收集神迹或道德教训。叙事不断推动读者面对耶稣的言行、见证、记号、冲突、十字架与复活，并作出回应。约翰福音 20:30–31 是阅读钥匙：记号指向耶稣的身份，信靠指向在祂名里的生命。

```text
Revelation through the incarnation (1:1–18)
道成肉身的启示（1:1–18）
        ↓
Signs, witnesses, and initial faith (1:19–4:54)
记号、见证与初步信心（1:19–4:54）
        ↓
Growing conflict: Sabbath, bread of life, light, and shepherd (5–10)
冲突加深：安息日、生命之粮、光与牧者（5–10）
        ↓
Lazarus and the entry into Jerusalem: life, death, and glory (11–12)
拉撒路与进耶路撒冷：生命、死亡与荣耀（11–12）
        ↓
Last Supper and farewell teaching: love, Spirit, and abiding in Christ (13–17)
最后晚餐与临别教导：爱、圣灵、住在基督里（13–17）
        ↓
Passion, resurrection, and commissioning (18–21)
受难、复活与差遣（18–21）
```

<a id="two-threads"></a>
## Two Threads to Trace | 两条贯穿线索

<a id="seven-signs"></a>
### Seven Signs | 七个记号

A common reading scheme identifies seven signs: water into wine at Cana (2:1–11), healing the official’s son (4:46–54), healing at Bethesda (5:1–18), feeding five thousand (6:1–15), walking on water (6:16–21), healing the man born blind (9:1–41), and raising Lazarus (11:1–44). This count helps show narrative structure, but is not the only scholarly division. The important point is that each sign opens interpretation and conflict about Jesus’ identity and faith.<br>
常见的阅读安排把下列事件称作“七个记号”：迦拿婚宴变水为酒（2:1–11）、医治大臣之子（4:46–54）、医治毕士大池边的人（5:1–18）、喂饱五千人（6:1–15）、水面行走（6:16–21）、医治生来瞎眼的人（9:1–41）、叫拉撒路复活（11:1–44）。这种数法有助于看见叙事结构，但不是唯一的学术划分；重点在于每个记号都引出关于耶稣身份与信心的解释和冲突。

Questions to trace: What does each sign reveal? How do people respond? Does Jesus redirect attention from the miracle itself toward his identity, mission, or cross?<br>
追踪问题：每个记号显出什么？人物如何回应？耶稣有没有把注意力从“神迹本身”转向祂自己的身份、使命或十字架？

<a id="i-am-sayings"></a>
### The “I Am” Sayings | “我是”宣告

Trace seven predicate-bearing I Am sayings: bread of life (6:35), light of the world (8:12), gate for the sheep (10:7), good shepherd (10:11), resurrection and life (11:25), way, truth, and life (14:6), and true vine (15:1). Also notice uses without a predicate, such as John 8:58. Their relationship to the divine-name theme in Exodus needs careful reading in context and across Scripture, rather than a conclusion based on one Greek word alone.<br>
可重点标记七个带谓语的“我是”宣告：生命的粮（6:35）、世界的光（8:12）、羊的门（10:7）、好牧人（10:11）、复活和生命（11:25）、道路、真理、生命（14:6）、真葡萄树（15:1）。也要注意约翰福音中无谓语的“我是”表达，如 8:58；这些经文与出埃及记的神名主题如何关联，需要在上下文和整本圣经中仔细处理，不应只靠一个希腊字就下结论。

## How to Use the 46 Lessons | 怎样使用这本 46 课教材

<a id="four-steps"></a>
### Four Steps for Each Lesson | 每课四步

1. **Read the passage before the questions.** Mark people, places, repeated words, connections, contrasts, quotations, and narrative turns.
   **先读经文，不先看问题。** 圈出人物、地点、重复词、连接词、对比、引文和叙事转折。
2. **Then work through the workbook questions.** Let the passage answer first; use word studies, translation comparisons, and questions to notice what you missed.
   **再做教材的问题。** 先让经文回答，再用教材的字义、译本与提问帮助你看见原来忽略的细节。
3. **Distinguish three levels of answer:** what the text directly says, how I have interpreted it, and how I should respond today. Do not merge these levels.
   **区分三层答案。** “经文直接说什么”“我据此作了什么解释”“我今天应当如何回应”。三层不能混写。
4. **Apply the text with Christ at the center.** John’s application is not to make Jesus an example of techniques for success, health, or relationships. It calls people to trust him, abide in him, love his people, and be sent into the world.
   **以基督为中心地应用。** 约翰福音的应用不是把耶稣变成成功、健康或关系技巧的例子，而是信靠祂、住在祂里面、爱祂的子民并被差到世界。

<a id="guardrails"></a>
## Exegetical Guardrails for Group Leaders | 带领小组的释经界线

| Good practice / 好的做法 | Avoid / 需要避免 |
| --- | --- |
| Ask what the passage means within its original narrative before asking about application today. | Do not detach any saying of Jesus from its audience, setting, and context. |
| 先问段落在当时叙事中是什么意思，再问今天如何应用。 | 把任何一句耶稣的话抽离对象、场景与上下文。 |
| Compare translations to identify questions for study. | Do not turn translation differences into conflict or assume one rendering settles every issue. |
| 比较译本来发现需要查考的地方。 | 用不同译本的差异制造彼此对立，或以为某一译法自动解决全部问题。 |
| Treat Greek-word meanings as clues and check usage and context. | Do not derive the passage’s sole meaning directly from etymology or a dictionary gloss. |
| 把希腊文字义当作观察线索，核对用法与语境。 | 从词源或一个字典义直接推出经文唯一含义。 |
| Involve everyone while letting the text guide discussion. | Do not let sharing become a competition of experiences or replace textual reasons with personal impressions. |
| 让所有人参与，同时由经文约束讨论。 | 让分享变成经验竞赛，或以“我觉得”取代文本理由。 |
| Acknowledge John’s Jewish setting and complex characters. | Do not use the narrative term for the Jews to justify antisemitism or stigmatize a group. |
| 承认约翰福音的犹太语境和书中复杂人物。 | 用“犹太人”这一叙事用语做反犹或群体污名化的结论。 |
| Read signs, cross, and resurrection as John’s witness to Jesus. | Do not reduce miracles to a formula promising good outcomes whenever someone has faith. |
| 把神迹、十字架与复活读成约翰对耶稣的见证。 | 把神迹降格为只要有信心就能得到好结果的公式。 |
