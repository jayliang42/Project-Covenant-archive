# How to Name Christian Groups | 怎样给基督教群体命名

> Status: Draft (AI draft; not human reviewed) | 状态：Draft（AI 草稿，未人工审阅）

<a id="in-three-sentences"></a>
> **In Three Sentences | 三句话读懂本篇**
>
> Christian labels can describe a church body, a wider tradition, a movement, or an approach to theology. These levels should not be treated as interchangeable. Check a group’s own descriptions before drawing conclusions about its members.<br>
> 基督教标签可能指教会组织、大传统、运动或神学取向。这些层次不能互相替代。判断成员之前，请先核对群体如何描述自己。

> A vocabulary map for distinguishing church bodies, cross-denominational movements, theological approaches, and political labels.
> 这是一张术语地图，用来区分教会组织、跨宗派运动、神学取向与政治标签。

<a id="four-levels"></a>
## Four Levels of Labels | 四层标签

| Level / 层级 | What it identifies / 它在识别什么 | Examples / 例子 | Frequent mistake / 常见误区 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Communion or broad tradition / 教会共融体或大传统 | A historically connected church body or family with shared ministry, worship, and authority structures / 具有共同圣职、敬拜与权威结构的历史性教会整体或家族 | Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, Anglican Communion / 罗马天主教、东正教、圣公宗共融体 | Treating every tradition as if it were organized like a Protestant denomination / 把每个传统都当作新教式“宗派” |
| Denomination or church body / 宗派或教会组织 | A named organization with governance and formal standards / 具有名称、治理制度与正式规范的组织 | Southern Baptist Convention, Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.), Assemblies of God / 美南浸信会、美国长老会、神召会 | Assuming every congregation or member agrees with every policy / 以为每个堂会或会友都完全同意所有政策 |
| Cross-denominational movement / 跨宗派运动 | A network or emphasis present in several denominations / 跨越多个宗派的网络或重点 | Evangelical, charismatic, ecumenical / 福音派、灵恩运动、合一运动 | Calling the movement one denomination / 把运动当成单一宗派 |
| Theological or political orientation / 神学或政治取向 | A way of interpreting doctrine, ethics, society, or public power / 解释教义、伦理、社会或公共权力的路径 | Liberation theology, progressive, conservative, Christian nationalism / 解放神学、进步、保守、基督教民族主义 | Inferring the label from race or party registration alone / 只凭族裔或党派推断神学标签 |

**English:** These levels overlap. A person may be a PC(USA) Presbyterian, identify with the Reformed tradition, value evangelical conversion, use charismatic gifts, and vote in different ways across elections. One label never supplies the whole profile.<br>
**中文：** 这些层级会彼此重叠。一个人可以属于 PC(USA) 长老会、认同改革宗传统、重视福音派的归信，也接受灵恩恩赐，并在不同选举中作不同选择；一个标签不能代替完整画像。

**English:** Some words can name more than one level. “Reformed,” for example, may describe a confessional family, a church body with “Reformed” in its name, or a wider theological orientation. The intended level has to be learned from context.<br>
**中文：** 有些词可以指向不止一个层级。例如“改革宗”可以指一个信条传统家族、名称中带有 Reformed 的教会组织，也可以指更宽的神学取向；必须从语境判断所指层级。

<a id="family-map"></a>
## Large Church Families | 主要教会传统

| Family / 传统 | A useful first description / 合适的第一句介绍 | Important internal diversity / 重要内部差异 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roman Catholic / 罗马天主教 | A global communion centered on bishops in communion with the bishop of Rome; Scripture, apostolic Tradition, sacramental life, and the teaching office belong together. / 以与罗马主教共融的主教体系为中心的全球教会；圣经、使徒传统、圣礼生活与训导职彼此相连。 | Religious orders, spiritualities, liturgical forms, and political views vary widely. / 修会、灵修传统、礼仪形式与政治观点差异很大。 |
| Eastern Orthodox / 东正教 | A communion of self-governing churches shaped by the ecumenical councils, liturgy, bishops, Scripture within Holy Tradition, and salvation as communion with God. / 由多个自治教会构成，重视大公会议、礼仪、主教制、圣传统中的圣经，以及与神相交的救恩。 | Greek, Antiochian, Slavic, Arab, and other jurisdictions differ culturally and administratively. / 希腊、安提阿、斯拉夫、阿拉伯等辖区在文化与行政上不同。 |
| Anglican / Episcopal / 圣公宗／圣公会 | A Reformation-era communion retaining episcopal and liturgical forms while holding Protestant and catholic streams together. / 宗教改革时期形成，保留主教制与礼仪传统，同时容纳新教与大公传统脉络。 | Evangelical, Anglo-Catholic, charismatic, and liberal members coexist, sometimes tensely. / 福音派、高教会、灵恩与自由派群体并存，有时关系紧张。 |
| Lutheran / 路德宗 | Churches shaped by the Lutheran confessions, especially justification by grace through faith and a strong sacramental theology. / 受路德宗信条塑造，特别强调因恩典、借信心称义，并具有较强圣礼观。 | Confessional and mainline Lutheran bodies differ on biblical interpretation, ministry, and ethics. / 信条型与主流型路德宗在释经、圣职与伦理上有分歧。 |
| Reformed and Presbyterian / 改革宗与长老宗 | Traditions emphasizing God’s sovereignty, covenant, Reformed confessions, and ordered church government. / 强调神的主权、圣约、改革宗信条与有秩序的教会治理。 | Conservative, evangelical, and mainline denominations reach different conclusions on Scripture and social ethics. / 保守、福音派与主流宗派在圣经观和社会伦理上结论不同。 |
| Baptist / 浸信会 | Congregational churches usually emphasizing believer’s baptism, local-church responsibility, conversion, and religious liberty. / 通常强调信徒洗礼、地方教会责任、个人归信与宗教自由的会众制教会。 | Baptist bodies differ on Calvinism, women in ministry, charismatic gifts, politics, and cooperation. / 浸信会内部在加尔文主义、女性圣职、灵恩、政治与合作边界上有差异。 |
| Methodist and Wesleyan / 卫理宗与循道宗 | Traditions shaped by Wesleyan teaching on grace, holiness, discipleship, and connectional church life. / 受卫斯理传统塑造，重视恩典、圣洁、门徒训练与连接制教会生活。 | Recent institutional divisions reveal substantial disagreement over authority, ministry, and sexual ethics. / 近年的制度性分裂显示其在权威、圣职与性伦理上存在重大分歧。 |
| Anabaptist / Mennonite / 再洗礼派／门诺会 | Believers’ churches often emphasizing discipleship, peace, community discipline, and a church distinct from state power. / 信徒教会传统，常强调门徒生活、和平、群体纪律以及教会与国家权力的区别。 | Old Order, conservative, evangelical, and progressive communities should not be collapsed into one profile. / 旧规、保守、福音派与进步群体不能合并成单一画像。 |
| Wesleyan-Holiness / 卫斯理—圣洁传统 | Traditions growing from Wesleyan teaching and the nineteenth-century Holiness movement, with characteristic emphases on grace, entire sanctification or Christian holiness, holy living, discipleship, and mission. / 源自卫斯理教导与十九世纪圣洁运动的传统，特别强调恩典、完全成圣或基督徒圣洁、圣洁生活、门徒训练与宣教。 | Methodist roots and later Wesleyan, Free Methodist, Nazarene, and other Holiness bodies have distinct histories and standards. The Holiness movement helped prepare the setting for modern Pentecostalism but is not identical to it. / 这一传统具有卫理宗根源，后来形成卫斯理宗、自由卫理会、拿撒勒人会与其他各有历史和规范的圣洁宗派。圣洁运动为现代五旬节运动预备了部分背景，却不等同于五旬节派。 |
| Pentecostal / 五旬节派 | Modern church bodies formed around Spirit baptism, continuing spiritual gifts, mission, and Spirit-empowered Christian life. A body's own confession must be checked: for example, the Assemblies of God explicitly teaches the Trinity. / 围绕圣灵洗、属灵恩赐持续、宣教与圣灵赋能的基督徒生活而形成的现代教会组织。必须查看具体组织自己的信仰告白；例如，神召会明确教导三一神。 | Trinitarian classical Pentecostal bodies and Oneness Pentecostal bodies differ at the doctrine-of-God level. Oneness teaching rejects the Nicene Trinitarian formula and therefore lies outside this guide's Nicene shared center. The charismatic movement crosses older denominations and is not one Pentecostal denomination. / 古典三一论五旬节组织与一位论五旬节组织在神论层面不同。一位论教导拒绝尼西亚三一公式，因此不属于本导读所说的尼西亚共同中心。灵恩运动跨越较早的宗派，也不是一个五旬节宗派。 |
| Nondenominational churches / 非宗派教会 | Congregations without a denominational name or formal tie, often influenced by Baptist, evangelical, charismatic, or seeker-oriented models. / 不使用宗派名称或没有正式宗派隶属的堂会，常受浸信会、福音派、灵恩或慕道友导向模式影响。 | “Nondenominational” does not mean “without doctrine”; governance and theology must be checked church by church. / “非宗派”不等于“没有教义”；治理与神学必须逐堂会查明。 |

<a id="confused-labels"></a>
## Frequently Confused Labels | 常被混淆的标签

### Evangelical | 福音派

**English:** Evangelicalism is a cross-denominational Protestant movement commonly associated with the authority of Scripture, the need for personal conversion, the saving work of Christ—especially the cross and resurrection—and active evangelism or mission. It includes people of many races and many Baptist, Reformed, Anglican, Wesleyan, Pentecostal, and independent churches.<br>
**中文：** 福音派是跨宗派的新教运动，通常重视圣经权威、个人归信、基督的救赎工作——尤其十字架与复活——以及积极传福音或宣教。它涵盖不同族裔，也分布于浸信会、改革宗、圣公宗、卫斯理宗、五旬节和独立教会。

**English:** In population surveys, “evangelical Protestant” is an analytic classification. Pew first uses a respondent’s denomination and may then use self-identification and race when the denominational answer is vague. The resulting percentage is not a complete test of theology.<br>
**中文：** 在人口调查中，“福音派新教徒”是一种分析分类。Pew 先依据受访者的宗派；宗派回答模糊时，还可能使用个人自认与族裔资料归类。因此该比例不是一套完整的神学考试。

### Fundamentalism and “holding to the fundamentals” | 基要主义与“持守基要真理”

**English:** Historical Protestant fundamentalism arose in the early twentieth-century conflict with modernist theology. It defended doctrines regarded as fundamental and often added a separatist posture toward churches or institutions judged to compromise them. Evangelicalism is broader; the two terms overlap but are not synonyms.<br>
**中文：** 历史上的新教基要主义形成于二十世纪初与现代主义神学的冲突中，捍卫被视为根本的教义，并常对被认为妥协的教会或机构采取分离立场。福音派范围更广；两者有重叠，却不是同义词。

**English:** Do not automatically translate the Chinese phrase “持守基要真理” as “fundamentalist.” The phrase alone does not establish identification with the historical movement, its separatism, its anti-modernist conflicts, or its institutional culture. Ask the speaker or inspect the church’s own confession before assigning the English label.<br>
**中文：** 不要把“持守基要真理”自动翻译成 Fundamentalist。单凭这个短语，不能证明说话者认同历史上的基要主义运动、分离主义、反现代主义冲突或制度文化。应先询问当事人，或查看该教会自己的信仰告白，再决定是否使用这个英文标签。

### Mainline Protestant | 主流新教

**English:** “Mainline” is a U.S. historical and survey category for particular Protestant denominations and networks; it does not mean “most Protestants,” “moderate,” or “progressive” in every case. Mainline bodies contain conservative, centrist, and progressive congregations and members.<br>
**中文：** “主流新教”是美国历史与调查中的特定分类，指若干宗派与网络；它不等于“多数新教徒”，也不保证每个人都是温和派或进步派。主流宗派内部同时有保守、中间与进步堂会及会友。

### Progressive Christianity or theology | 进步基督教或进步神学

**English:** For this guide, “progressive Christianity” is a broad approach rather than one church body. Networks using the label commonly emphasize openness to revisiting inherited formulations, inclusion, social justice, interfaith learning, and resistance to coercive religious power; particular thinkers may also use historical-critical interpretation. This is a working map, not a creed shared by everyone called progressive, and progressive politics alone does not establish progressive theology.<br>
**中文：** 本导读把“进步基督教”作为一种宽泛取向，而不是一个教会组织。使用该名称的网络常强调重新审视继承表述的开放性、包容、社会公义、宗教间学习及抵制强制性宗教权力；具体思想者也可能使用历史批判方法。这只是一张工作地图，不是所有被称为进步派的人都共享的信条；政治上进步也不能单独证明神学上进步。

### Pentecostal and charismatic | 五旬节派与灵恩运动

**English:** Classical Pentecostalism includes denominations formed around a theology of Spirit baptism and continuing gifts. The charismatic movement brought similar practices into Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, mainline Protestant, evangelical, and independent settings; therefore “charismatic” is not one denomination.<br>
**中文：** 古典五旬节派包括围绕圣灵洗与持续恩赐形成的宗派。灵恩运动则把类似实践带入天主教、东正教、圣公宗、主流新教、福音派与独立教会，因此“灵恩”不是单一宗派。

**English:** “Pentecostal” also does not settle the Nicene question by itself. The Assemblies of God explicitly confesses the Trinity, while the United Pentecostal Church International teaches Oneness. That is a creedal boundary, not merely a difference in worship style.<br>
**中文：** “五旬节派”这个名称本身也不能自动回答是否接受尼西亚信仰。神召会明确认信三一神，而 United Pentecostal Church International 则教导一位论；这是信经边界，不只是敬拜风格差异。

### Christian nationalism | 基督教民族主义

**English:** For this guide, “Christian nationalism” names a contested political ideology that fuses Christian identity with national identity and claims about state power. It is not a denomination, creed, or synonym for ordinary Christian participation in public life. Researchers operationalize it differently, while Christians within the same denomination may support, reject, or define it differently.<br>
**中文：** 本导读用“基督教民族主义”指一种有争议的政治意识形态：它把基督教身份与国家身份融合，并对政府权力提出主张。它不是宗派、信经，也不等于基督徒正常参与公共生活。不同研究者会用不同指标测量它，同一宗派内的基督徒也可能支持、反对，或对它作不同定义。

<a id="inference-boundary"></a>
## What Can and Cannot Be Inferred | 可以与不可以怎样推断

| Evidence / 已知材料 | A warranted conclusion / 可以得出的结论 | An unwarranted leap / 不可以跳到的结论 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A denomination’s current constitution / 宗派现行章程 | Its official rule at the recorded date / 该宗派在记录日期的正式规则 | Every member personally agrees or obeys / 每位成员都认同或遵守 |
| A congregation’s statement of faith / 堂会信仰告白 | What that congregation publicly teaches / 该堂会公开教导什么 | Every church with the same ethnic language teaches the same / 同语种的所有教会都如此教导 |
| A survey category / 调查分类 | Estimated population patterns under that survey’s method / 该调查方法下的群体估计 | A person’s doctrine, conversion, politics, or character / 某个人的教义、归信、政治或品格 |
| A public figure’s speech / 公众人物的讲话 | What that person publicly argued in that setting / 此人在该场合公开主张了什么 | The complete position of a denomination or all progressives/conservatives / 整个宗派或所有进步／保守基督徒的完整立场 |

**English:** These links establish definitions and representative official positions. They do not prove that every congregation or member fits a type perfectly.<br>
**中文：** 这些链接用于建立定义与代表性正式立场，不能证明每个堂会或成员都完全符合某一类型。
