## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus addresses the hidden motive beneath outwardly good religion: the desire to be seen. Giving, prayer, and fasting can become performances even when the activity itself is good. The sermon locates the answer not in merely hiding our practices better, but in Christ. He gave himself without public acclaim, was seen by the Father, and opens a relationship in which God's presence, rather than human approval, becomes the reward.<br>
耶稣触及的是“看起来很敬虔”的行为背后那个隐藏动机：想被人看见。施舍、祷告和禁食本来是好的，却都可能变成表演。讲道指出，出路不只是把行为藏得更隐秘，而是回到基督：祂没有寻求人间喝彩地献上自己，被父看见；祂也为我们打开一段关系，使神的同在、而不是人的认可，成为真正的赏赐。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Giving: generosity can seek an audience | 施舍：慷慨也可能在找观众

Jesus assumes that his people give to those in need, but asks why they give. The warning about trumpets exposes the wish to turn mercy into reputation. “Do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing” presses beyond public display: even secret generosity can become self-congratulation. The goal is not self-admiration but a quiet response to the Father's love.<br>
耶稣预设祂的子民会帮助有需要的人，但祂追问的是动机。吹号的画面揭露了人想把怜悯变成名声的心。“不要叫左手知道右手所做的”不只禁止公开炫耀，也触及隐秘行善后暗自欣赏自己的诱惑。目标不是自我肯定，而是安静地回应天父的爱。

### 2. Prayer: access to the Father is not a credential | 祷告：亲近天父不是宗教资历

Jesus does not forbid public prayer; he tests whether a person can pray when no human audience remains. Nor is prayer a way to impress or inform God with many words. The Father already knows our need. Prayer is the child coming into the Father's presence, made possible through the Son's faithful and surrendered prayer.<br>
耶稣不是禁止公开祷告，而是在检验：当没有人观看时，人是否仍愿意祷告。祷告也不是用许多话来打动神、或把神不知道的信息告诉祂；父早已知道我们的需要。祷告是儿女来到父面前，而这条路是藉着圣子忠心、顺服的祷告为我们开启的。

### 3. Fasting: do not turn longing for God into a transaction | 禁食：不可把对神的渴慕变成交易

Fasting makes inner hunger visible only if we choose to display it. Jesus therefore exposes both the desire for recognition and the attempt to make God act in exchange for our discipline. In light of Isaiah 58, fasting is not leverage over God; it is a truthful, dependent response that seeks him.<br>
禁食中的饥饿本来是私密的，除非我们刻意展示。耶稣因此揭露两种偏离：想让人称赞，也想以操练换取神为我做事。结合以赛亚书 58 章，禁食不是操控神的筹码，而是诚实、倚靠地寻求祂的回应。

### 4. The Father himself is the reward | 天父自己就是赏赐

The sermon connects the passage to the cross and resurrection. Jesus' self-giving appeared unsuccessful to many, but the Father saw and raised him. In Christ, believers are not performers trying to earn approval; they are welcomed sons and daughters. This changes spiritual practices from transactions into fellowship.<br>
讲道把经文连到十字架与复活。耶稣的舍己在许多人眼中像失败，但父看见并叫祂复活。在基督里，信徒不再是努力赚取认可的表演者，而是被接纳的儿女；属灵操练也因此从交易变成与神相交。

## Notes | 注释

### “Hypocrite” | “假冒为善的人”

The Greek term can refer to an actor or stage performer. That background fits the sermon's “audience problem” language, but Jesus' concern is moral and relational: devotion is distorted when human recognition becomes its purpose.<br>
这个希腊词可用来指演员或舞台表演者，这与讲道所说的“观众问题”相呼应；但耶稣所关心的是道德和关系层面：当人的认可成为敬虔的目的时，敬虔就变质了。

### Secret practice is not a technique for earning reward | 隐秘不是赚取赏赐的技巧

Matthew 6 does not teach that secrecy automatically earns a better spiritual score. It confronts divided motives and directs disciples to the Father who sees. The gospel emphasis in this sermon is a helpful guard against treating hidden practice as a new performance.<br>
马太福音 6 章不是说，只要隐秘就能获得更高的属灵分数。它揭露分裂的动机，并把门徒带向那位看见的父。讲道所强调的福音，有助于防止我们把“隐秘”本身变成另一种表演。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. In which spiritual practice do I most want to be noticed?
   我最容易在哪一种属灵操练里想被人看见？
2. Can I name a hidden act of mercy, prayer, or fasting that is simply for the Father?
   我能否实践一件只为天父而做、隐秘的怜悯、祷告或禁食？
3. When I pray or fast, am I seeking God's presence or trying to control an outcome?
   我祷告或禁食时，是在寻求神的同在，还是想控制结果？
