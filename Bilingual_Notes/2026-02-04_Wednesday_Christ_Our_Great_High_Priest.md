## Big Idea | 核心信息

The study reads the Old Testament priesthood as finding its fulfillment in Jesus. He is not distant from human weakness: he became flesh, suffered, offered himself, and lives to intercede. [Salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation) is described in deeply personal terms—[Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) carries his people before the Father and welcomes them into the Father's love through the Spirit.<br>
这场学习把旧约祭司职分理解为在耶稣里得着成全。祂并不远离人的软弱：祂取了肉身、受苦、献上自己、并且永远代求。[救恩](../Glossary/README.md#salvation)被描述为极具关系性的事：[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)把祂的子民带到父面前，借着[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)把他们接进父的爱里。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Jesus fulfills priestly mediation | 耶稣成全祭司的中保职分

The high priest represented the people before God; Jesus does so perfectly because he is both fully human and the Son. His once-for-all [sacrifice](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice) surpasses repeated sacrifices that could not finally cleanse the conscience.<br>
大祭司代表百姓来到神面前；耶稣因完全为人又是圣子，而完美地成全这职分。祂一次永远的[献祭](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice)超越了不能最终洁净良心的重复祭祀。

### 2. Christ carries his people before the Father | 基督把祂的子民带到父面前

Images of the priest bearing the names of [Israel](../Glossary/README.md#israel) help express Christ's faithful intercession. Believers are not remembered by God only when they remember to perform; they are held by the Son's finished work and present advocacy.<br>
祭司把[以色列](../Glossary/README.md#israel)名字带在身上的图像，有助于表达基督信实的代求。信徒不是只有在记得表现时才被神记念；他们被圣子完成的工作和现在的代求托住。

### 3. Salvation is trinitarian fellowship | 救恩是三一的相交

The Father sends the Son, the Son gives himself and brings his people near, and the Spirit unites believers to Christ. This prevents salvation from being reduced to legal acquittal alone; [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness) opens communion with God.<br>
父差遣子，子舍己并使祂的子民亲近，圣灵使信徒与基督联合。这防止人把救恩缩小成只有法律上的无罪宣告；[赦免](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness)为与神相交打开道路。

## Notes | 注释

### One sacrifice, ongoing intercession | 一次献祭，持续代求

Hebrews holds both together: Christ's sacrifice is complete and unrepeatable, while his priestly care for his people continues. Christians do not add to his [atonement](../Glossary/README.md#atonement); they continually rely on him.<br>
希伯来书同时持守两点：基督的献祭是完整、不可重复的，而祂对祂子民的祭司性关怀持续进行。基督徒不是为祂的[赎罪](../Glossary/README.md#atonement)加添什么，而是持续倚靠祂。
