## Big Idea | 核心信息

The study confronts caricatures of God as a cosmic monitor, harsh supervisor, or needy ruler. Holiness is not the cold side of God that limits his love; it is the perfection and beauty of the Father, Son, and Spirit's love. For that reason, God's wrath is not a tantrum. Love rightly opposes what destroys the beloved, and at the cross God's judgment of sin and mercy toward sinners meet.

这场学习面对人对神的刻板想象：全天候监控者、严苛主管或需要人服侍的统治者。圣洁不是限制神之爱的冷酷一面，而是父、子、圣灵之爱完全而美丽的样式。因此，神的忿怒不是发脾气；爱必然反对毁坏所爱者的邪恶，而在十字架上，神对罪的审判与对罪人的怜悯相遇。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Holiness is beautiful, not loveless | 圣洁是美丽的，不是无爱的

God is set apart from sinful creatures, but not in lonely distance. His holiness is the pure, radiant, incorruptible love shared by Father, Son, and Spirit, and it draws people toward the goodness they were made for.

神与有罪受造者有分别，却不是孤独的距离。祂的圣洁是父、子、圣灵所共享的纯全、光辉、不可败坏的爱，并把人吸引向他们本为之造的美善。

### 2. Wrath protects what love values | 忿怒保护爱所珍视的

The study argues that indifference toward evil is not loving. God's righteous opposition to evil protects his creatures and honors the good. His patience toward sinners shows that judgment is not impulsive cruelty.

学习指出，对邪恶无动于衷并不是爱。神对邪恶公义的反对保护祂的受造者，也尊荣美善。祂对罪人的忍耐表明审判不是冲动的残酷。

### 3. The cross reveals judgment and mercy | 十字架显明审判与怜悯

At the cross, the Son willingly gives himself; he is not a third party forced to suffer. The Father and Son are united in the costly rescue of sinners, and the Spirit applies this saving work to believers.

在十字架上，圣子甘心献上自己，并不是被迫受苦的第三方。父与子在代价高昂地拯救罪人这件事上合一，圣灵把这救恩工作施行在信徒身上。

## Notes | 注释

### Wrath requires careful reading | 理解忿怒需要谨慎读经

Biblical texts about divine judgment must not be used to justify human cruelty, retaliation, or abuse. God's holiness exposes and judges evil while commanding his people to love enemies, seek justice, and leave final vengeance to God.

圣经中关于神审判的经文，绝不可被用来合理化人的残酷、报复或虐待。神的圣洁揭露并审判邪恶，同时吩咐祂的子民爱仇敌、寻求公义，并把最终的报应交给神。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Which false image of God most shapes my fear or distance from him?
2. Where do I confuse love with indifference toward evil?
3. How does the cross show both the seriousness of sin and the depth of mercy?

1. 哪一种错误的神形象最塑造我对祂的恐惧或疏离？
2. 我在哪里把爱误认为对邪恶无动于衷？
3. 十字架怎样同时显明罪的严重与怜悯的深度？
