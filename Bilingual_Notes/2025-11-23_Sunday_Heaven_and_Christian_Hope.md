## Big Idea | 核心信息

The sermon replaces sentimental pictures of heaven with biblical hope: God prepares a real future for his people, sin and death will not have the final word, and the redeemed will belong to a diverse worshiping multitude. This hope is meant to reshape present faithfulness, not merely satisfy curiosity about the afterlife.<br>
讲道以圣经的盼望取代感伤化的天堂图像：神为祂的子民预备真实的将来，罪与死亡不能作最后的结论，蒙救赎的人要成为多族、多方言的敬拜群体。这盼望的目的不是满足来世的好奇，而是重塑今天忠心的生活。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Jesus prepares a place | 耶稣预备地方

John 14 grounds hope in Jesus himself. His promise is relational—“I will come again and take you to myself”—not merely a promise of better surroundings. Belonging to Christ is the center of future joy.<br>
约翰福音 14 章把盼望建立在耶稣自己身上。祂的应许是关系性的：“我必再来接你们到我那里去”，不只是应许更好的环境。属乎基督，才是将来喜乐的中心。

### 2. Heaven is for a redeemed people | 天堂属于蒙救赎的子民

Revelation pictures an innumerable multitude from every nation before God. The sermon rightly emphasizes diversity, worship, and the end of sin's reign. Eternal life is not lonely spiritual individualism but communion with God and his people.<br>
启示录描绘各国、各族、各民、各方言中数不尽的人站在神面前。讲道正确强调多样性、敬拜以及罪权势的终结。永生不是孤独的属灵个人主义，而是与神及祂子民的相交。

### 3. Resurrection and new creation | 复活与新创造

The Christian future includes bodily resurrection and God's renewed creation. This prevents two errors: treating material creation as worthless, or reducing hope to the continuation of present desires. The final vision is God dwelling with his people.<br>
基督徒的将来包括身体复活和神更新的创造。这避免两种错误：把物质受造界当作无价值，或把盼望缩小为延续今日的欲望。终末异象是神与祂的子民同住。

## Notes | 注释

### What the text clearly says—and does not say | 经文明说与未明说的事

John 14 and Revelation 21 strongly affirm Christ's presence, resurrection hope, and renewal. They do not settle every popular question about pets, occupations, exact geography, or how deceased believers observe earthly events. Where Scripture is quiet, this note remains quiet.<br>
约翰福音 14 章和启示录 21 章清楚肯定基督的同在、复活的盼望和万物更新；但它们没有解答每一个流行问题，例如宠物、职业、精确地点，或已逝信徒如何观看地上之事。经文没有说明的地方，本笔记也不作断言。

### Hope changes present life | 盼望改变今天的生活

Future hope is never permission for present neglect. It strengthens grief with comfort, makes holiness meaningful, and gives courage for witness because Christ's victory is certain.<br>
将来的盼望从不是忽略今天的许可；它在悲伤中带来安慰，使圣洁有意义，也让人有勇气见证，因为基督的得胜是确定的。
