## Big Idea | 核心信息

Set against the New Year desire for renewal, the sermon names a deeper fatigue that vacations and productivity cannot cure. People become weary when they try to find identity, security, and life in work, money, achievement, relationships, or self-rule. Jesus' invitation is not escape from all vocation, but his gentle presence and leadership in the middle of life.<br>
讲道以新年“重新开始”的愿望为背景，指出一种假期和效率都不能医治的更深疲惫。人试图从工作、金钱、成就、关系或自我作主中得到身份、安全与生命时，就会疲乏。耶稣的邀请不是逃离所有职责，而是在生活当中领受祂温柔的同在与带领。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. False masters make the soul weary | 错误的主人使灵魂疲惫

Good gifts become heavy yokes when they become masters. The sermon identifies misdirected love as a root of restlessness: people seek from created things what only the Creator can give.<br>
美好的礼物一旦成为主人，就会变成沉重的轭。讲道指出，爱的方向错置是不得安息的根源：人从受造之物索取只有造物主能赐下的东西。

### 2. Jesus shares his yoke | 耶稣与我们同负一轭

Christ's yoke is not a new achievement system. It is learning from the gentle and humble Lord, trusting the Father as he did, and living in his presence. The [gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel) promise is companionship: believers are not yoked alone.<br>
基督的轭不是新的成就系统，而是向温柔谦卑的主学习，如祂一样信靠父，并活在祂的同在中。福音的[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)是陪伴：信徒不是独自负轭。

### 3. Peace is practiced dependence | 平安是被操练的倚靠

Philippians 4 supplies a daily pattern: bring requests to God with thanksgiving and set the mind on what is true and good. Peace does not deny trouble; it guards the heart in Christ amid trouble.<br>
腓立比书 4 章提供每日模式：带着感恩把祈求交给神，并思想真实、美善的事。平安不是否认患难，而是在患难中在基督里保守人的心。

## Notes | 注释

### Rest is not inactivity | 安息不等于不作为

Jesus calls [disciples](../Glossary/README.md#disciple) into his yoke, which includes learning, obedience, and mission. Rest is freedom from proving oneself and carrying life alone, not indifference to work, relationships, or responsibility.<br>
耶稣呼召[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)进入祂的轭，其中包括学习、顺服和使命。安息是脱离自我证明、脱离独自承担人生的自由，而不是对工作、关系或责任漠不关心。
