## Big Idea | 核心信息

The study rejects treating the Bible as a collection of disconnected information or moral slogans. Its many genres, genealogies, promises, histories, and commands belong to one story whose center is Christ. The Spirit who inspired Scripture unites the church to Christ and enables reading to become relational knowledge rather than mere data gathering.

学习拒绝把圣经当作互不相连的信息或道德口号的集合。它的各种文体、家谱、应许、历史与命令，都属于一个以基督为中心的故事。启示圣经的圣灵使教会与基督联合，也使读经成为关系性的认识，而不只是收集资料。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Scripture has a unified center | 圣经有统一中心

Christ does not flatten the Old Testament or erase its original settings. Rather, he is the one to whom its promises, patterns, hopes, and story ultimately lead. Reading context carefully and reading canonically belong together.

基督不会把旧约压平，也不会抹去它原有的处境；相反，祂是旧约的应许、模式、盼望与故事最终所指向的那一位。认真读上下文与按正典读经可以并行。

### 2. The Spirit makes Scripture living | 圣灵使圣经成为活的

The Spirit does not give private meanings that contradict the text. He illumines the written word, gives believers faith to receive Christ, and forms the church under Christ its head.

圣灵不会赐下与经文相矛盾的私人意义；祂光照写成的话语，使信徒凭信心领受基督，并在以基督为元首的教会中塑造人。

### 3. Study becomes worship and obedience | 学习成为敬拜与顺服

Careful study remains necessary, yet its goal is more than mastery. The reader is invited to know, love, trust, and obey the God made known in Christ.

认真研读仍然必要，但目标不只是掌握知识；读者受邀认识、爱、信靠并顺服那位在基督里显明的神。

## Notes | 注释

### Christ-centered is not context-free | 以基督为中心不等于脱离上下文

A Christ-centered reading must not force Jesus into every detail in an arbitrary way. Ask first what a passage says in its literary and historical setting, then how it participates in the Bible's larger story fulfilled in Christ.

以基督为中心的读法不能任意地把耶稣硬塞进每个细节。应先问经文在文学和历史处境中说什么，再问它如何参与那在基督里成全的整本圣经故事。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Do I treat Scripture mainly as information, a rulebook, or a place to meet Christ?
2. What passage have I avoided because it feels obscure or uninteresting?
3. How can I read one passage this week with both careful context and prayerful trust?

1. 我主要把圣经当成信息、规则书，还是遇见基督之处？
2. 哪一段经文因显得晦涩或无趣而被我回避？
3. 这周我怎样能带着认真看上下文和祷告的信靠来读一段经文？
