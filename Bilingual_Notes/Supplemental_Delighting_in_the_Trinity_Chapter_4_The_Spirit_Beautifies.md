## Big Idea | 核心信息

The Holy Spirit is not a spiritual force Christians use to improve themselves. He is the fully divine, personal Spirit who gives life, unites believers to [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ), makes them know the Father's welcome, and patiently reshapes their desires and lives into the likeness of the Son.<br>
圣灵不是基督徒用来改善自己的属灵力量；祂是完全属神、具有位格的圣灵，赐下生命，使信徒与[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)联合，使人认识父的接纳，并耐心地把人的渴望与生命塑造成圣子的样式。

## What Chapter 4 Helps Us See | 第四章帮助我们看见什么

### 1. The Spirit is a person, not a tool | 圣灵是位格，不是工具

It is easy to talk about “getting more power,” “having a better feeling,” or “making prayer work.” The biblical picture is relational: the Spirit teaches, testifies, leads, grieves, gives, and glorifies the Son. A person is received, trusted, and obeyed; a tool is controlled. This distinction guards us from treating God as a technique for our goals.<br>
人很容易把属灵生命说成“得到更多能力”“感觉更好”或“让祷告见效”。但[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)呈现的是关系：圣灵教导、见证、引导、担忧、赐恩，也荣耀圣子。位格是被领受、信靠、顺服的；工具则是被控制和使用的。这个区别保护我们不把神当作达成自己目标的方法。

### 2. The Spirit joins believers to Christ | 圣灵使信徒与基督联合

The Christian life is not first an attempt to imitate Jesus from a distance. By the Spirit, believers belong to Christ and share the benefits of his saving work: [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness), adoption, access to the Father, and a new pattern of life. This is why chapter 4 belongs after the book's chapter on [salvation](../Glossary/README.md#salvation): what the Son accomplishes is personally applied by the Spirit.<br>
基督徒生命首先不是隔着距离模仿耶稣。借着圣灵，信徒属于基督，并分享祂[救赎](../Glossary/README.md#redemption)工作所带来的益处：[赦免](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness)、收纳、来到父面前的权利，以及新的生命样式。这也说明第四章为何接在救恩之后：圣子所成就的救恩，借着圣灵真实地施行在人的生命中。

### 3. The Spirit changes what we love | 圣灵改变我们所爱慕的

Christian growth involves outward obedience, but it goes deeper than behavior management. The Spirit opens our eyes to Christ's beauty, exposes disordered loves, and forms new desires for God and neighbor. Change may be gradual and contested; it is not a claim that sincere Christians never struggle. The question is not merely, “What must I do?” but also, “What am I learning to love?”<br>
基督徒成长当然包含外在顺服，却比行为管理更深。圣灵使人看见基督的荣美，揭露失序的爱，也塑造人重新爱神、爱邻舍的渴望。改变可能缓慢，也常有挣扎；这并不表示真信徒从不软弱。问题不只是“我必须做什么？”，也包括“我正在学习爱什么？”

### 4. The Spirit makes Christ-centered practices living | 圣灵使以基督为中心的操练成为活的

The Spirit does not compete with Scripture, prayer, baptism, the Lord's Supper, or the gathered [church](../Glossary/README.md#church). He uses God's ordinary gifts to point people to Christ and form trust, [repentance](../Glossary/README.md#repentance), worship, and love. Seeking the Spirit's work therefore does not mean chasing novelty or private messages that contradict Scripture.<br>
圣灵并不与圣经、祷告、洗礼、圣餐或教会聚集相竞争。祂使用神所赐下的平常[恩典](../Glossary/README.md#grace)，把人指向基督，塑造信靠、[悔改](../Glossary/README.md#repentance)、敬拜与爱。因此，寻求圣灵的工作并不等于追逐新奇，或接受与圣经相矛盾的私人信息。

### 5. The Spirit forms a people, not isolated spiritual consumers | 圣灵塑造群体，不是孤立的属灵消费者

The Spirit gives diverse gifts to one body. No gift, intensity of experience, or public role makes one believer more necessary or more loved by God than another. In a discipleship group, the fitting fruit is mutual service, truthfulness, patience, confession, encouragement, and a shared focus on Christ.<br>
圣灵把多样的恩赐赐给同一个身体。没有一种恩赐、体验的强烈程度或公开角色，会使某位信徒比别人更不可缺少，或更被神所爱。在门徒小组中，合宜的果子是彼此服事、诚实、忍耐、认罪、鼓励，并共同以基督为中心。

## Doctrinal Guardrails | 教义界线

| Keep clear | Avoid | 为什么重要 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Spirit is fully God and personally distinct from the Father and the Son. | 把圣灵说成一种能量、情绪、技能或可操控资源。 | [三一](../Glossary/README.md#trinity)信仰要求我们敬拜、信靠并顺服圣灵，而不是利用祂。 |
| The Spirit glorifies Christ and applies Christ's work. | 把所谓“属灵经历”放在[福音](../Glossary/README.md#gospel)、基督和圣经之上。 | 圣灵的工作不会使人离开基督或否定神已启示的话。 |
| Transformation includes both desire and conduct. | 只靠羞耻、表现或瞬间经历来衡量成熟。 | 圣灵结出长期的、以爱为形状的生命果子。 |
| Gifts are given for the common good. | 用恩赐、职位或体验作属灵等级。 | 教会是一个身体，彼此需要，也一同归荣耀给神。 |

This note does not decide every question about spiritual gifts or every way Christians describe the Spirit's guidance. Hold the Nicene center firmly; discuss secondary questions with Scripture, humility, and your local church's teaching.<br>
本篇不替你定下所有关于属灵恩赐的问题，也不穷尽基督徒如何描述圣灵引导。三一正统的核心要坚定持守；次要问题则要带着圣经、谦卑与本地教会的教导继续讨论。

## 75-Minute Group Study | 75 分钟小组查考

1. **5 min — Opening prayer | 开始祷告：** Ask the Father to make Christ known through the Spirit.
2. **15 min — Read Scripture | 读经：** Read John 14:15–27 and Romans 8:9–17 aloud. Notice who acts and what is promised.
3. **15 min — Read the book | 阅读原书：** Read chapter 4 in your own copy. Mark statements about the Spirit's personhood, Christ, adoption, desire, and the church.
4. **20 min — Discuss | 讨论：** Use the questions below. Require each conclusion to be tied to the text or clearly named as a personal application.
5. **15 min — Respond | 回应：** Pray in pairs: confess one distorted desire, thank God for adoption, and ask for a concrete act of love during the week.
6. **5 min — Close | 结束：** Read 2 Corinthians 13:14 and remember that Christian life is shared life with God and his people.

## Discussion and Personal Practice | 讨论与个人操练

1. Where do I treat the Spirit as a solution, a feeling, or a source of ability rather than as the Lord who brings me to Christ?
   我在哪些地方把圣灵当作解决方案、感觉或能力来源，而不是把我带到基督面前的主？
2. Which verse shows that the Spirit's work is inseparable from the Son and the Father's love?
   哪一节经文表明，圣灵的工作与圣子及父的爱不可分开？
3. What desire—approval, control, comfort, status, safety, resentment—most needs to be brought before Christ this week?
   这周我最需要带到基督面前的是哪一种渴望：被认可、控制、舒适、地位、安全感或怨恨？
4. What is one ordinary means of [grace](../Glossary/README.md#grace) I tend to neglect: Scripture, prayer, gathered worship, confession, communion, or serving the church?
   我最容易忽略哪一种平常恩典：读经、祷告、聚会、认罪、圣餐或服事教会？
5. How can I use my gifts this week to strengthen another believer without comparison or performance?
   我这周怎样使用自己的恩赐去坚固另一位信徒，而不陷入比较或表现？

## Prayer | 祷告

Holy Spirit, do not let us reduce you to a power we can manage. Unite us more deeply to Christ, assure us of the Father's welcome, reorder our loves, and make our life together bear witness to his beauty.<br>
圣灵，求你不让我们把你缩小成可操控的力量。求你使我们更深与基督联合，使我们确知父的接纳，重整我们所爱的，并使我们的共同生活见证祂的荣美。
