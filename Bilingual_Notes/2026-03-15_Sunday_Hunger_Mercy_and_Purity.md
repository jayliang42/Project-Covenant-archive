## Big Idea | 核心信息

The Beatitudes are not a checklist that buys kingdom [blessing](../Glossary/README.md#blessing). They portray the flourishing God forms in people already received through Christ. Hunger for righteousness, mercy, and purity are connected movements of a transformed heart: longing for what is right, extending faithful love, and becoming whole-hearted before God.<br>
八福不是用来换取[天国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)[祝福](../Glossary/README.md#blessing)的检查表，而是描绘神在已经借基督被接纳之人身上所塑造的丰盛。饥渴慕义、怜悯和清心，是被更新之心相连的动作：渴望公义、施行信实之爱，并在神面前成为专一的人。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Hunger and thirst for righteousness | 饥渴慕义

Jesus blesses not people who imagine they have arrived, but those whose new desires ache for right relationship with God and neighbor. Righteousness includes the gift of being made right in Christ and the Spirit-shaped life that follows.<br>
耶稣称有福的不是自以为已经达到的人，而是那些被更新的愿望深切渴慕与神、与邻舍有正确关系的人。义包括在基督里被算为义的礼物，也包括随后被圣灵塑造的生活。

### 2. Mercy is faithful love in action | 怜悯是信实之爱付诸行动

Mercy includes [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness) but reaches further: it sees a person in need and acts with loyal compassion. Because believers have received mercy, they are freed to move toward others without superiority.<br>
怜悯包括饶恕，却不止于此：它看见人的需要，并以信实的同情采取行动。因为信徒已经领受怜悯，他们得以不带优越感地走向别人。

### 3. Purity of heart seeks God whole-heartedly | 清心寻求神

Purity is more than outward rule-keeping. It names an undivided heart whose desires are being gathered toward God. The promise of seeing God is the deepest hope of such a life.<br>
清心不只是外在守规条，而是指一颗不再分裂、其欲望正被聚集归向神的心。得见神的[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)，是这种生命最深的盼望。

## Notes | 注释

### Justification and transformation | 称义与更新

The sermon distinguishes Christ's righteousness credited to believers from the righteous life the Spirit grows in them. These must not be separated: transformation does not earn acceptance, but genuine grace forms new desires and practices.<br>
讲道区分了基督的义归算给信徒，与圣灵在信徒中培育的公义生命。两者不可分开：生命更新不赚取接纳，但真实的恩典会塑造新的愿望与实践。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. What do I actually hunger for most, and what does that reveal?
   我真正最饥渴的是什么？这显明了什么？
2. Who needs mercy from me in a concrete form?
   谁需要我以具体方式施怜悯？
3. What competing desire is dividing my heart before God?
   在神面前，哪一种竞争性的欲望正在分裂我的心？
