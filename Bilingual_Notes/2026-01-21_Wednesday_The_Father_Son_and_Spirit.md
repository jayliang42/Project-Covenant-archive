## Big Idea | 核心信息

This theological discussion begins with God's identity as Father rather than treating “Father” as merely a human metaphor added after creation. The Father eternally loves the Son, the Son delights in the Father, and the Spirit makes that love known. Salvation is therefore not admission to an impersonal system, but the Son bringing people into the Father's love by the Spirit.<br>
这场神学讨论从神作为父的身份开始，而不是把“父”仅当作创造之后借用的人类比喻。父永恒爱子，子以父为喜乐，圣灵使这爱被显明。救恩因此不是进入一个非人格的体系，而是圣子借着圣灵把人带进父的爱中。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. God is eternally Father and Son | 神永恒是父与子

The discussion stresses that the Father did not become loving after creating the world. Before creation, God is already living, giving, and delighted love in the communion of Father and Son.<br>
讨论强调：神不是在创造世界后才变得有爱。在创造之前，神已经在父与子的相交中活着、赐生命并喜悦地爱。

### 2. The Spirit makes divine love known | 圣灵使神圣之爱被显明

At Jesus' baptism, Father, Son, and Spirit are distinctly present. The Spirit is not an impersonal force; he is given to unite believers to Christ and move them toward the Father's love.<br>
在耶稣受洗时，父、子、圣灵各自清楚同在。圣灵不是非人格的力量；祂被赐下，使信徒与基督联合，并被带向父的爱。

### 3. Fatherhood reshapes prayer and identity | 父性重塑祷告与身份

God's fatherhood does not erase his kingship, holiness, or judgment. It shows that his rule and correction flow from his holy love. In Christ, believers approach him as adopted children rather than anxious strangers.<br>
神的父性并不抹去祂的君王身份、圣洁或审判；它表明祂的掌权和纠正来自圣洁的爱。在基督里，信徒作为被收纳的儿女，而不是焦虑的陌生人，来到祂面前。

## Notes | 注释

### The Trinity is not three gods | 三一不是三位神

Christian confession holds that the Father, Son, and Spirit are distinct persons who fully share the one divine being. Analogies from human family or marriage may illuminate limited aspects, but none can map God exhaustively or prove every conclusion.<br>
基督教信仰宣认：父、子、圣灵是彼此有别的位格，却完全同享独一的神性。人类家庭或婚姻的类比或许能照亮有限层面，却不能穷尽神，也不能证明每一个结论。
