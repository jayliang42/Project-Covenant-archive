## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus is not lowering the standard of the law. He is revealing its true depth. Murder is not only an outward act. It begins in the heart through anger, contempt, dismissal, and refusal to reconcile. The kingdom ethic therefore reaches deeper than behavior; it reaches motives, desires, and posture toward others.

耶稣不是降低律法的要求，也不是推翻旧约；祂是在显明律法真正的深度。杀人不只是外在行为，它往往先从内心开始，表现为愤怒、轻看、贬低别人，以及拒绝和好。所以，天国的伦理不只管“你做了什么”，也管“你心里怎样看人”。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Jesus corrects shallow readings of the law

#### English

The command “You shall not murder” is clear, but many people reduce obedience to external behavior only. Jesus exposes that shallow reading. He shows that the law always pointed to the heart. A person may avoid literal murder and still carry a destructive spirit toward others.

#### 中文

“不可杀人”这条诫命本身很清楚，但很多人把顺服律法理解成“只要外面没做出来就可以”。耶稣指出，这是一种很浅的理解。律法从来不只针对外在行为，也一直指向人心。一个人即使没有真正杀人，也可能已经在心里活出一种毁坏他人的态度。

### 2. Anger and contempt are spiritually serious

#### English

Jesus links anger, insult, and contempt with judgment. The point is not that every moment of frustration equals homicide in the same social sense. The point is that the same root of devaluing another image-bearer is already present. When we belittle others, we treat someone made in God's image as if that person has reduced worth.

#### 中文

耶稣把愤怒、辱骂、轻蔑和审判联系在一起。这里的重点不是说每一次情绪波动都等同于社会意义上的杀人，而是说：当人心里开始贬低另一个按神形象被造的人时，杀人的根已经在那里了。我们轻看别人时，其实是在否认那个人作为神形象承载者的尊严。

### 3. Reconciliation should not be delayed

#### English

Jesus gives two practical pictures. First, if worship and broken relationship collide, reconciliation matters enough that it must be addressed seriously. Second, if conflict is already moving toward judgment, settle quickly. The thrust is urgency. Kingdom people do not nurture bitterness and call it righteousness.

#### 中文

耶稣用了两个很具体的画面。第一，如果敬拜与破裂的人际关系同时摆在面前，人要认真面对和好的责任。第二，如果冲突已经走向审判和对抗，就要尽快处理。整段经文强调的是“不要拖延”。天国子民不能一边喂养 bitterness {苦毒、怨恨积压}，一边又把它包装成义。

### 4. The gospel makes reconciliation possible

#### English

The sermon's strongest pastoral move is this: we can pursue peace only because Jesus first moved toward us. We were not neutral people who needed minor improvement. We were sinners, debtors, and enemies. Christ absorbed judgment in our place, paid the debt we could not pay, and released us from the prison of guilt. That gospel frees us to stop clinging to anger.

#### 中文

这篇讲道最重要的牧养性转折在这里：我们之所以能去寻求和平，是因为耶稣先向我们走来。我们不是“稍微需要改善的人”，而是罪人、欠债者、与神为敌的人。基督替我们承受审判，偿还我们无力偿还的债，把我们从罪疚的监牢中释放出来。因此，福音使我们有可能不再死抓着愤怒不放。

## Key Observations | 关键观察

- Jesus fulfills the law by bringing out its intended meaning.
- External obedience without heart transformation is inadequate.
- Anger often appears respectable while doing deep spiritual damage.
- Contempt is a hidden form of violence.
- Worship of God cannot be detached from how we treat people.
- Reconciliation is not weakness; it is kingdom-shaped humility.

- 耶稣成全律法，是把律法真正的意思显明出来。
- 只有外在顺服、没有内心更新，是不够的。
- 愤怒常常伪装得很“合理”，但属灵破坏力很强。
- 轻蔑是隐藏起来的暴力。
- 对神的敬拜，不能与我们如何对待别人分开。
- 和好不是软弱，而是天国样式的谦卑。

## Notes | 注释

### Note 1. “Raca” and contempt

#### English

In many discussions of Matthew 5, “Raca” is explained as an insult expressing contempt or emptiness. The exact tone may vary, but the sermon's main emphasis is right: contemptuous speech reveals a heart that dismisses another person's value.

#### 中文

在很多关于马太福音 5 章的讨论中，`Raca` 常被解释为一种带着轻蔑意味的辱骂，类似“废物”“空心的人”之类。它具体语气可能有细微差别，但这篇讲道抓住了核心：轻蔑性的言语，暴露的是一种否认他人价值的心。

### Note 2. Image of God

#### English

The sermon connects murder ethics to Genesis 9:6. That is important because biblical ethics are grounded not merely in social order, but in the dignity of human beings as bearers of God's image.

#### 中文

讲道把“不可杀人”的伦理基础连到创世记 9:6，这一点很重要。圣经的伦理不只是为了维持社会秩序，更是建立在人是按神形象所造、具有尊严这一事实上。

### Note 3. Reconciliation is not identical to restored intimacy

#### English

A useful pastoral clarification: reconciliation does not always mean immediate trust, restored closeness, or removal of all boundaries. It does mean moving away from hatred, revenge, and stubborn refusal to seek peace where obedience to Christ makes peace possible.

#### 中文

一个很有帮助的牧养性澄清是：和好不一定等于立刻恢复亲密、完全重建信任，或取消一切界限。它首先意味着，人不再活在仇恨、报复和刚硬拒绝和平的状态中；在顺服基督的前提下，尽可能走向和平。
