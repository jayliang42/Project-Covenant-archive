## Big Idea | 核心信息

Peter calls Christians to arm themselves with Christ's mindset: his suffering has broken sin's rule, so their remaining time is no longer for former desires but for God's will. This freedom takes visible shape through sober prayer, steadfast love, hospitality without grumbling, and the faithful use of gifts.<br>
彼得呼召基督徒以基督的心志武装自己：祂的受苦已经破除了罪的辖制，所以余下的日子不再为从前的私欲，而为神的旨意。这样的自由会在警醒祷告、恒切相爱、不发怨言的接待，以及忠心使用恩赐中显出来。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Christ's suffering changes our allegiance | 基督的受苦改变我们的归属

Jesus truly took on flesh and suffered for sin once for all. Union with him means Christians need not answer every pressure, irritation, or hardship with self-centered sin; they can pursue sacrificial love.<br>
耶稣真实取了肉身，并一次永远地为罪受苦。与祂联合意味着，基督徒不必用自我中心的罪来回应每一次压力、烦扰或艰难，而能追求舍己的爱。

### 2. Former patterns no longer define us | 从前的生活方式不再定义我们

Peter names patterns that marked life apart from God and expects believers to be misunderstood when they no longer join them. The sermon does not turn this into superiority; it grounds distinct living in Christ's grace and coming judgment.<br>
彼得列出离开神时的生活样式，也预期信徒不再参与时会被人误解。讲道没有把这变成优越感，而是把不同的生活建立在基督的恩典和将来的审判上。

### 3. The end awakens love and service | 终局催生爱与服事

“The end of all things is near” calls for clear-minded prayer, earnest love, open homes, and active service. Gifts are not private identity markers; they are entrusted grace for building up others and glorifying God through Jesus.<br>
“万物的结局近了”呼召人清醒祷告、切实相爱、开放家庭并积极服事。恩赐不是私人的身份标签，而是受托的恩典，为建立别人、并借耶稣荣耀神。

## Notes | 注释

### “Ceased from sin” | “与罪断绝”

1 Peter 4:1 should not be read as a claim that Christians become sinless through suffering. In context, it points to a decisive break with sin's former dominion and a new orientation toward God's will in Christ.<br>
彼得前书 4:1 不应被理解为基督徒因受苦就达到无罪。在上下文中，它指向与罪从前统治的决定性断裂，以及在基督里转向神旨意的新方向。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Which former desire or pattern needs to lose its claim on me?
   哪一种从前的欲望或习惯需要不再辖制我？
2. How can love become concrete in my home, schedule, and speech this week?
   这周爱怎样能在我的家庭、时间安排和言语中变得具体？
3. What gift might I discover by serving rather than waiting for certainty?
   我可以在服事中发现哪一种恩赐，而非等到完全确定才行动？
