## Big Idea | 核心信息

Peter first addresses elders, then expands the call to humility across the whole church. Christian leadership is not domination, image management, or personal gain. It is willing, eager, exemplary care under the authority of Jesus, the Chief Shepherd; all believers are called to clothe themselves with humility toward one another.

彼得先对长老说话，随后把谦卑的呼召扩展到全教会。基督徒式带领不是支配、形象经营或个人获益，而是在大牧者耶稣的权柄下，甘心、热心、以身作则地关怀；所有信徒都蒙召彼此以谦卑束腰。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Elders are under-shepherds | 长老是副牧者

The church is God's flock, never a leader's possession. Elders are to shepherd as accountable servants who point people to Christ rather than gathering control around themselves.

教会是神的羊群，绝不是带领者的私有物。长老要作将来交账的仆人牧养人，把人指向基督，而不是把控制权聚在自己身上。

### 2. Lead willingly, eagerly, and by example | 甘心、热心、以身作则地带领

Peter rejects leadership driven by compulsion, shameful gain, or lording it over others. The sermon connects this to Jesus washing feet and laying down his life: grace received becomes grace given.

彼得拒绝出于勉强、贪图不义之财或辖制他人的带领。讲道把这连到耶稣洗脚与舍命：领受的恩典会变成给出去的恩典。

### 3. Humility belongs to everyone | 谦卑属于每一个人

Those who lead and those who follow need humility, prayer, teachability, and mutual care. Submission is not blind obedience to sin or abuse; it is a gospel-shaped posture that takes godly oversight seriously while holding every human leader under Christ.

带领的人和跟随的人都需要谦卑、祷告、受教与彼此关怀。顺服不是对罪或虐待的盲从，而是一种福音塑造的姿态：认真看待敬虔的监督，同时承认每一位人类领袖都在基督之下。

## Notes | 注释

### Leadership is not control | 带领不是控制

The transcript's application is important: a leader can be tempted to manufacture outcomes through pressure. Biblical shepherding instead teaches, serves, models repentance, protects the vulnerable, and trusts God to work.

逐字稿的应用很重要：带领者可能会试图借压力制造结果。圣经的牧养则是教导、服事、活出悔改、保护脆弱者，并信靠神自己工作。

### Authority has limits | 权柄有边界

Church authority is real but derivative. It must be accountable to Scripture, exercised for the good of the flock, and never used to cover sin, coercion, or harm. Seeking help and safety in cases of abuse is not rebellion against Christ.

教会权柄是真实的，却是从属的。它必须向圣经负责、为羊群益处而行，绝不可用来遮掩罪、胁迫或伤害。在虐待情形中寻求帮助和安全，并不是反叛基督。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Where am I tempted to lead through control, recognition, or fear?
2. How can I serve someone this week in a willing and concrete way?
3. What would it look like to receive godly correction with humility?

1. 我在哪些地方受诱惑，想用控制、认可或恐惧来带领？
2. 这周我怎样能甘心而具体地服事一个人？
3. 谦卑地接受敬虔的劝正，对我会是什么样子？

## Prayer | 祷告

Chief Shepherd, free us from pride and control. Make our leaders faithful servants and make all of us humble, teachable, and eager to care for one another in your love.

大牧者，求你释放我们脱离骄傲和控制。使我们的带领者成为忠心仆人，也使我们全体谦卑、受教，乐意在你的爱中彼此关怀。
