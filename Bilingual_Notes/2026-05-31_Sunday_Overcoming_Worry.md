## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus' command not to worry is neither denial of hardship nor a demand to “try harder.” The sermon traces worry to the attempt to rule a life we cannot control, rather than trusting the good and powerful Father. Jesus directs anxious disciples to the Father's care for creation and calls them to seek first God's kingdom and righteousness.

耶稣“不要忧虑”的命令，既不是否认艰难，也不是叫人“更努力地不焦虑”。讲道把忧虑追溯到一种企图：人想掌控自己无力掌控的人生，而不肯信靠良善且有能力的父。耶稣把焦虑的门徒带向父对受造界的看顾，并呼召他们先求神的国和神的义。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Worry becomes a false attempt at control | 忧虑会变成错误的掌控尝试

The sermon distinguishes appropriate concern for a spouse or church family from anxiety that becomes consuming and self-ruling. At its root, worry says that I must secure what only God can finally secure. It generates more insecurity rather than adding life.

讲道区分了对配偶或教会家人的恰当关心，与吞噬人、让人以自我为主的焦虑。忧虑的根部像是在说：我必须保证那些最终只有神能保证的事。它不会增加生命，反而制造更多不安全感。

### 2. The Father knows and cares | 父知道，也看顾

Birds and flowers are not arguments for passivity; they are signs of the Father's generous care. If God attends to his creation, Jesus asks how much more he values his children. This care does not promise an easy life, but it grounds trust amid need, suffering, and uncertainty.

飞鸟和百合花不是鼓励人消极不作为，而是天父慷慨看顾的记号。神既看顾受造界，耶稣就问：祂岂不更看重祂的儿女吗？这份看顾并不保证生活顺利，却使人能在需要、受苦和未知中信靠祂。

### 3. Seek first the kingdom | 先求神的国

Seeking first the kingdom changes life's first direction and priority. Instead of continually staring at circumstances and fear, believers look to Christ's reign and righteousness. The sermon presents this not as ignoring responsibilities, but as ordering them under the Father’s kingdom.

先求神的国，会改变人生最优先的方向。信徒不再不断盯着环境和恐惧，而是转向基督的掌权和义。讲道不是要人忽略责任，而是把一切责任重新放在父国度的秩序之下。

### 4. Four practices for anxious moments | 面对忧虑的四项操练

The sermon recommends: gaze at Christ's gospel love; pray specifically about worries; interrupt anxious inner speech with Scripture; and stay connected to a community that encourages faith. These practices do not replace wise help, but they give concrete ways to turn concern into dependent trust.

讲道提出四项实践：注视基督的福音之爱；具体地为忧虑祷告；用经文打断焦虑的内在对话；留在彼此劝勉信心的群体中。这些操练不取代合宜的帮助，却提供具体路径，使关心转变为倚靠的信任。

## Notes | 注释

### Worry and mental-health suffering | 忧虑与心理健康困境

Matthew 6 addresses anxious, divided allegiance and calls disciples to trust the Father. It should not be used to shame someone with anxiety disorders, depression, trauma, or severe distress. Prayer, church community, pastoral care, and qualified medical or counseling support can belong together.

马太福音 6 章处理的是焦虑、分裂的忠诚，并呼召门徒信靠父；它不应被用来羞辱有焦虑症、抑郁、创伤或严重困扰的人。祷告、教会群体、牧养关怀，以及合格的医疗或心理咨询支持，可以彼此配合。

### “Seek first” does not erase daily work | “先求”不取消日常责任

Jesus is not telling disciples to abandon work, planning, or provision. He reorders what they serve and trust. The passage challenges anxious self-sufficiency, not faithful vocation or ordinary wisdom.

耶稣不是叫门徒放弃工作、计划或供养；祂重新安排人所事奉和所信靠的对象。经文挑战的是焦虑的自给自足，不是忠心的职业责任或日常智慧。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. What worry am I trying to solve through control rather than trust?
2. Which truth about the Father do I need to speak into that worry today?
3. Who can pray with me and help me resist carrying this alone?

1. 我正试图用掌控而非信靠来解决哪一种忧虑？
2. 今天我需要用哪一条关于天父的真理来回应它？
3. 我可以邀请谁与我一起祷告，不再独自承担？

## Prayer | 祷告

Father, you know every need and fear I carry. Forgive my attempts to rule what belongs in your hands. Help me seek your kingdom first, receive today's grace, and entrust tomorrow to your wise and loving care.

天父，你知道我所背负的每一个需要和惧怕。求你赦免我想掌控本该交在你手中的事。帮助我先求你的国，领受今日的恩典，并把明天交托给你智慧又慈爱的看顾。
