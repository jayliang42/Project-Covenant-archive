## Big Idea | 核心信息

Peter tells believers not to be surprised by fiery trial. Suffering for Christ does not prove that God has abandoned his people; it can reveal and refine faith, identify believers with Christ, and become an occasion for the Spirit's sustaining presence. The enduring call is neither passivity nor denial: entrust yourself to God and keep doing good.

彼得告诉信徒，不要以火炼般的试验为奇怪。为基督受苦并不证明神离弃祂的子民；它能显明并炼净信心，使信徒与基督认同，也成为圣灵扶持同在的处境。持续的呼召既不是消极，也不是否认痛苦：把自己交托给神，并继续行善。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Fiery trials test and reveal faith | 火炼显明并检验信心

The sermon distinguishes suffering from a sign that something has necessarily gone wrong. Trials expose what faith is built on and press believers to depend on Christ rather than comfort or control.

讲道区分了受苦与“事情必定出了错”的结论。试炼会显明信心建立在什么之上，也催促信徒倚靠基督，而不是倚靠舒适或掌控。

### 2. Sharing Christ's sufferings has meaning | 参与基督的苦难有意义

Peter can command joy because suffering for Christ is held within the path from Christ's suffering to glory. This does not make pain pleasant; it says pain is not empty or ultimate when the Spirit of glory rests on God's people.

彼得能呼召人喜乐，因为为基督受苦被放在“基督受苦而进入荣耀”的道路中。这不是说痛苦变得令人愉快，而是说当荣耀的灵住在神子民身上时，痛苦不是空洞的，也不是终极的。

### 3. Entrust yourself and do good | 交托自己并继续行善

The final command names God “faithful Creator.” Believers may not control the outcome, but they know the One to whom they belong. Jesus' trust of the Father at the cross gives the pattern and ground for persevering obedience.

最后的命令称神为“信实的创造主”。信徒也许不能掌控结果，却知道自己属于谁。耶稣在十字架上对父的交托，为持续顺服提供榜样和根基。

## Notes | 注释

### Not every suffering is persecution | 并非每一种受苦都是逼迫

The immediate focus is suffering connected to Christ's name, not a blanket explanation for every illness, loss, or injustice. Yet the call to trust the faithful Creator and do good offers real comfort in many kinds of hardship.

直接的重点是为基督的名所受的苦，不是为每一种疾病、失去或不公提供一概而论的解释。然而，信靠信实的创造主并继续行善的呼召，能在许多艰难中带来真实安慰。

## Prayer | 祷告

Faithful Creator, hold us in our suffering. Keep us from shame and despair, make us steadfast in doing good, and fix our eyes on Jesus, who entrusted himself to you.

信实的创造主，求你在受苦中托住我们。保守我们脱离羞愧与绝望，使我们坚定行善，并把眼目定在那位将自己交托给你的耶稣身上。
