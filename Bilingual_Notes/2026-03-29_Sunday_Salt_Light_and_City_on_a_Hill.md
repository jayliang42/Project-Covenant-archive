## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus calls people whom the world may regard as insignificant “salt of the earth” and “light of the world.” Their identity is not self-generated influence. Jesus is the true light and the fulfillment of God's [covenant](../Glossary/README.md#covenant) promises; united to him, his people preserve, illuminate, and make the Father's goodness visible through good works.<br>
耶稣称那些在世界看来或许微不足道的人为“世上的盐”“世上的光”。他们的身份不是自我制造的影响力。耶稣是真光，也是神[圣约](../Glossary/README.md#covenant)[应许](../Glossary/README.md#promise)的成全；与祂联合，祂的子民借着善行保守、照明，使父的美善被人看见。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. A new identity comes from the King | 新身份来自君王

The Beatitudes have described the kind of people Jesus blesses; salt and light name their public vocation. Kingdom identity is received from [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) before it becomes visible in mission.<br>
八福已经描述耶稣所称为有福的人；盐和光则指出他们公开的使命。天国身份先从[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)领受，之后才在使命中被看见。

### 2. Salt points to covenant faithfulness | 盐指向圣约的信实

Salt could preserve food and appears in [Old Testament](../Glossary/README.md#old-testament) sacrificial and covenant imagery. The sermon uses these backgrounds to describe a people whose faithful presence resists decay and points to God's enduring promise.<br>
盐能保存食物，也出现在旧约的[献祭](../Glossary/README.md#sacrifice)和圣约图像中。讲道借这些背景描述一种子民：他们忠心的同在抵抗败坏，并指向神长存的应许。

### 3. Jesus is the true light | 耶稣是真光

Isaiah's servant and John’s [Gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel) show that the light for the nations is ultimately Christ. [Disciples](../Glossary/README.md#disciple) shine only as a reflected light, so that people glorify the Father rather than the disciples themselves.<br>
以赛亚书的仆人和约翰福音表明，照亮万邦的光最终是基督。[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)只是反射的光，因此人当荣耀父，而不是荣耀门徒自己。

### 4. Good works make God visible | 善行使神被看见

The city on a hill is not a call to self-promotion. It is a communal witness: ordinary acts of mercy, truth, justice, hospitality, and courage make the Father's character credible before neighbors.<br>
山上之城不是自我宣传的呼召，而是群体性的见证：平凡的怜悯、真实、公义、接待与勇气，使父的品格在邻舍面前变得可信。

## Notes | 注释

### Salt and light should not become culture-war slogans | 盐与光不应成为文化战争口号

Jesus gives this identity to people marked by poverty of spirit, mercy, meekness, and peacemaking. Witness therefore cannot be separated from humility, enemy-love, truthfulness, and costly good works.<br>
耶稣把这种身份赐给灵里贫穷、怜悯、温柔、使人和睦的人。因此见证不能与谦卑、爱仇敌、真实和付代价的善行分开。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Am I seeking visibility for myself or visibility for the Father's goodness?
   我是在寻求自己被看见，还是寻求父的美善被看见？
2. Where can my ordinary faithfulness resist decay and bring light this week?
   这周我可以在哪个地方借着平凡的忠心抵抗败坏、带来光？
3. How can my [church](../Glossary/README.md#church) become a more truthful and welcoming “city” for neighbors?
   我的[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)怎样能成为对邻舍更真实、更有接待感的“城”？

## Prayer | 祷告

Light of the world, make us reflect you rather than ourselves. Preserve us in faithfulness, give us courage for good works, and let our shared life lead our neighbors to glorify the Father.<br>
世界的光，求你使我们反映你，而非反映自己。保守我们忠心，赐我们行善的勇气，并让我们共同的生命带领邻舍荣耀父。
