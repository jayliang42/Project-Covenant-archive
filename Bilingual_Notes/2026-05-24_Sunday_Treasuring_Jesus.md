## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus does not condemn responsible provision or wise stewardship. He exposes the heart that stores up wealth for itself, fixes its vision on temporary gain, and tries to serve both God and money. [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) is the better treasure: he treasures his people, gives the light of life, and frees them from slavery to greed.<br>
耶稣不是定罪负责的供养或明智的管理；祂所揭露的是那颗为自己积攒、把目光钉在短暂收益上、又想同时事奉神和金钱的心。[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)才是更好的宝藏：祂珍爱祂的子民、赐下生命之光，也释放人脱离贪婪的奴役。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Treasures reveal the direction of the heart | 财宝显明心的方向

Earthly goods can decay, be lost, or fail to survive death. This is why they cannot bear the weight of ultimate hope. “Store up treasures in heaven” is connected in the sermon to generosity, hospitality, [grace](../Glossary/README.md#grace), [forgiveness](../Glossary/README.md#forgiveness), and other good works flowing from love for Christ.<br>
地上的财物会朽坏、失去，也不能越过死亡，因此不能承受终极盼望的重量。讲道把“积攒财宝在天上”连到慷慨、接待、恩慈、饶恕和其他由爱基督所流出的善行。

### 2. The eye needs the light of Christ | 眼目需要基督的光

The sermon reads the “healthy eye” passage as a call to examine what shapes our spiritual vision. Greed, comparison, status, and destructive online habits can fill life with darkness. Jesus, the light of the world, reorients the gaze through Scripture and prayer.<br>
讲道把“眼睛就是身上的灯”应用为：要省察什么正在塑造我们的属灵眼光。贪婪、比较、地位追逐，以及破坏性的网络习惯，都可能使生命充满黑暗。耶稣是世界的光；祂借着[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)和祷告重新校准人的眼目。

### 3. We cannot serve God and money | 人不能同时事奉神和玛门

Jesus presents a matter of allegiance, not simply budgeting technique. Money is a useful servant but a destructive master. The sermon contrasts slavery to self-serving wealth with belonging to a gracious King who gives himself to set his people free.<br>
耶稣所提出的是忠诚归属的问题，不只是理财技巧。金钱可以是有用的仆人，却是毁坏人的主人。讲道把服事自我中心的财富，与归属于那位为释放子民而舍己的恩慈君王作对比。

### 4. Stewardship is a response of trust | 管家职分是信靠的回应

Practical applications include giving, planning, budgeting, generosity, and examining ambition. These are not ways to earn entrance into God's kingdom. They are possible responses of people who trust that God provides and who want their work and resources to honor Christ.<br>
实际应用包括奉献、计划、预算、慷慨和省察野心。这些不是赚取进入[神国](../Glossary/README.md#kingdom)资格的方法，而是信靠神供应、并愿意让工作与资源尊荣基督之人的回应。

## Notes | 注释

### Money is not the root of all evil | 金钱不是万恶之根

1 Timothy 6:10 says that the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. The distinction matters. Scripture can commend work, provision, saving, and generosity while warning against wealth becoming identity, security, or master.<br>
提摩太前书 6:10 说的是“贪财是万恶之根”，并非“钱本身是万恶之根”。这一区分很重要：圣经可以肯定工作、供养、储蓄和慷慨，同时警告财富不能成为人的身份、安全感或主人。

### Heavenly treasure is not a prosperity formula | 天上的财宝不是成功神学公式

Good works do not purchase prosperity or guarantee a preferred outcome. They belong to the life of [faith](../Glossary/README.md#faith) and point toward God's coming kingdom. The sermon’s practical examples are best received as [wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) for stewardship, not universal rules for every financial situation.<br>
善行不会购买繁荣，也不保证我们想要的结果。它们属于[信心](../Glossary/README.md#faith)生活，并指向神将来的国度。讲道中的实际例子应当被理解为管理资源的[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)，而不是适用于每一个人财务状况的普遍规则。

2. What is currently shaping my vision: Christ's word or comparison and consumption?

3. What one concrete act of generosity or faithful stewardship could I take this week?
   这周我可以作出哪一项具体的慷慨或忠心管理的行动？

## Prayer | 祷告

Lord Jesus, you are the treasure that does not perish. Free me from serving what cannot save me. Give me clear sight, a generous heart, and wisdom to use every resource as a grateful response to your love.<br>
主耶稣，你是永不朽坏的至宝。求你释放我，不再事奉那些不能拯救我的事物。赐我清明的眼光、慷慨的心，并赐我智慧，使我以感恩回应你的爱来使用每一项资源。
