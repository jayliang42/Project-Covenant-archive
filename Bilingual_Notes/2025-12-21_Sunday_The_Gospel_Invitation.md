The sermon treats invitation as ordinary but potentially life-changing faithfulness. Albert McMakin's invitation to Billy Graham illustrates how God may use a small act beyond what the inviter can see. Philip and the Ethiopian official provide the biblical pattern: listen, notice spiritual openness, explain Scripture around Jesus, and leave the response to God.<br>
讲道把邀请看作平凡却可能改变生命的忠心。Albert McMakin 对 Billy Graham 的邀请说明，神能使用邀请者看不见其后果的一小步。腓利与埃塞俄比亚太监提供[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)模式：倾听、留意属灵的开放、围绕耶稣解释经文，并把回应交给神。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. God works through ordinary witnesses | 神借着普通见证人工作

Philip obeys God's direction, comes near, asks a genuine question, and opens Scripture. The focus is not an impressive sales pitch but availability to the Spirit and care for a particular person.<br>
腓利顺服神的引导，走近对方、提出真诚的问题，并打开经文。重点不是一段高明的话术，而是愿意顺服[圣灵](../Glossary/README.md#holy-spirit)，并关心一个具体的人。

### 2. The message is Jesus | 所传的是耶稣

The Ethiopian official is reading Isaiah 53, and Philip explains the good news about Jesus. Christian invitation is not chiefly an invitation to improve one's life or join an event; it is an invitation to encounter the crucified and risen [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ).<br>
埃塞俄比亚太监正在读以赛亚书 53 章，腓利向他传讲耶稣的福音。基督徒的邀请首先不是邀请人改善生活或参加活动，而是邀请人遇见被钉十字架又[复活](../Glossary/README.md#resurrection)的[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)。

### 3. Courage and freedom belong together | 勇气与自由必须并存

The congregation is encouraged to name people, pray, and make a clear invitation. At the same time, conversion belongs to God. Love speaks truthfully without pressure, respects a person's freedom, and continues to care whether or not the invitation is accepted.<br>
讲道鼓励会众具体地想起人名、祷告、作出清楚邀请；同时，归信属于神。爱会真实开口，却不施压，尊重人的自由，并且无论邀请是否被接受，都继续关怀。

## Notes | 注释

### Evangelism is not filling seats | 传福音不是填满座位

A [church](../Glossary/README.md#church) gathering can be a helpful place for someone to hear the [gospel](../Glossary/README.md#gospel), but the [New Testament](../Glossary/README.md#new-testament) goal is reconciliation to God and incorporation into Christ's people. Numbers never justify treating people as projects.<br>
[教会](../Glossary/README.md#church)聚会可以是人听见福音的有益场所，但新约的目标是人与神和好，并被接入基督的子民中。人数从不能成为把人当项目的理由。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Who has helped me hear the good news of Jesus?
   谁曾帮助我听见耶稣的好消息？
2. Who is God placing on my heart to pray for and listen to?
   神把谁放在我心上，让我为他祷告并认真倾听？
3. What is one natural, respectful invitation I could extend?
   我可以发出哪一个自然、尊重人的邀请？

## Prayer | 祷告

Jesus, thank you for those who spoke your good news to me. Give me love rather than fear, [wisdom](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom) to listen, and courage to speak your name with gentleness and respect.<br>
耶稣，感谢你使用他人向我传讲你的好消息。求你赐我爱而不是惧怕，赐我倾听的[智慧](../Glossary/README.md#wisdom)，也赐我以温柔和尊重说出你名的勇气。
