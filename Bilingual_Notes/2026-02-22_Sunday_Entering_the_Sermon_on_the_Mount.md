## Big Idea | 核心信息

The introduction contrasts Jesus' teaching with modern life systems promising security, efficiency, wealth, or self-mastery. The Sermon on the Mount is not tame advice that can be adjusted to suit our preferences. It exposes every false foundation and invites hearers to build on Jesus by hearing and doing his words.

这篇导论把耶稣的教导与现代人生方案作对比：这些方案承诺安全、效率、财富或自我掌控。登山宝训不是可以按个人喜好调节的温和建议；它揭露每一个错误根基，并邀请听众听见、遵行耶稣的话，把生命建在祂之上。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Competing stories promise the good life | 竞争的故事都在承诺好生活

Financial strategies, career plans, lifestyle brands, and self-help systems may contain useful wisdom, but they become rival gospels when they promise ultimate security or identity. Jesus calls people to repent because God's kingdom has come near in him.

财务策略、职业计划、生活方式品牌和自助系统可能包含有用智慧，但当它们承诺终极安全或身份时，就会变成竞争性的福音。耶稣呼召人悔改，因为神的国已经在祂里面临近。

### 2. Jesus speaks with authority | 耶稣带着权柄说话

The sermon is demanding because Jesus is not merely offering opinions. He reveals the Father's will, exposes heart-level sin, and describes the life of those who live under his reign.

登山宝训之所以令人震撼，是因耶稣不只是在提出意见。祂显明父的旨意，揭露内心层面的罪，并描述活在祂掌权之下的人会有的生命。

### 3. Hearing must become doing | 听见必须成为遵行

Matthew 7's builders show that knowledge without obedience is unstable. Doing Jesus' words does not earn the kingdom; it displays a life that has received and trusts the King.

马太福音 7 章的两种盖房者表明，只有知识而没有顺服是不稳固的。遵行耶稣的话不是赚取天国，而是显明一个已经领受并信靠君王的生命。

## Notes | 注释

### Grace and obedience belong together | 恩典与顺服属于一起

The Sermon on the Mount cannot be reduced either to legalism or to optional ideals. Jesus' commands are impossible apart from grace, yet grace brings people into a real, embodied obedience shaped by the Spirit.

登山宝训既不能被缩小成律法主义，也不能被当作可选的理想。离开恩典，耶稣的命令不可能被活出；但恩典会把人带进圣灵塑造、真实具身的顺服。

3. 这周我需要领受并实践耶稣的哪一项命令？

## Prayer | 祷告

君王耶稣，求你使我们离开虚假的根基，教我们走你的道路。赐我们聆听的恩典、信靠的信心，以及每天顺服你话语的勇气。
