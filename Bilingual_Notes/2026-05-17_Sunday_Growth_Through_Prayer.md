## Big Idea | 核心信息

The Lord's Prayer is presented as a pattern for fellowship, not a formula for controlling God. Prayer begins with the gift of calling God “our Father,” then moves through praise, repentance, dependence for daily needs, and yielded trust in trial. The central invitation is not pressure to perform but a child's honest approach to a loving Father.<br>
主祷文在这篇讲道中被看作相交的模式，而不是操控神的公式。祷告从“我们在天上的父”这份恩典开始，接着经过赞美、悔改、每日的倚靠，以及在试炼中降服的信靠。中心邀请不是要人表现得更好，而是让儿女诚实地来到慈爱的父面前。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. P — Praise: begin with “our Father” | P — 赞美：从“我们在天上的父”开始

“Our Father” names adoption, welcome, and a shared family. The sermon emphasizes that Christians do not approach God as employees seeking evaluation, but as children received through Christ. “Hallowed be your name,” “your kingdom come,” and “your will be done” turn prayer first toward God's character, reign, and purposes.<br>
“我们在天上的父”宣告收纳、欢迎和共同的家。讲道强调，基督徒不是作为等待考核的雇员来到神面前，而是因基督被接纳的儿女。“愿人都尊你的名为圣”“愿你的国降临”“愿你的旨意成全”先把祷告转向神的品格、掌权和旨意。

### 2. R — Repent: receive forgiveness and extend it | R — 悔改：领受赦免，也饶恕他人

“Forgive us our debts” treats sin as a real debt before God, one Christ has paid at the cross. The sermon carefully frames forgiveness of others as the fruit of received grace, not a payment that purchases God's forgiveness. Deep wounds may require a long, grace-dependent path; the call is to keep moving away from bitterness toward forgiveness.<br>
“免我们的债”把罪看作人在神面前真实的亏欠，而基督已在十字架上担当这债。讲道谨慎说明：饶恕他人是已经领受恩典的果子，不是用来购买神赦免的代价。深重的伤害可能需要漫长、倚靠恩典的过程；呼召是持续离开苦毒，走向饶恕。

### 3. A — Ask: bring daily needs to the Father | A — 祈求：把每日需要带到父面前

“Give us today our daily bread” makes room for ordinary needs as well as spiritual ones. The wilderness manna provides the sermon’s picture of daily dependence: God does not invite anxious self-sufficiency, but repeated trust that he knows and cares for his children.<br>
“我们日用的饮食，今日赐给我们”让日常实际需要和属灵需要都可以被带到父面前。旷野的吗哪成为讲道的图像：神没有邀请人焦虑地自给自足，而是呼召人反复信靠祂知道、也看顾祂儿女的需要。

### 4. Y — Yield: trust God in testing and temptation | Y — 降服：在试炼与诱惑中信靠神

“Lead us not into temptation” is read alongside James 1:13: God does not tempt anyone. The prayer acknowledges human weakness in testing and asks for protection from evil and self-rule. Jesus' prayer in Gethsemane—“not my will, but yours”—is the decisive model and saving ground for this yielded life.<br>
“不叫我们遇见试探”要与雅各书 1:13 一起理解：神不试探人。这祷告承认人在试炼中的软弱，求神保守我们脱离恶者和自我作主。耶稣在客西马尼园“不要成就我的意思，只要成就你的意思”的祷告，是这种降服生命最决定性的榜样和救恩根基。

## Notes | 注释

### The Lord's Prayer is pattern and prayer | 主祷文既是范式，也可直接祷告

The sermon rightly resists treating the prayer as a magical formula. At the same time, the historic church has also prayed Jesus' words directly. These uses belong together: the prayer shapes our desires and can be prayed as Christ gave it.<br>
讲道正确地拒绝把主祷文当成有魔力的公式；同时，历代教会也一直直接祷告耶稣所教的话。两者可以并存：主祷文塑造我们的愿望，也可以照着基督所赐的文字来祷告。

### “Our” Father | “我们”的父

The plural matters. Prayer is personal but not private property. The Father gathers believers into one family, which places forgiveness, provision, and spiritual struggle inside a communal life.<br>
复数的“我们”很重要。祷告是个人性的，却不是私人所有物。父把信徒聚为一个家，因此赦免、供应和属灵争战都发生在群体生活之中。

## Practice | 实践

Set aside five unhurried minutes each day this week. Begin only with “Father,” then use one line from the prayer: praise his name, name a need, confess a debt, or yield a situation you cannot control.<br>
这一周每天留出五分钟，不急着完成任务。先只称呼“父”，然后用主祷文中的一句：赞美祂的名、说出一个需要、承认一项亏欠，或把一个无法控制的处境交给祂。

## Prayer | 祷告

Our Father, thank you for receiving us through your Son. Teach us to seek your name, kingdom, and will before our own control. Give us what we need today, form forgiving hearts in us, and keep us near you in every trial.<br>
我们在天上的父，感谢你借着爱子接纳我们。教我们先寻求你的名、你的国和你的旨意，而不是自己的掌控。求你赐下我们今日所需，塑造我们饶恕的心，并在一切试炼中保守我们亲近你。
