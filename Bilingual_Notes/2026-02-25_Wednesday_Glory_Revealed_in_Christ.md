## Big Idea | 核心信息

The study asks what Christians mean when they say “give God glory.” God does not need human applause to become greater. To glorify God is to recognize and declare the weight, beauty, and goodness that are already his. Jesus overturns proud images of divinity: his miracles reveal glory, and his cross reveals most deeply that God's glory is self-giving love.<br>
学习追问基督徒说“荣耀归给神”时究竟是什么意思。神不需要人的掌声来变得更大。荣耀神是承认并宣告祂本已有的重量、美丽和良善。耶稣颠覆了骄傲的神观：祂的神迹显明荣耀，而祂的十字架最深地显明，神的荣耀是舍己的爱。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Glory is God's radiant goodness | 荣耀是神光辉的良善

Glory names God's weight, worth, brightness, and life-giving reality. It is outgoing rather than self-enclosed: God makes himself known so creatures may be enlightened and live.<br>
荣耀指向神的重量、价值、光辉与赐生命的真实。它不是封闭自守的，而是向外显明：神使自己被人认识，好叫受造者得光、得生命。

### 2. Jesus reveals the Father | 耶稣显明父

In Jesus' compassion, signs, humility, and service, people see what God is like. The Son is not an exception to an otherwise harsh God; he is the true revelation of the Father.<br>
在耶稣的怜悯、神迹、谦卑与服事中，人看见神是什么样。圣子不是一位本来严酷之神的例外；祂是真正显明父的那一位。

### 3. The cross is glory in its deepest form | 十字架是荣耀最深的形状

The cross appears humiliating by worldly standards, yet it reveals the depth of God's self-giving love. It judges false ideas of a God who merely demands service and opens a new way of knowing the Father.<br>
按世界标准，十字架似乎是羞辱；但它显明神舍己之爱的深度。它审判那种只会要求人服侍的假神观，并为认识父开启新的道路。

## Notes | 注释

### Glory is not divine vanity | 荣耀不是神的虚荣

Because God is the source of all goodness, delighting in his glory is not like a creature demanding praise to repair insecurity. His self-revelation is gift: it draws people away from false glories that cannot give life.<br>
神是一切良善的源头，所以祂以自己的荣耀为乐，不像受造者为了弥补不安全感而索求赞美。祂的自我显明是礼物，把人从不能赐生命的虚假荣耀中引出。
