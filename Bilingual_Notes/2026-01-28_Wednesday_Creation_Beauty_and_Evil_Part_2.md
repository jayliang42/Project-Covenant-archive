## Big Idea | 核心信息

The study continues by seeing the created world as good, beautiful, and expressive of God's outgoing generosity. It also asks how evil can exist in a world made by a good God. The proposed framework is that God grants creatures real existence and meaningful freedom; evil is a parasitic turning away from the good, not an independent power equal to God.

学习继续把受造界看作美好、良善、显明神向外慷慨的现实；它也追问良善的神所造世界中为何有邪恶。所提出的框架是：神赋予受造者真实的存在与有意义的自由；邪恶是寄生于良善之上的背离，不是与神同等的独立力量。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Creation is good and worth receiving | 创造是美好的，值得领受

Beauty, abundance, music, and harmony can become occasions for worship because they point beyond themselves to the Giver. The study resists treating the world as a meaningless warehouse or as a rival to God.

美、丰盛、音乐与和声都能成为敬拜的契机，因为它们把人指向赐予者。学习拒绝把世界视为无意义的仓库，也拒绝把世界看成与神竞争的对手。

### 2. Evil distorts rather than creates | 邪恶是扭曲，不是创造

Evil does not have equal status with God's goodness. It twists creaturely love inward and damages relationships, yet it cannot finally undo the harmonious life of Father, Son, and Spirit.

邪恶并不与神的良善同等。它把受造者的爱扭向自我、破坏关系，却不能最终破坏父、子、圣灵和谐的生命。

### 3. Love does not control | 爱不控制

The study makes a pastoral application: love gives space and seeks another's good, rather than trying to control another person. This is a limited human analogy, but it helps distinguish self-giving love from possessiveness.

学习作出牧养性应用：爱会给空间、寻求对方益处，而不试图控制另一个人。这只是有限的人类类比，却能帮助人分辨舍己的爱与占有欲。

## Notes | 注释

### The problem of evil remains weighty | 邪恶问题仍然沉重

Freedom explains neither every tragedy nor God's full providence. Christian hope ultimately looks to the cross, resurrection, final judgment, and new creation. These truths allow lament as well as trust; they must never be used to give easy answers to sufferers.

自由并不能解释每一场悲剧，也不能穷尽神的护理。基督徒盼望最终仰望十字架、复活、末后审判和新创造。这些真理容许人哀哭也容许人信靠，绝不可被用来向受苦者给出轻率答案。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Where do I notice beauty in creation and receive it with gratitude?
2. How has self-centered love distorted one of my relationships?
3. What lament do I need to bring honestly to God rather than explain away?

1. 我在哪里注意到创造之美，并以感恩领受它？
2. 以自我为中心的爱怎样扭曲了我的一段关系？
3. 我需要把哪一种哀伤诚实地带到神面前，而不是急着解释？
