## Big Idea | 核心信息

The message calls believers to be the church rather than merely attend church. Christmas is presented as a season when people may be more open to spiritual conversation, welcome, and invitation. The aim is not attendance for its own sake, but helping people encounter Christ and take an honest next step toward him.

信息呼召信徒不只是去教会，而是活出教会。圣诞被看作人可能更愿意进行属灵对话、接受接待与邀请的时节。目标不是为了人数本身，而是帮助人遇见基督，并诚实地向祂迈出下一步。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. The church is a people, not an audience | 教会是一群子民，不是一批观众

To belong to Christ is to participate in a community of worship, care, and witness. The sermon challenges passive consumption and invites believers to take responsibility for welcome, prayer, and relationship.

属乎基督就意味着参与一个敬拜、关怀与见证的群体。讲道挑战被动消费，并邀请信徒为接待、祷告和关系承担责任。

### 2. Christmas gives a natural opening | 圣诞提供自然的切入点

The birth of Jesus is good news of God's nearness and grace. Seasonal gatherings can create a hospitable setting for people who are curious, lonely, grieving, or spiritually searching.

耶稣的降生是神亲近人、施恩给人的好消息。节期聚会能为好奇、孤独、哀伤或正在寻找的人提供一个有接待感的场景。

### 3. Invitation needs presence and care | 邀请需要陪伴与关怀

An invitation is strongest when it is joined to prayer, listening, practical welcome, and continued friendship. The congregation is called not simply to invite someone to a program, but to make room for that person in real community.

当邀请配合祷告、倾听、实际接待和持续友谊时，才最有力量。教会被呼召不只是邀请人参加一个节目，而是在真实群体中为那个人腾出位置。

## Notes | 注释

### Invitation is witness, not manipulation | 邀请是见证，不是操控

Christian witness tells the truth about Christ while honoring the other person's dignity and freedom. A faithful invitation does not coerce a decision or make a person's response a measure of the inviter's worth.

基督徒见证是在尊重对方尊严和自由的同时，说出关于基督的真理。忠心的邀请不强迫人作决定，也不把对方的回应当作邀请者价值的指标。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Who may need a sincere welcome this Christmas season?
2. How can I pray and listen before I speak?
3. What would it look like to help someone belong, not merely attend once?

1. 这个圣诞季，谁可能需要真诚的接待？
2. 我怎样能先祷告、先倾听，再开口？
3. 我怎样能帮助一个人归属，而不只是参加一次？

## Prayer | 祷告

Jesus, make our church a truthful and welcoming witness to you. Give us love for our neighbors, courage to speak naturally, and patience to walk with people wherever they are.

耶稣，求你使我们的教会成为对你真实又有接待感的见证。赐我们爱邻舍的心、自然开口的勇气，以及陪伴人在他们所在之处的耐心。
