## Big Idea | 核心信息

The service looks back on the congregation's history, its new building, ministries, and changed lives in order to say that the church's story is fundamentally God's work. Buildings and programs are not treated as the goal. They are means through which people may meet Jesus, receive care, and join his mission.<br>
聚会回顾教会的历史、新堂、事工与生命改变，为要宣告：教会的故事根本上是神的工作。建筑和项目不是终点，而是让人认识耶稣、得着关怀、加入祂使命的途径。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Remember God's faithfulness | 记念神的信实

The service recalls earlier seasons of prayer, sacrificial giving, growth, hardship, and provision. Remembering does not glorify an institution; it trains gratitude and reminds a congregation that its future is received, not self-made.<br>
聚会回顾过去的祷告、牺牲的奉献、成长、艰难与供应。这样的记念不是高举机构，而是操练感恩，并提醒教会：未来是领受而来，不是靠自己制造出来。

### 2. The church is people on mission | 教会是被差派的群体

Testimonies of children, families, recovery, care, and local service show that “church” is more than a place to attend. It is a people who bear one another's burdens and point neighbors to Jesus.<br>
儿童、家庭、恢复、关怀与社区服事的见证显示，“教会”不只是一个去参加的地点，而是一群彼此担当重担、并把邻舍指向耶稣的人。

### 3. Build for the next generation | 为下一代建造

The move and expansion are framed as a stewardship question: how can present members serve people they may never meet? The answer offered is prayerful faithfulness, not confidence in scale, resources, or a building itself.<br>
搬迁与扩展被放在一个管家的问题里：今天的成员如何服事那些他们也许永远不会见到的人？讲道给出的答案是祷告中的忠心，而不是倚靠规模、资源或建筑本身。

## Notes | 注释

### Jesus builds his church | 耶稣建立祂的教会

The language echoes Matthew 16:18. Human planning, generosity, and service matter, but they cannot replace [Christ](../Glossary/README.md#christ) as the church's foundation and Lord. This keeps gratitude from becoming institutional self-congratulation.<br>
这里呼应马太福音 16:18。人的规划、奉献和服事都重要，但不能取代[基督](../Glossary/README.md#christ)作为教会的根基与主；这也使感恩不至于变成对机构的自我夸耀。

### Buildings are gifts, not the church itself | 建筑是礼物，不是教会本身

Scripture calls believers God's household and [temple](../Glossary/README.md#temple). A building may greatly serve worship and mission, but church health is measured by faithful discipleship, love, truth, and witness rather than facilities alone.<br>
[圣经](../Glossary/README.md#bible)称信徒为神的家和[圣殿](../Glossary/README.md#temple)。建筑可以很好地服务于敬拜与使命，但教会健康应看忠心的[门徒](../Glossary/README.md#disciple)训练、爱、真理与见证，而不只看设施。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. What evidence of God's faithfulness should I remember with gratitude?
   有哪些神信实的证据，我需要带着感恩记念？
2. How can I serve the church as a people and mission, not merely attend an event?
   我怎样能把教会看作一个人群与使命，而不只是参加一场活动？
3. What resource, gift, or time has God entrusted to me for someone else?
   神托付给我的哪一种资源、恩赐或时间，可以为了别人使用？

## Prayer | 祷告

Lord Jesus, thank you for your faithfulness to your people. Keep us from trusting buildings or success more than you. Form us into a humble, generous, and courageous church that helps people know you.<br>
主耶稣，感谢你对你子民的信实。保守我们不去更倚靠建筑或成功，而是倚靠你。塑造我们成为谦卑、慷慨、有勇气的教会，使人能认识你。
