## Big Idea | 核心信息

Jesus begins the Beatitudes by blessing the people a success-driven culture overlooks. Spiritual poverty, mourning, and meekness are not techniques for earning favor or romanticizing pain. They describe the posture of people who know their need, grieve what is broken, and entrust strength to God's kingdom.<br>
耶稣以八福开始，称那些成功文化容易忽略的人为有福。灵里贫穷、哀恸与温柔不是赚取恩宠的技巧，也不是浪漫化痛苦；它们描述一群人的姿态：知道自己的需要，为破碎而哀伤，并把力量交给神的国。

## Outline | 讲道结构

### 1. Poor in spirit receive the kingdom | 虚心的人领受天国

To be poor in spirit is not self-hatred or lack of personality. It is the honest recognition that we have no independent spiritual capital to bring before God. The kingdom is received as gift.<br>
虚心不是自我厌弃或没有个性，而是诚实承认：我们没有独立的属灵资本可以带到神面前。天国是作为礼物领受的。

### 2. Those who mourn will be comforted | 哀恸的人必得安慰

Kingdom mourning includes grief over loss, evil, sin, and a world not yet made new. Jesus does not say sorrow is good in itself; he promises God's comfort and future restoration.<br>
天国里的哀恸包括为失去、邪恶、罪以及尚未更新的世界而忧伤。耶稣不是说忧伤本身就是好，而是应许神的安慰与将来的复兴。

### 3. The meek will inherit the earth | 温柔的人必承受地土

Meekness is strength yielded to God, not weakness or silence in the face of injustice. It refuses the world's grasping way of securing an inheritance and trusts God's promise of renewed creation.<br>
温柔是把力量交给神，不是软弱，也不是面对不公时沉默。它拒绝世界那种抓取产业的方式，信靠神对更新创造的应许。

## Notes | 注释

### Blessedness is deeper than a feeling | “有福”比感觉更深

The Beatitudes announce God's favor and kingdom reality; they do not guarantee constant emotional happiness. A person may mourn deeply and still be called blessed because God is near and his promise is secure.<br>
八福宣告神的恩眷与国度的真实，不保证人持续感到快乐。一个人可以深深哀恸，却仍被称为有福，因为神亲近他，祂的应许是稳固的。

## Reflection Questions | 反思问题

1. Where am I pretending to have enough spiritual strength on my own?
   我在哪些地方假装自己有足够的属灵力量？
2. What brokenness do I need to mourn before God rather than ignore?
   我需要在哪一种破碎上来到神面前哀恸，而不是忽略？
3. Where can I practice meek strength instead of grasping control?
   我可以在哪里操练温柔的力量，而不是抓取掌控？
